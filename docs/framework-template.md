## Year-round operating cycle

Plan the calendar and evidence requests; operate controls; collect and preserve
evidence; test against pre-approved methods; remediate findings; prepare
read-only audit support; monitor source and system changes; renew scope yearly.

## AI-agent rules

The agent may search, organize, draft, trace, test, and critique. It must cite
material assertions, keep evidence immutable, identify uncertainty, and obtain
approval before any write, configuration change, submission, closure, external
communication, risk acceptance, attestation, or certification statement.

## Evidence and test design

Record source, custodian, collection time, access restrictions, period covered,
integrity information, relevance, reliability, test objective, method,
population, sampling rationale, result, and exceptions.

## Specialized review

Require independent source, control, evidence, test-design, and skeptical
review. Preserve disagreements and have a named human adjudicate them.

## Findings, exceptions, and annual renewal

Separate observations, findings, and risks. Record owner, root cause, corrective
action, due date, human risk decision, retest criteria, result, and renewal
triggers.
