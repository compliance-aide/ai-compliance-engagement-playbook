## Source and applicability

Identify the publisher, official title, edition, publication/status date,
authoritative URLs, retrieval date and rights boundary. Distinguish binding law,
contract terms, voluntary guidance, technical specification and assurance scheme.
State applicability inputs and who resolves uncertainty. Identify historical,
superseded or draft material explicitly; never infer the governing edition from
the filename. Link the guide's source/review record and state actual review status.

## Engagement focus

Name the concrete deliverable and the process, system, people or organization it
covers. State what the work can establish and the conclusions it cannot support.
Explain this framework's distinct purpose rather than repeating a generic
security or privacy lifecycle.

## Roles

Name the accountable decision roles, operator/evidence custodians and independent
review function. Define authorized AI help and decisions reserved to people.
Reuse existing valid authority. Never represent the author as an independent
reviewer or assume that AI capability expands authority.

## Before starting

List framework-specific input records and required decisions. For each missing
input, say what may be drafted or collected and what must wait. Link the
[agent runbook](agent-runbook.md) (use `../agent-runbook.md` inside a guide) and
[work-item record](../templates/work-item.md) (use `../../templates/work-item.md`
inside a guide). Explain any role, jurisdiction, edition or licensing gate.

## Failure branches and decisions

Describe this framework's likely wrong-scope, wrong-version, missing-evidence,
conflicting-result and authority traps. Use the runbook result labels. Specify
what may continue, the exact decision that blocks dependent work, its owner and
required input. Escalate potentially time-critical events through the approved
route instead of waiting for a routine review.

## Cadence and renewal

Separate sourced binding deadlines from engagement-planning defaults. Record
trigger, owner, expected artifact, next due date/timezone and overdue escalation.
Include source/edition, system, data, supplier and incident changes where relevant.
Do not impose an annual certification or assessment cycle on a technical guide
or voluntary framework that does not require one.

## Completion and handoff

List the tangible final packet, outstanding matters, human review/decision,
retest evidence and next work queue. Make completion testable by another agent
with no conversation history. State the conclusion boundary. Completing a guide
is not compliance, a legal opinion, certification or an approved submission.
