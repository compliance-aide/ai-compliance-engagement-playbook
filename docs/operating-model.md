# Operating model

Use this lifecycle for each engagement: charter and scope; authoritative-source
inventory; interpretation notes; applicability and implementation planning;
evidence collection; testing; findings and remediation; audit support;
continuous monitoring; annual renewal.

The AI agent starts read-only and maintains separate records for source facts,
observed evidence, analysis, and recommendations. It preserves originals,
records provenance and collection time, and escalates conflicts or gaps rather
than synthesizing certainty.

Operator work implements and operates controls. Auditor work independently
examines, interviews, and tests. The AI may help either role organize work, but
it must not blur independence, approve its own work, or make the final audit
conclusion.
