# Source research policy

For each guide, cite the publisher's official title, edition, publication date,
access link, license, jurisdiction or contractual context, and change-monitoring
method. Prefer primary publisher material. Do not reproduce a source's
requirements, mappings, or assessment procedures unless its terms clearly allow
that public use.

The agent must label a statement as a source fact, a bounded paraphrase,
engagement-specific inference, or an open question. A second reviewer checks
each citation, version, and license before a guide is published.
