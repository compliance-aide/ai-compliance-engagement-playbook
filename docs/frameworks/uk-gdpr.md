# UK GDPR — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not UK legal advice. Check the [ICO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/)
> and current UK law with qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing and ownership records, schedule rights, supplier, retention,
training, and incident reviews, perform privacy-impact review before high-risk
changes, assess evidence quality after material events, and close with a
management-approved remediation and risk plan.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own factual inventory, decisions, and approved remediation. Independent
reviewers challenge scope and evidence without operating controls. AI organizes
evidence and change records and flags inconsistencies; it cannot choose a lawful
basis, decide individual rights, or submit reports. Recheck ICO updates and
jurisdictional context at every cycle.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [ICO accountability and governance guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/) and qualified UK privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not determine territorial scope, controller/processor role, lawful basis, exemption, transfer mechanism, breach-reporting duty, or an individual's rights outcome.

### 1. Accountability, processing, and DPIA decision record

- **Request and owner:** Governance and DPO/advice records, processing inventory, purpose/data-category/recipient fields, processor and international-transfer register, retention decisions, and human-approved DPIA screening inputs from privacy, product, and vendor owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected activity through system flow, stated purpose, owner, recipient/transfer, retention record, and DPIA decision trail. This supports a factual basis for review; it cannot decide lawful basis, DPIA threshold, role, or transfer legality.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized metadata and identify missing owners or stale links. Privacy and legal humans decide classifications and actions. Refresh before high-risk or materially changed processing, processor, transfer, or retention change.

### 2. Transparency, rights, security, and breach-operation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Notice/version history, rights-request and identity-verification procedures/logs, access/security/change evidence, processor instructions, breach exercise or incident timeline, and corrective-action records from operational, security, and privacy owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted sample to source, dates, owner, escalation, and follow-up while limiting personal data in workpapers. This can evidence recorded workflow operation; it cannot decide a rights response, establish security effectiveness, or determine notification duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare an evidence index and flag internal deadlines; it cannot communicate with data subjects, notify an authority, or decide an outcome. Recollect after a complaint trend, security event, material collection change, or exercise failure.

### 3. Processor, exception, and management-review record

- **Request and owner:** Processor due-diligence/change records, contract/instruction evidence, exception and remediation register, management-review minutes, risk decisions, retests, and ICO/source-change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception, processor change, or remediation item to approved authority, supporting facts, expiry, and retest. This can support governance traceability; it cannot accept risk, make a regulatory representation, or issue assurance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag overdue reviews and assemble a redacted review packet. Named humans approve risk, communications, contracts, and closure. Review annually and after material supplier, processing, or regulatory change.
