# Bermuda PIPA engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Bermuda legal text or a compliance conclusion. Confirm current requirements through the [Bermuda Privacy Commissioner’s Guide to PIPA](https://www.privacy.bm/guide-to-pipa).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a privacy-program record: processing and purpose inventory, responsible owners, notices, use and sharing decisions, supplier handling, individual requests, incidents, change reviews, and remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business and privacy owners maintain the record; security and supplier owners provide operating evidence; Bermuda counsel confirms applicability; independent reviewers test traceability. AI organizes approved evidence, identifies aging reviews, and drafts questions, but cannot interpret law, resolve rights requests, decide reportability, or submit to PrivCom. Review material changes before release and retain human decisions with rationale.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Bermuda Privacy Commissioner's Guide to PIPA](https://www.privacy.bm/guide-to-pipa) and qualified Bermuda counsel; checked 2026-07-31. This is original operational guidance; it does not reproduce PIPA material or decide organizational role, processing conditions, privacy-impact assessment need, individual rights, breach notification, or a PrivCom filing.

### 1. Purpose, accountability, and processing inventory

- **Request and owner:** Processing register tying purposes, personal-information categories, individuals, systems, accountable owners, service providers, disclosures, locations, notices, and retention to the privacy program and business owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processing change or route to purpose, source, owner, system, provider/disclosure record, and retention evidence. This supports a documented accountability trail; it cannot decide permissibility, consent, organizational role, or privacy-impact assessment requirements.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may detect missing relationships and stale reviews in approved metadata. Privacy/legal humans decide classifications and approvals. Refresh for a new purpose, system, provider, disclosure, or high-impact change.

### 2. Individual, access, and lifecycle operations

- **Request and owner:** Individual-request procedure and redacted case log, identity-verification controls, access review, notice/version record, correction/quality workflow, retention/disposal evidence, and workforce training.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a redacted request or disposal event from intake through human authority, source record, action, escalation, and closure. This supports operational traceability; it cannot decide identity, a request outcome, exception, or legal timing.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a redacted evidence index and internal review prompts; it cannot resolve requests or communicate outcomes. Recollect after a request pattern, notice/process change, or failed lifecycle test.

### 3. Service-provider, incident, and change-assurance record

- **Request and owner:** Service-provider register and instruction/assurance records, change-review or privacy-impact assessment inputs, incident/exercise timeline, complaint/escalation records, management decisions, and remediation/retest log.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected provider change, incident, or assessment input to factual basis, human authority, expiry/review date, corrective action, and retest. This supports accountable oversight; it cannot prove safeguards effective, accept risk, determine notification, or submit to PrivCom.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag gaps and prepare an approved-metadata review packet. Counsel, privacy, security, and communications owners decide external actions and closure. Refresh after a provider, incident, material change, or annual review.
