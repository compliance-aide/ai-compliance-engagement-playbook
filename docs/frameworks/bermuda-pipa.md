# Bermuda PIPA — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Bermuda legal advice or a compliance conclusion.

## Source and applicability

Use [PrivCom's Guide to PIPA](https://www.privacy.bm/guide-to-pipa) and the
[organisations hub](https://www.privacy.bm/organisations-hub) for legislation and
amendment references. Checked 2026-09-04. PrivCom confirms full effect from
1 January 2025 in its [implementation report](https://www.privacy.bm/post/the-office-of-the-privacy-commissioner-for-bermuda-privcom-is-releasing-statistics-for-q1-2025).
Resolve the organisation, use of personal information, exceptions and any parallel
sector duties with Bermuda counsel; do not treat an old implementation checklist
as the operative law.

[PrivCom breach guidance](https://www.privacy.bm/breach-of-security) describes the
likely-adverse-effect test and notification without undue delay to the Commissioner
and then affected individuals. Do not replace that standard with a generic 72-hour
timer or wait for a final investigation before escalating. See the
[author record](../refresh-reviews/bermuda-pipa.md).

## Engagement focus

Make privacy accountability observable across actual uses, providers, individual
requests and incidents. Tie each proposed conclusion to a source, scoped population,
observation and accountable decision rather than the existence of a privacy policy.

## Roles

The privacy officer coordinates the program; business owners supply purposes and
handling facts; security, records and providers supply operational evidence.
Bermuda counsel and authorised owners decide legal conditions, exceptions, rights
outcomes and notification. Independent reviewers challenge evidence. AI may index
approved metadata and draft workpapers; it cannot decide adverse effect, accept
risk, send rights responses or file with PrivCom without authority.

## Before starting

Obtain the current source and scope decisions, privacy officer/contact, processing
inventory, notices, provider/country register, retention rules, approved request
clocks, incident route and evidence permissions. Use fictional QA records and the
[agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Preserve unresolved scope without assuming
an exemption or exporting private evidence to this repository.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Establish accountability | Privacy/legal resolve scope, current source, owners and decision authority. | Dated applicability record and named privacy officer/decision route. |
| 2. Map actual use | Business/privacy connect purposes, information, systems, providers, recipients and retention. | Versioned inventory with uncovered uses visible. |
| 3. Review changes and providers | Privacy/legal compare notices, conditions and overseas provider arrangements with facts. | Approved decisions/instructions and open issues linked to each activity. |
| 4. Define rights verification | Privacy sets approved fictional access, correction and other applicable request cases, including identity and exception paths. | Expected outputs and source-specific clocks are documented before testing. |
| 5. Observe and reconcile | Authorised QA operators run cases and lifecycle checks; AI indexes results. | Actual actions/readback match the authorised decision and scoped record. |
| 6. Exercise breach escalation | Security/privacy use a fictional provider incident to test fact capture and timely legal/notification handoff. | Adverse-effect decision inputs, sequence and draft packet are traceable; no real notification sent. |
| 7. Correct and renew | Owners implement approved corrections; reviewer retests and governance reviews residual issues. | Findings, change receipts, retests and next queue with accountable decisions. |

## Evidence and test plan

| Evidence and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose/notice inventory, privacy | Trace actual use to the correct notice and approved decision. | Facts and representations agree. | A policy cannot prove all uses are covered. |
| Overseas provider records, supplier/privacy | Reconcile data routes, instructions and incident escalation with provider facts. | Relevant responsibilities and evidence owners are explicit. | Vendor assurance alone does not settle PIPA duties. |
| Fictional rights/disposal cases, records | Compare approved action and timing with observed source-system result. | Correct person's QA record changes or is returned; unrelated records stay protected. | A workflow status alone is insufficient readback. |
| Fictional breach timeline, security/privacy | Trace detection to owner review, decision and notification preparation. | Escalation does not wait for every forensic detail; unknowns are preserved. | An exercise cannot prove actual delivery or decide legal reportability. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Missing provider facts create a coverage gap and an owned request. New purposes or
recipients require the appropriate decision before the affected disclosure.
Potential breaches use prompt incident/privacy escalation with facts and uncertainty;
AI must not infer no adverse effect from no confirmed harm. Failed rights or disposal
cases remain findings. After interruption, preserve the original case clock and
verify whether a response/action already occurred before retrying.

## Cadence and renewal

Maintain separately approved rights and incident timing rules. Quarterly inventory,
provider and training reviews and annual governance review are planning defaults.
Reassess after purpose, provider, incident, system or source changes; do not reuse
pre-commencement readiness evidence as proof of current operating effectiveness.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope and accountability records, processing/provider map, notice versions,
protected evidence, QA cases, findings/retests and unresolved decisions. Actual
communications require authority and independent receipt before claiming completion.
Independent source, engagement and skeptical review and named human publication
approval remain pending. Only original workflow and source links are reproduced.
