# India Digital Personal Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a statutory applicability conclusion. Check the [MeitY Act and policy resources](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa) for staged commencement.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a readiness roadmap keyed to actual commencement dates. Map digital data flows, notices, consent and request routes, vendors, security, breach, retention, child-data, and complaint evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal and compliance owners determine applicability and timing. Independent reviewers test readiness claims against configured systems and signed evidence. AI can maintain a readiness register and draft evidence requests; it cannot select legal basis, lodge reports, or approve production changes.
