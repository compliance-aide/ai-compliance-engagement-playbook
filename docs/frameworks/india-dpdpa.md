# India Digital Personal Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a statutory applicability conclusion. Check the [MeitY Act and policy resources](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa) for staged commencement.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a readiness roadmap keyed to actual commencement dates. Map digital data flows, notices, consent and request routes, vendors, security, breach, retention, child-data, and complaint evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal and compliance owners determine applicability and timing. Independent reviewers test readiness claims against configured systems and signed evidence. AI can maintain a readiness register and draft evidence requests; it cannot select legal basis, lodge reports, or approve production changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [MeitY Act and policy resources](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa) and qualified Indian legal review; checked 2026-07-31. Track actual commencement and implementing developments. This original plan does not decide applicability, commencement effect, valid consent, child-data treatment, request outcomes, breach reporting, or legal obligations.

### 1. Commencement-aware readiness and digital-data map

- **Request and owner:** Legal change log, staged-readiness roadmap, digital personal-data inventory, collection/channel map, notice and consent-interface versions, owner assignments, and implementation milestones from legal, privacy, product, engineering, and program owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one planned or operating data route to its system, owner, notice/consent interface, readiness milestone, legal-change assumption, and review date. This supports planning evidence; it cannot determine whether a requirement is in force or whether an interface is legally sufficient.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved milestones and inventories and flag assumptions needing review. Legal humans decide timing and interpretation; product owners approve changes. Refresh after official commencement, rule, product, or collection-flow changes.

### 2. Consent, individual, child-data, and vendor workflow record

- **Request and owner:** Consent/withdrawal and request workflow designs and logs, identity/authorization procedures, child-data escalation inputs, grievance/complaint route, processor/vendor register, and contractual oversight from privacy, legal, product, procurement, and customer-operations owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted request, withdrawal, vendor change, or child-data escalation through facts, human authority, action, communication, and follow-up. This provides workflow traceability; it cannot decide consent validity, a request outcome, or a child's status.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare authorized evidence indexes and draft internal questions; it may not contact individuals, choose a legal basis, or approve a vendor. Recollect after a new processor, interface, age-assurance change, or recurring request issue.

### 3. Security, breach, retention, and accountability readiness

- **Request and owner:** Security-operation evidence, breach exercise/event timeline, escalation and notification decision inputs, retention/deletion evidence, risk exceptions, remediation/retest, and management-readiness review from security, incident, records, privacy, and executive owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an exercise or remediation item through detection, facts, authority, action, retest, and recorded legal-change assumption. This does not prove security effectiveness or determine reporting duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale readiness actions and assemble a redacted packet. Named humans decide escalation, notification, risk, and closure. Review quarterly, annually, and at each material commencement or source update.
