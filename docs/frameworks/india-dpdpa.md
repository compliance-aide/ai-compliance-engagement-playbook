# India Digital Personal Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, an applicability decision or an approved regulatory submission.

## Source and applicability

Start with the [MeitY rules, corrigendum and commencement resources](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa) and the [published 2025 Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf). English rule 1 and portions covering notice, security, breach and retention were checked on 2026-09-04. The complete Act, all Rules and schedules, corrigendum, commencement notification and later notifications have not been reconciled in this draft. Qualified legal review must resolve them before obligation-level conclusions.

Rule 1 distinguishes rules effective on publication, rule 4 after one year, and rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 after eighteen months. Do not infer that all substantive duties are already effective from an announcement that the Rules were notified. Record the controlling publication date, actual effective date and any correction for each provision; Act commencement must be checked separately. Maintain present duties and future readiness as separate fields.

## Engagement focus

Turn commencement-aware planning into a traceable data lifecycle: purpose, collection, authorization, processing, vendors, rights, security, incidents and erasure. An implementation milestone is not legal effectiveness, and a documented design is not a working control.

## Roles

Legal decides applicability, lawful processing routes, timing, exceptions and notifications. Privacy owns the processing and rights registers. Product and engineering own interfaces and implementation; security owns safeguards and incident facts; procurement owns processor evidence; records owners implement approved retention. An independent reviewer challenges source interpretation and behavior evidence. AI may organize approved records and prepare draft questions, but cannot select a legal basis, validate a person's consent, decide child status, contact individuals, lodge reports, accept risk or approve production changes.

## Before starting

Identify entities, services, processing purposes, data subjects, territorial facts, systems, vendors, data locations, role assignments and review period. Record possible Data Fiduciary, processor, Consent Manager or Significant Data Fiduciary issues for legal determination rather than assigning status from a product name. Obtain approved metadata and private evidence access; do not put personal data, credentials or restricted legal advice in this public repository.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Build the source and timing register.** For each proposed obligation, record Act section, rule/schedule, notification, correction, effective date, applicability decision, owner and next verification date. Distinguish in-force, future, disputed and unverified source status from task progress. Missing source support blocks the affected legal conclusion while inventory work can continue.
2. **Reconcile every known scoped data route.** Compare service, system and procurement inventories. Record data categories, collection channels, purposes, subjects, destinations, processors and retained copies with stable IDs. Include digital processing originating from offline collection where relevant for review. Preserve unresolved coverage rather than silently sampling, trimming or capping the evidence population.
3. **Approve purpose and processing routes.** Legal reviews each purpose, claimed authorization and any exception. Link approved notice versions to the actual fields and uses. Plan language and accessibility checks. Where consent applies, record the grant, withdrawal and propagation design; do not substitute a prechecked interface or generic privacy-policy link for evidence of the approved workflow.
4. **Trace individual and special-case workflows.** Map access, correction, erasure, grievance and nomination routes to applicable approved requirements. Record identity/authority checks, receipt time, responsible owner, decision and completion evidence. Route child-data, guardian, Consent Manager and Significant Data Fiduciary questions to qualified reviewers with the relevant rule/schedule. Avoid collecting extra identity material merely to make an AI review easier.
5. **Connect processors, safeguards and retention.** Reconcile actual destinations with contracts, authorized purposes, access controls and supplier evidence. Maintain an approved schedule by data category and purpose, including legally required retention and source-specific erasure conditions. Resolve conflicts before deletion; withdrawal or account closure does not automatically prove every retained record must be erased immediately. Track propagation, backups, failed jobs and remaining copies.
6. **Prepare incident and rights execution.** Record clocks, escalation contacts, approval owners, draft contents and delivery channels using the applicable source version. For rule 7 readiness, distinguish notification without delay to affected people, initial Board information without delay and detailed Board information within 72 hours of awareness or an allowed extension. A 72-hour detail deadline is not permission to postpone initial notices. Verify commencement and other applicable reporting regimes separately; AI does not send notices.
7. **Verify, remediate and hand off.** Use authorized synthetic cases against exact configurations. Define expected behavior before execution and retain observed results, deviations, owners and retest links. Reconcile the complete scope with completed, failed, unknown and untested assertions. Hand off a readiness package with effective-date assumptions and explicit unresolved decisions, not a claim of compliance.

## Evidence and test plan

The three packages below preserve the prior tailored plan and replace single-route sampling with full known population accounting. Technical test coverage remains separately stated.

| Package | Minimum records | Reviewer challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commencement-aware readiness and digital-data map | Provision-level source register, complete known routes, roles, milestones and approved timing decisions | Does each milestone correspond to the correct duty, version and scope? |
| Consent, individuals, child data and vendors | Versioned interfaces, private authorization references, request/withdrawal logs, special-case decisions and processor actions | Did the action reach every intended system, and were exceptions authorized? |
| Security, breach, retention and accountability | Safeguard evidence, awareness timeline, notification decision references, deletion results, exceptions and retests | Are design, execution and legal determination clearly distinguished? |

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md): use `supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` and `not_tested` for individual assertions. Split compound claims. A future effective date does not make an observed implementation failure disappear; label it as a readiness gap rather than an established present statutory violation.

Fictional author desk case: a withdrawal action updates the application, but an identified processor continues the consent-dependent activity. The application-only success cannot support end-to-end completion. Record the processor gap, preserve the withdrawal timestamp and obtain the responsible owner's correction and verification. Counsel determines the applicable duties and timing.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Missing corrigendum or commencement evidence: keep the relevant legal conclusion unverified and assign source reconciliation; do not substitute a draft consultation paper.
- Conflicting retention and erasure instructions: obtain a category-specific legal decision before destructive action; keep the conflict and deadline visible.
- Unverified age or representative authority: use the approved escalation route; do not infer age from appearance or accept an unsupported declaration as proof.
- Failed notification or processor propagation: retain the failed outcome with an owner and follow-up. A generated message, queued job or signed contract is not completion.

## Cadence and renewal

Schedule readiness work backwards from verified commencement dates and approved implementation lead times. Reopen affected records after source, purpose, interface, vendor, designation, data-location, retention or incident changes. Legal owners identify any specific periodic duties for the applicable role; this guide imposes no blanket quarterly or annual review rule.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the source/timing register, scoped data map, evidence index, verification results, outstanding decisions and named owners. Separate current obligations, future readiness and other-law duties. Independent source, skeptical and rights review remain pending before guide publication. No actual assessment, reporting or cross-model usability validation is implied by an author desk check.
