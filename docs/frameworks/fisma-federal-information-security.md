# FISMA federal information security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an authorization or compliance assertion. Check [44 U.S.C. §§3551–3558](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title44-section3551).

## Engagement focus

Maintain system/service inventory, responsibilities, boundary changes, configuration, vulnerability, incident, supplier, inheritance, remediation, and reporting-readiness evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain asset/data/service links; independent reviewers sample scope and closure. AI organizes evidence and gaps, but cannot grant authorization, determine impact, submit reports, or assert compliance. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [FISMA statutory text](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title44-section3551) together with applicable agency policy and current reporting direction; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not reproduce statute text, submit federal reporting, or make a compliance/authorization conclusion.

### 1. Inventory, ownership, and boundary-accountability package

- **Request and owner:** Agency/component security, program, and system owners provide current system/service/asset inventories, information and interface records, responsible officials, interconnection/dependency references, and material boundary-change history.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a selected system or shared service to accountable owner, current inventory, documented boundary/dependency, and review date. This cannot establish inventory completeness, decide system impact, or approve an authority boundary.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare authorized inventory extracts, flag orphaned ownership, and identify version conflicts. Human officials determine accountability and scope. Refresh quarterly and after material acquisition, decommissioning, interface, or ownership change.

### 2. Operational-security, incident, and remediation package

- **Request and owner:** Security operations, system, and incident owners provide monitoring outputs, vulnerability/remediation records, incident records, configuration/change references, exception approvals, and retest or current-state evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a documented vulnerability, incident, or configuration deviation from source record through owner assignment, action, exception/decision, and retest or open status. A sample does not demonstrate continuous coverage or close a finding.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize evidence, flag stale actions, and generate read-only traceability questions. It cannot modify systems, set severity, close incidents, or accept exceptions. Refresh on scheduled monitoring and after incidents, failed retests, or material changes.

### 3. Independent-review, reporting-readiness, and decision package

- **Request and owner:** Program leadership supplies independent-review workpapers, evidence limitations, corrective-action status, reporting inputs, approvals, and an accountable official’s decision or escalation record.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one reported measure or corrective action to source population, method/date, reviewer challenge, limitation, accountable human approval, and follow-up date. This cannot validate all reporting data, submit a report, or assert statutory compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied values to their evidence references and flag absent provenance. Authorized humans approve conclusions, reports, risk treatment, and escalation. Refresh before reporting cycles, annual review, and after a material data-quality issue.
