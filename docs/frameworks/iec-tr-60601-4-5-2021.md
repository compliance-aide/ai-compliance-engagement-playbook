# IEC TR 60601-4-5:2021 medical-device security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, clinical advice or a conformity claim.

## Source and applicability

The [IEC publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/64703), checked 2026-09-04, describes safety-related technical security specifications for medical devices with external data interfaces. It includes medical electrical equipment, systems and medical-device software, while excluding IVD devices and other network software that is not medical-device software. A human interface processing confidential data can be relevant even without a network connection.

The record distinguishes device capability from medical-network target security levels. Full licensed specifications, current amendments, regulatory recognition and product-specific requirements remain unverified. Confirm the exact edition, intended use and applicable source permissions before detailed evaluation; do not reproduce protected material here. Use of this Technical Report alone does not establish regulatory clearance or clinical safety.

## Engagement focus

Trace each device version, interface and deployment assumption to approved security specifications, verification evidence and accountable safety decisions. Keep device capability, configured integration and clinical outcome separate.

## Roles

Product and regulatory owners determine applicability and claims. Engineering and security owners supply implementation and verification evidence. Clinical-safety and healthcare delivery owners approve safety and operating-environment decisions. An independent reviewer challenges assumptions and evidence limits. AI may organize authorized records and draft questions; it cannot evaluate clinical safety, test a device, release software, change clinical environments, accept risk or claim conformity.

## Before starting

Record product identity, hardware/software versions, intended use, device classification decision, interfaces, data types and operating environment. Identify manufacturer and healthcare-provider responsibilities. Obtain licensed references, approved criteria and permitted evidence. Use synthetic data for exercises. No patient-device access, active scanning or clinical change is authorized by this review workflow.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Confirm applicability.** Qualified owners decide whether the product falls within the report's scope and identify other applicable obligations. Record excluded products and the correct follow-up route; exclusion from this report does not mean no security duties.
2. **Reconcile device and environment.** Inventory all scoped variants, interfaces, external services and deployment assumptions. Include local human interfaces and maintenance paths. Record where the device ends and the surrounding network or clinical process begins.
3. **Approve security objectives.** Link applicable licensed specifications to intended use, risk inputs and accountable decisions. Preserve safety constraints and customer prerequisites. Do not invent a security level or infer it from a product brochure.
4. **Trace implementation.** Associate each objective with exact configuration, design and dependency evidence. Distinguish native capability from controls supplied by the environment. Record unverified assumptions and conflicting evidence rather than selecting favorable records.
5. **Review verification.** Qualified owners approve methods, expected results, configuration and safety constraints. Examine permitted results and preserve failures, untested conditions and artifact identity. Lab evidence must state its limits; no test may be performed on a patient-connected device under this guide.
6. **Resolve defects and lifecycle changes.** Assign vulnerabilities, update decisions and affected versions to owners. Obtain change, safety and release approvals before action. Retest affected behavior and update integration instructions. A released correction does not prove installation at healthcare sites.
7. **Hand off and maintain.** Deliver capability evidence, deployment prerequisites, limitations, incident contacts and support commitments. Record actual recipient acknowledgment separately from deployment verification. Reopen affected work after interface, supplier, vulnerability, version or intended-use changes.

## Evidence and test plan

### 1. Applicability and configuration package

Product and regulatory owners provide scope decisions, complete variant/interface inventories, intended-use records and source versions. Reconcile each assessment claim to the exact device and environment. Do not silently omit difficult configurations.

### 2. Security and safety-interface package

Engineering, security and clinical-safety owners provide approved specifications, risk references, implementation records and verification results. Trace each objective to evidence and limitations. Preserve all evidence supplied for assessment without silent trimming or sampling. Inventory completeness is not exhaustive technical or clinical validation.

### 3. Integration and maintenance package

Manufacturer support and healthcare delivery owners provide integration assumptions, vulnerability dispositions, update guidance, incident handling and retirement records. Identify who must configure, monitor and maintain each dependency. Keep documentation availability, deployment and effective operation as separate assertions.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Product is an IVD or non-device software: document the scope mismatch and obtain the appropriate framework decision.
- Safety implication is unresolved: escalate to the qualified safety owner before dependent changes or conclusions.
- Verification covers a different version: reconcile applicability or obtain new evidence before reusing results.
- Network prerequisite is not met: retain the integration gap; device capability alone cannot prove the deployed outcome.
- Update may interrupt care: route scheduling and risk decisions to authorized clinical/operational owners; AI cannot choose the tradeoff.

Use [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) assertions: supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable and not_tested. These are not clinical judgments, security levels or regulatory approvals.

## Cadence and renewal

Review material releases, vulnerabilities, interfaces, suppliers and support changes. Set other intervals through approved obligations and risk decisions. Do not invent a universal annual certification cycle or use publisher stability dates as product expiry dates.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the applicability decision, configuration population, approved objectives, evidence coverage, integration limits and open actions with owners and dates. Full licensed-source, independent skeptical and rights reviews remain pending. No actual device test or clinical decision occurred in preparing this draft.

Fictional author desk case: a device's encrypted communication capability is documented, but the healthcare deployment still enables a legacy unencrypted interface. Capability evidence does not support a claim that all deployed traffic is protected. Record the configuration gap for authorized review without changing clinical equipment.
