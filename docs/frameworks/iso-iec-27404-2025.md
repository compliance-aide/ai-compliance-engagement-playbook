# ISO/IEC 27404:2025 consumer-IoT cybersecurity-labeling engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a label issuance, a conformity decision, a consumer claim, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27404:2025 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80138.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a consumer-IoT labeling-readiness record connecting product scope, accountable program and product owners, authorized evaluation evidence, claims and label artifacts, supplier dependencies, issuance and maintenance records, exceptions, changes, and independent review. Use AI to organize evidence and draft questions while strictly separating it from any label, conformity, or consumer-facing decision.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product leadership, security, legal, marketing, conformity, risk, operations, and issuing authorities retain accountable decisions; program and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, claim traceability, evidence quality, maintenance triggers, and consumer-communication boundaries. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify missing records, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot issue or maintain a label, determine conformity, approve a consumer claim, accept risk, alter production configurations, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review each product release and material change, conduct quarterly program reviews, and perform an annual labeling-readiness review.

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
