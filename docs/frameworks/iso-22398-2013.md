# ISO 22398:2013 — exercise engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or performance certification. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/50294.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain multi-year exercise calendar, risk linkage, objectives, scope, injects, observers, safety, ethics, evidence, debrief, remediation, and retest records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans approve scenarios and evaluate performance; independent reviewers test traceability. AI proposes scenarios and indexes evidence, but cannot judge results. Exercise periodically and review annually.

### 1. Exercise governance and design package

- **Request and owner:** Resilience and exercise owners provide the multi-period exercise calendar, approved objectives, scope, participants, scenario basis, safety and ethics boundaries, observer assignments, and change approvals.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one planned exercise from its stated objective and scenario basis to human approvals, participant scope, and safety boundary. This demonstrates planning traceability; it cannot decide whether an exercise is adequate, safe, or representative.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved scenario inputs and flag missing owners or dates. Humans approve objectives, scenario content, safety constraints, and participation. Refresh after a material threat, operational, safety, or dependency change.

### 2. Delivery and observation package

- **Request and owner:** Exercise leads provide briefing acknowledgements, inject or event logs, observer notes, attendance records, communications artifacts, safety/issues log, and evidence-retention references.
- **Validate and limit:** Select an exercise event and trace it to the planned objective, delivery record, observer evidence, and any safety escalation. This supports a reliable record; it cannot determine participant competence or independently score performance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may time-order supplied records and identify incomplete observations, but cannot issue instructions, evaluate people, or declare a result. Recollect after an aborted exercise, safety event, or material delivery deviation.

### 3. Debrief, remediation, and retest package

- **Request and owner:** Exercise, service, and leadership owners provide debrief outputs, findings, action assignments, risk/escalation decisions, remediation status, retest rationale, and management-review evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one finding from observation to a human-approved action, owner, due date, follow-up, and any retest evidence. This shows improvement traceability; it cannot prove that a remediation will work or close an unresolved risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may cluster themes and flag overdue actions. Humans evaluate results, accept risk, set remediation priority, and close actions. Review after each exercise and close the annual cycle with independent sampling.
