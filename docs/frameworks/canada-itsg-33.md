# Canada ITSG-33 security-risk management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Canadian government guidance, a control catalogue, or an authorization decision. Confirm current applicability and source material through the [Canadian Centre for Cyber Security ITSG-33 record](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/info/a2fe4b67-a020-48a8-a5b0-d303966d5f2d).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a defensible lifecycle record for organizational and system security risk: service context, accountable roles, assessed changes, evidence of operating safeguards, assessment results, authorizing decisions, conditions, and continuous monitoring.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business and system authorities own the service context and risk decisions; security practitioners assemble evidence; assessors independently evaluate the record; authorizing officials make authorization decisions. AI organizes approved evidence, traces conditions to owners, and prepares review questions, but cannot select safeguards, assess effectiveness, authorize operation, or accept risk. Reassess after material change, review open conditions routinely, and preserve human decisions and their dates.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Canadian Centre for Cyber Security ITSG-33 record](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/info/a2fe4b67-a020-48a8-a5b0-d303966d5f2d), the current authorized source material, and organization-approved risk-management direction; checked 2026-07-31. This plan uses original language and does not reproduce ITSG-33 content, select safeguards, assess effectiveness, authorize operation, or make a conformance claim. The engagement owner confirms source rights and the applicable edition before work begins.

### 1. Organization, system, and risk-context package

- **Request and owner:** Business/system authorities provide approved service purpose, organizational and system boundaries, dependencies, accountable roles, risk assumptions, information/asset context, and material-change history.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one significant system dependency or risk assumption to a current context record, accountable owner, evidence date, and stated impact. This is a traceability test, not a determination of applicability, completeness, or risk acceptability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare authorized inventories for missing ownership and conflicting versions. Humans approve the organization/system context and risk assumptions. Refresh after material mission, boundary, supplier, data, or architecture change.

### 2. Security-assessment and corrective-action package

- **Request and owner:** Security practitioners and assessment owners provide original implementation records, evidence references, assessment plans/results, limitations, findings, corrective-action register, retest records, and evidence coverage dates.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one assessment observation from its scope and evidence reference through finding/limitation, accountable owner, corrective action, and retest/open status. This does not establish assessment completeness, safeguard effectiveness, or acceptable residual risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble read-only traceability workpapers and flag missing evidence links or overdue action dates; it cannot test systems without authorization, modify evidence, select a safeguard, or close a finding. Human security and assessment owners judge results and action closure. Refresh on assessment cadence, failed test, incident, or material implementation change.

### 3. Authorization-input and continuous-monitoring package

- **Request and owner:** The system owner and authorizing-decision authorities provide decision records, conditions, monitoring outputs, incident/change records, exception/risk decisions, reassessment schedule, and source-change log.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one open condition or material change from detection to human decision, required follow-up, monitoring evidence, and next review. This can show a governed record; it cannot accept risk, authorize operation, or attest that monitoring is complete.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag aging conditions and prepare non-authoritative review questions. Only named humans make authorization and risk decisions. Review routinely and before a decision, renewal, or material transition.
