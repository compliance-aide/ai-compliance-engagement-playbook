# FIDO certification program — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not FIDO criteria or certification claim. Consult [FIDO certification](https://fidoalliance.org/fido-certification-/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain authentication product, exact certification claim, product/version, procurement, directory validation, enrollment, recovery, loss, phishing, test, vendor, and claim evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans validate claims and procurement; independent reviewers test evidence. AI flags unsupported claims, but cannot use logos or assert certification. Review releases and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [FIDO certification program information](https://fidoalliance.org/fido-certification-/), the applicable public certification-directory record, and approved procurement/legal decisions; checked 2026-07-31. This is original evidence-planning language, not FIDO program criteria, a certification result, an authorization to use a mark, or a product-security conclusion. FIDO and authorized humans determine certification and trademark status.

### 1. Product, version, and claim-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Product, procurement, security, legal, and marketing owners provide the in-scope authenticator or server product inventory, manufacturer/model and firmware/software-version records, intended deployment context, purchase/support references, proposed claim text, directory lookup capture, and named owner.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one proposed internal or external claim to the exact product/version, manufacturer, directory lookup date and result record, deployment boundary, owner, and approval. This can reveal unsupported or stale evidence; it cannot establish certification, applicability to a different version/configuration, interoperability, or permission to use a logo.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare supplied product identifiers and flag version or approval mismatches. Humans validate directory results, procurement facts, claim language, and trademark permissions. Refresh before publication, procurement, release, firmware/software update, or material vendor-directory change.

### 2. Enrollment, recovery, and phishing-resistance operations package

- **Request and owner:** Identity, security, help-desk, product, and privacy owners provide approved enrollment and binding procedures, recovery/loss workflows, authenticator inventory references, administrative-access controls, training records, support-event categories, exception records, and relevant data-handling decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an approved enrollment, recovery drill, or lost-device case from procedure and owner to authorization record, support outcome, access-control reference, and follow-up. This cannot authenticate a person, prove phishing resistance, prove recovery security, or establish that an individual account was properly handled.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize redacted workflow evidence and identify missing owners, stale procedures, or overdue follow-up. Humans approve account recovery, exceptions, access changes, and user communications; AI cannot enroll credentials, reset accounts, or grant access. Refresh after workflow, threat, support-pattern, or product changes.

### 3. Deployment, supplier, and release-change package

- **Request and owner:** Engineering, endpoint, procurement, supplier-management, and security owners provide integration and deployment references, relying-party/application inventory, supported platform/version records, vendor advisories, patch/release decisions, test references, supplier contacts, and retirement plans.
- **Validate and limit:** Link one deployed integration to its product/version baseline, approved change, platform/application boundary, test reference, owner, vendor notice, and next review. This cannot test the authenticator, certify conformance, establish secure configuration, or guarantee interoperability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale versions, unlinked advisories, or missing retirement owners. Humans decide rollout, remediation, supplier escalation, risk acceptance, and any certification-related communication. Review quarterly and after a release, advisory, integration change, or supplier status update.
