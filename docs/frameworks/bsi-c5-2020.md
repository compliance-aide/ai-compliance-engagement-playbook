# BSI C5:2020 cloud-service engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not BSI content, an audit report, or an attestation. Confirm the applicable edition and engagement context through the [BSI C5 report and evaluation resources](https://www.bsi.bund.de/EN/Themen/Unternehmen-und-Organisationen/Informationen-und-Empfehlungen/Empfehlungen-nach-Angriffszielen/Cloud-Computing/Kriterienkatalog-C5/C5_AktuelleVersion/Auswertung/Auswertung_node.html).

## Engagement focus

Keep a service boundary, customer commitments, service-operation evidence, material changes, supplier dependencies, exception decisions, incident evidence, and customer-consumable assurance materials current throughout the year.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Cloud service owners maintain the operational record; customer-facing owners define usable evidence and shared-responsibility communications; independent reviewers test a representative evidence trail. AI organizes approved artifacts, identifies stale ownership or missing dates, and drafts questions, but cannot determine conformance, issue an assurance conclusion, or represent BSI. Reconcile scope and material changes quarterly, rehearse evidence retrieval before the expected reporting period, and obtain human approval for every external statement.

## Tailored evidence plan

BSI material and any assurance report remain protected. This plan does not reproduce criteria, testing procedures, report language, or mappings, and it is not an attestation. The accountable cloud-service and assurance-contact owners confirm edition, service scope, and external-use decisions.

### 1. Cloud-service boundary and shared-responsibility package

- **Request and owner:** Cloud-service, architecture, product, and customer-facing owners provide service descriptions, environment/region and tenant boundary records, supported commitments, shared-responsibility communications, material supplier/dependency inventory, and named control/evidence owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected customer-facing service statement to its approved boundary, accountable owner, supplier dependency, and dated shared-responsibility record. This can support transparent service scoping; it cannot establish that commitments are complete, determine customer obligations, or make an assurance conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare supplied boundary metadata and flag inconsistent ownership or stale customer documentation. Humans approve service scope, commitments, and external wording. Refresh after material architecture, region, tenancy, supplier, product, or customer-commitment change.

### 2. Service-operation, change, and incident package

- **Request and owner:** Operations, security, engineering, and supplier owners provide approved operating procedures, access/change records, monitoring or service-operation evidence, incident records, supplier notices, exceptions, remediation actions, and validation/retest references.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected material change or incident from affected service boundary through source records, owner decision, customer-impact assessment, remediation, and follow-up evidence. This can support traceability for a sampled event; it cannot prove uninterrupted secure operation, complete incident detection, or effectiveness across all tenants.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved artifacts and highlight missing dates, owner links, or open actions; it cannot alter cloud services or close incidents. Humans authorize changes, incident communications, exceptions, and closure. Recollect after material change, incident, supplier event, failed validation, or exception expiry.

### 3. Assurance-readiness and customer-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Assurance, security, legal/commercial, and customer-facing owners provide evidence-index records, reporting-period boundary, artifact provenance/access restrictions, independent-review findings, exceptions, approved customer materials, and management decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected customer-evidence item to its source artifact, reporting period, access/handling constraint, owner approval, and reviewer challenge. This can support controlled evidence retrieval; it cannot issue an audit report, establish conformance, or authorize disclosure of restricted evidence.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare read-only evidence indexes and draft retrieval questions, never publish customer materials or represent BSI. Humans approve customer disclosure, report use, risk decisions, and external claims. Rehearse before the reporting period and review quarterly or after a source/commitment change.
