> Original operational guidance, not BSI criteria, an audit report or an attestation.

## Engagement focus

Prepare service-specific evidence or evaluate a received assurance report. Identify
which route is requested before collecting records. A provider report does not
automatically establish that a customer's deployment satisfies its responsibilities.

## Roles

Service owners define boundaries and commitments; operations/security supply actual
records; supplier owners supply dependencies. The independent auditor determines
assessment method and conclusions. Customer security owns report reliance and user
controls. Legal/commercial owners approve disclosure. AI may reconcile permitted
metadata and draft workpapers; it cannot issue attestation, represent BSI, accept
risk or release restricted reports without authority.

## Before starting

Obtain the requested route, edition, service/region/version, reporting date or period,
auditor instructions, provider boundaries, customer responsibilities and artifact
access rights. If source or report access is missing, prepare metadata questions
while marking dependent conclusions untested. Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md).

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Pin the engagement | Assurance/customer owner records edition, route, service and intended reliance. | Approved scope and date/period; edition uncertainty remains explicit. |
| 2. Reconcile boundaries | Service owner maps regions, components, providers and shared responsibilities. | Versioned boundary with excluded services and dependencies identified. |
| 3. Define coverage | For preparation, auditor/assurance sets evidence methods; for report evaluation, customer maps report coverage to its use. | Every scoped claim has an evidence/report reference or a gap. |
| 4. Examine evidence | Authorised reviewers inspect actual operating records or the received report, including limitations and exceptions. AI indexes provenance. | Observations reference service, period and source; missing periods are not filled by inference. |
| 5. Address customer responsibilities | Customer owner connects relevant responsibilities to its own configuration and operating evidence. | Owned action/evidence list; provider assurance alone cannot close it. |
| 6. Correct and retest | Provider/customer owners perform authorised fixes; reviewer repeats affected checks. | Finding/change/retest links and unresolved reliance risks. |
| 7. Review disclosure and reliance | Auditor issues any report within its authority; customer decides reliance; legal approves exact external artifact. | Separate decision and delivery records with permitted recipients and readback. |

## Evidence and test plan

| Evidence and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service/report boundary, assurance | Match product, regions and reporting date/period to the intended use. | Scope overlap and exclusions are explicit. | A report for another service is not transferable evidence. |
| Operating/change records, operations | Trace an approved test from expected behavior to observed result and period. | Actual implementation and operation are evidenced. | A point-in-time observation cannot prove a full period. |
| Customer responsibility list, customer security | Trace each relevant responsibility to owned implementation/evidence. | Customer work remains visible alongside provider assurance. | A vendor report does not implement customer controls. |
| Exceptions/disclosure record, assurance/legal | Trace exceptions to impact, treatment and permitted report use. | Reliance rationale and artifact permissions are documented. | A marketing summary cannot substitute for the restricted report. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Unknown edition blocks criteria-level conclusions. Missing or restricted report
content requires an authorised reviewer, not an invented clean opinion. Uncovered
regions, providers or periods stay gaps. Adverse results require explicit impact
review and retest; they cannot be removed from the customer packet. If interrupted,
verify artifact version and prior delivery before resuming disclosure work.

## Cadence and renewal

Follow the agreed assurance period and source-change review. Quarterly boundary
housekeeping and pre-report evidence retrieval rehearsals are planning defaults.
Reassess after regions, suppliers, product commitments, incidents or report exceptions
change. Record any 2020-to-newer-edition migration as a separate approved delta.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver edition/scope decision, service boundary, evidence/report index, coverage
and customer-responsibility records, findings/retests and reliance/disclosure decisions.
Name the current source retrieval gap. Independent source, engagement and skeptical
reviews and named human publication approval remain pending. No protected criteria,
report text or customer evidence is reproduced in this original workflow.
