# China Data Security Law engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not statutory text, Chinese legal advice, a regulatory filing, or a compliance claim. Confirm the applicable text and implementing measures through the [National People’s Congress English reference](https://www.npc.gov.cn/englishnpc/c2759/c23934/202112/t20211209_385109.html) and qualified local counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a jurisdiction-aware data-security record for in-scope activities: data assets and classifications, processing and sharing purposes, systems and suppliers, accountable owners, security measures and tests, incident evidence, retention and disposal decisions, government-data interactions, and domestic or cross-border transfer questions. Preserve evidence that distinguishes actual system behavior from policy intent, and route uncertain or sensitive classifications for qualified legal and business determination.

## Source and applicability

Source check: 2026-09-04. Use the NPC English reference above alongside the [authoritative Chinese publication](https://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202106/t20210610_311888.html) and current implementing measures. The law addresses data processing, not only personal information. Its classification, important-data, outbound-data and foreign-authority request provisions require distinct applicability decisions. Do not equate internal business sensitivity with a legally determined data category, or a commercial sharing approval with authority to answer a foreign judicial request. Current sector catalogues and implementing rules require qualified local review; the statute alone does not settle every route.

## Roles

Data owners provide purposes, lineage and proposed classifications. China-qualified counsel resolves legal categories, obligations, transfers and government requests. Security/system owners provide operating evidence; supplier owners reconcile external handling. Management approves risk and resources; independent reviewers challenge observations. AI prepares metadata and questions, not legal classification, transfer approval, incident reporting or disclosure decisions.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record the entity, data activity, systems, locations, period, owner, inputs, expected output and exit check. Obtain approved scope, classification criteria, legal obligation register and handling permissions. Missing determinations stay explicit. Use authorized metadata rather than exposing sensitive datasets in chat or this repository.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Establish scope | Legal/data owners identify activities, applicable sources, sector direction and unresolved classifications. | Versioned applicability record; missing classification is not a low-risk conclusion. |
| 2. Reconcile lineage | System/data owners trace collection, creation, use, transformation, sharing, storage and disposal against actual systems and suppliers. | Data-flow inventory with owners and discrepancies, including derived datasets. |
| 3. Confirm classification and duties | Authorized owners document category decisions, supporting criteria and resulting safeguards, assessments and reporting requirements. | Complete scoped obligation register; AI does not select a legal category or invent deadlines. |
| 4. Examine protection | Security reviewers compare approved requirements with actual access, monitoring, change and protection evidence for the relevant period. | Evidence-linked workpapers distinguishing policies from operation and preserving missing coverage. |
| 5. Review external handling | Supplier/legal owners reconcile recipients, locations, purposes and proposed transfers or government interactions with applicable decision requirements. | Exact decision packet with conditions; no disclosure or transfer is authorized by preparation. |
| 6. Test response and disposal | Owners supply approved exercise or retained event evidence. Reviewers trace incident escalation, retention decisions and disposal outcomes. | Expected/actual records, legal questions and unresolved copies; simulation stays labelled. |
| 7. Correct and retest | Owners obtain required change approval, implement assigned treatment and submit evidence for review. | Finding/action/retest chain with adverse results retained. |
| 8. Record and monitor | Coordinator reads back authorized decisions and assigns monitoring of classification, processing, recipients and source changes. | Owned review queue with scope and conditions; an old decision cannot automatically authorize a changed route. |

## Evidence and test plan

Use the complete approved obligation register. These original examples are not a statutory catalogue. Preserve the full evidence corpus without trimming material supplied to a scorer.

| Package and owner | Verification | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lifecycle/classification — data/legal | Trace a dataset and derived output to purpose, owner, category decision, system and recipients; reconcile inventory discrepancies. | A selected trace cannot prove all data known or establish legal classification. |
| Protection/response — security | Trace a control issue through affected activity, observation, escalation, corrective action and retest. | Missing monitoring is a gap, not proof of no exposure. |
| Suppliers/sharing — vendor/legal | Compare actual recipient and location with the approved decision, conditions and provider evidence. | Contract promises alone do not demonstrate permitted or effective handling. |
| Government requests — legal | Check an approved synthetic case's routing, identity verification, authority review and recorded decision. | Do not disclose real data or treat a foreign request as an ordinary vendor transaction. |
| Retention/disposal — records | Compare approved treatment with system/provider readbacks and unresolved copies or holds. | A completed deletion job does not prove all relevant data disposed. |

Record source, collector, date, period, data activity, requirement reference, expected/actual result, evidence pointer, limitation, reviewer and next action. State selected test populations and untested scope. No live intrusive testing, transfer or destructive operation is authorized here.

## Failure branches and decisions

- **Unclear category or catalogue:** preserve factual lineage and seek the named legal/data-owner decision; do not infer important-data status from a keyword.
- **Unknown derived data or recipient:** reopen the map and identify the activity owner before relying on prior scope.
- **Failed safeguard or exposed route:** retain adverse evidence and escalate to the security/incident owner, preserving original timing.
- **Missing transfer decision:** prepare the exact route and requirements question; do not move data to test whether it works.
- **Foreign authority request:** route to legal through the approved process, preserving the request and access restrictions; no automatic disclosure.
- **Supplier or disposal evidence absent:** retain the missing scope/period and assigned request; do not substitute a contractual promise for readback.
- **Interrupted action or uncertain submission:** preserve last completed step, versions and pending decisions; inspect the destination before retrying.

## Cadence and renewal

Use applicable legal and sector requirements and actual decision conditions. Internal annual review is an owner-selected practice, not a universal statutory timer. Reassess after changes in purpose, classification inputs, derived data, systems, recipients, locations, incidents or official direction.

## Completion and handoff

A preparation packet includes approved scope/classification decisions, lineage, complete obligations, protection evidence, recipient/transfer questions, incident and disposal records, findings/retests and monitoring owners. Mark omissions. Filings, disclosures and transfers are separate authorized milestones requiring destination evidence. Handoff specifies next action, owner, due date and dependency.
