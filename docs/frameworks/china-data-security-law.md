# China Data Security Law engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not statutory text, Chinese legal advice, a regulatory filing, or a compliance claim. Confirm the applicable text and implementing measures through the [National People’s Congress English reference](https://www.npc.gov.cn/englishnpc/c2759/c23934/202112/t20211209_385109.html) and qualified local counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a jurisdiction-aware data-security record for in-scope activities: data assets and classifications, processing and sharing purposes, systems and suppliers, accountable owners, security measures and tests, incident evidence, retention and disposal decisions, government-data interactions, and domestic or cross-border transfer questions. Preserve evidence that distinguishes actual system behavior from policy intent, and route uncertain or sensitive classifications for qualified legal and business determination.

## Roles and annual rhythm

China-qualified legal leadership determines obligations and regulatory interactions; data owners classify and approve business use; security and engineering teams maintain technical evidence; procurement and supplier owners maintain third-party records; independent reviewers test evidence traceability. AI may catalog approved data records, highlight incomplete lineage, and draft review questions, but cannot classify data as a legal conclusion, approve a transfer, determine a security incident’s reportability, make a filing, or represent compliance. Reassess at least annually and before material data, system, supplier, location, sharing, or cross-border changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [National People’s Congress English reference](https://www.npc.gov.cn/englishnpc/c2759/c23934/202112/t20211209_385109.html), current authoritative Chinese materials, and qualified China counsel; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not determine data-category treatment, important-data status, data-security assessment or reporting duty, export legality, or a compliance outcome.

### 1. Data activity, lifecycle, and accountable-owner map

- **Request and owner:** Approved inventory of China-connected data activities; business purpose; categories and lineage inputs; collection, storage, use, sharing, retention, deletion, and system/location records; supplier/recipient context; and named data, product, security, privacy, and legal owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected activity from collection or receipt to purpose, system, lifecycle record, location, owner, and disclosed supplier or recipient context. This supports a factual data-activity record; it cannot determine data classification, importance, legal purpose, local-storage duty, or whether an activity is permitted.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved catalog, integration, and retention metadata and flag missing lineage or ownership. Human data owners and China-qualified counsel decide classifications and scope. Refresh for a new data source, system, purpose, location, sharing arrangement, or material data-model change.

### 2. Security, risk, and incident-response evidence

- **Request and owner:** Security design/operation references; access-review and monitoring records; data-protection test and exercise references; vulnerability and remediation tickets; selected incident timelines; recovery/retest evidence; and human approvals from security, engineering, operations, and data owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Follow a selected material vulnerability, control issue, or incident from affected data activity through evidence provenance, assigned owner, decision, remediation, recovery/retest, and retained limitation. Exclude personal data and sensitive security details. This can evidence a reviewable response trail; it cannot prove security effectiveness, determine incident severity, or decide reporting/notification obligations.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index sanitized records and surface aging remediation or incomplete handoffs; it cannot investigate live systems, classify an incident, direct response, notify an authority, or close an issue. Authorized humans make those decisions. Trigger on an incident, material control failure, exposed data route, or failed test.

### 3. Sharing, supplier, and government-interaction decision trail

- **Request and owner:** Human-approved sharing/entrusted-processing and cross-border review inputs; supplier instructions and change notices; due-diligence records; government-request escalation records where applicable; exception/risk decisions; management review; and regulatory-change monitoring owned by privacy, procurement, legal, security, and leadership.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier, sharing, outbound-flow, or government-interaction case to factual inputs, authorized decision-maker, conditions, expiry, action owner, and later review. This supports accountable governance; it cannot approve a transfer, determine disclosure legality, direct a response to government, submit a filing, or accept risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a redacted source-linked packet and flag changed suppliers, routes, or expired reviews. Named humans authorize sharing, transfer, external communication, submissions, risk, and closure. Reassess at least annually and before a material supplier, recipient, cross-border, or regulatory change.
