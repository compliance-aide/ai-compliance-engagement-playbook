# NIST SP 800-66 Revision 2 — HIPAA security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST content, HIPAA legal advice, or a compliance determination. Confirm current status at the [NIST publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/66/r2/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain ePHI scope, risk decisions, workforce and system evidence, safeguards operations, incident records, business-associate dependencies, remediation, and the evidence needed for a HIPAA security review.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, security, legal, and healthcare leadership determine obligations and responses; independent reviewers form conclusions separately. AI coordinates approved evidence and drafts gap questions, but cannot interpret HIPAA, make determinations, or notify regulators. Review quarterly and annually.
