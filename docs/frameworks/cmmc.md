# CMMC — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a DoD publication or a certification claim.
> Before each milestone, check the active solicitation, contract, and current
> [DoD CMMC materials](https://dodcio.defense.gov/CMMC/Resources-Documentation/).

## Engagement focus

Start with the actual contractual requirement, data type, required level, and
current program status. Preserve scope decisions and evidence provenance.
Separate readiness support from the independent assessment role, sustain
readiness throughout contract performance, and re-check official rules before
any assertion, submission, or assessor engagement.

## Roles

The organization owns its scope, implementation, and representations. An
authorized assessor performs the independent work when required. AI coordinates
evidence, detects inconsistencies, and prepares drafts; it cannot represent
the organization to DoD, certify a result, make submissions, or approve scope or remediation decisions.

## Annual rhythm

Revalidate contract and program status, CUI flows, scope, and evidence; test
operation; track remediation; and trigger early review after award changes,
significant system changes, or program-rule updates.
