> Original operational guidance, not BSI standard text or a certification conclusion.

## Engagement focus

Show that leadership decisions lead to owned work, observed operation, review and
improvement. Avoid equating a collection of policies with a functioning management
system. Keep organisational boundaries, objectives and dependencies visible when
changes affect risk or evidence coverage.

## Roles

Management approves scope, objectives, resources and residual risk. Security
leadership maintains the program; service owners supply implementation evidence;
risk and compliance owners maintain decision inputs. Independent reviewers challenge
the record. AI may reconcile metadata and draft review packets; it cannot approve
budgets, accept risk, make legal conclusions or certify the organisation.

## Before starting

Obtain the charter, pinned source, approved scope and objectives, accountable roles,
service/dependency inventory, risk method, existing decisions, evidence permissions
and review calendar. If authority or scope is missing, prepare factual inputs and
mark dependent decisions pending. Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md).

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Set management context | Leadership confirms boundaries, objectives and applicable commitments. | Dated scope and objective decisions with unresolved assumptions. |
| 2. Assign accountable work | Security maps services, dependencies, risks and evidence to owners. | Ownership register; no orphan material service or action concealed. |
| 3. Plan treatment and measurement | Owners propose work, resources, expected outcomes and evidence; management decides. | Approved plan and decision trail; proposed work is not implementation. |
| 4. Observe operation | Authorised operators collect configuration, process and monitoring records under the agreed method. | Actual results linked to service, period and objective. |
| 5. Evaluate effectiveness | Security and independent reviewers compare evidence to expected outcomes and coverage. | Supported findings, contradictory evidence and limitations remain visible. |
| 6. Review with leadership | Security presents risks, findings, resource needs and options; leadership records decisions. | Decisions name owner, due date and any conditions; AI does not infer acceptance. |
| 7. Improve and verify | Owners perform approved corrective work; reviewer checks the original issue again. | Finding/change/retest trail and renewed evidence/review queue. |

## Evidence and test plan

| Evidence and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope/objectives, leadership | Trace a material service to its boundary, owner and objective. | Intent and accountable scope agree. | A signed policy alone does not establish full coverage. |
| Treatment/operation records, service owner | Follow a risk decision to approved action and actual operating evidence. | Planned and implemented states are distinguishable. | A purchase or change request is not evidence of effectiveness. |
| Review packet, security | Compare reported measures with underlying evidence and missing populations. | Denominators, adverse results and limitations are explicit. | A favourable average cannot conceal an untested service. |
| Improvement register, management/reviewer | Trace a finding to decision, owner, correction and retest. | Closure addresses the original condition. | A closed ticket without retest leaves effectiveness unproven. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Missing full source prevents requirement-level claims while program inventory
continues. An unowned action escalates to leadership rather than disappearing from
the queue. Conflicting metrics require source reconciliation before presentation.
Expired exceptions remain unresolved until an authorised decision. After interruption,
verify actual action state and receipts before restarting changes or issuing reports.

## Cadence and renewal

Use the approved monitoring and review calendar. Quarterly evidence housekeeping
and annual scope/leadership review are planning defaults, not asserted universal
BSI frequencies. Reassess after organisational, service, provider, incident or source
changes. Preserve prior decisions and explain changes rather than overwriting history.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope/objectives, accountable ownership, risk/treatment decisions, protected
evidence, review findings, management decisions and corrective-action retests.
Identify untested objectives and the source-verification gap. Independent source,
engagement and skeptical review and named human publication approval remain pending.
