# IEC TR 62443-2-3:2015 IACS patch-management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a patch-approval decision, an industrial safety decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC TR 62443-2-3:2015 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/22811).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an industrial patch-management record that connects IACS assets, software and supplier dependencies, accountable operations and maintenance owners, safety and process constraints, vendor advisories, authorized test evidence, patch and compensating-decision records, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Preserve the separation between AI-assisted evidence organization and accountable human decisions about patching, maintenance windows, safety, operational continuity, and acceptable risk.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, maintenance, security, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; asset and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and safety-aware readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify stale advisories, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot approve or schedule a patch, decide compensating measures, set maintenance windows, accept risk, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review advisories continuously, conduct quarterly operating reviews, and reassess annually and before material asset, supplier, safety, incident, or maintenance changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [IEC TR 62443-2-3:2015 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/22811) and an authorized copy; checked 2026-07-31. IEC text is licensed. This original plan does not reproduce requirements, approve a patch, prescribe a maintenance window, or make a safety, risk, or compliance conclusion.

### 1. Asset, software, and advisory intake package

- **Request and owner:** OT asset inventory, approved software/firmware and supplier-dependency records, supplier advisory intake, accountable asset owners, and safety/process constraints, from OT operations and maintenance.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected advisory to the affected asset/service record, owner, supplier reference, and stated operational constraint. This supports intake completeness and traceability; it cannot establish that every affected asset is known or that a patch is suitable.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized records and flag missing links. Humans decide scope and relevance. Refresh upon an advisory, inventory, supplier, or lifecycle change.

### 2. Human-authorized evaluation and disposition package

- **Request and owner:** Authorized test-environment evidence, compatibility and rollback considerations, patch/change decision records, compensating-measure rationale, maintenance-window approval, and named safety/operations review inputs.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected disposition to the responsible decision-makers, approved environment evidence, constraints, and next review date without scanning or changing OT. This supports governed decision records; it cannot validate engineering fitness, safety, or operational acceptability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a read-only comparison packet and identify expired dispositions. OT, safety, operations, and risk authorities approve all actions. Reopen before a patch, exception expiry, or material process change.

### 3. Deployment follow-through and independent challenge package

- **Request and owner:** Approved work order/change record, implementation confirmation, post-change observation, unresolved issues, exception/remediation tracker, and independent review workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected completed or deferred action to authorization, accountable owner, evidence date, follow-up, and exception expiry. This supports follow-through; it cannot prove resilient operation or close an independent assessment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index evidence and flag overdue follow-up; it cannot schedule, deploy, or close a change. Conduct quarterly review and reopen after an incident, rollback, or material maintenance change.
