# Uruguay personal-data-protection engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Uruguayan law or a legal conclusion. Confirm current requirements through the [URCDP Law 18.331 record](https://www.gub.uy/unidad-reguladora-control-datos-personales/institucional/normativa/ley-n-18331-fecha-18082008-ley-proteccion-datos-personales).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a processing and accountability record: data uses, owner and database decisions, rights-request evidence, supplier handling, security events, changes, registrations where applicable, and remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business and privacy owners retain the processing record; security and supplier owners provide operating evidence; Uruguayan counsel confirms scope; independent reviewers test traceability. AI organizes approved evidence and drafts review questions, but cannot interpret law, register a database, decide rights requests, or make regulatory submissions. Review material changes before release and retain human decisions with their rationale.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm current material through the [URCDP Law 18.331 record](https://www.gub.uy/unidad-reguladora-control-datos-personales/institucional/normativa/ley-n-18331-fecha-18082008-ley-proteccion-datos-personales) and qualified Uruguayan privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide database-registration scope, controller status, authorization, transfer conditions, rights outcome, or a regulator submission.

### 1. Processing, database, and purpose record

- **Request and owner:** Business and privacy owners maintain a processing/database register with purposes, data categories, source, systems, owner, database/registration assessment, notices or authorization rationale, recipients, retention, and change history.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected records to source, system, owner, stated purpose, recipient, and retention evidence. This supports factual accuracy checks; it cannot determine registration, authorization, legitimacy, or an exemption.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved records and flag incomplete owners. Counsel and privacy owners decide scope and legal treatment. Refresh before a new database, purpose, collection route, recipient, or material release.

### 2. Rights, security, and quality record

- **Request and owner:** Redacted access/correction/suppression request log, identity-verification procedure, data-quality action records, access-control/security-operation evidence, incident exercise/timeline, remediation, and training record.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a request and incident scenario to intake, source system, authorized reviewer, action, escalation, and closure. This cannot decide identity, a rights result, security effectiveness, incident reporting, or legal deadlines.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare redacted traceability and overdue-review prompts; it cannot send communications or decide outcomes. Recollect after request, complaint, incident, control failure, or quality remediation.

### 3. Supplier, sharing, and renewal record

- **Request and owner:** Supplier/recipient register, processing instruction and contract evidence, cross-border/sharing review inputs, exception/risk decisions, corrective-action/retest, management review, and authority-guidance watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier or sharing route to human authority, evidence, contract/instruction, review date, and corrective action. This supports a skeptical review; it cannot approve sharing, transfers, risk acceptance, or attest compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compile an evidence packet and flag stale supplier records. Privacy, counsel, security, and supplier owners approve decisions. Review annually and on supplier, transfer, or regulatory change.
