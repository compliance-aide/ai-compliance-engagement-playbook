# ETSI EN 303 645 V3.1.3 consumer-IoT engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ETSI standard text, testing criteria, or a conformity claim. Confirm the current edition through the [ETSI EN 303 645 publication record](https://portal.etsi.org/webapp/workprogram/Report_WorkItem.asp?WKI_ID=69970).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a product-lifecycle record for consumer IoT: intended use, supported services, security ownership, software and supplier dependencies, vulnerability intake, update and retirement decisions, testing evidence, incidents, and material changes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product owners decide intended use and releases; engineering and operations maintain lifecycle evidence; security teams manage vulnerability workflow; independent reviewers test traceability. AI organizes approved records and drafts questions, but cannot test products, make safety decisions, release software, or claim conformance. Reassess at material product and service changes, and retain human approval for every customer-facing or regulatory statement.
