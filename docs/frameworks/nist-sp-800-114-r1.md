# NIST SP 800-114 Revision 1 telework and BYOD engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST text, a user-access decision, a remote-access authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [NIST SP 800-114 Revision 1 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/114/r1/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a telework and bring-your-own-device engagement record that connects user populations, device ownership, approved business uses, remote-access paths, accountable owners, user acknowledgements, configuration and support boundaries, data-handling expectations, incidents, exceptions, offboarding, and review dates. Make the limits of organizational visibility and control explicit for personally owned or third-party devices.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business, endpoint, identity, security, privacy, legal, people, service desk, risk, and incident-response owners retain accountable decisions. Business owners approve legitimate work needs; identity owners govern access; endpoint and support teams maintain authorized configuration records; privacy and legal owners determine handling boundaries; and incident leaders decide response actions. Independent reviewers sample user and device paths from enrollment through remote access, user communications, exceptions, offboarding, and incident handling to challenge unsupported control claims.

AI may organize authorized policy acknowledgements and evidence metadata, flag stale reviews, compare documented populations, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot grant or revoke access, change endpoint or network configuration, inspect personal content, send user instructions without approval, determine suitability of a personal device, make a legal decision, accept risk, or claim compliance. Reassess before material remote-access, device, application, data, identity, employment, incident, or legal changes; review exceptions quarterly and complete an annual independent readiness review.
