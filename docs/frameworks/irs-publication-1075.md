# IRS Publication 1075 / FTI safeguarding — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IRS content or an approved safeguard report. Check the [IRS Safeguards Program](https://www.irs.gov/privacy-disclosure/safeguards-program).

## Engagement focus

Map FTI receipt, flow, storage, processing, transmission, disposal, labels, approved environments, access, logging, incidents, suppliers, media, and annual report evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain labels and evidence; independent reviewers sample boundaries and report support. AI cannot receive FTI in unapproved environments, submit reports, authorize services, or decide incident reporting. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [IRS Safeguards Program](https://www.irs.gov/privacy-disclosure/safeguards-program), binding agreement terms and current authorized organizational records; checked 2026-07-31. Handle any federal tax information only in authorized systems and channels. This original plan does not reproduce IRS safeguards material, authorize FTI processing, submit reports, or determine incident-reporting duties.

### 1. FTI boundary, flow, and authorization package

- **Request and owner:** Safeguards, data-governance, system, and program owners provide authorized FTI system inventory, receipt/use/flow diagrams, approved environments, data labels, connection/transfer approvals, custodians, and scope-change decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected FTI flow to its approved source and destination, system owner, authorization reference, labeling record, and review date. This tests a bounded evidence path; it cannot approve a system, transfer, or use of FTI.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize sanitized metadata and flag flow entries without owners or current approvals; it must not ingest FTI in an unapproved environment. Authorized humans decide system authorization and data handling. Recollect after a new interface, hosting change, acquisition, or material data-flow change.

### 2. Access, operations, and incident packet

- **Request and owner:** Security operations, identity, infrastructure, service-provider, and safeguards owners provide authorized-access rosters, provisioning/deprovisioning evidence, logging/monitoring records, media/disposal records, supplier oversight, incident procedures, and exercise or incident follow-up.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a selected access change, operational event, or incident drill to its approved evidence location, owner, timing, escalation, limitation, and corrective action. This cannot determine whether an event is reportable or establish that all FTI activity was logged.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag incomplete metadata and aging actions from approved, minimized records. Humans decide access, containment, notifications, external reporting, and remediation. Refresh after an access-control change, incident, supplier transition, or audit observation.

### 3. Reporting, governance, and independent-review packet

- **Request and owner:** Safeguards program leadership, legal, compliance, executive sponsors, and independent reviewers provide required-report calendar, internal approvals, assessment/monitoring results, exception register, management decisions, remediation plans, and closure/retest evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected reporting or governance milestone to its accountable official, approval record, evidence provenance, due date, and review result. It cannot file, approve, or attest to a report.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may maintain a factual evidence index and flag upcoming milestones without complete support. Authorized humans approve content, risk acceptance, submissions, and remediation closure. Review quarterly and after an IRS direction, material finding, or scope change.
