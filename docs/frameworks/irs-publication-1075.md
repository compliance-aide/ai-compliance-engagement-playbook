# IRS Publication 1075 / FTI safeguarding — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IRS content or an approved safeguard report. Check the [IRS Safeguards Program](https://www.irs.gov/privacy-disclosure/safeguards-program).

## Engagement focus

Map FTI receipt, flow, storage, processing, transmission, disposal, labels, approved environments, access, logging, incidents, suppliers, media, and annual report evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain labels and evidence; independent reviewers sample boundaries and report support. AI must not receive FTI in unapproved environments, submit reports, authorize services, or decide incident reporting. Review quarterly and annually.
