# Bahamas data protection — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Bahamian legal advice or a compliance conclusion.

## Source and applicability

The [official Act PDF](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2003/2003-0003/2003-0003.pdf)
is Chapter 324A, LRO 1/2008, checked 2026-09-04. Its scope provisions distinguish
establishment and equipment-based processing; exclusions need a specific legal
basis. Its definition of days is working days. Do not import another jurisdiction's
calendar-day request rule.

An official [2025 bill](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/BILLS/2025/2025-0079/2025-0079.pdf)
is also available. This review has not established its enactment, commencement or
replacement effect. A bill is not proof of operative law. Before legal conclusions,
counsel must verify current law, amendments and commencement and pin the applicable
version. See the [author record](../refresh-reviews/bahamas-data-protection.md).

## Engagement focus

Connect each personal-data activity to an accountable owner, approved purpose,
actual handling, rights process and evidence. Continue factual preparation during
source uncertainty while marking all version-dependent conclusions pending.

## Roles

Business owners supply processing facts; privacy owns the program; security and
records owners provide operating evidence. Bahamian counsel decides legal scope,
rights, transfers and regulatory obligations. Independent reviewers challenge
workpapers. AI may reconcile permitted metadata and draft cases; it cannot decide
lawfulness, disclose personal data, accept risk or submit to a regulator.

## Before starting

Obtain entity/equipment locations, controller/processor decisions, source register,
processing inventory, recipients, retention/hold rules, request procedures and
authorised evidence access. Identify current-law uncertainty before setting legal
timers. Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md); real data remains in approved
storage and fictional records are used for QA.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Resolve law and scope | Counsel checks operative version, entity/equipment facts and exclusions. | Source-backed scope record; unresolved transition is visible. |
| 2. Map handling | Business/privacy identify collection, purpose, systems, recipients, overseas routes and retention. AI reconciles IDs. | Complete declared population with gaps and owners; no inferred lawful purpose. |
| 3. Link decisions | Privacy/legal compare actual activities with approved handling and supplier instructions. | Each activity has a decision reference or an open question. |
| 4. Define rights cases | Privacy/legal set access, rectification/erasure and marketing-objection routes with approved clocks and exceptions. | Fictional normal/refusal/escalation cases and expected outputs. |
| 5. Observe operation | Authorised QA operators execute approved cases and retention/hold checks. | Actual output/readback linked to the correct source record and authority. |
| 6. Correct gaps | Owners resolve adverse results through approved changes; reviewer retests. | Finding/change/retest chain retains failed observations. |
| 7. Handoff and renew | Privacy assembles evidence and decisions; counsel resolves open legal questions. | Review packet and next queue explicitly distinguish draft from approved conclusions. |

## Evidence and test plan

| Evidence and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entity/equipment map, business/legal | Trace scope rationale to actual processing locations and source version. | Decision and facts agree. | A Bahamas address alone does not settle all scope questions. |
| Handling/supplier register, privacy | Trace a collection to purpose, recipients and approved retention. | Material routes have owners and evidence. | A missing overseas route remains unassessed. |
| Fictional rights records, privacy | Run access, correction and marketing cases separately under approved rules. | Correct route, timer and authorised outcome are observed. | One successful access case cannot prove all rights paths. |
| Disposal/hold receipts, records | Compare eligible and held fictional records before/after authorised action. | Observed state matches the approved disposition. | A retention policy is not proof of deletion. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Missing current-law verification blocks legal conclusions, not permitted factual
inventory. Conflicting identities or rights requests go to privacy, without AI
inventing verification requirements. Security incidents escalate immediately to
the approved incident/privacy route; do not assume another law's breach clock.
After interruption, restore the original request timeline and inspect action
receipts before repeating disclosure or erasure.

## Cadence and renewal

Use counsel-approved clocks and working-day calendars for the applicable source;
record trigger, timezone and escalation owner. Quarterly inventory and annual
program review are planning defaults. Reassess before new purposes, suppliers,
overseas handling, material releases or legislative changes.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope/source decisions, handling map, protected evidence index, QA results,
findings/retests and next queue. Name the unresolved 2025 bill/current-law check.
No legal readiness claim is complete until that gap is resolved. Independent
reviews and named human publication approval remain pending; original prose and
links only, with no statutory text or personal evidence reproduced.
