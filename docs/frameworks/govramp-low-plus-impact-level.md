# GovRAMP Low+ terminology and assessment-route engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not program content, a current Low+ availability claim or an authorization decision. Draft pending independent source, skeptical and rights review.

## Source and applicability

Check the [current document library](https://govramp.org/document-library), [Ready route](https://govramp.org/ready), [program directory](https://govramp.org/program-participants) and the actual government requirement. The library read 2026-09-04 lists Low, Moderate and High packages; a Low+ package was not observed in its returned catalog. Older indexed material describes Low+ or uses different category lists. The older Ready URL redirects to the current route.

These observations do not by themselves prove that Low+ is retired, unavailable or equivalent to another level. Obtain a dated program/partner decision identifying what “Low+” means in this engagement and which exact baseline, additions and status route apply. Full current policies, historical transition instructions and contract interpretation remain unverified.

## Engagement focus

Resolve ambiguous or historical Low+ terminology into a traceable assessment plan without inventing a baseline or erasing contractual additions. Preserve the original requirement, authoritative clarification and resulting evidence obligations. If the route is not confirmed, continue safe inventory work but do not claim Low+ readiness or authorization.

## Roles

Government procurement/security owners interpret their requirement. Program authorities resolve program terminology and route availability. Offering, legal and security owners approve scope and documented obligations. Assessors and PMO retain their formal decisions; an independent reviewer challenges reconciliation and evidence. Operators supply current implementation records.

AI may compare sources, retain contradictions and draft decision packets. AI cannot select a replacement level, waive contract requirements, construct an official Low+ baseline, accept risk, submit attestations or make authorization/procurement decisions.

## Before starting

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record the exact source using Low+, issuer, date, contract version, offering, boundary, data and intended program milestone. Collect related current sources and any written clarification. Do not normalize the plus sign away in inventories or proposals; it may identify additional obligations that must be resolved.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Preserve the original requirement.** Capture its exact identifier, context, dates and authority without reproducing restricted content publicly. Output: requirement provenance record.
2. **Resolve the route.** Ask the responsible program/partner owners to identify the accepted level, baseline version, additions and decision path in the authorized engagement channel. Output: dated clarification or an explicit unresolved status. No external contact is authorized merely by this guide.
3. **Build the obligation register.** Separate the confirmed base requirements, individually identified additions and government-specific conditions. Record source, owner and acceptance criteria for each. Output: complete register; do not infer extra controls by choosing arbitrary Moderate controls.
4. **Reconcile offering coverage.** Map every scoped component, region, feature, data flow and inherited/customer responsibility to those obligations. Output: full boundary and responsibility matrix, retaining uncovered dependencies.
5. **Collect and test evidence.** Under the approved route, follow the corresponding [Low](govramp-low-impact-level.md) or other authorized level workflow plus every confirmed addition. Record source/version, expected result, actual result, build/period and coverage. Output: evidence manifest with separate baseline and addition results.
6. **Resolve findings and review.** Track each failure, exception, correction and retest without deleting original observations. An independent reviewer checks that the clarification was applied throughout the package and that no required addition disappeared. Output: disagreements, remediation and decision packet.
7. **Verify final representations.** Compare the actual program decision and buyer acceptance with the proposal and service catalog. Preserve the official status/level wording and describe contractual additions separately when appropriate. Output: approved claim record backed by authoritative evidence, not a fabricated Low+ badge.

## Evidence and test plan

### Terminology and authority package

Maintain the original Low+ references, current program sources, historical versions, conflicting statements, clarification owner and final decision. Record whether clarification concerns program recognition, a procurement condition or an internal security target. One authority's decision need not settle the other two. A failed directory search is not a retirement notice.

### Baseline and additions package

Keep every confirmed obligation, source version, owner, implementation, test and disposition. Prevent a migration to a current template from dropping extra requirements. Retain a complete before/after reconciliation with unresolved mappings; do not publish proprietary crosswalks. Completion of the base set cannot close an untested addition.

### Operating boundary and status package

Tie results to actual offering versions, regions, shared services and customer responsibilities. Preserve assessment findings, retests, monitoring gaps, incidents and status sources. Track submissions and authority decisions separately. The [general GovRAMP guide](govramp-licensed-program.md) governs status-evidence distinctions; listing presence does not establish a particular status or buyer acceptance.

Maintain the full known inventory and all additions in the record. Disclose any technical test selection and untested coverage rather than silently sampling or capping evidence. Keep licensed program material, private contracts and customer records in authorized restricted storage.

## Failure branches and decisions

- No authoritative meaning for Low+: mark route applicability inconclusive; do not prescribe a made-up control set.
- Clarification identifies Low plus specific requirements: retain both parts and their authorities; do not claim this creates a separately recognized program status.
- Clarification selects another level: use the confirmed route, preserve historical evidence and reconcile every original contractual condition before closing it.
- Contract and program instructions disagree: escalate the conflict to both decision owners; AI must not waive the contract or overwrite the program baseline.
- Base controls pass but an addition is untested: report the base results within their scope and the addition as not_tested. Do not declare the full obligation complete.

Fictional desk case: a buyer clarifies its Low+ wording as Low plus a specific log-retention condition. Low evidence is available, but retention was never tested. The base evidence remains bounded; the additional condition is not_tested, and complete buyer acceptance is unproven.

## Cadence and renewal

Recheck terminology at procurement changes, source updates and renewal planning. After route confirmation, use that route's verified monitoring and reassessment schedule plus contract-specific deadlines. Do not invent an annual Low+ renewal or treat website omission as automatic certificate expiration.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the provenance/clarification record, confirmed route, full baseline/additions register, boundary matrix, test results, gaps, disagreements and named next owners. Classify assertions supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable or not_tested. This workflow is complete only to the extent its evidence and decisions are actually recorded; it does not resolve current Low+ availability by itself.
