# Singapore Data Protection Trustmark engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Data Protection Trustmark requirements, legal advice, a certification assessment, or a compliance claim. Confirm current materials through the [IMDA Data Protection Trustmark certification program](https://www.imda.gov.sg/how-we-can-help/data-protection-trustmark-certification).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an accountable data-protection engagement record that connects personal-data processing context, accountable privacy and business owners, data and service-provider inventories, approved policies and operating evidence, incidents, remediation, cross-border considerations, and independent review. Keep evidence preparation separate from decisions about privacy law, certification, and the appropriate handling of affected individuals' information.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, privacy, legal, security, risk, and business authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine legal obligations, select a certification body, assign an assessment result, approve data uses or transfers, decide a notification obligation, access personal data without authorization, submit an application, or claim certification or compliance. Review annually and before material personal-data, supplier, cross-border, incident, policy, or certification-scope changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [IMDA Data Protection Trustmark certification program](https://www.imda.gov.sg/how-we-can-help/data-protection-trustmark-certification) as the official program source; checked 2026-07-31. Current criteria, applicable scope, assessment method, certification process, and reuse rights need confirmation through IMDA material and qualified Singapore privacy/legal review. This original plan neither reproduces criteria nor decides certification readiness.

### 1. Personal-data scope, accountability, and policy package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, business, product, data, and security owners provide a dated processing/data-flow inventory; accountable roles; data categories and purposes; systems; policy and notice versions; retention context; supplier/recipient register; cross-border decision inputs; and scope questions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processing route to owner, system, purpose record, policy/notice reference, recipient/vendor, and retention context. This supports a factual preparation record; it cannot determine legal obligations, consent/notice sufficiency, or transfer permissibility.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved records and flag changed flows or unowned evidence. Privacy/legal humans decide applicability and actions. Refresh for material processing, purpose, supplier, transfer, policy, or system change.

### 2. Rights operation, safeguards, and incident package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, customer operations, records, and security owners provide approved rights-request and identity-verification procedures, de-identified case metadata, access/security-operation references, training records, incident/exercise timelines, communications approvals, and remediation evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a de-identified request or incident exercise through trigger, owner, human decision/escalation, action, and follow-up. This supports workflow traceability; it cannot decide an individual outcome, prove all safeguards effective, or determine notification/reporting obligations.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a restricted evidence index and flag incomplete records; it may not access unapproved personal data, respond to individuals, notify authorities, or make a certification conclusion. Recollect after an incident, failed exercise, complaint trend, or material security change.

### 3. Supplier, assurance, and certification-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, legal, privacy, security, assurance, and executive owners provide vendor diligence/instruction records, assurance and change evidence, exceptions, independent-review observations, remediation/retest, management decisions, source-change watch, and application-approval records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected vendor change, exception, or readiness observation to facts, accountable human authority, expiry, action owner, and retest. This supports accountable governance; it cannot select a certification body, submit an application, accept risk, or claim certification.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare non-authoritative workpapers and flag stale approvals. Humans approve contracts, exceptions, representations, submissions, and closure. Refresh annually and after source, supplier, incident, scope, or certification-process change.
