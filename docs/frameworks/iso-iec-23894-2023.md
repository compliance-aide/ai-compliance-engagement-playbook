# ISO/IEC 23894:2023 AI risk-management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO/IEC standard text, legal advice, a model evaluation, or a certification claim. Confirm the current edition through the [ISO/IEC 23894:2023 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an AI risk record that connects each intended use to accountable owners, affected people, model and data dependencies, design and deployment decisions, evaluation evidence, monitoring signals, incident reports, change decisions, and retirement conditions. Preserve uncertainty and limitations rather than converting them into a binary compliant/noncompliant label, and connect material issues to the organization’s broader risk process.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business owners define intended use and operating context; technical teams preserve version, evaluation, and monitoring evidence; risk and privacy teams challenge risk analysis; legal and safety owners decide obligations and unacceptable outcomes; independent reviewers test evidence traceability. AI may organize approved records, identify incomplete lineage, and draft review questions, but cannot set risk appetite, approve a model for use, decide fairness or safety, make legal conclusions, or certify a system. Reassess before material data, model, purpose, population, deployment, supplier, or incident changes and perform a documented annual review.
