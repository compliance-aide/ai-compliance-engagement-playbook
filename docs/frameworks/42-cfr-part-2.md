# 42 CFR Part 2 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, a patient decision or an
> authorization to disclose substance use disorder records.

## Source and applicability

Use [current eCFR Part 2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-2)
and the [HHS final-rule fact sheet](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/regulatory-initiatives/fact-sheet-42-cfr-part-2-final-rule/index.html).
Checked 2026-09-04: HHS states a February 16, 2026 compliance date for applicable
final-rule requirements, with the accounting-of-disclosures date subject to a
separate future rule. It distinguishes broad treatment/payment/operations
consent from protections for counseling notes and proceedings against patients.
Have counsel confirm current provisions, applicable dates and state-law overlays;
HIPAA alignment does not make the two regimes identical.

Rights: source-linked original workflow, no patient data or reproduced regulatory
text. The [author record](../refresh-reviews/42-cfr-part-2.md) identifies the source

## Engagement focus

Maintain approved program/record scope and demonstrate that actual record routes
follow the applicable consent, recipient and disclosure decisions. Test both
permitted and blocked routes using fictional cases. Keep real patient decisions
with authorized privacy/legal personnel.

## Roles

Program owners supply services and record-flow facts. Privacy and counsel resolve
coverage, consent validity, disclosure conditions and legal requests. Records and
technology owners implement approved routing; incident leadership handles events.
Independent reviewers challenge evidence. AI may reconcile approved metadata and
draft workpapers; it cannot decide coverage, release records, respond to legal
process or send sensitive data to unapproved tools.

## Before starting

Obtain program/entity inventory, counsel-approved applicability decision,
record-flow map, consent and notice versions, role/access map, disclosure procedures,
escalation contacts and approved QA cases. If scope is unresolved, collect only
permitted metadata and prepare questions. Follow the
[agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) and the engagement's evidence restrictions.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Resolve program and record scope | Program/privacy owners map services, entities, systems and recipients; counsel records coverage and overlays. | Approved applicability record and unresolved routes. Do not infer coverage from a diagnosis code alone. |
| 2. Map disclosure branches | Privacy/counsel distinguish applicable consent-based, legally exceptional, counseling-note and legal-proceeding routes. AI records the approved decision rules by reference. | Authorized routing matrix with decision owner; no automatic disclosure from a generic HIPAA permission. |
| 3. Prepare forms and operating controls | Owners reconcile current forms, notice versions, recipient handling, workforce instructions and configuration with that matrix. | Approved versions linked to implementation records and effective dates. Drafting a form is not deploying it. |
| 4. Exercise permitted and blocked cases | Reviewer approves fictional cases; authorized operators execute them in QA. Record intake, authority check, routing, output and audit trail. | Observed route matches the approved expectation, including refusal/escalation; no real patient disclosure. |
| 5. Review events and complaints | Privacy/incident/legal owners follow the appropriate escalation route for suspected disclosures, complaints or demands. AI assembles timestamped permitted facts. | Decision trail, preservation references and clock owner; no delay until periodic review. |
| 6. Correct and retest | Owners implement approved fixes; reviewer repeats affected cases. | Retest shows the intended route and retained history; an updated policy alone cannot close the issue. |
| 7. Renew the operating record | Privacy owner reviews changes in programs, recipients, forms, law and technology. | Updated scope/route matrix, training evidence, remaining findings and next checks. |

These are original engagement steps, not a replacement for regulatory provisions
or a complete legal decision tree.

## Evidence and test plan

| Request and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approved program/flow map from program and privacy owners | Trace reviewer-selected routes across originating program, storage and recipient. | Each route has a coverage decision and accountable owner. | Metadata traceability alone cannot settle legal coverage. |
| Consent/form/notice versions from privacy | Match a fictional case to the approved version and purpose-specific route. | Correct version and decision path are observable. | A broad consent must not be assumed sufficient for every record or purpose. |
| QA outputs and access records from technology | Exercise authorized, missing-authority and escalation cases under approved expectations. | Permitted output is bounded; blocked cases expose no record. | A denial message with leaked data is an adverse result. |
| Event/legal-request records from incident and counsel owners | Trace preservation, escalation, decision and approved communication references. | Facts and authority are distinguishable and timestamps retained. | A subpoena or apparent urgency is not AI authority to disclose. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Unknown consent status, unverified recipient or conflicting directions block that
disclosure route and go to privacy/counsel. A potentially urgent clinical event
uses the approved clinical/legal escalation route; the AI does not adjudicate an
exception. Suspected exposure activates incident procedures immediately. Maintain
counsel's effective-date record for accounting requirements rather than treating
every new right as having the same implementation date. Keep complaints and
legal demands separate from routine evidence collection.

## Cadence and renewal

Review forms and routing before material changes; exercise negative paths after
configuration changes and periodically under the approved plan. Quarterly records
review and annual exercise are planning defaults, not statutory intervals.
Record binding event deadlines and owners separately; the 2026 transition date
is not an annual renewal deadline.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver applicability and route decisions, approved form/version inventory,
protected evidence index, QA case receipts, training records, findings/retests and
change queue. Identify untested routes and pending counsel decisions. No patient
information belongs in this public guide or its publication review record.
