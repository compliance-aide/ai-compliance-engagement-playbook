# IEC TS 62443-6-2:2025 component evaluation — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC methodology text or an evaluation/certification decision.

## Source and applicability

The [IEC publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67463), checked 2026-09-04, describes repeatable and reproducible evaluation against IEC 62443-4-2. It does not define a complete certification scheme or prescribe particular testing tools. It identifies an existing IEC 62443-4-1 secure development lifecycle as a prerequisite, while excluding evaluation of that lifecycle process from this methodology's scope.

Full licensed methodology, prerequisite evidence rules and any selected certification scheme remain unverified. Obtain authorized sources and qualified evaluation decisions before execution. Do not copy restricted criteria into this public repository or AI prompts without permission. A test report is not automatically a certificate.

## Engagement focus

Make each component evaluation reconstructable from its exact subject, source criteria, environment, evidence, evaluator reasoning and outcome. Keep technical findings separate from scheme approval and industrial deployment decisions.

## Roles

Product owners identify the evaluation subject and supply evidence. Qualified evaluators approve methods, determine findings and resolve applicability. Independent quality reviewers challenge reproducibility and reasoning. Certification authorities retain scheme decisions where applicable. AI may index permitted records and flag missing links; it cannot execute tests, determine official results, waive findings, issue certificates or change industrial systems.

## Before starting

Record component category, model, hardware revision, firmware/software identity, configuration, interfaces, dependencies and intended use. Identify source editions, evaluator competence, permitted workspace and the evidence supporting lifecycle prerequisites. Resolve missing prerequisites before claiming readiness for the methodology; do not assume an unsupported product is eligible.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Agree evaluation scope.** Reconcile every included variant and interface with the intended claim. Record exclusions and the qualified authority approving them. Distinguish a component evaluation from a whole-system assessment.
2. **Verify prerequisites and source baseline.** Link lifecycle evidence to the actual product scope. Confirm the licensed methodology and referenced requirement editions. Record unresolved eligibility questions for the evaluator; this workflow cannot waive them.
3. **Approve the evaluation plan.** Assign applicable criteria, evidence needs, methods, expected outcomes and responsible evaluators. Record environment, tool versions, configuration, safety constraints and authorization. Tool selection is a justified evaluator choice, not automatically mandated by this document.
4. **Reconcile evaluation evidence.** Preserve exact input artifacts, procedures, observations, errors and conditions. Check that another qualified evaluator can identify what was examined and how the result was reached. No active test or industrial access is authorized by this documentation guide.
5. **Determine findings through qualified review.** Link each conclusion to the criterion and supporting evidence. Preserve contradictions, missing coverage and differences between variants. A successful tool run does not establish that an evaluation criterion is satisfied.
6. **Remediate and retest.** Assign findings to owners, identify changed artifacts and have evaluators determine affected reevaluation scope. Preserve original failures and later results. Do not automatically reuse old evidence after a material change.
7. **Issue the bounded handoff.** Reconcile all criteria and open findings, then obtain authorized review and any separate scheme decision. State exact evaluated configuration and limitations. Maintain evidence and change triggers so later claims can be checked against the original evaluation.

## Evidence and test plan

### 1. Subject and prerequisite package

Keep the complete variant/configuration register, source baseline, evaluator assignment and lifecycle prerequisite references. Trace each scope assertion to an accountable decision. Missing evidence remains visible rather than becoming implied eligibility.

### 2. Reproducibility package

Keep approved criteria, method references, environment identities, steps, inputs, raw outcomes and evaluator reasoning in the authorized repository. Preserve all evidence supplied for assessment without silent trimming or sampling. Record actual criterion coverage separately from inventory completeness. A rerun producing a different result requires investigation, not selection of the favorable run.

### 3. Findings and decision package

Keep finding records, corrected artifact identities, reevaluation decisions, results and authorized report versions. Distinguish report preparation, evaluation completion, certification issuance and customer deployment. Each remaining action needs an owner and due date.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Lifecycle prerequisite is unsupported: obtain qualified determination before dependent readiness claims.
- Test artifact identity is missing: resolve it before applying results to a product release.
- Results cannot be reproduced: preserve both outcomes and investigate environment, procedure and artifact differences.
- New configuration changes an interface: have evaluators decide affected criteria and evidence reuse.
- Marketing claims certification from an evaluation report: verify the actual scheme artifact and restrict wording to supported scope.

Use [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) assertions: supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable and not_tested. They support internal evidence tracking and do not replace official evaluation verdicts.

## Cadence and renewal

Review every evaluation and material change to subject, methodology, requirements or evidence. Set other reviews through approved obligations and scheme rules. Do not invent universal annual certification duties.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the exact evaluation subject, prerequisite decisions, approved plan, complete evidence index, finding rationale, reevaluation records and bounded report. Full licensed-source, independent skeptical and rights reviews remain pending. No real evaluation test or certification occurred in preparing this draft.

Fictional author desk case: two reports claim the same firmware version, but one tested a configuration with the maintenance interface disabled and the other enabled. Matching version labels do not establish comparable coverage. Preserve the configuration difference and ask evaluators to resolve its impact.
