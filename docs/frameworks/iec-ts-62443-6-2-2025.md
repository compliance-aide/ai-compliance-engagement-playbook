# IEC TS 62443-6-2:2025 component evaluation — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC content or an evaluation/certification decision. Check the [IEC record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67463); do not place IEC text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain evaluation scope, component versions, evaluator competence, authorized test plans, evidence provenance, reproducibility records, findings, remediation, retest evidence, and decision separation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Qualified evaluators and certification authorities retain method and conclusion authority; independent quality reviewers test process integrity. AI indexes evidence and identifies missing traceability, but cannot execute tests, determine results, or issue a certificate. Review each evaluation and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
