# ISO/IEC 27557:2022 organizational privacy-risk engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a privacy-risk conclusion, a legal determination, a risk-acceptance decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27557:2022 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71675.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an organizational privacy-risk record that connects PII processing contexts, accountable owners, individual and organizational impact assumptions, authorized evidence, treatment choices, exceptions, incidents, remediation, enterprise-risk integration, and independent review. AI may organize authorized evidence and questions but cannot determine privacy impacts or make risk decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, legal, risk, security, product, operations, finance, and business owners retain accountable decisions; risk and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test context, assumption quality, impact traceability, treatment evidence, and escalation paths. AI may organize authorized inputs, identify gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine legal obligations, assess or accept privacy risk, approve treatment, decide processing, alter systems, initiate changes, or claim compliance. Review material processing changes continuously, conduct quarterly risk reviews, and perform an annual independent program review.
