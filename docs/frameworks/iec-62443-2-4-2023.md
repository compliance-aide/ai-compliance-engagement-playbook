# IEC 62443-2-4:2023 IACS service-provider security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC content or a service-provider assurance claim. Check the [IEC record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67631); do not place IEC text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain service scope, integration and maintenance procedures, customer boundaries, personnel competence, change records, remote-support controls, incident handling, supplier evidence, exceptions, and service-review records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Service-provider leaders and customer asset owners approve work boundaries and operational changes; independent reviewers test evidence. AI correlates approved work records, but cannot access IACS, perform changes, or claim conformity. Review customer engagements quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
