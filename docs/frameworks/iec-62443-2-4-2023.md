# IEC 62443-2-4:2023 IACS service-provider security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC content or a service-provider assurance claim. Check the [IEC record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67631); do not place IEC text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain service scope, integration and maintenance procedures, customer boundaries, personnel competence, change records, remote-support controls, incident handling, supplier evidence, exceptions, and service-review records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Service-provider leaders and customer asset owners approve work boundaries and operational changes; independent reviewers test evidence. AI correlates approved work records, but cannot access IACS, perform changes, or claim conformity. Review customer engagements quarterly and annually.

### 1. Customer-service boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Service delivery, customer, contract, and security owners provide the approved service description, customer/provider responsibility decisions, supported assets/environments, remote-support boundaries, authorized contacts, and stated exclusions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected service engagement to an accountable provider owner, customer owner, authorization boundary, and contract/service reference. This tests clarity of a sampled boundary; it cannot establish contract performance or responsibility for unrecorded services.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized service records and flag unclear ownership. Humans approve engagement and responsibility decisions. Refresh upon customer onboarding, scope change, remote-access change, or contract amendment.

### 2. Personnel, remote work, and change-control package

- **Request and owner:** Service management and operations owners provide personnel authorization/competence records, approved remote-support sessions or access-review evidence, maintenance/change tickets, customer approvals where required, and exception records.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected change or remote-support event for authorized requester, accountable performer, recorded approvals, evidence trail, and exception handling. This supports sample traceability; it cannot prove every session was authorized or every change was safe.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may link approved tickets, access evidence, and customer approvals, and flag stale access. It must not connect to IACS, approve access, or perform changes. Recollect after an access anomaly, failed review, material change, or personnel role change.

### 3. Incident, supplier, and service-review package

- **Request and owner:** Incident, supplier, service, and customer-relations owners provide incident/escalation records, supplier dependencies, corrective actions, customer communications approved for release, performance/review records, and open-risk decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one incident or material service issue to escalation owner, customer interface, supplier dependency, corrective action, and authorized closure or risk escalation. This tests accountability in a sample; it cannot determine root cause, guarantee service continuity, or accept residual risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble an authorized timeline and surface overdue dependencies. Humans decide incident communications, supplier action, closure, and risk acceptance. Review quarterly and after an incident, significant supplier issue, or service-boundary change.
