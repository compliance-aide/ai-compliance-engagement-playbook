# Australia Cyber Security (Security Standards for Smart Devices) Rules 2025 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Australian legislation, legal advice, a product-scope decision, a statement of compliance, or a compliance claim. Confirm current requirements through the [Cyber Security (Security Standards for Smart Devices) Rules 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L00276/latest/versions) and applicable authority and counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a product-compliance record for smart devices placed in the relevant Australian market: product identity and version, manufacturer and supplier roles, product-scope analysis, supported software and services, password and vulnerability-management evidence, support-period and update records, statements of compliance, recall or failure-notification artifacts, supplier evidence, and material changes. Separate legal applicability decisions, statutory records, customer representations, and voluntary good-practice evidence. When a product scope, role, release, supplier, or update commitment changes, route the change through accountable product, legal, and security owners before making any external claim.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable product, security, engineering, support, supplier-management, legal, records, and executive owners. Operators preserve product and version inventories, design and test evidence, vulnerability intake and response records, update and end-of-support evidence, statement records, supplier confirmations, and approved external communications. Review in-scope products, statement validity, support commitments, vulnerabilities, supplier changes, and external claims quarterly and before releases. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples products and records for traceability and consistency; auditors test the evidence trail without determining legal applicability, approving statements, making market-access decisions, or assuming management responsibility.

AI may organize supplied evidence, reconcile product and support inventories, flag missing ownership or conflicting records, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot determine statutory applicability, approve a statement of compliance, decide product safety or market access, make a legal or compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [Cyber Security (Security Standards for Smart Devices) Rules 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L00276/latest/versions), checked 2026-07-31, with applicable authority guidance and counsel. This original plan does not reproduce statutory text, decide whether a device or role is in scope, or create a statement of compliance. Product, legal, security, and authorized signatories retain all applicability, release, statement, and market-access decisions.

### 1. Product, role, and scope-decision register

- **Request and owner:** Product, legal, supply-chain, and security owners provide product/model/version catalogue entries, manufacturer/importer/distributor role records, Australian market channel context, connected-service/dependency records, dated scope analyses, and accountable approvals.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected marketed model/version to its role record, scope-decision source, support owner, and approval date. This supports auditable product context; it cannot determine statutory applicability, decide market access, or prove a complete catalogue.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied model, version, role, and owner metadata and flag conflicts. Humans approve scope and role decisions. Refresh before a market launch, model/version change, channel or supplier change, or ownership change.

### 2. Credential, vulnerability, update, and support-period package

- **Request and owner:** Engineering, security, support, and supplier owners provide approved credential/security design records, vulnerability intake and response records, test references, update/release history, published support-period records, and supplier assurance artifacts.
- **Validate and limit:** Follow a selected vulnerability or update from intake through accountable assessment, remediation/release decision, customer-impact record, and support communication. This supports a selected operational trail; it cannot establish absence of vulnerabilities, prove ongoing effectiveness, or authorize a release.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may produce read-only evidence inventories and flag unsupported dates or ownership gaps. Humans approve remediation, updates, support commitments, and supplier decisions. Recollect after vulnerabilities, releases, support-period changes, or material supplier/dependency changes.

### 3. Statement, notification, and independent challenge workpaper

- **Request and owner:** Product, legal, records, communications, and assurance owners provide controlled statement drafts/versions, source and product references, signer authorities, distribution records, failure/recall-notification procedures and records where applicable, exceptions, and review findings.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected statement or notification artifact to the model/version, retained support, authorized reviewer/signatory, and distribution or decision record. This supports controlled representation; it cannot approve a statement, determine a statutory notification duty, or certify a product.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag drafts with missing version, support, or authority links. Humans approve statements, notifications, exceptions, and closure. Review before release or external distribution, quarterly for active products, and after a material issue or legal update.
