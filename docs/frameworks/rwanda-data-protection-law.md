# Rwanda data-protection law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Consult the [official law record](https://www.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RISA/Publications/2.Laws/Law_relating_to_the_protection_of_personal_data_and_privacy.pdf).

## Engagement focus

Maintain data inventory, accountable processing and rights records, processor and transfer evidence, incident readiness, and a corrective-action log; obtain quarterly owner attestations and annual sampling.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators preserve operational proof; independent reviewers reperform samples and test closure. AI synthesizes gaps but cannot make legal determinations, approve processing, decide notifications, or contact authorities.
