# Rwanda data-protection law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Consult the [official law record](https://www.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RISA/Publications/2.Laws/Law_relating_to_the_protection_of_personal_data_and_privacy.pdf).

## Engagement focus

Maintain data inventory, accountable processing and rights records, processor and transfer evidence, incident readiness, and a corrective-action log; obtain quarterly owner attestations and annual sampling.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators preserve operational proof; independent reviewers reperform samples and test closure. AI synthesizes gaps but cannot make legal determinations, approve processing, decide notifications, or contact authorities.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Rwanda official law record](https://www.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RISA/Publications/2.Laws/Law_relating_to_the_protection_of_personal_data_and_privacy.pdf), checked 2026-07-31. Confirm current status, implementing guidance, jurisdictional scope, and reuse rights with qualified Rwandan counsel. This is original operational guidance, not a legal interpretation or authority submission.

### 1. Data inventory, purpose, and accountable-decision package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, product, and data owners provide a dated processing inventory, data-flow and system records, purpose descriptions, categories, retention context, accountable owners, notices, and change-review decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected collection-to-use route to its collection point, documented purpose, owner, system, recipient, and lifecycle record. This supports a factual accountability trail; it cannot determine legal conditions, data-subject status, or notice adequacy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved flow and release records and flag unexplained processing. Named humans and counsel decide classifications and approvals. Refresh for new collection, purpose, system, recipient, or material release.

### 2. Data-subject, retention, and security-operation package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, support, security, and records owners provide approved request/verification procedures, redacted case metadata, access-control review references, retention/disposal evidence, staff training, and complaint/escalation records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a de-identified request or disposal event through intake, authority, source record, action, communication approval, and closure. This supports selected workflow evidence; it cannot decide a request, establish identity, or prove security control effectiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a restricted evidence index and identify missing dates; it cannot contact individuals, access unapproved personal data, or change systems. Humans decide responses. Recollect after complaints, a failed exercise, or changed workflow.

### 3. Processor, transfer, incident, and remedy package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, legal, security, privacy, and incident owners provide processor records and instructions, supplier review artifacts, transfer-review inputs, incident/exercise timelines, remediation/retest history, and management decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one supplier change, cross-border question, or incident exercise to supporting facts, human authority, corrective action, and retest. This supports accountable oversight; it cannot authorize a transfer, determine notification, accept risk, or conclude supplier conformance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag expiring supplier reviews and overdue remediation. Humans approve contracts, communications, reporting, and closure. Refresh after a processor change, incident, remediation failure, or annual scope review.
