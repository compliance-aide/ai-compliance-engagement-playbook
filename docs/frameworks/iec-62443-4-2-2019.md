# IEC 62443-4-2:2019 IACS component security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC content or a component-security conclusion. Check the [IEC record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/34421); do not place IEC text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain component scope, use context, technical evidence, version and vulnerability records, deployment assumptions, test records, customer documentation, exceptions, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product and security owners decide component security posture; independent assessors reach conclusions separately. AI organizes approved test and lifecycle evidence, but cannot test components, declare a security level, or certify. Review releases continuously and annually.
