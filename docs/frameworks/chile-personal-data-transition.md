# Chile personal-data transition — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Track the [current Chilean law](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/Navegar?dt=open&idLey=19628) and the scheduled new regime separately.

## Engagement focus

Maintain distinct current-operation and transition-readiness plans; inventory processing and service dependencies; assign dated owners for rights, governance, and evidence changes; rehearse requests and breaches before transition.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own milestones and evidence; independent reviewers validate status against dates and test live workflows. AI compares inventories and produces milestone views, but cannot interpret transitional law, set compliance go-live, or make regulator representations.
