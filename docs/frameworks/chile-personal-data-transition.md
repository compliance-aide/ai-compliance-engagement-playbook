# Chile personal-data transition engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, a transition determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current and effective-date materials through [Chile Law 21.719](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=1209272) and the then-current Chilean personal-data law.

## Engagement focus

Maintain distinct current-operation and transition-readiness records that connect processing activities, personal-data and supplier dependencies, accountable privacy and business owners, dated implementation decisions, rights and incident workflows, remediation, and independently reviewable evidence. Preserve a clear boundary between gathering transition evidence and making legal, regulatory, notification, or data-use decisions.

## Source and applicability

Source check: 2026-09-04. The official BCN record identifies December 1, 2026 for the deferred effectiveness of Law 21.719's reform. Maintain the [Law 19.628 record](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/Navegar?dt=open&idLey=19628) for the applicable current text and the reform record for transition planning. Verify the version selected in the source viewer: a future consolidated text is not the law governing every current action. Qualified Chilean counsel identifies provision-specific transitional rules, implementing direction, scope and applicable duties. Do not assume every institutional transitional action waits until the general date.

## Roles

Privacy/legal owners decide applicability, lawful processing, rights outcomes, transfers and communications. Business/system owners provide actual processing evidence. Vendor, security and records owners address their dependencies. Leadership approves resources and transition decisions; independent reviewers challenge results. AI prepares registers and questions, not legal conclusions, data-use approvals or regulatory representations.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record the entity, processing activity, current date, source versions, owner, inputs, output and exit check. Require the approved legal transition register, processing inventory and handling permissions. Mark missing legal decisions explicitly while continuing independent fact gathering. Use synthetic QA cases and keep personal information out of this repository.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Separate time periods | Legal owner records current duties, future duties and any special transitional provisions, with source/version and trigger for each. | Approved obligation calendar; no blanket switch based only on a page title. |
| 2. Map processing | Data/system owners reconcile purposes, categories, recipients, locations, retention and transfers against actual systems. | Complete inventory and discrepancies; every route has an owner. |
| 3. Identify changes | Privacy/legal owners compare the current operation with each applicable future obligation. Assign actions, dependencies and required approvals. | Transition register linking obligation, gap, implementation and verification; planned is not implemented. |
| 4. Prepare operations | Product/operations owners draft notices, request routing, verification, retention and escalation procedures appropriate to the approved period and role. | Versioned drafts with activation decisions and unresolved legal questions. |
| 5. Verify behavior | Reviewers trace approved synthetic rights, disposal and incident exercises through intake, owner decision, system actions and resulting records. | Expected/actual workpapers preserving failures, original timestamps and untested coverage. |
| 6. Align suppliers | Vendor owners reconcile contracts/instructions and actual recipient handling with approved transition requirements. | Supplier action register with evidence, gaps and decision owners; promises do not prove operation. |
| 7. Correct and authorize transition | Owners implement separately approved changes and reviewers retest. Leadership/legal owners decide readiness and effective activation against the exact packet. | Recorded decision with conditions and readback; no automatic declaration of compliance. |
| 8. Monitor after change | Privacy owner checks deployed versions and ongoing requests, incidents and legal updates; reopens affected obligations. | Owned monitoring queue and retained historical evidence. |

## Evidence and test plan

These original examples support the complete approved obligation register. Preserve the full evidence corpus; do not trim evidence sent to a scorer.

| Package and owner | Verification | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current/future obligations — legal/privacy | Compare each transition action with its approved source version, applicable period and decision. | An effective-date label alone does not establish all transitional rules. |
| Processing/dependencies — system owner | Trace a data use through source, purpose, recipient, location and retention; reconcile discrepancies with the full inventory. | A selected route cannot prove complete discovery. |
| Rights/notices — operations/product | Follow a synthetic case through period-appropriate procedure, decision, actual system outcome and response draft. | Test success does not decide a real person's entitlement or deadline. |
| Suppliers/disposal — vendor/records | Compare approved instructions with provider and system readbacks, retaining holds and unresolved copies. | A contract or job-success message is not proof of completed deletion. |
| Incidents/improvement — security/reviewer | Trace an exercise observation through escalation, corrective action and retest. | Simulation does not establish actual breach reporting compliance. |

Record source, date, covered period, legal-version reference, expected/observed result, evidence pointer, limitation, reviewer and next action. Document test populations and untested scope. Do not perform destructive disposal or production changes under this guide.

## Failure branches and decisions

- **Future text mistaken for current law:** isolate affected conclusions, obtain the correct version and revisit dependent actions.
- **Unknown legal basis or transfer condition:** preserve processing facts and route the precise question to counsel; do not infer consent or permission.
- **Missed milestone:** identify the affected obligation and dependency, assign recovery work and escalate the readiness impact.
- **Failed request or disposal test:** retain the failure and original timing; correct and retest before claiming implementation.
- **Supplier evidence missing:** request exact scope/period and retain the gap in the transition decision.
- **Incident or real request:** use approved operational routing and legal clocks; do not invent deadlines or send notices without authorization.
- **Interrupted work or uncertain update:** preserve versions, last completed step and pending decisions; read back the destination before retrying.

## Cadence and renewal

Work backward from counsel-confirmed applicable dates and record internal preparation milestones separately. Review after source changes, new processing, suppliers, transfers, incidents and missed milestones. Annual review alone is not sufficient transition scheduling and is not asserted as a universal legal requirement.

## Completion and handoff

A preparation packet includes current/future obligation records, processing map, transition actions, versioned procedures, supplier evidence, test results, open gaps and decision owners. Mark omissions. Activation, notices and regulatory submissions are separate authorized milestones with actual destination evidence. Handoff specifies next action, owner, due date, applicable period and dependency.
