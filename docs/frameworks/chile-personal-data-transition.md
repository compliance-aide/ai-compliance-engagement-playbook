# Chile personal-data transition engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, a transition determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current and effective-date materials through [Chile Law 21.719](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=1209272) and the then-current Chilean personal-data law.

## Engagement focus

Maintain distinct current-operation and transition-readiness records that connect processing activities, personal-data and supplier dependencies, accountable privacy and business owners, dated implementation decisions, rights and incident workflows, remediation, and independently reviewable evidence. Preserve a clear boundary between gathering transition evidence and making legal, regulatory, notification, or data-use decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, privacy, legal, security, and business authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers test date-sensitive readiness and traceability. AI may organize authorized records, identify milestones at risk, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot interpret transitional law, determine scope or effective dates, approve data uses or transfers, decide notification obligations, access personal data without authorization, submit a regulatory representation, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material personal-data, supplier, cross-border, incident, policy, or legal-transition changes.
