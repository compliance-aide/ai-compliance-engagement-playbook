# Chile personal-data transition engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, a transition determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current and effective-date materials through [Chile Law 21.719](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=1209272) and the then-current Chilean personal-data law.

## Engagement focus

Maintain distinct current-operation and transition-readiness records that connect processing activities, personal-data and supplier dependencies, accountable privacy and business owners, dated implementation decisions, rights and incident workflows, remediation, and independently reviewable evidence. Preserve a clear boundary between gathering transition evidence and making legal, regulatory, notification, or data-use decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, privacy, legal, security, and business authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers test date-sensitive readiness and traceability. AI may organize authorized records, identify milestones at risk, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot interpret transitional law, determine scope or effective dates, approve data uses or transfers, decide notification obligations, access personal data without authorization, submit a regulatory representation, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material personal-data, supplier, cross-border, incident, policy, or legal-transition changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use [Chile Law 21.719](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=1209272) as the official statutory source. Source status: official statutory record linked; current consolidated text, effective-date interpretation, implementing material, and republication rights are **unknown** pending qualified Chilean legal review. This is original planning language, not copied law, legal advice, a transition determination, or a compliance conclusion.

### 1. Current-state, transition, and owner record

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, legal, product, records, and system owners provide a dated processing and data-flow inventory; stated purposes; data categories; systems and locations; recipients, processors, and cross-border dependencies; current policy/notice versions; named accountable owners; and an approved transition-assumption log.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processing route to its source system, purpose record, owner, recipient/dependency, current evidence, and transition assumption with date and reviewer. This supports a factual readiness record; it cannot determine current or future legal scope, effective date, lawful processing, transfer conditions, or sufficiency.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized metadata and flag unowned routes, stale assumptions, or missing decision links. Qualified Chilean counsel and accountable humans decide interpretation and transition actions. Refresh after a new use, data category, supplier, transfer, material system change, official update, or scheduled legal review.

### 2. Rights, transparency, and incident workflow evidence

- **Request and owner:** Privacy and customer-operations owners provide notice/version history, request-intake and escalation procedures, de-identified workflow logs, identity-verification design, retention/disposal records, incident/exercise timeline, communications approvals, and remediation tracking.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a de-identified request, disposal event, or exercise from intake or trigger through assigned human owner, recorded action, escalation, and follow-up. This can demonstrate bounded process traceability; it cannot determine an individual's entitlement, a deadline, a notification duty, or whether a response is legally correct.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a restricted evidence index and internal reminders; it may not authenticate people, decide outcomes, communicate externally, or file notices. Humans and counsel approve outcomes and communications. Recheck after an incident, complaint pattern, interface/process change, or transition milestone.

### 3. Supplier, change, and accountable readiness review

- **Request and owner:** Vendor/procurement, security, privacy, and legal owners provide supplier/data-recipient register, contract and instruction references, change notices, security-assurance inputs, exception records, legal-review questions, remediation/retest evidence, and management decision records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier or material change to the processing record, accountable review, documented limitation, human decision, action owner, and next review date. This supports governance traceability; it cannot establish contract adequacy, supplier conformity, risk acceptance, regulatory posture, or compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag expiring reviews and missing provenance. Human legal, privacy, security, and executive authorities approve contracts, risk decisions, transition commitments, external representations, and closure. Refresh after supplier/subprocessor change, material incident, missed milestone, source update, or annual reassessment.
