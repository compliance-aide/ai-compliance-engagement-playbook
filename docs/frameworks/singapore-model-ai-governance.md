# Singapore Model AI Governance Framework — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a conformity claim. Consult the [IMDA AI governance hub](https://www.imda.gov.sg/how-we-can-help/ai-verify).

## Engagement focus

Govern lifecycle from problem framing and human decision design through data/model evaluation, deployment communication, feedback, correction, and retirement; retain test results and owner decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators document human involvement, changes, evaluations, feedback, and fixes; independent reviewers test human involvement and monitoring. AI can draft artifacts and analyze evaluation output, but cannot determine acceptable harm, make consequential decisions, or suppress escalation. Review quarterly and annually.


## Tailored evidence plan

### 1. Problem framing, human role, and deployment boundary

- **Request and owner:** Request the approved use-case/problem statement, intended users and affected parties, decision-consequence classification, human-involvement design, deployment boundary, and named accountable owners from product, business, legal, and risk owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected use case from approved purpose to the designated human decision role, deployment record, and review date. This supports a documented accountability trail; it cannot determine whether human involvement is sufficient or whether deployment is appropriate.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved use-case metadata and flag incomplete ownership. Humans decide purpose, acceptable impact, consequential decisions, and go/no-go. Refresh for changed use, user population, decision consequence, or deployment channel.

### 2. Data, model, evaluation, and communication evidence

- **Request and owner:** Request data-governance records, model/version register, evaluation design and summarized results, known-limitations log, monitoring plan, communication or notice approvals, and change-control records from data, engineering, product, and communications owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Select a production version and trace it to authorized data/model records, evaluation review, stated limitations, and communication decision. This evidences provenance of an evaluated release; it cannot prove accuracy, absence of bias, or effective user understanding.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved versions and summarize results for review, but may not alter evaluation, suppress limitations, or approve publication. Recollect after a data, model, prompt, evaluation, interface, or disclosure change.

### 3. Feedback, response, and governance-review record

- **Request and owner:** Request feedback/appeal channels, issue and incident log, escalation and correction records, exception decisions, leadership-review materials, and source-change log from operations, risk, legal, and leadership owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected feedback item or issue to recorded facts, accountable triage, a human remedy or escalation decision, and closure evidence. This supports reviewability; it cannot decide liability, individual outcomes, or adequacy of redress.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a minimized workpaper and identify aging actions. Humans approve remediation, external communications, risk acceptance, and closure. Revisit annually and after a material issue, incident, regulatory development, or unresolved exception.
