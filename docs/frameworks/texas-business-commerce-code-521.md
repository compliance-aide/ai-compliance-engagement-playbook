# Texas Business & Commerce Code Chapter 521 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not statutory text, legal advice, an incident-notification determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current requirements through the [Texas Business & Commerce Code, Chapter 521](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/BC/htm/BC.521.htm).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a defensible record of covered personal-information handling that joins data inventory and ownership, supplier and service-provider relationships, safeguards evidence, security events, legal and regulatory decision records, remediation, and independently reviewable engagement evidence. Keep legal interpretation and incident-response decisions separate from AI-assisted record organization.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business leaders, privacy counsel, security leadership, and incident-response authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve operating evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify incomplete evidence, compare current facts against an approved engagement plan, and draft questions, but cannot decide legal applicability, breach or notification obligations, risk acceptance, supplier approval, remediation completion, production changes, or compliance status. Review annually and before a material personal-information, system, service-provider, incident, legal, or regulatory change.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the live [Texas Business & Commerce Code, Chapter 521](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/BC/htm/BC.521.htm), checked 2026-07-31, with qualified Texas counsel. This original plan does not reproduce statutory text or decide coverage, personal-information classification, incident status, notification/reporter obligations, timing, recipients, or compliance.

### 1. Personal-information inventory, disposal, and safeguard package

- **Request and owner:** Data inventory and ownership records; collection/receipt and disposal lifecycle evidence; systems, repositories, and access-role records; safeguard configuration references; security-test/remediation references; service-provider list; and policy/review history from privacy, security, technology, records, and procurement owners. State the systems, business units, providers, and period sampled.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one selected data path from receipt/collection through storage, access, use, provider transfer where applicable, and disposal record; inspect artifact integrity and access restriction. This supports a factual lifecycle and safeguard record; it cannot determine statutory data classification, prove disposal for all records, or establish reasonable security as a legal conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories and flag missing owners, retention fields, or evidence links. Human legal, privacy, security, and records owners decide applicability, classification, safeguards, and exceptions. Refresh for a material data, system, retention, provider, or control change.

### 2. Incident investigation and notification-decision package

- **Request and owner:** Incident intake and triage records; investigation timeline; preserved log/forensic references; scope and fact-source register; counsel-directed legal analysis record; contact and communications governance; decision approvals; and de-identified exercise evidence from incident response, security, legal, communications, and executive owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a de-identified exercise or closed incident from intake through evidence preservation, scope updates, human decision points, communications approval, and recovery handoff. This supports readiness and traceability; it cannot decide whether an event is covered, determine a deadline, select recipients, or validate a notice.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may sequence authorized event metadata and surface incomplete timelines; it cannot make breach or notice decisions, send communications, submit reports, or modify source evidence. Counsel and authorized officials retain all legal and external-communication decisions. Trigger on an event, exercise finding, material logging change, or contact-path update.

### 3. Supplier, remediation, and management-assurance package

- **Request and owner:** Supplier due-diligence and change records; approved contract/instruction references; security assurance and exception records; corrective-action register; recovery/retest results; management review; and source-change monitoring from procurement, security, privacy, legal, and business owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one provider change, corrective action, or exception to the request, human authority, evidence source, stated limitation, corrective action, retest, and closure decision. This supports accountable oversight; it cannot establish supplier performance, accept risk, prove complete remediation, or make a compliance determination.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved workpapers and flag stale reviews. Human owners approve supplier decisions, risk acceptance, remediation closure, and any external statement. Refresh after a supplier change, significant vulnerability, incident, remediation, source update, or annual review.
