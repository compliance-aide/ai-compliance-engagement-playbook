# ISO 28000:2022 — supply-chain security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/79612.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain facilities, partners, routes, logistics technology, dependencies, threats, disruptions, onboarding, exceptions, corrective actions, and leadership-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain partner evidence; independent reviewers validate scope and provenance. AI detects stale attestations and drafts questions, but cannot accept risk or certify. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
