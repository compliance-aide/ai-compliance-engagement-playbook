# UK employment data protection — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ICO guidance or employment-law advice. Check the [ICO employment guidance hub](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain lifecycle processing, guidance-watch, hiring, retention, health, monitoring, biometric, home-working, sharing, request, automated-screening, and accommodation evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide legal and employment-relations issues; independent reviewers sample notices and requests. AI drafts workpacks, but cannot make interpretations or decisions. Review quarterly and annually.
