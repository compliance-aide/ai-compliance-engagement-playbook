# IEC 62443-3-2:2020 IACS system-design risk assessment engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, an industrial safety decision, a security-level determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC 62443-3-2:2020 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/30727).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an industrial-system design risk record that connects the system under consideration, accountable operations and engineering owners, safety and process constraints, zones and conduits, dependencies, authorized evidence, risk decisions, design requirements, remediation, and independent review. Preserve the separation between AI-assisted record organization and accountable human decisions about safety, operational disruption, system design, security levels, and acceptable risk.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, maintenance, security, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; system and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and safety-aware readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify missing evidence, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot perform intrusive discovery, change zones or conduits, set security levels, decide a safety or design outcome, accept risk, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material system, process, zone, conduit, supplier, safety, incident, or design changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [IEC 62443-3-2:2020 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/30727) and an authorized copy; checked 2026-07-31. IEC text is licensed. This original plan does not reproduce requirements, change zones or conduits, determine security levels, or make a safety, design, risk, or compliance conclusion.

### 1. System-context and approved-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Human-approved system purpose/boundary, asset and interface inventory, operations and engineering owners, process/safety constraints, supplier dependencies, and approved zone/conduit representation.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected asset or interface to its owner, represented boundary, dependency, and stated constraint using authorized records only. This supports a scoped evidence population; it cannot validate design completeness or establish a security level.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied metadata and flag missing owners. Humans approve all boundaries. Revisit before material asset, process, interface, or supplier change.

### 2. Human risk-design input package

- **Request and owner:** Authorized risk inputs, threat/concern records, assumptions, safety/operations review inputs, human-approved security-level decision references, and documented design constraints.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected design input to its source, accountable authority, date, boundary, and stated limitation. This supports decision traceability; it cannot perform a risk assessment, set a security level, or decide a design outcome.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized inputs and surface unlinked assumptions; it cannot infer decisions. Reopen after an incident, material vulnerability, or design change.

### 3. Exception, change, and independent-challenge package

- **Request and owner:** Change/exception records, compensating-measure documentation, safety/risk approvals, remediation ownership, retest/review criteria, and independent-review workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception to authority, duration, operational constraint, evidence link, and follow-up. This supports governed follow-through; it cannot accept risk or replace independent engineering review.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare read-only workpapers and flag expired exceptions; it cannot initiate scanning or configuration changes. Review annually and before material zone, conduit, safety, or system change.
