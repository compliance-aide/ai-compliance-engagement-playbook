# SSA electronic-information-exchange security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not SSA requirement text, an agreement interpretation, a security certification, or a compliance claim. Confirm current obligations through the [SSA data-exchange security page](https://www.ssa.gov/dataexchange/security.html), the executed agreement, and authorized SSA contacts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a controlled exchange record connecting the executed agreement, authorized data exchange purpose, participating organizations, accountable contacts, approved systems and interfaces, access boundaries, evidence custodians, incident and loss procedures, review dates, exceptions, and renewal or termination actions. Treat agreement-specific requirements and SSA-provided detailed materials as restricted source material; do not reproduce them in public workpapers or external AI systems.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Agreement owners, data-exchange program leads, security, privacy, legal, operations, system, records, and incident-response owners retain accountable decisions. Authorized contacts manage agreement communications; system owners preserve authorized operating evidence; legal and privacy owners determine handling boundaries; and incident authorities decide escalation and reporting under the agreement. Independent reviewers sample the path from approved exchange purpose through system and access evidence, incident readiness, exception handling, periodic review, and agreement lifecycle actions, escalating any conflict or uncertainty to authorized SSA contacts.

AI may organize authorized metadata, identify missing ownership or review dates, prepare non-authoritative evidence indexes, and draft questions for human review. AI cannot access SSA data, interpret agreement terms, determine eligibility, transmit or receive exchange data, modify exchange systems, make a security certification, report an incident, accept risk, or claim compliance. Use only sanitized, permitted metadata in AI tools. Reassess before material agreement, participant, system, interface, access, incident, or data changes; perform scheduled reviews and an annual independent readiness review.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [SSA data-exchange security page](https://www.ssa.gov/dataexchange/security.html), the executed agreement, and authorized SSA contacts; source status: current public information page, retrieved 2026-07-31. Agreement-specific requirements may be restricted. This original plan does not interpret an agreement or authorize an exchange.

### 1. Agreement, participant, purpose, and interface boundary

- **Request and owner:** Request the executed-agreement reference, authorized exchange purpose, participating-organization and contact register, approved data/interface inventory, system boundary, and agreement lifecycle calendar from agreement, legal, privacy, and system owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected interface or exchange purpose to its approved agreement reference, accountable owner, system record, and current authorization period. This supports a bounded evidence trail; it cannot interpret terms, determine eligibility, or authorize data transfer.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize sanitized metadata only and flag gaps. Authorized contacts and agreement owners decide scope, participant changes, and agreement actions. Refresh before a participant, purpose, interface, system, or agreement change.

### 2. Access, safeguarding, and operating-evidence record

- **Request and owner:** Request authorized-access metadata, account-review evidence, interface/change records, security-training records, approved handling procedures, incident-contact roster, and supplier responsibilities from security, operations, training, and procurement owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Select an approved access or interface change and trace it to the owner, dated authorization record, operating evidence, exception, and corrective action. This confirms a documented process record; it cannot prove all access was proper or security was effective.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index permitted evidence and surface overdue reviews, but may not receive SSA data, administer access, or modify an interface. Recollect after a personnel, access, supplier, system, or handling-process change.

### 3. Incident, renewal, and readiness-review record

- **Request and owner:** Request sanitized incident metadata, authorized escalation/reporting decisions, periodic review records, corrective-action log, renewal/termination decisions, and source-change watch from incident, legal, agreement, and leadership owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected incident, exception, or lifecycle action to contemporaneous facts, authorized human decision, follow-up, and closure or renewal record. This supports accountability; it cannot report an event, make an SSA representation, or accept risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble a restricted-access evidence index and flag aging action dates. Humans decide reporting, remediation, renewal, termination, and closure. Revisit annually and after an event, agreement amendment, audit request, or unresolved exception.
