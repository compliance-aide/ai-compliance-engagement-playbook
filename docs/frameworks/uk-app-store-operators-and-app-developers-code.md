# UK Code of Practice for App Store Operators and App Developers engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not UK government text, a security determination, a legal interpretation, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [UK Code of Practice for App Store Operators and App Developers](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/code-of-practice-for-app-store-operators-and-app-developers) and applicable authority.

## Engagement focus

Set the engagement boundary around the organisation's role in the app ecosystem: operator, app developer, platform developer, or more than one of those roles. Maintain a register of stores, apps, developer accounts, distribution channels, versions, permissions, support status, security and privacy decision points, and the customer or regulator communications that apply. Separate voluntary code readiness from binding privacy, consumer, sector, contract, and incident-notification obligations; record the owner and governing source for each separate duty.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Name accountable leaders for marketplace policy, application security, privacy, platform operations, vulnerability handling, releases, user communications, and legal escalation. Operators and platform teams preserve records of submission checks, policy exceptions, removals, appeals, developer notices, and store-level vulnerability handling. Developers preserve their application security design, dependency review, test, update, disclosure, and incident records. Review the register and material security decisions each quarter; exercise the removal, emergency-update, and vulnerable-app communication paths at least annually. Before renewal, an independent reviewer samples applications, store decisions, and end-to-end records to assess whether the stated process operated; auditors test the record trail without becoming the decision maker.

AI may organize supplied evidence, reconcile app and release inventories, identify gaps in documented ownership or records, and prepare review workpapers for human review. AI cannot determine that an app is safe, approve publication or removal, decide legal notification duties, make a compliance or certification conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [UK Code of Practice for App Store Operators and App Developers record](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/code-of-practice-for-app-store-operators-and-app-developers); checked 2026-07-31. This plan is original operational guidance, not government text, store-policy interpretation, or a compliance claim. Keep unpublished builds, signing material, vulnerability reports, user data, and confidential store records in approved protected repositories; use authorized links, hashes, or redactions.

### 1. App, store, and distribution-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Approved app/service inventory, store/operator roles, distribution channels, platform/SDK dependencies, signing/release ownership, supported-version records, data-flow context, and security-risk decisions from product, mobile engineering, platform, security, and legal owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one app and release channel through accountable roles, dependencies, distribution boundary, supported version, and risk-review date. This cannot prove all apps are in scope or decide legal applicability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized inventories and identify unowned versions or dependencies. Humans decide store participation, release scope, data use, and risk treatment. Refresh after a new app, store, SDK, signing method, data flow, or material platform change.

### 2. Secure build, publication, and vulnerability package

- **Request and owner:** Authorized development/security reviews, build and release approvals, code-signing custody, testing outputs, publication/takedown records, vulnerability/incident tickets, and remediation/retest evidence from engineering, release, security, and operations owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Follow one release, vulnerability, or removal event through decision, authorized action, corrective work, and retest. This cannot prove malware absence or continuous review quality.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved artifacts and flag missing approvers, timing, or retests. Humans approve publication, validate findings, decide escalation, and revoke signing authority. Recollect after a material defect, compromise, vulnerability, or rollback.

### 3. Store oversight and assurance package

- **Request and owner:** Review/moderation records where applicable, user-report/abuse handling, supplier/SDK notices, monitoring summaries, exceptions, management reporting, and independent-review observations from store, product, security, privacy, and audit owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one security or user-safety signal to classification, accountable review, decision, remediation/escalation, and independent challenge. This cannot replace operator judgment or accept residual risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may cluster authorized reports and draft questions without enforcement decisions. Humans decide moderation, notices, disclosures, exceptions, and external claims. Review routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness challenge.
