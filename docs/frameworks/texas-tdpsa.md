# Texas Data Privacy and Security Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Confirm the live [Texas Attorney General overview](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-privacy-rights/texas-data-privacy-and-security-act).

## Engagement focus

Refresh personal/sensitive-data and processor maps; validate notices, consent, rights, appeals, preferences, security, and high-risk-processing evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal owners decide scope; independent reviewers sample requests and disclosed flows. AI clusters evidence and tracks remediation, but cannot decide exemptions, cure posture, respond to consumers, or execute external changes. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Texas Attorney General TDPSA overview](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-privacy-rights/texas-data-privacy-and-security-act) and qualified Texas privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide applicability, an exemption, sensitive-data consent, a consumer response, cure posture, or an enforcement outcome.

### 1. Data, purpose, notice, and processor package

- **Request and owner:** Personal/sensitive-data inventory, collection/purpose/retention records, notice versions, system map, processor/subprocessor register, and material-change approvals from privacy, product, engineering, and procurement owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected data flow to collection channel, purpose, owner, system, notice version, and processor entry. This supports an auditable fact base; it cannot determine whether an entity or activity is covered or whether processing is lawful.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare authorized inventory and vendor metadata and flag unowned changes. Legal/privacy humans approve scope and classifications. Refresh for a new collection channel, sensitive-data use, processor, or material product change.

### 2. Consent, preference, request, and appeal package

- **Request and owner:** Consent/permission and preference configuration evidence where used, request and appeal procedures/logs, identity-verification controls, response review samples, and staff-training/remediation records from privacy, support, and engineering owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted sample from intake to identity step, system search, decision authority, response, and appeal record. This can show that an approved workflow produces records; it cannot authenticate an individual, decide an exception, or establish a legally sufficient response.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare de-identified sample lists and flag missed internal service targets. Humans authenticate, decide responses/appeals, and communicate externally. Recheck after a complaint pattern, channel change, or workflow failure.

### 3. Risk, security, remediation, and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Human-approved risk/assessment inputs for relevant processing, security-operation and test references, processor oversight records, exceptions, cure/remediation decisions, retest results, management review, and source-change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected risk, exception, or remediation item to authority, evidence, limitation, expiry, action owner, and retest. This supports accountable governance; it cannot determine cure posture, accept risk, or make a compliance conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index evidence and flag stale actions. Authorized humans approve risk, cure decisions, external responses, and closure. Review quarterly and renew scope annually.
