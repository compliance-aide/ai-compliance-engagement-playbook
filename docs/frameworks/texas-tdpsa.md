# Texas Data Privacy and Security Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Confirm the live [Texas Attorney General overview](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-privacy-rights/texas-data-privacy-and-security-act).

## Engagement focus

Refresh personal/sensitive-data and processor maps; validate notices, consent, rights, appeals, preferences, security, and high-risk-processing evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal owners decide scope; independent reviewers sample requests and disclosed flows. AI clusters evidence and tracks remediation, but cannot decide exemptions, cure posture, respond to consumers, or execute external changes. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
