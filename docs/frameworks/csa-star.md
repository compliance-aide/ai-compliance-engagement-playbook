# CSA STAR — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CSA content or an assurance statement. Check the [CSA STAR program](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star).

## Engagement focus

Confirm service scope and target assurance route; maintain service inventory, system description, evidence, customer statement, change, and remediation records.

## Roles

Operators maintain evidence; independent auditors/assessors validate eligible paths. AI reconciles evidence and gaps, but cannot answer questionnaires, represent providers, attest, submit, or publish. Review quarterly and annually.

## Source and applicability

Use the [current STAR program](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/) and [submission guidance](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/submit). Distinguish cloud Level 1 self-assessment from Level 2 independent certification or attestation. The [CSA Level 2 explanation](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/blog/2024/03/23/csa-star-level-2-all-about-star-attestations-and-certifications) associates STAR Certification with ISO/IEC 27001 and STAR Attestation with SOC 2. Existing ISO or SOC evidence alone does not establish a STAR designation.

STAR for AI is a separate route using AI-specific materials; do not substitute an AI-CAIQ for a cloud questionnaire or transfer a designation between services. Verify current scheme requirements, assessor eligibility, forms, fees, prerequisites, coverage periods and renewal rules before booking or submission. Record authoritative versions and unresolved requirements rather than relying on an old scheme diagram.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Name the service owner, evidence owners, route decision-maker, independent assessor where applicable and authorized signatory. Define the exact legal entity, service, region, assessment period and intended public claim. Store confidential evidence separately from the public submission package. AI may organize evidence and draft questions for owners; final questionnaire answers and representations remain accountable human decisions.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and accountable owner | Output and exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Select the route | Management reviews intended customer assurance and current scheme conditions. | Approved cloud/AI, level and certification/attestation choice with prerequisites and budget decisions. |
| 2. Fix the service boundary | Service owner reconciles system description, deployments, dependencies and customer responsibilities. | Approved scope consistent across evidence, questionnaire and proposed public record. |
| 3. Establish requirements | Engagement lead retrieves the correct questionnaire and assessment materials. | Complete requirement register with version, evidence owner and assessor needs; no omitted questions hidden by summary. |
| 4. Collect and challenge evidence | Owners provide source-linked operational records; reviewers test the relevant assertions. | Dated workpapers with service/period match, contradictions and unresolved gaps visible. |
| 5. Resolve answers and findings | Human answer owners reconcile each response with evidence; remediation owners correct approved gaps. | Approved responses or explicit unresolved items, plus retest evidence; AI does not select favorable answers. |
| 6. Complete route-specific review | Eligible independent parties perform the required assessment where applicable. | Actual issued report/certificate and scope, or documented remaining assessment work; booking is not assurance. |
| 7. Prepare publication | Authorized owner reviews exact files, confidentiality, claims, fees and submission authority. | Reviewable final package with approval record; distinguish prepared, submitted, accepted and publicly listed states. |
| 8. Verify and maintain | After authorized submission, owner checks the registry entry and ongoing scheme obligations. | Matching entity/service/level, artifact version and status; renewal owners and dates recorded. |

## Failure branches and decisions

- **Marketing calls Level 1 a certification:** return the claim for correction to match the verified designation and service scope.
- **Certificate covers another entity or service:** record the scope mismatch; do not reuse its status for the requested listing.
- **Provider answers conflict with tests:** retain both records and require owner/reviewer resolution before approving the answer package.
- **Submission receipt exists but listing does not:** record submitted only; track the actual acceptance or correction request without claiming publication.
- **Assessor or prerequisite eligibility unknown:** prepare evidence while resolving eligibility before committing money or representing assessment readiness.
- **Confidential evidence appears in a public attachment:** prepare a permitted public version without hiding assessment gaps; retain complete protected evidence for authorized reviewers.

## Evidence and test plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [CSA STAR program](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star) and the organization’s applicable STAR route materials; prior snapshot 2026-07-31; verify current route requirements. Program materials, questionnaires, and assessment routes have their own conditions. This plan is original evidence-planning guidance, not CSA content, a completed questionnaire, a STAR submission, or an assurance statement.

### 1. Cloud-service scope, customer commitment, and responsibility record

- **Request and owner:** Service/system description, deployed-service and data-boundary inventory, customer commitment and shared-responsibility references, relevant provider/subprocessor inventory, and named service/security owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected customer-facing service to its boundary, dependencies, customer commitment, and accountable owners. This can identify gaps in the declared service story; it cannot determine STAR eligibility, prove all tenant/provider responsibilities, or make a customer assurance claim.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized inventories and flag mismatch between service descriptions and ownership records. Humans approve scope and public/customer representations. Refresh for material service, data, hosting, subprocessor, or commitment change.

### 2. Cloud-control operation and evidence-provenance package

- **Request and owner:** Time-bounded policy/procedure references, access/change/security-operation records, monitoring or incident summaries, technical evidence pointers, and evidence handling/provenance metadata from service, security, and platform owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a selected operational assertion to source, period, system scope, responsible owner, and documented exception. This can support a reviewable evidence package; it cannot establish continuous effectiveness, complete control coverage, or a successful assessment result.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a non-destructive index and identify missing time/scope fields; it may not alter cloud resources or choose evidence to obtain a desired result. Humans approve evidence sufficiency and remediation. Refresh quarterly and after material operational, incident, or architecture changes.

### 3. STAR route, assessor, and remediation governance workpaper

- **Request and owner:** Human-selected STAR route inputs, assessor/auditor engagement records where applicable, questionnaire or assessment workpapers retained under their terms, findings, remediation/retest evidence, and management/exception decisions from the engagement lead and executive owner.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected finding or exception to authority, evidence, remediation owner, due date, retest, and external-submission boundary. This can support readiness governance; it cannot select a route, answer a questionnaire, attest, submit, or publish on behalf of the provider.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft traceability and escalate unresolved gaps. Humans approve assessor engagement, risk treatment, submission, and external statements. Review before an assessment, at least annually, and after program or provider changes.


## Cadence and renewal

Quarterly and annual internal reviews are engagement conventions. Track the actual selected scheme's renewal, surveillance and artifact-refresh requirements separately. Reopen scope and claims after service, ownership, architecture, assurance-period or program changes. An old listing is not evidence that a changed service remains covered.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver route/source decisions, full scope and requirements, evidence workpapers, approved answers, findings/retests and actual assessment/submission/registry records where authorized. Identify the next owner, action and required evidence without prior chat. State exactly which stage is complete; readiness does not mean independent assurance, submission or acceptance.
