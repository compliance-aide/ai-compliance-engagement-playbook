# CSA STAR — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CSA content or an assurance statement. Check the [CSA STAR program](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star).

## Engagement focus

Confirm service scope and target assurance route; maintain service inventory, system description, evidence, customer statement, change, and remediation records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain evidence; independent auditors/assessors validate eligible paths. AI reconciles evidence and gaps, but cannot answer questionnaires, represent providers, attest, submit, or publish. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [CSA STAR program](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star) and the organization’s applicable STAR route materials; checked 2026-07-31. Program materials, questionnaires, and assessment routes have their own conditions. This plan is original evidence-planning guidance, not CSA content, a completed questionnaire, a STAR submission, or an assurance statement.

### 1. Cloud-service scope, customer commitment, and responsibility record

- **Request and owner:** Service/system description, deployed-service and data-boundary inventory, customer commitment and shared-responsibility references, relevant provider/subprocessor inventory, and named service/security owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected customer-facing service to its boundary, dependencies, customer commitment, and accountable owners. This can identify gaps in the declared service story; it cannot determine STAR eligibility, prove all tenant/provider responsibilities, or make a customer assurance claim.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized inventories and flag mismatch between service descriptions and ownership records. Humans approve scope and public/customer representations. Refresh for material service, data, hosting, subprocessor, or commitment change.

### 2. Cloud-control operation and evidence-provenance package

- **Request and owner:** Time-bounded policy/procedure references, access/change/security-operation records, monitoring or incident summaries, technical evidence pointers, and evidence handling/provenance metadata from service, security, and platform owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a selected operational assertion to source, period, system scope, responsible owner, and documented exception. This can support a reviewable evidence package; it cannot establish continuous effectiveness, complete control coverage, or a successful assessment result.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a non-destructive index and identify missing time/scope fields; it may not alter cloud resources or choose evidence to obtain a desired result. Humans approve evidence sufficiency and remediation. Refresh quarterly and after material operational, incident, or architecture changes.

### 3. STAR route, assessor, and remediation governance workpaper

- **Request and owner:** Human-selected STAR route inputs, assessor/auditor engagement records where applicable, questionnaire or assessment workpapers retained under their terms, findings, remediation/retest evidence, and management/exception decisions from the engagement lead and executive owner.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected finding or exception to authority, evidence, remediation owner, due date, retest, and external-submission boundary. This can support readiness governance; it cannot select a route, answer a questionnaire, attest, submit, or publish on behalf of the provider.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft traceability and escalate unresolved gaps. Humans approve assessor engagement, risk treatment, submission, and external statements. Review before an assessment, at least annually, and after program or provider changes.
