# ISO/IEC 27000:2026 ISMS overview engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a framework interpretation, a certification conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27000:2026 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/27000).

## Engagement focus

Use the overview standard to establish an explicit engagement vocabulary and relationship map before planning detailed framework work. Maintain a record that connects the selected management-system scope, applicable source editions, related framework guides, accountable owners, stated objectives, evidence locations, interfaces with other programs, assumptions, exclusions, and change watch. An overview document helps orient work; it does not determine applicability or replace the source material governing a specific engagement.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive sponsors, security leadership, risk, compliance, internal audit, technology, privacy, legal, and service owners retain accountable decisions. The engagement owner maintains the source and scope map; framework owners preserve their operational evidence; management approves objectives and resourcing; and independent reviewers challenge source currency, boundary claims, unowned interfaces, and unsupported assertions. Independent review must remain separate from management’s implementation and assurance claims.

AI may organize authorized source metadata, identify mismatched editions or unowned interfaces, draft questions, prepare an engagement map, and assemble non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot determine applicability, select a certification scope, interpret requirements, approve a management system, make an assurance conclusion, accept risk, or claim compliance. Reassess the map after material organizational, source, service, technology, supplier, incident, or regulatory changes; conduct quarterly stewardship reviews and an annual independent readiness review.
