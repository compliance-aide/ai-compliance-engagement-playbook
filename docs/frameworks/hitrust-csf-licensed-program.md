# HITRUST CSF — licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance. This draft does not reproduce HITRUST requirements or provide an assessment, score, or certification.

## Source and applicability

The [HITRUST assessment portfolio](https://hitrustalliance.net/assessments-and-certifications) distinguishes e1, i1 and r2, with different assurance scope; it also describes separate AI products and authorized external assessors. Reusing prior work or provider controls does not establish certification of a new scope. Select the actual required product with the engagement owner and assessor; do not infer it from organization size alone.

Source check: 2026-09-04, public portfolio page. Before assessment execution, the licensee must verify the current Assessment Handbook, advisories, applicable CSF version, assessment options, platform terms and authorized assessor requirements. Full current program rules and license permissions were not verified for this draft. The [directory terms](https://directory.hitrustalliance.net/terms-of-service) are not proof of permission to process licensed framework content with an AI service.

## Engagement focus

Produce a traceable readiness and handoff record for one named entity, assessment product, system boundary and evidence period. Keep organization-owned operational evidence separate from licensed requirements and official assessment outputs. Readiness findings support remediation; they do not determine official scores or certification.

## Roles

The engagement owner approves objectives, resources and scope. The licensee controls permitted content, accounts and workspace access. System and evidence owners explain implementation and preserve records. The authorized external assessor performs the applicable assessment role; HITRUST's program process controls issuance. An independent reviewer challenges evidence coverage and unsupported claims. A named human approves external submissions, binding representations and publication.

AI may organize permitted records, identify missing evidence and draft questions. It cannot ingest unlicensed material, invent proprietary mappings or official scores, impersonate an assessor, certify, accept risk, or approve its own work. Verify both license permission and customer-data authorization before processing any restricted input.

## Before starting

Create an engagement record containing entity, sponsor, business requirement, selected assessment type, intended use, system/service boundary, locations, providers, assessment period, version, assessor, license evidence and approval owners. Record unknowns explicitly. Obtain authorized current program instructions before deciding evidence eligibility, assessment timing or scoring treatment.

Use only the approved workspace. Define allowed data, recipients, retention and export routes. A subscription receipt alone does not authorize every model, integration or redistribution. If permission is unresolved, continue with nonrestricted planning and stop dependent content processing.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Confirm the route.** Compare the buyer's actual requirement with the selected product and scope. Record approval and any discrepancy. An e1 readiness project cannot silently become an r2 certification claim.
2. **Establish the boundary.** Inventory every included system, location, data flow, operator and dependency. Record exclusions and their approved rationale. Reconcile diagrams, service lists and assessment workspace settings; preserve changes rather than overwriting the original boundary.
3. **Build the permitted evidence plan.** Within the licensed environment, associate each applicable assessment item with an accountable owner, evidence period, source and approved method. Keep protected text out of public records. Record the entire scoped population and identify unavailable evidence; do not silently truncate, sample or cap material supplied for assessment scoring.
4. **Check implementation and gaps.** Inspect permitted organization-owned evidence against the approved test objective. Distinguish a written policy from operation, a provider capability from customer configuration, and an old result from current evidence. Record observed failures, uncertainty and untested work separately. Do not translate internal statuses into official HITRUST scores.
5. **Remediate and retest.** Assign each gap an owner, action, deadline and evidence of closure. Obtain change authorization before implementation. Retest the failed behavior and affected dependencies; a closed ticket without demonstrated correction remains unresolved. Have the assessor resolve program eligibility and evidence-period questions.
6. **Prepare the assessor handoff.** Reconcile every scoped item and evidence link, including failures and missing records. Verify recipient permissions. Preserve submitted artifact version, authorization, platform receipt and subsequent requests separately. Upload success is not assessor acceptance, successful quality review or certification.
7. **Verify the outcome and maintain scope.** Read the actual issued artifact through an authorized path. Record entity, covered services, assessment product, dates, conditions and exclusions. Approve any external wording against that artifact. Track changes, ongoing obligations and renewal milestones using current program instructions; reopen affected evidence when the boundary or implementation changes.

## Evidence and test plan

### 1. Scope and entitlement package

Keep the approved boundary, complete inventory, product selection, version, licenses, approved processing routes and assessor assignment. Test whether every proposed evidence source belongs to the authorized entity and scope. An inaccessible or unlicensed source is a blocker to that processing, not evidence of control failure or success.

### 2. Implementation and inheritance package

Keep organization-owned configurations, operational records, provider responsibility statements, evidence provenance and test results in the authorized repository. For each claimed inherited function, identify provider coverage, applicable service and period, customer responsibilities and approved program treatment. Test the customer configuration and remaining responsibilities. Provider certification does not automatically certify its customers.

Record population totals, examined coverage, authorized testing limits and remaining work. Complete accounting does not mean every technical combination was tested. Preserve all relevant source evidence and adverse results; any method limitation must remain visible to the assessor.

### 3. Outcome and continuity package

Keep handoff manifest, unresolved findings, authorized submissions, receipt history, issued artifacts, approved claim wording and renewal/change calendar. Compare every customer-facing scope claim with the actual artifact. Review new services, acquisitions, changed providers, incidents and expired evidence for assessment impact with the responsible authority.

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) statuses for internal assertions: supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable and not_tested. Split compound claims. A verified failure stays visible even if another part is uncertain.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Missing current program rules: assign retrieval to the licensee; stop dependent eligibility or assessment conclusions.
- Restricted material arrives in an unapproved workspace: stop further processing, restrict access and follow the authorized handling procedure; never copy it into this repository.
- Boundary or product changes: obtain a documented decision and reconcile all affected evidence before reusing prior work.
- Evidence conflicts: retain both records and ask the owner/assessor to resolve the discrepancy; do not choose the favorable result.
- Platform errors or missing receipt: preserve the error, check actual submission state and avoid duplicate submission until resolved.
- No issued artifact: describe the actual readiness or assessment stage. Do not display a certification claim.

## Cadence and renewal

Set operational evidence checks according to risk and the approved engagement plan. The public portfolio describes one-year e1/i1 and two-year r2 validity; confirm actual dates, interim requirements, advisories and renewal obligations from the selected program and issued artifact. Never treat a two-year term as permission to ignore changes or ongoing obligations.

## Completion and handoff

Readiness handoff requires reconciled scope, permitted evidence, explicit unresolved work, named owners and reviewer findings. Certification status requires the authoritative outcome, not this checklist. Record next action, owner, due date and source for every unresolved item; label publication review and source/rights gaps separately.

Fictional author desk case: a provider holds an issued certification, but a customer's newly added service has no approved inheritance treatment or customer-side tests. The provider artifact supports only its stated scope. Customer readiness remains incomplete, and a customer certification claim is unsupported. This is not an independent review or a real assessment.
