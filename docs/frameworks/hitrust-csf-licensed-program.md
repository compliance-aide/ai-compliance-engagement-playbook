# HITRUST CSF — licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for licensed users only, not HITRUST content, an assessment, or certification. Confirm current terms at the [HITRUST directory terms](https://directory.hitrustalliance.net/terms-of-service).

## Engagement focus

Verify entitlement, scope, and version; use only the licensee-approved workspace; assign evidence custodians; operate evidence, change, and exception cycles; and coordinate authorized readiness and assessor activity.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Licensee and authorized assessor roles control framework content and results. Independent reviewers examine organization-owned process evidence. AI cannot receive unlicensed content, generate mappings or scores, certify, act as an assessor, or approve a licensee decision. Preserve version and decision records through annual planning.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
