# NIST SP 800-30 Revision 1 — risk-assessment engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST content or a risk-acceptance decision. Confirm status at the [NIST publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain assessment purpose, scope, assets, assumptions, threat and weakness inputs, impact rationale, uncertainty, treatment decisions, residual-risk approvals, and update triggers.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Risk owners validate context and accountable leaders accept or decline risk; independent reviewers test the decision trail. AI connects evidence and surfaces stale assumptions, but cannot rate risk or accept it. Refresh on material change and review annually.
