# Singapore Cybersecurity Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a designation or reporting decision. Check the [CSA legislation hub](https://www.csa.gov.sg/legislation/cybersecurity-act/).

## Engagement focus

Determine regulated-system status, accountable contacts, security-operations, testing, supplier, material-change, and incident-escalation evidence; reconcile actual systems to any designations.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain controls and records; independent reviewers sample evidence and reporting readiness. AI can identify timing gaps, but cannot interpret designations, decide reportability, notify CSA, or submit responses. Review quarterly and scope annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
