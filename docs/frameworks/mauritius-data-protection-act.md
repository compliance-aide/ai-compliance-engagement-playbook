# Mauritius Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Confirm current rules with the [Mauritius Data Protection Office](https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Pages/The%20Law/Data-Protection-Act-2017.aspx).

## Engagement focus

Refresh data, service, processor, access, incident, registration-lifecycle, automated-processing, transfer, and exception evidence; exercise requests and incidents.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain records and drills; independent reviewers sample system and vendor proof. AI drafts evidence requests and trends exceptions, but cannot interpret law, register, accept risk, or submit externally. Review quarterly and annually.
