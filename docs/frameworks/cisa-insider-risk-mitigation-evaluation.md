# CISA Insider Risk Mitigation Program Evaluation engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CISA assessment content, a personnel action, a threat determination, a legal interpretation, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [CISA Insider Risk Mitigation Program Evaluation](https://www.cisa.gov/insider-risk-self-assessment-tool) and the organization’s approved legal, privacy, human-resources, security, and ethics direction.

## Engagement focus

Operate a privacy-aware insider-risk readiness program that connects governance, workforce processes, data handling, reporting channels, security operations, case-management boundaries, training, and improvement work. Use a maturity evaluation as a structured self-assessment rather than as a score that identifies a person, proves misconduct, or authorizes monitoring, investigation, discipline, or disclosure.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, insider-risk program, security, legal, privacy, human-resources, ethics, physical security, records-management, communications, and service-owner roles. Operators maintain program scope, approved data-use and access records, training evidence, reporting and escalation records, self-assessment artifacts, exercise results, privacy and legal approvals, corrective actions, and closure status. Review governance, data handling, reporting pathways, and open improvements quarterly; conduct and document an approved readiness evaluation at least annually and after material workforce, legal, privacy, technology, or threat changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples governance and self-assessment evidence; auditors test the evidence trail without identifying individuals, conducting investigations, making personnel decisions, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied program evidence, flag missing ownership or stale review artifacts, summarize approved self-assessment results, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot profile people, access restricted workforce data, make a threat or misconduct finding, initiate an investigation, recommend discipline, determine legal obligations, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [CISA Insider Risk Mitigation Program Evaluation](https://www.cisa.gov/insider-risk-self-assessment-tool) and approved organizational legal, privacy, human-resources, security, and ethics direction at engagement start; checked 2026-07-31. This is original readiness guidance, not CISA assessment content, legal advice, a person-level assessment, or a personnel decision.

### 1. Governance, authority, and privacy-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Program leadership, legal, privacy, human-resources, ethics, security, and records owners provide charter/scope records, role assignments, approved data-use boundaries, retention/access decisions, escalation paths, policy review history, and documented limitations.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected program activity or data category to an approved purpose, accountable owner, access/retention boundary, legal/privacy review record, and current review date. This cannot authorize monitoring, determine legal sufficiency, or identify a person as a risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved records and flag missing owners, expired reviews, or unlinked boundaries. Authorized humans approve policy, data use, and escalation authority. Refresh after legal, privacy, workforce, technology, or organizational change.

### 2. Readiness, training, and escalation-process package

- **Request and owner:** Insider-risk, human-resources, communications, physical-security, security-operations, and service owners provide training/awareness records, reporting-channel tests, exercise artifacts, escalation procedures, access-controlled case-handling process evidence, and improvement tickets.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one training, exercise, or channel-readiness record through owner, approved procedure, completion/test evidence, limitation, and corrective follow-up. This does not test a person, initiate an investigation, or prove reporting channels will detect every concern.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize approved aggregate readiness evidence and flag stale exercises or unowned actions. Humans conduct exercises, handle reports, and decide any escalation. Refresh after a material process failure, exercise, workforce change, or reporting-channel change.

### 3. Self-assessment, independent challenge, and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Program leadership, privacy/legal, independent-review, and executive owners provide approved self-assessment records, scoring/maturity rationale, evidence references, reviewer challenges, management decisions, remediation plans, and closure/retest evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected self-assessment observation from cited program evidence through limitation, independent challenge, accountable human decision, and corrective-action status. This cannot validate a maturity score, make a threat determination, or attest for management.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize evidence references and flag unsupported rationale or overdue follow-up. Authorized humans approve self-assessment conclusions, risk decisions, and external representations. Refresh annually and after material program, legal, privacy, workforce, or threat changes.
