# EU Cyber Resilience Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not product classification or conformity advice. Verify transitions in [Regulation (EU) 2024/2847](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/2024-11-20/eng).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a product portfolio, economic-role and support-life register, secure-development, vulnerability, release, component, supplier, support, and customer-communication evidence by version.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product owners control lifecycle evidence; independent reviewers sample release and vulnerability history. AI connects product records and gaps, but cannot determine classification, sign conformity material, or submit notifications. Review releases continuously and the portfolio annually.
