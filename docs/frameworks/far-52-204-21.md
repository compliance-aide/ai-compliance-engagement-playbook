# FAR 52.204-21 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a contract interpretation or assertion.
> Use the clause incorporated in the award and the current [official text](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.204-21).

## Engagement focus

Obtain the executed award and amendments; identify where relevant contract
information enters, moves, and is retained; keep a living system and information
boundary; reconcile suppliers, access owners, open risks, and flowdown coverage
to the actual contract; and route incidents and notices through authorized legal
and contract channels.

## Roles and annual rhythm

The contractor owns safeguarding, records, corrective work, and approved
notifications. Independent reviewers challenge scope and evidence against the
incorporated clause. AI flags evidence gaps and change signals and drafts neutral
status questions; it cannot determine applicability, attest compliance, or send
notices. Review midyear, before renewal, and after material change.
