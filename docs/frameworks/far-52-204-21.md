# FAR 52.204-21 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a contract interpretation or assertion.
> Use the clause incorporated in the award and the current [official text](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.204-21).

## Source and applicability

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Source check, 2026-09-04: read
the complete [official clause](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.204-21), displayed
as November 2021 under FAC 2026-01. Paragraph (a) defines the information/system
boundary; (b)(1) contains fifteen safeguards; (b)(2) preserves other safeguarding
duties; (c) addresses flowdown, including commercial acquisitions with a COTS
exception. The exact award, amendments, agency deviations and any overhaul
implementation still require verification. A different online edition does not
by itself amend an executed contract.


## Engagement focus

Obtain the executed award and amendments; identify where relevant contract
information enters, moves, and is retained; keep a living system and information
boundary; reconcile suppliers, access owners, open risks, and flowdown coverage
to the actual contract; and route incidents and notices through authorized legal
and contract channels.

## Before starting

Record the review period, award identifiers, approved information/system boundary,
asset and supplier populations, evidence permissions and named decision owners.
Use [work items](../../templates/work-item.md) with an approved criterion for each
observable assertion. Evidence collection must not move FCI or customer records
into this public repository. Use synthetic records for demonstrations.

Distinguish reviewing existing evidence from executing a new test. Record the
QA-named environment, test authorization, expected result and stop condition
before a technical action. Do not probe customer or production systems under a
general instruction to improve this guide.

## Ordered workflow


1. Obtain the award, incorporated clause/version, modifications and relevant
   agency instructions. Have contracts/legal resolve their relationship before
   applying a generic checklist. Record missing instruments and their owners.
2. Map information supplied by or generated for the Government to actual workflows.
   Ask the authorized owner to resolve public-release and simple-transactional
   exclusions using the clause's definition. A file lacking a CUI marking is not
   automatically outside the FCI boundary; do not equate all business data with FCI.
3. Trace each in-scope flow through email, endpoints, shared storage, collaboration,
   backups, exports, subcontractor systems and disposal/reuse paths. Reconcile
   actual use against the declared boundary; a policy naming one approved folder
   does not prove that all copies remain there.
4. Build a complete clause-to-evidence matrix for the incorporated safeguards.
   Define one assertion per action/system, its criterion, owner, evidence location
   and authorized method. Keep technical behavior distinct from policy existence.
   Do not substitute a CMMC certificate or another framework score for this matrix.
5. Execute approved checks in QA-named workspaces with synthetic information.
   Separate identity verification from permitted functions, device coverage from
   agent installation, and backup existence from protected access. Retain failed,
   inaccessible and not-run checks; never trim evidence before scoring.
6. Reconcile subcontract scope, potential information handling, required flowdown
   and executed agreement evidence. Legal decides exclusions and required language.
   A purchasing label of “commercial” does not alone settle the clause's flowdown
   conditions. A template clause does not show it reached the actual subcontract.
7. Assign each observed gap an affected system/flow, owner and correction plan.
   Retest approved changes and preserve earlier failures. Identify other applicable
   contractual incident or CUI duties separately; do not invent a reporting deadline
   or certification obligation from this safeguarding clause alone.
8. Hand off the complete boundary, clause matrix, subcontract decisions, evidence,
   test results and unresolved questions. Have authorized humans approve final
   contract representations and external notices; preparation does not constitute
   attestation or acceptance by the Government.

## Failure branches and decisions

 missing award text leaves the applicable-version conclusion
`inconclusive`. A failed authorized access test is `not_supported` for that
assertion. An unreachable endpoint is `not_tested`, not compliant by omission.
A flowdown document can support inclusion of language but cannot alone demonstrate
that the subcontractor implements the safeguards.

**Fictional desk case:** a QA boundary names only a document repository, but an
approved trace shows a synthetic contract attachment synchronized to a laptop.
The repository-only boundary assertion is `not_supported`. If laptop access
controls cannot be tested, their operation is `not_tested`; correcting the diagram
does not fix or verify those controls. No real FCI, customer system or award was used.

## Roles

The contractor owns safeguarding, records, corrective work, and approved
notifications. Independent reviewers challenge scope and evidence against the
incorporated clause. AI flags evidence gaps and change signals and drafts neutral
status questions; it cannot determine applicability, attest compliance, or send
notices. Use the approved review schedule and change triggers described below.


## Evidence and test plan

Retain these three PR #340 packages with full in-scope coverage. Earlier review
of another revision does not approve this guide. Keep all scoped evidence and
observations available to the scorer without sampling or truncation.

### 1. Award, scope, and information-flow package

- **Request and owner:** Contracts, program, security, and information-governance owners provide the executed award and modifications, clause ledger, approved applicability record, relevant contract-information flow, system/service boundary, owner roster, and subcontractor list.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace each in-scope clause entry to the award record, approved scope decision, named owner, affected information flow, and current review date. This tests traceability only; it cannot interpret the clause, determine whether information is covered, or decide flow-down duties.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved records and flag unowned flows, stale modifications, or missing boundaries. Humans decide scope and applicability. Refresh after award, modification, system, information-flow, or supplier change.

### 2. Safeguarding-operation package

- **Request and owner:** System, endpoint, operations, identity, and security owners provide approved inventories, access-administration records, configuration/change evidence, maintenance or remediation records, and dated observations tied to the scoped environment.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace each in-scope operational assertion to a dated source artifact, accountable owner, bounded asset or process, observed limitation, corrective action, and follow-up date. This supports evidence readiness; it cannot attest implementation across the environment or conclude contractual compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize read-only evidence, detect missing dates or owners, and draft neutral follow-up questions. Humans validate artifacts and approve remediation closure. Refresh after failed observation, material technical change, or scheduled review.

### 3. Supplier, incident, and exception-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Supplier management, contracts, incident response, legal, and executive owners provide supplier assurance/flow-down records, escalation contacts, incident and notification decision trails, exceptions, approved risk decisions, and renewal-review records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace each in-scope supplier, incident scenario, or exception to its source, authority, timing, limitation, approval path, and follow-up. This can show governance preparedness; it cannot decide notification, accept contractual risk, or send a notice.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale contacts, unresolved exceptions, and approaching review dates. Authorized humans make notification, representation, and risk decisions; independent reviewers challenge the record. Refresh after an incident, supplier event, exception, or renewal trigger.

### Safeguard assertion design

The following are original test-design recommendations, not additional clause
requirements. The owner defines criteria and methods for the incorporated duties.

- Reconcile identities, devices and service accounts to the actual boundary.
  Test an approved allowed function and a prohibited function independently;
  successful login does not prove function restrictions.
- Trace external connections and public-facing components to their approved
  configuration and observed path. A diagram is supporting documentation;
  an authorized observation tests whether the path matches it.
- Link media disposition to the specific item, approved method and resulting
  record. A purchase of disposal services does not prove an identified device
  was sanitized or destroyed. Do not perform destructive testing under this guide.
- Compare flaw/remediation records and malicious-code protection evidence to the
  complete scoped asset list. An installed agent and an available update do not
  prove that protection operated or that the update reached each asset.
- Reconcile physical-access evidence to authorized individuals, visitors and
  relevant locations. Missing logs remain a coverage gap rather than evidence
  that no access occurred.

For each observation, preserve system/version, period, method, original result,
limitations and reviewer. When a correction changes a shared control, identify
all affected scoped systems and retest the relevant assertions. Keep inherited
provider evidence bounded to the service and configuration it actually covers.

## Cadence and renewal

Reassess after award changes, new flows, acquisitions, supplier changes, boundary
changes or failed safeguards. Use a documented owner-approved review cadence;
do not describe an internal midyear review as a universal clause deadline.
Reconcile the boundary before relying on an older assessment for renewal or a
new contract representation.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the incorporated-clause ledger, information/system boundary, complete
safeguard matrix, evidence references, observed results, subcontract dispositions,
open gaps and correction/retest records. Every unresolved item needs an owner and
next action. Keep a supplier's contractual promise separate from tested behavior.
State whether the packet supports readiness, observed implementation or an
explicitly authorized representation; do not claim certification from this review.
