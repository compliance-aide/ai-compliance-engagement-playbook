# Saudi NCA Data Cybersecurity Controls DCC-1:2022 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCA control text, a regulatory interpretation, an assessment result, or a compliance claim. Confirm current materials through the [Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority Data Cybersecurity Controls publication](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/dcc/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a data-lifecycle cybersecurity engagement record that connects data categories and accountable owners, system and supplier dependencies, authorized handling decisions, operating evidence, security events, corrective work, and independently reviewable assurance. Maintain a clear boundary between evidence coordination and accountable decisions about data handling, regulatory applicability, risk, or system operation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, data, cybersecurity, privacy, and legal authorities retain accountable decisions; platform and supplier owners preserve operating evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and assurance readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify incomplete evidence, prepare review questions, and draft non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine scope or applicability, decide legal obligations, accept risk, approve suppliers, alter production systems, issue an assessment, or claim compliance. Review annually and before a material data, system, supplier, incident, regulatory, or service change.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Retain the official [NCA Data Cybersecurity Controls publication](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/dcc/), retrieved 2026-07-31, and have authorized entity leadership confirm the DCC-1:2022 source/version, current status, and permitted use. This plan uses original operational language only; it is not NCA control text, a data classification decision, a control mapping, an assessment result, or a compliance claim. Do not publish confidential data inventories or source material.

### 1. Data boundary, ownership, and handling-decision package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain approved data-category and system/service inventory, data-owner roster, processing/storage and transfer context, legal-entity/service boundary, retention or handling decisions, supplier dependencies, and counsel-led applicability questions from data, privacy, security, legal, platform, and service owners. Define the data and system population, period, and exclusions before evidence review.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected data category or service to its accountable owner, boundary, relevant system/dependency record, authorized handling decision or open question, and review date. Use summaries where content or personal data cannot be viewed. This supports scope provenance; it cannot classify data, decide legal obligations, establish full population coverage, or authorize processing.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved metadata and flag missing owner, location, dependency, or review fields. Human data, privacy, legal, and service leaders decide handling and escalation. Trigger on a new data use, system or transfer change, supplier change, source update, or annual review.

### 2. Data-platform access, change, and event-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain access-approval/review records, platform configuration and change references, data-protection operation or test references, monitoring/event and incident records, exception approvals, and remediation links from platform engineering, security operations, data owners, and service owners. Keep sensitive outputs in their authorized repositories.
- **Validate and limit:** For a selected data service, access event, or material change, inspect the evidence chain from accountable approval through time-bounded record, relevant operational/test reference, exception or incident handling, and remediation/retest link. Check source integrity and reviewer authorization. This supports traceability of stated practice; it cannot prove protection effectiveness, absence of exposure, or DCC conformance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize permitted evidence metadata and surface missing dates, approvals, or action links; it cannot change platforms, grant access, view restricted content without authorization, or decide a security response. Named humans decide response and change action. Trigger on access change, data-platform release, alert/incident, failed test, or annual refresh.

### 3. Supplier assurance, exception, and accountable-remediation package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain supplier responsibility and assurance references, risk/exception decisions, independent-review observations, corrective-action plans, retest records, management-review minutes, and any proposed external communication approvals from procurement, data, security, legal, and accountable leaders.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier dependency, exception, or finding to the data/service boundary, owner, approved decision, evidence reference, corrective action, and retest or documented closure basis. Preserve unresolved legal and supplier constraints. This supports accountable remediation governance; it cannot accept risk, determine supplier compliance, or authorize a regulatory statement.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a source-linked evidence queue and flag overdue action or expiring decision records. Independent reviewers challenge completeness; named humans approve risk, closure, and external communications. Trigger on supplier issue, material finding, incident, expired exception, source update, or annual readiness cycle.
