# Saudi NCA Data Cybersecurity Controls DCC-1:2022 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCA control text, a regulatory interpretation, an assessment result, or a compliance claim. Confirm current materials through the [Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority Data Cybersecurity Controls publication](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/dcc/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a data-lifecycle cybersecurity engagement record that connects data categories and accountable owners, system and supplier dependencies, authorized handling decisions, operating evidence, security events, corrective work, and independently reviewable assurance. Maintain a clear boundary between evidence coordination and accountable decisions about data handling, regulatory applicability, risk, or system operation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, data, cybersecurity, privacy, and legal authorities retain accountable decisions; platform and supplier owners preserve operating evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and assurance readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify incomplete evidence, prepare review questions, and draft non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine scope or applicability, decide legal obligations, accept risk, approve suppliers, alter production systems, issue an assessment, or claim compliance. Review annually and before a material data, system, supplier, incident, regulatory, or service change.
