# ISO/IEC 27559:2022 de-identification engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a release authorization, a re-identification-risk conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27559:2022 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71677.html).

## Engagement focus

Operate a repeatable, evidence-led lifecycle for planned de-identification uses. Maintain a register that links each approved use case to the accountable data owner, authorized source data, intended recipients and purpose, data-handling environment, proposed de-identification approach, risk assumptions, test evidence, residual-risk decision, release conditions, downstream restrictions, retention, and review date. Treat the register as a decision trail, not proof that a data set is safe for every context or recipient.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, data, security, legal, analytics, product, and receiving-environment owners retain accountable decisions. The data owner confirms purpose and authorized inputs; privacy and risk owners decide whether the stated use may proceed; security and platform owners attest to the controlled environment; and legal or contractual owners approve disclosures and recipient terms where needed. Independent reviewers test selected records for traceability from approved purpose through release conditions, challenge scenario assumptions and recipient controls, verify that exceptions have named owners and expiry dates, and report unresolved evidence gaps.

AI may inventory authorized artifacts, compare required record fields, flag inconsistent lineage or aging decisions, draft evidence requests, and prepare reviewer workpapers. AI cannot de-identify production data, select or tune a technique, infer that re-identification risk is acceptable, approve a disclosure, weaken recipient restrictions, release data, make a legal determination, or claim compliance. Keep AI inputs minimized and authorized; use representative metadata and redacted evidence where full records are unnecessary. Reassess before a material change in purpose, data attributes, recipients, environment, or analysis capability; review open exceptions quarterly and complete an annual independent readiness review.
