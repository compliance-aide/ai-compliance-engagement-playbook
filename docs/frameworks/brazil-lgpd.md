# Brazil LGPD — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Brazilian legal advice. Consult the
> [ANPD source](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/outros-documentos-e-publicacoes-institucionais/lgpd-en-lei-no-13-709-capa.pdf/%40%40display-file/file)
> and authoritative Portuguese materials with qualified review.

## Engagement focus

Establish accountable owners and a processing map; document purpose, lifecycle,
safeguards, third parties, transfers, and rights handling; review material new
processing before launch; exercise security and incident readiness; and conduct
annual management review of evidence, corrective action, and regulatory change.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own classifications, processing decisions, remediation, and approved
communications. Independent reviewers test whether evidence supports claims. AI
normalizes inventory and sequences approved work; it cannot decide legal bases,
transfers, notifications, or individual outcomes. Recheck ANPD guidance and
applicable sector requirements each cycle.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [ANPD source](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/outros-documentos-e-publicacoes-institucionais/lgpd-en-lei-no-13-709-capa.pdf/%40%40display-file/file), authoritative Portuguese materials, and qualified Brazilian review; checked 2026-07-31. This plan does not decide legal basis, controller/operator role, transfer conditions, incident reporting, or a rights outcome.

### 1. Processing, role, and lifecycle map

- **Request and owner:** Controller/operator and DPO inputs, processing-purpose/data-category map, sensitive/child-data flags where applicable, system and vendor/transfer register, retention/disposal decision record, from privacy, product, and vendor owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processing activity to system flow, purpose, owner, vendor/transfer entry, and retention record. This can support a fact base for human legal decisions; it cannot determine lawful basis, role, or international-transfer legality.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved metadata and flag an unowned flow. Privacy/legal humans approve classifications. Refresh for a new purpose, sensitive-data use, processor, transfer, or material system change.

### 2. Notice, rights, safeguards, and incident-operation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Notice/version records, rights intake/response records, safeguards evidence, access/change records, vendor instructions, incident exercise/timeline, and remediation records from operational owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a human-approved selection to source, timing, owner, limitation, and follow-up while excluding personal data from the workpaper. This can support that declared workflows produce records; it cannot establish rights outcomes, security effectiveness, or notification duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a redacted evidence index and deadline warnings; it may not decide individual responses or notification. Refresh after a complaint, incident, new collection channel, or security/process change.

### 3. Impact, exception, and annual governance record

- **Request and owner:** Human-approved impact/risk inputs where warranted, exceptions and remediation/retest records, management-review decisions, source-change watch, and annual scope review from privacy and leadership owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception or assessment input to authority, evidence, expiry, and retest. This can support accountable governance; it cannot make a legal conclusion or accept risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale decisions and assemble a review packet. Humans approve risk, transfers, communications, and closure. Revisit annually and after material processing or regulatory change.
