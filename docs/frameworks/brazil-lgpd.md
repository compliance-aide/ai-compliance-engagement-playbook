# Brazil LGPD — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Brazilian legal advice or a compliance conclusion.

## Source and applicability

Use the [consolidated Portuguese LGPD](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm),
checked 2026-09-04, rather than relying solely on an older English translation.
The consolidated page includes 2026 amendments. Article 3 addresses territorial
connections beyond headquarters location; articles 7 and 11 distinguish processing
conditions for ordinary and sensitive data. Counsel must resolve applicable scope,
roles, conditions, exceptions and sector rules for the actual activity.

[ANPD incident guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento/agente-de-tratamento/comunicado-de-incidente-de-seguranca-cis)
identifies three working days under Resolution 15/2024, subject to applicable
specific legislation and relevant eligibility rules. Do not replace working days
with 72 hours or assume a small-entity extension without legal verification.
Rights requests have separate rules; incident timing is not a universal LGPD timer.
See the [author record](../refresh-reviews/brazil-lgpd.md).

## Engagement focus

Trace each processing purpose through role, approved legal decision, actual data
flow, safeguards, rights and retention. Preserve sensitive-data, children/adolescent,
public-sector and international-transfer questions for qualified review rather
than treating consent or a vendor contract as a universal answer.

## Roles

Business owners supply activity facts; privacy/encarregado coordinates accountability;
security and records owners supply operating evidence. Brazilian counsel and
controller leadership decide scope, legal bases, transfers, impact-report duties
and rights/notification outcomes. Independent reviewers challenge workpapers.
AI may reconcile permitted metadata, calculate approved clocks and draft packets;
it cannot make those legal decisions, accept risk or send unauthorised responses.

## Before starting

Obtain the charter, current source, controller/operator decisions, processing map,
notices, suppliers/countries, approved retention and hold rules, rights procedures,
incident contacts and evidence permissions. Identify uncertain translations and
resolve material meaning against Portuguese source. Use fictional QA records and
the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md); keep private evidence outside this repo.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Resolve scope and roles | Privacy/legal evaluate territorial facts, roles and relevant regimes. | Dated applicability record with unresolved questions and owners. |
| 2. Map processing | Business owners identify purposes, categories, systems, collection, recipients and retention; AI reconciles IDs. | Activity population and data-flow map with explicit gaps. |
| 3. Link legal decisions | Counsel/privacy review conditions, sensitive/child-data issues, notices and transfer routes. | Decision references for each activity; no invented default legal basis. |
| 4. Plan evidence and QA | Privacy/security define rights, safeguard and lifecycle cases with approved clocks and expected results. | Coverage plan with owners, fictional records and limits. |
| 5. Observe operation | Authorised operators execute approved cases and collect actual system readback. | Observation matches the scoped record/version; failed paths remain findings. |
| 6. Exercise incident reporting | Incident/privacy owners use a fictional event to test awareness capture, relevance review and timely draft preparation. | Separate incident clock, legal decision and submission authority records; no live filing. |
| 7. Correct and renew | Owners implement approved fixes; reviewer retests; governance reviews residual issues and changes. | Finding/change/retest chain and next queue with accountable decisions. |

## Evidence and test plan

| Evidence and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Processing/decision register, privacy | Trace an actual activity to purpose, role, decision and notice version. | Facts and approved representations reconcile. | A consent form cannot prove all processing conditions. |
| Supplier/transfer map, business/legal | Follow each declared route to destination and approved mechanism/decision. | Dependencies and unresolved transfers are visible. | A domestic contract does not settle international-transfer legality. |
| Fictional rights/disposal cases, records | Compare approved outcome and timing with source-system state. | Correct record returned/changed/disposed as authorised, with readback. | A closed ticket alone proves no underlying action. |
| Incident exercise, security/privacy | Compare awareness timestamp, approved working-day calendar and review route. | Escalation and draft preparation continue while unknown facts are labelled. | An exercise cannot prove reportability or actual notification. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Missing legal basis or role evidence blocks the affected conclusion, while factual
inventory continues. Conflicting translations require qualified interpretation.
New recipients or purposes use the approved decision route before disclosure.
A suspected incident escalates promptly; lack of confirmed harm is not a legal
non-reportability decision. After interruption, preserve original request/event
clocks and inspect prior receipts before repeating erasure or communication.

## Cadence and renewal

Maintain a source-specific calendar with trigger, timezone, working-day rule,
qualified exceptions and escalation owner. Review the current ANPD rules before
setting timers. Quarterly processing/provider reviews and annual governance review
are operating defaults. Reassess after new purposes, sensitive-data use, transfers,
incidents, material systems changes or regulatory amendments.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope/role and decision records, processing/transfer map, protected evidence,
QA observations, findings/retests, legal questions and next queue. Separate draft
reports from approved and actually submitted artifacts. Independent source, legal,
engagement and skeptical reviews and named human publication approval remain
pending. Original workflow and links only; no statutory tables or personal data.
