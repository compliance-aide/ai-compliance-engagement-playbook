# ISO/IEC 27041:2015 incident-investigation method assurance guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, an investigative conclusion, a legal opinion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27041:2015 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/44405.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an assurance case for material incident-investigation methods before they are relied on in consequential matters. Connect each method to its intended scenario, authorized users, functional and non-functional expectations, tool and environment dependencies, version history, validation evidence, known limitations, training evidence, approvals, review triggers, and retained workpapers. Record the basis for use in a particular investigation separately from the underlying incident conclusion, and make uncertainty visible rather than filling gaps with confident narrative.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Incident-response, forensic, security, legal, privacy, technology, risk, and records-management owners retain their decisions. Method owners maintain the method record and validation evidence; investigation leaders approve case use within the stated boundary; legal and privacy owners decide applicable handling and disclosure constraints; and accountable authorities decide any consequential action. Independent reviewers test whether validation evidence matches the claimed use, trace selected case work to the approved method and version, challenge unsupported assumptions, and verify that limitations, exceptions, and third-party dependencies are explicit.

AI may organize approved documentation, compare version and training records, identify missing validation artifacts, draft reviewer questions, and create non-authoritative workpaper summaries. AI cannot collect evidence, execute an investigative method, determine suitability, alter or delete records, make findings, decide attribution, provide legal advice, or claim compliance. Do not place sensitive case material into AI tools unless expressly authorized and governed. Review methods after material tool, environment, threat, or legal changes; review the assurance inventory quarterly and perform an annual independent readiness review.
