# ISO/IEC TS 27110:2021 cybersecurity-framework development guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a framework-design decision, a framework approval, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC TS 27110:2021 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72435.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a framework-development record that connects intended users and purpose, accountable sponsors, governance and stakeholder inputs, scope, terminology, evidence expectations, lifecycle decisions, revision history, implementation feedback, and independent review. This supports an AI agent’s role in consistent documentation and question preparation without letting it become the framework’s authorizing authority.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Framework sponsors, security, risk, privacy, engineering, legal, audit, and business owners retain accountable decisions; framework stewards preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test stakeholder coverage, decision traceability, version control, and implementation feedback. AI may organize authorized material, identify missing decisions, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot create or approve a binding framework, determine scope or applicability, set policy, accept risk, alter systems, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Conduct quarterly stewardship reviews, an annual framework review, and targeted reassessment before material regulatory, technology, incident, organizational, or mission changes.
