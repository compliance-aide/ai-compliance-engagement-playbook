# New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a notification decision. Check the [New Zealand Privacy Act](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2020/0031/latest/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing and collection-channel inventories; test notices, rights, retention, disclosure, security, processors, cross-border sharing, and breach escalation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy owners operate individual and breach workflows; independent reviewers sample timeliness and integrity. AI drafts non-final reports and triages evidence, but cannot decide serious harm, notification, exemptions, or binding responses. Review quarterly and annually.
