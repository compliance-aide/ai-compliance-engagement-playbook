# China Personal Information Protection Law engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not statutory text, Chinese legal advice, a regulatory filing, or a compliance claim. Confirm the applicable text and implementing measures through the [National People’s Congress English reference](https://en.npc.gov.cn.cdurl.cn/2021-12/29/c_694559_3.htm) and qualified local counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a jurisdiction-aware record of personal-information activities connected to China: processing purpose, data categories, people and organizations involved, system and supplier data flows, individual-facing notices and request handling, security and incident evidence, retention, cross-border activities, and material changes. Track uncertainty separately from facts and preserve the legal and operational decisions that determine applicability.

## Source and applicability

Use all parts of the NPC reference and current authoritative Chinese materials, not only the linked final installment. Source check: 2026-09-04. Include the [CAC compliance audit measures](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-02/14/c_1741233507681519.htm), effective May 1, 2025, in the source register. Qualified China counsel confirms applicable audit scope, frequency and implementing requirements; do not substitute the earlier draft or invent a universal annual audit.

Record applicability, processing role, lawful basis, sensitive/child-information treatment, representative requirements, impact-assessment triggers and cross-border route separately. Consent is not an automatic answer to every processing question. Current transfer rules and exceptions require their own review; a supplier agreement alone does not approve a transfer.

## Roles

Privacy/legal owners decide legal treatment and individual outcomes. Product/data owners maintain purposes and actual flows. Operators execute approved procedures. Supplier and security owners provide evidence. Named authorities approve external communications and representations. Independent reviewers challenge findings. AI organizes permitted metadata and drafts questions; it cannot obtain consent, decide transfer legality, accept risk or make filings.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record entity, activity, systems, locations, period, owner, inputs, expected output and exit check. Require approved applicability, obligation register, processing map and handling permissions. Missing legal determinations stay explicit while independent factual work continues. Use synthetic QA cases; keep personal information out of this public repository.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Confirm legal route | Privacy/legal owners document applicable duties, roles, source versions, special processing and assessment/audit requirements. | Approved obligation register with unresolved questions and decision owners. |
| 2. Reconcile processing | Data/system owners trace collection, purpose, category, use, recipient, location and retention against actual systems. | Complete inventory and discrepancies; sensitive/child-data questions are routed for determination. |
| 3. Align transparency and basis | Product/privacy owners compare actual processing with approved notices, lawful-basis decisions and consent records where applicable. | Versioned notice/basis evidence; a checkbox alone cannot prove validity or coverage. |
| 4. Verify individual workflows | Operators exercise approved synthetic request and withdrawal routes, preserving intake time and type-specific verification and decision requirements. | Case workpapers linking decision, system/recipient action and response draft. |
| 5. Review suppliers and transfers | Supplier/legal owners reconcile entrusted processing, recipients and outbound routes with approved instructions, assessments and conditions. | Exact decision packet with evidence gaps; no transfer occurs under this preparation step. |
| 6. Check safeguards and assessments | Security/privacy owners supply operating and incident evidence and applicable assessment/audit workpapers under approved methods. | Coverage-linked observations and findings; this workflow does not itself complete a statutory audit. |
| 7. Remediate and decide | Owners obtain separate implementation approval and reviewers retest. Authorized decision makers review open limitations and exact proposed communications. | Finding/action/retest chain and recorded decisions with destination readback. |
| 8. Maintain | Privacy owner monitors changed purposes, vendors, locations, incidents and rules; reopen affected evidence and decisions. | Owned review calendar distinguishing internal reminders from applicable legal dates. |

## Evidence and test plan

Use the entire approved obligation register. These original examples do not reproduce legal requirements. Preserve the full evidence corpus and do not trim material supplied to a scorer.

| Package and owner | Verification | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Processing map — data owner | Trace a flow through purpose, category, system, recipient and retention; reconcile gaps against the full inventory. | A selected trace does not prove complete discovery or legal classification. |
| Notices/basis — product/privacy | Compare published version and actual collection with the approved decision and evidence of any required consent. | Interface presence alone does not establish lawful processing. |
| Rights/withdrawal — operations | Trace synthetic intake through approved verification, decision, downstream action and response record. | An account preference change may leave other systems processing; preserve untested coverage. |
| Suppliers/transfers — vendor/legal | Compare actual route with approved scope, conditions and evidence from recipients. | Contract promises do not establish transfer permission or recipient performance. |
| Safeguards/audits — security/reviewer | Follow a finding through operating evidence, correction and retest; reconcile audit preparation with actual review scope. | A prepared checklist is not an independent audit result. |

Record source, collector, date, period, activity, requirement, expected/actual result, evidence pointer, limitation, reviewer and next action. State test populations and untested scope. No production changes, real disclosures or intrusive testing are authorized here.

## Failure branches and decisions

- **Unknown basis or sensitive-data status:** retain facts and ask the designated legal owner; do not default to consent or ordinary data.
- **Purpose changed:** reopen notice, basis, assessment and recipient decisions before relying on old evidence.
- **Failed request or withdrawal propagation:** preserve original timing and adverse results; assign system/recipient correction and retest.
- **Missing transfer or representative decision:** document the exact route or entity question and keep the dependent action pending.
- **Incident or possible notification:** route facts through approved incident/legal procedures; do not invent reporting timers or send notices without authorization.
- **Audit scope unclear:** obtain the applicable method, frequency and reviewer requirements; distinguish preparation from completion.
- **Interrupted action or uncertain write:** preserve versions, last completed step and pending approval; read back the destination before retrying.

## Cadence and renewal

Use counsel-confirmed obligations, audit requirements and actual decision conditions. Internal annual review is not a universal PIPL compliance cycle. Reassess after material purpose, data, recipient, system, location, incident or legal changes. Assign owners to each trigger.

## Completion and handoff

A preparation packet includes approved applicability, complete processing/obligation records, notices/basis evidence, individual-workflow tests, supplier/transfer decisions, security and audit inputs, findings/retests and open questions. Mark omissions. Responses, transfers and filings are separate authorized milestones with actual readback. Handoff specifies next action, owner, due date and dependency.
