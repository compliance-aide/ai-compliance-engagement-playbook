# Saudi NCA CCC 2-2024 cloud cybersecurity engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCA control text, an assessment, a cloud configuration directive, or a compliance claim. Confirm the current edition through the [NCA Cloud Cybersecurity Controls page](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/ccc/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a service-by-service cloud evidence record: provider or tenant role, legal-entity and service boundary, data locations and flows, shared-responsibility decisions, administrative access, supplier commitments, change evidence, monitoring and incidents, recovery readiness, and remediation. Keep provider and tenant responsibilities distinct and treat localization and regulatory scope questions as accountable human legal decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Cloud service owners define the service boundary; security and operations retain technical evidence; data and privacy leaders decide data handling; procurement manages supplier commitments; qualified Saudi counsel determines scope; independent reviewers test traceability. AI may organize approved evidence, compare service versions, find missing ownership, and draft questions, but cannot determine data-localization obligations, configure cloud services, approve access or releases, complete an assessment, submit to NCA, or claim compliance. Review at least annually and before material service, region, data-flow, provider, access, or incident changes.
