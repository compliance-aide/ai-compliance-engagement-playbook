# Saudi NCA CCC 2-2024 cloud cybersecurity engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCA control text, an assessment, a cloud configuration directive, or a compliance claim. Confirm the current edition through the [NCA Cloud Cybersecurity Controls page](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/ccc/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a service-by-service cloud evidence record: provider or tenant role, legal-entity and service boundary, data locations and flows, shared-responsibility decisions, administrative access, supplier commitments, change evidence, monitoring and incidents, recovery readiness, and remediation. Keep provider and tenant responsibilities distinct and treat localization and regulatory scope questions as accountable human legal decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Cloud service owners define the service boundary; security and operations retain technical evidence; data and privacy leaders decide data handling; procurement manages supplier commitments; qualified Saudi counsel determines scope; independent reviewers test traceability. AI may organize approved evidence, compare service versions, find missing ownership, and draft questions, but cannot determine data-localization obligations, configure cloud services, approve access or releases, complete an assessment, submit to NCA, or claim compliance. Review at least annually and before material service, region, data-flow, provider, access, or incident changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Retain the official [NCA Cloud Cybersecurity Controls page](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/ccc/), retrieved 2026-07-31, and have an authorized entity representative confirm the specific CCC 2-2024 source/version, status, and permitted use before engagement reliance. This is original planning language, not NCA control text, a cloud configuration standard, a control mapping, an assessment score, or a submission. Do not reproduce restricted source material or publish tenant, provider, or customer evidence.

### 1. Cloud service boundary and shared-responsibility package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain approved cloud-service and tenant inventory, legal-entity boundary, provider and deployment model, region/data-location and data-flow context, named service/security/data/procurement owners, and shared-responsibility decisions from cloud, security, privacy, legal, and procurement owners. Establish the service population, account/subscription boundary, and review period before collecting evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected service to its approved boundary, owning entity, provider role, dependency, data-location question, and human-owned responsibility decision. Inspect access-controlled metadata without exporting secrets or customer data. This supports a bounded cloud record; it cannot determine localization duties, legal applicability, cloud-service coverage, or provider compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventory metadata and flag missing owner, region, or responsibility links. Human legal, privacy, and service owners decide scope and responsibility. Trigger on a new cloud service, tenant/account change, region or data-flow change, provider change, or annual review.

### 2. Cloud configuration, access, and operational-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain approved architecture/configuration references, privileged-access and identity-review records, change/release records, logging/monitoring or test references, vulnerability/incident records, backup/recovery evidence, and exception approvals from cloud operations, security, engineering, and service owners. Retain technical outputs in authorized repositories and document their time period and access restrictions.
- **Validate and limit:** For a selected service or material change, inspect the trail from accountable owner and approved design/change through authorized access, time-bounded operating reference, monitoring or test output, exception, and remediation/retest link. Verify integrity and appropriate reviewer access. This supports traceability of the stated process; it cannot prove secure configuration, continuous operation, recovery capability, or CCC conformity.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved metadata and identify stale change, access-review, or test links; it cannot configure cloud resources, grant access, alter logs, or approve a release. Named humans decide changes and exceptions. Trigger on privileged-access change, material configuration/release, failed test, incident, recovery exercise, or annual refresh.

### 3. Provider assurance, incident, and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain provider agreements and responsibility references, authorized assurance artifacts, supplier issue/escalation records, incident coordination evidence, management review, risk decisions, remediation plans, retest records, and any proposed NCA-facing communication approvals from procurement, cloud security, legal, and accountable leaders.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected provider dependency, incident, or exception to the service boundary, contractual/responsibility source, owner, decision authority, corrective action, and retest or approved closure rationale. Record unresolved provider gaps and communication constraints. This supports accountable supplier governance; it cannot establish provider conformance, timeliness, or authorization for an NCA submission.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a source-linked action queue and flag missing expiration or escalation fields. Independent reviewers challenge evidence; named humans approve risk, closure, and external communications. Trigger on provider terms change, material supplier finding, incident, unresolved exception, source update, or annual readiness cycle.
