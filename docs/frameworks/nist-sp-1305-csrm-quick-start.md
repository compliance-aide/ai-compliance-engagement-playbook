# NIST SP 1305 C-SCRM quick-start engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST guidance, a supplier risk rating, a procurement decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 1305](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1305/final) and the organization’s approved acquisition, supplier, risk, and security decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a repeatable cybersecurity supply-chain risk-management capability that connects material technology acquisitions and supplier relationships to business context, service dependencies, risk ownership, due diligence, contractual expectations, change monitoring, exception decisions, and offboarding. Treat the quick-start guide as an organizing resource for both acquirer and supplier responsibilities. Keep documented risk analysis separate from selection, contracting, acceptance, and risk-acceptance decisions made by authorized people.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, procurement, security, technology, product, privacy, legal, supplier-management, business, risk, and system-owner roles. Operators maintain supplier inventories, service and data-dependency records, due-diligence artifacts, contract and assurance records, risk assessments, exception approvals, change notifications, continuity evidence, and offboarding records. Review critical suppliers, high-impact services, assumptions, and open treatment plans quarterly; reassess when a material supplier, product, data flow, architecture, ownership, or threat changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples supplier records from intake through monitoring; auditors test the evidence trail without selecting suppliers, negotiating terms, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied supplier evidence, identify stale due-diligence records, relate suppliers to recorded service dependencies, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot select or approve suppliers, negotiate or approve contracts, set risk appetite, accept an exception, determine legal obligations, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
