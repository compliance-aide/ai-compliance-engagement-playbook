# ISO/IEC TS 42119-2:2025 AI system testing engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, a test result, a release decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC TS 42119-2:2025 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/84127.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a risk-based AI testing record that connects system and component scope, intended uses, test objectives and plans, data and environment authorization, accountable owners, reproducible evidence, findings, remediation, release decisions, and independent review. Preserve the separation between AI-assisted test administration and accountable decisions about test sufficiency, safety, deployment, and acceptable residual risk.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product, engineering, quality, security, privacy, safety, risk, and domain authorities retain accountable decisions; test and supplier owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and readiness. AI may organize authorized test records, identify evidence gaps, draft questions and workpapers, and correlate approved findings, but cannot select test sufficiency criteria, create or alter test data without authorization, decide a result, approve remediation or release, accept risk, alter production systems, access sensitive data without authorization, or claim conformance. Review each material model, data, system, supplier, or deployment change and conduct an annual test-governance review.
