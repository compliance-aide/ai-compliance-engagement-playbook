# ISO/IEC 27553-2:2025 remote mobile-biometric engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a biometric-authentication decision, a remote-processing decision, a security or privacy determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27553-2:2025 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/82891.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a remote mobile-biometric engagement record connecting mobile and remote-service scope, accountable owners, authorized security and privacy evidence, data-flow and supplier dependencies, lifecycle assumptions, exceptions, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Treat AI as an evidence organizer only; it never makes decisions about biometric handling, authentication, remote processing, or access.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product, security, privacy, identity, legal, operations, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test data-flow traceability, remote-service boundaries, lifecycle readiness, privacy escalation, and exceptions. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify missing records, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot capture or process biometric data, authenticate a person, grant access, decide remote processing, determine security or privacy sufficiency, accept risk, alter production systems, initiate changes, or claim compliance. Review releases and material changes continuously, conduct quarterly evidence reviews, and reassess annually.
