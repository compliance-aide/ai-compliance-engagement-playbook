# UAE National Information Assurance Framework engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not UAE framework text, a regulatory interpretation, an assurance determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [UAE National Information Assurance Framework](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/-/media/Documents-2023/National-Information-Assurance-Framework-NIAF.ashx).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an information-assurance engagement record that connects entity context, information and service assets, accountable owners, risk and operational evidence, dependencies, incidents, continuity decisions, remediation, and independent review. Treat the record as support for accountable assurance decisions, never as a substitute for national, sector, or entity authority.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Entity leadership, information-assurance, risk, security, continuity, privacy, and legal authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers challenge scope, traceability, and readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify stale evidence, prepare questions, and draft non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine applicability, accept risk, make a regulatory or assurance determination, approve suppliers or continuity decisions, alter production services, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material entity-context, asset, supplier, incident, continuity, legal, or regulatory changes.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [UAE National Information Assurance Framework](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/-/media/Documents-2023/National-Information-Assurance-Framework-NIAF.ashx); source status: public framework document, retrieved 2026-07-31. Link to the source and do not reproduce protected framework content. This original plan does not determine applicability or assurance.

### 1. Entity, information-service, and accountability boundary

- **Request and owner:** Request the entity and sector applicability analysis, approved engagement boundary, information/service and asset inventory, accountable-owner register, dependency map, and risk-context record from leadership, assurance, security, legal, and service owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected information service or asset to its owner, inventory entry, dependency record, and approved engagement scope. This supports a time-bounded population; it cannot decide regulatory applicability, criticality, or assurance status.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved registers and flag missing ownership. Designated human authorities decide scope, classification, risk appetite, and applicability. Refresh after entity, sector, asset, service, dependency, or legal-context change.

### 2. Safeguard operation, supplier, and continuity evidence

- **Request and owner:** Request access and change records, monitoring/remediation evidence, supplier due-diligence and responsibility records, continuity plans and exercise outputs, exception register, and action tracking from security, procurement, continuity, and service owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected service, supplier, change, or exercise to dated source records, accountable review, exception decision, corrective action, and retest where appropriate. This supports evidence of an operating process; it cannot certify safeguard effectiveness, supplier adequacy, or recoverability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved evidence and identify incomplete reviews; it may not approve a supplier, alter production services, or accept an exception. Recollect after a supplier, control, incident, exercise, or material service change.

### 3. Incident, assurance decision, and annual review record

- **Request and owner:** Request incident and escalation records, legal/regulatory decision log, assurance-review workpapers, management-review minutes, corrective-action closure evidence, and source-change watch from incident, legal, assurance, and leadership owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected incident, finding, or exception to facts, authorized human authority, approved follow-up, and closure evidence. This supports accountability; it cannot determine reportability, make an assurance conclusion, or submit to a regulator.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a redacted evidence packet and surface aging actions. Humans approve communications, risk acceptance, submissions, and closure. Revisit annually and after an incident, regulatory change, review finding, or unresolved exception.
