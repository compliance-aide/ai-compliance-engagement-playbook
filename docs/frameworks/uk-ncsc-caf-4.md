# UK NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework 4.0 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCSC assessment content or a regulatory conclusion. Use the [NCSC CAF collection](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/cyber-assessment-framework) to confirm current applicability.

## Engagement focus

Maintain service scope, essential-function dependencies, governance decisions, security operations evidence, resilience exercises, supplier records, metrics, findings, and improvement commitments.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Service owners validate critical-function reality; independent assessors reach their own conclusions. AI organizes evidence and signals inconsistencies, but cannot classify essential functions or determine adequacy. Test meaningful scenarios quarterly and review annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** At engagement start, retain the retrieval date, current-status check, and applicable use terms for the official [NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework collection](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/cyber-assessment-framework). Source status is current when the collection remains available. This plan is original language, not CAF assessment content, a reproduction of assessment questions, or a regulatory conclusion; a named human determines scope and applicability.

### 1. Essential-function, scope, and governance-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Executive, service, risk, security, architecture, asset, privacy, legal, and records owners provide critical-service or essential-function decisions, scope/dependency maps, accountable-role assignments, risk decisions, metrics, exceptions, and management-review records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one selected function from approved scope and dependencies through governance ownership, risk treatment, and a management-review record. This can support an evidence-traceability observation; it cannot classify an essential function, determine framework applicability, or conclude adequacy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied dependency maps and flag missing ownership or stale decisions. Humans define essential-function reality, approve boundaries and treatment, and accept risk. Refresh after a material service, dependency, supplier, data, or threat change and annually.

### 2. Protective-security and operational-response package

- **Request and owner:** Security operations, identity, engineering, platform, application, asset, data, vulnerability, and incident owners provide approved operating expectations, implementation/change records, access or vulnerability review evidence, monitoring records, selected alert/incident cases, and corrective-action evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Select a material system or service and trace one protection, detection, or response activity from approved expectation through time-bounded implementation and operating records. This can test a supplied record chain; it cannot prove continuous effectiveness, completeness, or resilience under untested conditions.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare evidence indexes and identify conflicts or missing dates. Humans authorize changes, direct incidents, judge adequacy, and decide remediation. Recollect after significant vulnerabilities, incidents, architecture changes, or monitoring changes.

### 3. Resilience, supplier, and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Continuity, service, supplier, procurement, security, incident, communications, legal, privacy, and independent-review owners provide exercise plans/results, recovery dependencies, supplier assurance and escalation records, material findings, remediation commitments, exception decisions, and follow-up reviews.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one disruption scenario or supplier dependency from documented planning through an exercise, incident, or review record to accountable corrective action and closure check. This can support a resilience-evidence observation; it cannot prove future recovery, validate a supplier assertion, or make an assessment conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize scenarios and flag overdue commitments. Humans approve exercises, continuity actions, supplier treatment, risk acceptance, and final assessment conclusions. Review after an exercise, incident, supplier change, or material business-continuity change.
