# GovRAMP Low impact-level engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not program content, an impact decision or authorization. Draft pending independent source, skeptical and rights review.

## Source and applicability

Use the current [GovRAMP document library](https://govramp.org/document-library), [Low provider package](https://govramp.org/blog/document/service-provider-package-for-low-impact/), [Low assessor package](https://govramp.org/blog/document/3pao-package-for-low-impact/) and government partner instructions. Indexed official descriptions identify separate provider and assessor materials for Low. Obtain the actual authorized packages and record their versions before assessing requirements.

Source limitation: package descriptions were located, but package contents, current baseline, categorization tool and sponsor-specific instructions were not reviewed in this draft. Search results also contain older StateRAMP revision-4 material; never select a baseline from a search snippet or a historical control count. Resolve source conflicts with the current program authority.

## Engagement focus

Execute an offering-specific evidence workflow after the responsible authorities approve Low as the required impact level. Keep level, program status and buyer acceptance separate. Neither a Low label nor public-facing data establishes that integrity, availability, administration or interconnections can be ignored.

## Roles

The government partner and accountable offering authorities own level/scope decisions. Security and operations owners maintain current evidence. Qualified assessors, PMO and sponsoring authorities retain their program decisions. An independent reviewer challenges the evidence and coverage. Legal/customer-assurance owners approve external wording.

AI may reconcile supplied inventories, compare evidence and draft questions. AI cannot select the level, approve a baseline deviation, accept risk, issue assessment conclusions, submit attestations or authorize procurement. Use only approved QA environments and methods for execution.

## Before starting

Apply the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Obtain the level decision, government requirement, offering version/regions/features, boundary, data-flow register, current source packages, owners and evidence authority. If Low is only proposed, keep categorization unresolved and continue independent collection rather than asserting eligibility.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Bind the level decision.** Match the government requirement and approved categorization to the actual offering, data and service consequences. Output: dated decision record and unresolved boundary questions.
2. **Freeze the baseline.** Record the exact Low package, revision, selected program route, required artifacts and any government additions. Output: complete requirement register; do not equate Ready evidence with the full Authorized route.
3. **Reconcile the boundary.** Inventory every included component, interface, administrative path, shared service and region. Map provider and customer responsibilities. Output: full dependency matrix with gaps and exclusions retained.
4. **Collect implementation evidence.** Link each applicable item to owner, current configuration/build, period and artifact. Output: evidence manifest. A policy or provider assurance does not prove this offering's implementation.
5. **Execute approved tests.** Record criterion, expected result, authorized method, actual result and population coverage. Output: test records and uncovered routes. Preserve failures, inaccessible assets and unobserved periods explicitly.
6. **Reconcile findings and changes.** Connect each observation to remediation, authorized decisions and retests on affected components. Reopen categorization when new data or service consequences challenge the approved Low decision. Output: finding/change register without automatic promotion or demotion by AI.
7. **Review and hand off.** Provide full evidence, source gaps and disagreements to the reviewer and designated program authorities. Output: decision packet with separate assessment, submission, status and buyer-acceptance records.

## Evidence and test plan

### Level and boundary package

Record the approved offering, information types, service purposes, government requirement, categorization rationale, regions/features and source versions. Reconcile actual data flows to the decision, including administrative and support paths. “Public website” is not a complete data inventory. Unknown classification remains an owner question, not an automatic Low result.

### Baseline and implementation package

Maintain one row per applicable requirement with owner, provider/customer division, implementation, evidence period, test criterion and outcome. Preserve government additions distinctly from standard baseline requirements. Track all scoped assets in coverage records even when technical testing uses a justified selection. No silent omission or cap is permitted in evidence handed to assessment.

### Lifecycle and status package

Retain findings, approved treatments, retests, source changes, monitoring failures, incidents and status references. Match public statements to exact offering, level, current program status and scope. The [general GovRAMP guide](govramp-licensed-program.md) explains status distinctions. Do not extend a Low listing to new regions/features or describe it as Moderate.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Required Low decision missing: mark applicability inconclusive and route it to the designated officials.
- New processing contradicts the approved boundary: mark boundary correspondence not_supported; obtain a new decision before claiming the existing assessment covers it.
- Current package unavailable: retain the source gap and stop requirement-level conclusions; do not substitute an old NIST baseline silently.
- Inherited control lacks customer implementation: separate verified inherited evidence from the untested customer portion.
- Retest unavailable: retain the finding's last supported state. A completed remediation ticket is not a passed retest.

Fictional desk case: the approved boundary covers a public-content service, but support records show an unassessed identity-document upload route. The boundary-completeness claim is not_supported. The appropriate impact level requires a new authorized decision; AI must not automatically select Moderate.

## Cadence and renewal

Use verified current program and partner requirements for monitoring, reassessment and renewal. Record source-backed deadlines and event triggers for data, features, regions, dependencies, incidents and failed tests. Remove unsupported universal quarterly scheduling; recheck actual changes before carrying forward old evidence.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the level/boundary decision, versioned baseline, full inventory, evidence/test manifest, findings, monitoring gaps, disagreements and named next actions. Classify assertions supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable or not_tested. Keep task completion separate from program and procurement decisions. No actual offering assessment or authorization occurred through drafting this guide.
