# NAIC Own Risk and Solvency Assessment engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NAIC guidance, an actuarial opinion, or a solvency conclusion. Confirm state-specific applicability through the [NAIC ORSA topic page](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/own-risk-and-solvency-assessment).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a current enterprise-risk record: material risk inventory, ownership, risk and capital decision inputs, scenario evidence, business-plan changes, governance discussions, and the controlled drafting and review history for any required summary reporting.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Insurer leadership owns risk and capital decisions; risk, finance, actuarial, and business teams maintain their inputs; independent reviewers challenge evidence and governance. AI compiles approved records, identifies incomplete ownership or stale assumptions, and drafts internal questions, but cannot quantify capital adequacy, make actuarial judgments, or file a report. Reassess after material strategy changes, schedule management review, and retain human sign-off for confidential reporting.
