# ISO 8000-8:2015 — data-quality engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a data-quality certification. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/60805.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain critical data products, owners, measures, thresholds, lineage, integration, migration, issue, remediation, and change evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Data owners approve tolerances; independent reviewers reperform samples. AI profiles patterns and evidence, but cannot change source data, set tolerance, or declare quality acceptable. Review quarterly and annually.
