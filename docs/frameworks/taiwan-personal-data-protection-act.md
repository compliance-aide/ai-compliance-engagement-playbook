# Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Taiwan legal advice or a regulatory conclusion. Confirm current text and effective dates through the [official law database](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/EngLawList.aspx?id=FL010627).

## Engagement focus

Maintain personal-data inventory, collection and use rationale, notices, sharing and vendor records, access and correction requests, security incidents, retention evidence, exception decisions, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, legal, security, and business owners decide applicability and responses; independent reviewers test the evidence chain. AI organizes approved records and drafts gap questions, but cannot interpret law, decide rights outcomes, or notify authorities. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Taiwan official law database](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/EngLawList.aspx?id=FL010627) for current text and effective-date verification; checked 2026-07-31. Applicability, definitions, conditions, regulator guidance, amendment status, and republication rights require qualified Taiwan legal review. This original plan does not provide legal advice or decide a rights, notification, or compliance outcome.

### 1. Processing, purpose, notice, and sharing package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, business, product, data, and procurement owners provide a dated processing inventory; data categories; collection channels; stated purpose; system/data-flow records; notice/version history; recipients/vendors; transfer context; and retention/disposal references.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected collection-to-sharing route to source channel, purpose record, system, owner, notice, recipient/vendor, and retention record. This supports a factual evidence packet; it cannot determine a lawful basis, notice sufficiency, consent validity, or sharing legality.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventory and release metadata and flag new flows or unowned recipients. Privacy/legal humans decide classifications and action. Refresh for a new purpose, data category, channel, vendor, transfer, or material product change.

### 2. Individual requests, security, and incident package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, customer operations, records, and security owners provide approved request and identity-verification procedures, de-identified workflow metadata, access/security operations, retention/disposal execution evidence, incident/exercise timelines, communications approvals, and remediation records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a de-identified request, disposal event, or exercise through intake, owner, human decision/escalation, action, and follow-up. This supports traceability for a selected workflow; it cannot authenticate an individual, decide a request, prove safeguards effective, or determine notification duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a restricted evidence index and flag missing dates; it may not access unapproved personal data, respond to individuals, notify authorities, or close incidents. Recollect after an incident, failed exercise, recurring complaint, or material security/process change.

### 3. Vendor, exception, and governance-review package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, privacy, legal, security, and leadership owners provide vendor review/instruction records, exception and risk decisions, management-review minutes, remediation/retest, annual scope renewal, and official-source change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected vendor change, exception, or remediation item to supporting facts, human authority, expiry, action owner, and retest. This supports accountable oversight; it cannot approve a transfer, accept risk, or claim legal compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare source-linked non-authoritative workpapers and flag stale reviews. Humans approve contracts, risk, communications, and closure. Refresh annually and after material vendor, transfer, processing, incident, or regulatory changes.
