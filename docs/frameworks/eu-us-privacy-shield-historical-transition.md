# EU–U.S. Privacy Shield historical-transition engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Privacy Shield program text, legal advice, a self-certification, or a transfer-mechanism conclusion. The U.S. Department of Commerce states that Privacy Shield is no longer a valid mechanism for EU personal-data transfers; confirm the current position through its [EU data privacy information](https://www.trade.gov/knowledge-product/european-union-data-privacy-and-protection), the current [Data Privacy Framework program](https://dataprivacyframework.gov/), and qualified counsel.

## Source and applicability

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). The official Court of Justice
[16 July 2020 announcement](https://curia.europa.eu/site/upload/docs/application/pdf/2020-07/cp200091en.pdf)
identifies the invalidation of Decision 2016/1250. Its indexed announcement was
checked on 2026-09-04; the full judgment and present transfer-law position were
not reviewed in this pass. Use the [current DPF guide](eu-us-data-privacy-framework.md)
for a separate current-recipient review, without treating it as an automatic
replacement for every historical arrangement.


Record the historical period, entities and transfers being examined. Have legal
identify any continuing commitments or disputes; a historical-status finding does
not determine their disposition. Current transfer decisions require their own
current sources and approvals.

## Engagement focus

Treat this as a legacy-transition record, not a new compliance target. Inventory historical Privacy Shield references in contracts, notices, vendor records, data-transfer documentation, and public statements; preserve evidence of the transition decision; identify current transfer and privacy obligations; and route any unresolved legacy representations to legal and privacy owners. Never infer that historical participation establishes current eligibility or a valid transfer mechanism.

## Before starting

Define the complete repository and publication inventory, evidence-access scope,
review period and accountable owners. Use [work items](../../templates/work-item.md)
to record one assertion at a time: reference context, current decision linkage,
correction approval or publication readback. Keep actual contracts, personal data
and privileged advice in their approved restricted locations.

Assign an owner to reconcile the source inventory against contract management,
website publishing, procurement and privacy records. A repository search alone
cannot prove coverage of externally hosted notices or signed agreements.

## Ordered workflow


1. Establish the complete authorized source inventory: active contract templates,
   executed agreements, notices, supplier records, transfer assessments, website
   pages and archives. Record unavailable repositories and unreadable documents.
   Keyword hits help locate references but do not prove full coverage; reconcile
   the source list and review each document's context.
2. For every reference, record location, version/date, owner, linked entity/data
   flow and use. Distinguish an accurate historical quotation from an active
   representation of current transfer authority. Preserve originals and labels;
   do not erase historic evidence to make a search return zero hits.
3. Link each active flow to the current counsel-approved mechanism and underlying
   evidence. A renamed field or substituted program logo does not establish a
   mechanism change. Missing approval or scope evidence remains an open question.
4. Prepare each proposed correction with before/after text, affected audiences,
   contract dependencies and owner. Have legal decide whether an amendment,
   notice, recipient follow-up or another action is needed. Do not change an
   executed agreement or publish a notice as part of read-only discovery.
5. After authorized action, retrieve the actual published artifact or completed
   agreement from its system of record. Compare it to the approved version and
   verify related templates and generated outputs. A merged source edit alone
   cannot prove the customer-facing page changed.
6. Preserve outstanding disputes, retained-data obligations and unresolved vendor
   actions in separate records. Legal decides their disposition; invalidating an
   adequacy decision does not give an agent authority to discard records or
   declare that all historical obligations vanished.
7. Reconcile all references to a disposition: historical context retained, current
   representation corrected and verified, or unresolved with owner and next action.
   Record inaccessible sources separately. Closure requires full scoped coverage,
   not an absence of search hits after deleting the search terms.

## Failure branches and decisions

 an unreadable archive is `not_tested`; full reference
coverage is `inconclusive`. An observed live page contradicting an approved
correction is `not_supported` for the publication-match assertion. Whether a
particular transfer is lawful remains a separate legal decision.

**Fictional desk case:** a 2018 archived agreement accurately mentions Privacy
Shield, while a live onboarding page presents it as today's transfer basis. The
archive can remain as a historical record with its approved disposition; the live
claim needs legal-owned correction. If a replacement draft exists but the live
page is unchanged, the correction is not verified. Neither item establishes the
recipient's current DPF coverage. No actual contract or public page was changed.

## Roles

Qualified privacy and legal owners determine transition obligations and current transfer mechanisms; business and communications owners approve changes to customer-facing statements; procurement maintains supplier records; independent reviewers test closure evidence. AI may locate references, organize approved evidence, and draft questions, but cannot select a transfer mechanism, self-certify, state that participation is current, modify public notices, or make a legal conclusion. Review the legacy record when a contract, data flow, supplier, notice, dispute, or legal-status change occurs, and keep an accountable annual confirmation that historical references have not re-entered active use.


## Evidence and test plan

| Evidence package | Owner and execution | Supported conclusion and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complete source inventory | Records and publishing owners reconcile every scoped repository, page family, template and agreement collection; record access failures and duplicates. | Shows the sources reviewed and missing; does not establish that unlisted sources do not exist. |
| Reference/context register | Privacy reviews each reference with surrounding text, document date, current use, entity and linked flow. | Distinguishes historical context from active reliance; cannot decide transfer legality. |
| Current-decision linkage | Legal supplies the approved mechanism decision and scope evidence for each active flow. | Establishes presence and traceability of a decision; the agent does not approve its substance. |
| Correction and readback | Content/contract owners link the approved revision to the actual resulting page, template or agreement and observation date. | Supports that the specified artifact changed; does not prove all related channels changed. |
| Outstanding obligations | Legal and procurement track retained-data, dispute and vendor actions through owned dispositions. | Shows accountability and unresolved work; does not extinguish obligations. |

For each correction, enumerate all surfaces that can reproduce the claim: source
text, rendered page, downloadable document, template-generated output and vendor
record where in scope. Verify each actual artifact after authorized changes.
Do not modify a historical signed artifact to make it match a newly approved
statement; link an approved amendment or explanatory record as appropriate.

Retain the full evidence set and per-source dispositions. If batching is needed,
use stable source identifiers and reconcile every batch to the parent inventory;
do not sample or truncate evidence bound for a scorer. Mark inaccessible sources
as unresolved even when all accessible sources have been reviewed.

## Cadence and renewal

Recheck after contract/template, supplier, data-flow, notice, dispute or legal
changes. Set a documented owner-approved recurrence to detect reintroduced active
references; an annual internal check is a recommendation, not a claim that the
historical program imposes a current renewal duty. Prioritize active public
representations and live transfer decisions when new references appear.

## Completion and handoff

Provide the complete source inventory, reference register, preserved historical
records, current-decision links, correction approvals, actual readbacks and open
obligations. Every reference needs an explicit disposition and every inaccessible
source an owner and next action. Historical references properly retained do not
prevent completion of reference cleanup; unresolved active claims do.

Report completion only for the defined review scope. Keep the broader current
transfer-law assessment separate and disclose unresolved evidence. Independent
source, skeptical, rights and publication reviews remain pending for this guide;
author desk cases and structural validation are not cross-model usability proof.
