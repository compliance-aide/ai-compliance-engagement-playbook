# NIST SP 800-18 Revision 2 system-planning engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST text, an authorization decision, a control determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-18 Revision 2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/18/r2/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an accountable system-plan record that connects each in-scope system’s purpose, ownership, authorization boundary, information and service dependencies, security, privacy, and supply-chain planning context, implemented and planned safeguards, evidence locations, exceptions, decisions, approvals, change history, and next review date. Use the record to make planning assumptions inspectable: distinguish a current fact, planned action, unresolved question, and accepted exception. Keep a named source and date for material statements so reviewers can test the record without treating it as self-proving.

## Roles and annual rhythm

System owners, information owners, security, privacy, supply-chain, architecture, operations, legal, records, and risk owners retain accountable decisions. Plan custodians coordinate updates; evidence owners attest to record availability and currency; designated authorities make authorization and risk decisions; and auditors evaluate the record in their independent role. Independent reviewers sample a system from scope through dependencies, implementation evidence, planned work, exception status, approvals, and material changes, challenging unsupported statements, stale links, unclear boundaries, and inconsistencies between plans and operating evidence.

AI may organize authorized plan metadata, detect missing ownership or review fields, compare an approved plan against an evidence register, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot determine a system boundary, select or allocate safeguards, approve a plan, authorize operation or use, accept risk, attest that implementation is current, alter systems or records, or claim compliance. Reassess the plan before material mission, data, architecture, supplier, control, privacy, incident, or authorization changes; review update status routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness review.
