# NIST SP 800-18 Revision 2 system-planning engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST text, an authorization decision, a control determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-18 Revision 2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/18/r2/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an accountable system-plan record that connects each in-scope system’s purpose, ownership, authorization boundary, information and service dependencies, security, privacy, and supply-chain planning context, implemented and planned safeguards, evidence locations, exceptions, decisions, approvals, change history, and next review date. Use the record to make planning assumptions inspectable: distinguish a current fact, planned action, unresolved question, and accepted exception. Keep a named source and date for material statements so reviewers can test the record without treating it as self-proving.

## Roles and annual rhythm

System owners, information owners, security, privacy, supply-chain, architecture, operations, legal, records, and risk owners retain accountable decisions. Plan custodians coordinate updates; evidence owners attest to record availability and currency; designated authorities make authorization and risk decisions; and auditors evaluate the record in their independent role. Independent reviewers sample a system from scope through dependencies, implementation evidence, planned work, exception status, approvals, and material changes, challenging unsupported statements, stale links, unclear boundaries, and inconsistencies between plans and operating evidence.

AI may organize authorized plan metadata, detect missing ownership or review fields, compare an approved plan against an evidence register, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot determine a system boundary, select or allocate safeguards, approve a plan, authorize operation or use, accept risk, attest that implementation is current, alter systems or records, or claim compliance. Reassess the plan before material mission, data, architecture, supplier, control, privacy, incident, or authorization changes; review update status routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [NIST SP 800-18 Revision 2 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/18/r2/final), recording version and use terms. This is original plan-evidence guidance, not an authorization package or a statement that safeguards are implemented.

### 1. System context and boundary package

- **Request and owner:** System, information, architecture, security, privacy, and supply-chain owners provide approved system purpose, boundary statement, information types, users, interfaces, dependencies, data flows, owner assignments, and material assumptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected planned boundary statement to its accountable owner, dependency/data-flow evidence, date, and unresolved assumptions. This cannot determine the boundary, authorize operation, or prove that every dependency is complete.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare plan metadata against supplied inventories and flag missing owners or stale dates. Humans decide boundaries and scope. Refresh before material mission, data, interface, or supplier change.

### 2. Safeguard, evidence, and planned-work package

- **Request and owner:** Security, privacy, operations, and system owners provide implemented/planned safeguard statements, evidence-register links, assessment references, planned milestones, resource/dependency assumptions, and evidence-owner attestations.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a plan statement to its evidence location or clearly labeled planned work, accountable owner, date, and limitation. This assesses whether claims are distinguishable and traceable; it cannot validate implementation or allocate safeguards.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify unsupported claims, broken links, or overdue milestones. Humans validate evidence, set priorities, and approve plans. Recollect after assessment, control, architecture, or incident change.

### 3. Decision, exception, and independent-readiness package

- **Request and owner:** Risk, designated-authority, legal/records, system, and independent-review owners provide decision records, exceptions, risk dispositions, approvals, change history, remediation plans, annual review workpapers, and follow-up evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception or material change to source plan impact, named human authority, limitation, approval/decision date, remediation or review date, and independent challenge. This cannot accept risk, authorize use, attest, or close an exception.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare change comparisons and overdue-review lists. Humans decide authorization, risk, and closure; independent reviewers challenge completeness and consistency. Review annually and after material plan changes.
