# UK NIS Regulations — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a designation or compliance conclusion. Consult the [UK NIS Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/506/contents) and relevant sector authority.

## Engagement focus

Confirm sector, critical-service boundaries, designation, authority, and reporting contacts. Preserve service-impact, resilience, incident, supplier, and remediation evidence in the applicable authority format.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain operating records; independent reviewers test reporting rehearsal and traceability. AI reconciles inventories and prepares drafts, but cannot determine designation or severity, notify authorities, or attest. Review quarterly and perform annual readiness review.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
