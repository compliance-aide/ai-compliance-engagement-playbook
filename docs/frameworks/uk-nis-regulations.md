# UK NIS Regulations — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a designation or compliance conclusion. Consult the [UK NIS Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/506/contents) and relevant sector authority.

## Engagement focus

Confirm sector, critical-service boundaries, designation, authority, and reporting contacts. Preserve service-impact, resilience, incident, supplier, and remediation evidence in the applicable authority format.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain operating records; independent reviewers test reporting rehearsal and traceability. AI reconciles inventories and prepares drafts, but cannot determine designation or severity, notify authorities, or attest. Review quarterly and perform annual readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Record retrieval date, applicable sector-authority guidance, and use conditions for the [UK NIS Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/506/contents). This is original evidence-planning language, not legal advice, a designation decision, incident notification, or compliance conclusion. Named legal, sector, security, executive, and authorized reporting owners decide applicability, severity, and any authority communication.

### 1. Applicability, essential-service, and authority-contact package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, regulatory, sector, executive, security, service, and records owners provide documented applicability/designation analysis, essential-service boundary, competent-authority and reporting-contact records, accountable-role map, exclusions, and review dates.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected service or scope decision to the stated legal/sector rationale, accountable owner, authority contact, and periodic review record. This supports decision traceability; it cannot determine designation, interpret law, or establish complete scope.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied service inventories with recorded owners and flag stale contacts. Humans decide applicability, service boundaries, and authority relationships. Refresh after organizational, service, sector, or authority change.

### 2. Security, resilience, supplier, and continuity package

- **Request and owner:** Security, technology, continuity, supplier, service, and risk owners provide service-dependency maps, risk and resilience assessments, security-control evidence references, continuity/recovery test records, supplier obligations, change assessments, and corrective-action records.
- **Validate and limit:** Follow a selected essential-service dependency or test from owner and risk record through planned resilience measure, test/change evidence, and tracked action. This supports a bounded operational trail; it cannot prove resilience, validate every supplier, or approve risk treatment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied dependencies and flag overdue actions. Humans approve designs, supplier decisions, tests, and exceptions. Recollect after material change, supplier issue, failed test, or service disruption.

### 3. Incident assessment, notification rehearsal, and assurance package

- **Request and owner:** Incident, legal, sector, communications, service, records, and assurance owners provide incident triage procedures, selected incident decision records, reporting rehearsal outputs, contact tests, communication approvals, post-incident reviews, and annual management-review evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected rehearsal or historical incident record through triage, human severity/notification decision, controlled communications, and follow-up. This supports readiness and traceability; it cannot determine reportability, notify an authority, or prove all incidents were reported correctly.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare draft timelines and identify missing records. Humans determine incident severity, notification, legal position, and external communication. Test quarterly and after material incident, authority change, or contact failure.
