# U.S. Cyber Trust Mark program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not FCC program text, a product certification, a label authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current materials through the [FCC U.S. Cyber Trust Mark program notice](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-354A1.pdf).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a consumer IoT product assurance record that connects product identity and scope, accountable manufacturer and service owners, component and supplier dependencies, security lifecycle evidence, vulnerability handling, authorized label program interactions, remediation, and independent review. Keep readiness support separate from testing, label authorization, product safety, market representation, and regulatory decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, product, engineering, security, legal, privacy, and quality authorities retain accountable decisions; product and supplier owners preserve lifecycle evidence; authorized program administrators and independent reviewers perform their defined review roles. AI may organize authorized records, identify missing evidence, correlate approved product facts, and draft questions or non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine product eligibility, approve a product release, select a test or program route, assign a certification result, authorize label use, access customer data without authorization, submit an application, or claim certification or compliance. Review annually and before material product, software, vulnerability, supplier, label-program, incident, or regulatory changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [FCC U.S. Cyber Trust Mark program notice](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-354A1.pdf), current authorized-label-administrator materials, and organization-approved legal direction; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not reproduce program criteria, decide eligibility, authorize a label, or substitute for program testing or a human legal decision.

### 1. Product boundary and program-route package

- **Request and owner:** Product, legal, security, quality, and program owners provide the product-family and model list, supported software/firmware versions, intended market and distribution boundary, accountable entities, supplier/component register, program correspondence, and material-change history.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one nominated product/version from the release register through its accountable manufacturer, component/supplier record, stated program route, and current ownership. This tests record linkage; it cannot establish eligibility, interpret label rules, or approve market release.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized product metadata and flag mismatched versions, owners, or supplier records. Humans decide scope, program route, legal posture, and release. Refresh after a model, firmware, manufacturer, supplier, or market change.

### 2. Lifecycle-security and vulnerability-response package

- **Request and owner:** Engineering, security, support, and supplier owners provide secure-development references, release approvals, update/support commitments, vulnerability intake and remediation records, security-test references, customer-support escalation records, and approved deviations.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a disclosed or internally tracked security issue from intake through product/version triage, accountable decision, remediation or documented limitation, release/support communication, and closure evidence. This cannot validate all code, test a product, or declare security sufficiency.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may correlate issue, version, and remediation dates in read-only records and surface stale ownership or missing closure artifacts. Humans assess severity, approve remediation, communicate externally, and authorize releases. Refresh after a vulnerability, release, support-window, or supplier change.

### 3. Label interaction and annual assurance package

- **Request and owner:** Program, legal, quality, product, and independent-review owners provide authorized test/assessment references, submission or correspondence logs, label-use approvals, marketing-review records, exceptions, corrective actions, and annual reviewer workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one proposed label or public product statement to authorized program evidence, product/version boundary, human approval, stated limitation, and follow-up action. This cannot authorize use of a label, make a certification claim, or transmit a submission.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare an evidence index and flag stale program, marketing, or reviewer records. Authorized humans approve statements, submissions, exceptions, and closure. Recheck before any label/public statement and during the annual independent review.
