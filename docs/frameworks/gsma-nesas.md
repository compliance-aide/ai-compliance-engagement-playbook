# GSMA NESAS — telecom assurance guide

> Original operational guidance, not vendor or product certification. Consult the [GSMA NESAS overview](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/technologies/security/network-equipment%20-security-assurance-scheme/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain vendor, product-release, market, lifecycle-security, process-audit, evaluation, change, remediation, and verified-result records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized laboratories perform evaluation; operators preserve evidence; independent reviewers test readiness. AI traces evidence, but cannot assert accreditation, certification, or conformance. Review releases and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [GSMA NESAS overview](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/technologies/security/network-equipment%20-security-assurance-scheme/), applicable market requirements, and approved supplier records; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not reproduce scheme requirements, perform laboratory evaluation, designate an evaluator, or claim product certification or conformance.

### 1. Product, market, and assurance-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Product security, regulatory, procurement, and release owners provide equipment/product-release inventory, target markets, version boundaries, supplier identities, lifecycle status, scheme applicability analysis, and accountable approvals.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected product release to its version identifier, intended market, supplier, assurance-boundary decision, evidence owner, and review date. This supports scoped readiness; it cannot determine scheme applicability or certify a product.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories and flag unowned releases, market changes, or stale scope records. Authorized humans decide applicability and release commitments. Recollect before a new release, market entry, supplier change, or lifecycle transition.

### 2. Supplier process and evaluation-readiness package

- **Request and owner:** Supplier assurance, engineering, secure-development, and laboratory-coordination owners provide supplier attestations, development-process artifacts, vulnerability/remediation records, evaluation-readiness plans, evidence handoffs, and exception decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an approved evidence handoff to its release, supplier owner, permitted artifact location, evaluation-readiness milestone, limitation, and corrective action. This does not evaluate a vendor process or substitute for an authorized laboratory.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may build an evidence index, identify missing handoff metadata, and surface aging remediation; humans authorize sharing, evaluation engagement, exceptions, and remediation closure. Refresh after a material vulnerability, process change, supplier substitution, or evaluation observation.

### 3. Release governance and improvement packet

- **Request and owner:** Product leadership, security governance, quality, and independent-review owners provide release decisions, security signoffs, change records, customer/market communications approvals, audit observations, remediation tracking, and management review minutes.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected release or finding to its authority, evidence version, decision date, owner, follow-up action, and retest. It cannot make an accreditation or certification statement.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize approved status and flag unresolved dependencies; humans approve release, external communications, risk acceptance, and closure. Review per release and at least annually.
