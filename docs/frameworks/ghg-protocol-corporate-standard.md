# GHG Protocol Corporate Standard — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not GHG Protocol content or assurance. Confirm methodology status at the [GHG Protocol Corporate Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard?page=1).

## Engagement focus

Maintain reporting entity, boundary, source-system, method, factor, estimate, base-year, activity-record, calculation, and report-evidence registers.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Data owners certify sources; methodology owners approve treatments; independent reviewers test evidence. AI assists reconciliation and drafts questions, but cannot choose accounting treatments, validate emissions, make public claims, or sign assurance.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [GHG Protocol Corporate Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard?page=1) and current methodology material; checked 2026-07-31. This plan requests evidence records, not a substitute for methodology, verification, or assurance. Methodology and reporting owners approve organizational boundary, accounting treatments, factors, estimates, and public claims.

### 1. Reporting boundary and methodology-decision record

- **Request and owner:** Reporting entity/organizational boundary choice, consolidation approach, reporting period, base-year decision, source-category register, methodology/factor version log, and approval record from sustainability and finance owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected source category to its boundary decision, methodology/factor version, accountable owner, and stated estimate treatment. This can support reproducible reporting inputs; it cannot validate accounting choices, completeness, or emissions results.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved records and identify version conflicts. Humans approve methodology and boundary decisions. Refresh for structural changes, base-year revisions, method/factor change, or reporting-cycle start.

### 2. Activity-data, calculation, and reconciliation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Activity-data extracts, source-system provenance, calculation workpapers, estimates/assumptions, unit conversions, quality checks, and reconciliations from operational data and finance owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Reperform an approved calculation selection or trace it to raw authorized source, period, factor, assumption, and review note. This can support a transparent calculation trail; it cannot provide assurance, eliminate estimation uncertainty, or prove all source data are accurate.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assist reconciliation and flag anomalies but cannot select treatments or publish numbers. Recollect for data correction, acquisition/divestiture, factor change, or reported-period close.

### 3. Disclosure, uncertainty, and review record

- **Request and owner:** Report draft/version, uncertainty and limitation disclosure, internal review/approval, external-assurance inputs where applicable, issue log, and change history from reporting owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected disclosed figure or caveat to its calculation/source and human approval. This can support disclosed provenance; it cannot validate emissions, issue assurance, or authorize a public claim.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a source-linked review packet. Humans approve publication, assurance engagement, corrections, and claims. Revisit before publication and annually.
