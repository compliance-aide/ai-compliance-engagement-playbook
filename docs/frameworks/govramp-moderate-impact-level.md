# GovRAMP Moderate impact-level engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not GovRAMP program material, an impact-level decision, an authorization decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current requirements through the [GovRAMP authorization process](https://www.stateramp.org/providers/authorized/) and applicable government partner direction.

## Engagement focus

Use this guide only after prospective government partners and accountable organization leaders document the required GovRAMP Moderate impact level for the offering. Maintain an offering-specific record of service boundaries, data and service classification inputs, architecture, interconnections, shared and inherited services, personnel and supplier dependencies, assessment scope, evidence owners, remediation, continuous-monitoring records, and customer-facing representations. Keep impact-level selection, authorization status, and government procurement outcomes as separate accountable decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable offering, security, engineering, operations, privacy, supplier-management, customer-assurance, incident-response, and executive owners. Operators preserve scope, inventory, architecture, access, change, assessment, incident, supplier, remediation, and monitoring evidence throughout the year. Review offering boundaries, classification inputs, government-partner requirements, interconnections, assessment status, customer representations, and open findings quarterly and after material changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples the impact-level record and operating evidence; auditors test the evidence trail without selecting the impact level, granting authorization, accepting risk, or making procurement decisions.

AI may organize supplied evidence, reconcile offering and dependency records, identify gaps or conflicts, summarize remediation status, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot select an impact level, determine authorization readiness, make an authorization or procurement decision, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
