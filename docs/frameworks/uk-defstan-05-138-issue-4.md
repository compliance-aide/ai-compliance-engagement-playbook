# UK Def Stan 05-138 Issue 4 defence-supplier engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Ministry of Defence standard text, Cyber Security Model content, questionnaire content, a certification, or a compliance claim. Confirm the current standard through the [UK MOD Def Stan 05-138 Issue 4 publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cyber-security-for-defence-suppliers-def-stan-05-138-issue-4).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a contract-aware supplier cybersecurity record: relevant contracts and subcontractors, authority-provided scope and risk-profile decisions, information and service boundaries, supplier flowdown evidence, security operating records, assurance-readiness evidence, remediation, approved improvement plans, and contract or material-change events. Keep customer or MOD determinations, certification, and submission actions separate from internal preparation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Contract and security owners maintain evidence; subcontract-management owners preserve flowdown and supplier records; legal and security leadership interpret contract obligations; independent reviewers test traceability; MOD and other authorized assessors retain independent decision rights. AI may organize approved evidence, identify gaps, and draft nonbinding questions, but cannot determine a contract risk profile, complete or submit a Supplier Assurance Questionnaire, certify a supplier, accept contractual risk, approve a Cyber Improvement Plan, or claim compliance. Review at contract award, renewal, subcontract, material service, incident, and annual engagement checkpoints.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [UK MOD Def Stan 05-138 Issue 4 publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cyber-security-for-defence-suppliers-def-stan-05-138-issue-4); checked 2026-07-31. This plan is original operational planning, not Def Stan text, Cyber Security Model or questionnaire content, contract advice, a defence suitability determination, or a compliance claim. Do not copy protected standard or contract material. Keep classified, export-controlled, OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE, contract-restricted, vulnerability, credential, and supplier evidence in designated protected environments; retain permitted references and handling labels only.

### 1. Defence-contract applicability and protected-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Approved contract/requirement references, in-scope entity/site/system boundaries, information/asset classifications, programme ownership, subcontractor/dependency records, handling constraints, and applicability decisions from contract, security, programme, legal, export-control, and information-assurance owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one programme/system boundary to authorized requirement references, accountable owners, classification/handling constraints, supplier dependencies, and review date. This cannot interpret a contract, determine classification, or decide applicability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized metadata and identify missing owners, handling labels, or review dates. Humans decide scope, classification, contract responses, and risk treatment. Refresh after contract, programme, system, supplier, data-handling, or MOD requirement changes.

### 2. Protective security operation and incident package

- **Request and owner:** Authorized access/configuration/change records, vulnerability/patch records, monitoring outputs, incident records, recovery/test evidence, and remediation/retest records from system, security, operations, and programme owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Follow one security change, vulnerability, or incident from protected source reference through authorized triage, accountable action, corrective work, and retest. This cannot prove system security, authorize operations, or establish reporting obligations.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a read-only index and flag missing timing, owners, or retests without accessing material outside authorization. Humans validate findings, authorize changes, declare/escalate incidents, and close remediation. Recollect after a material vulnerability, incident, failed test, or protected-system change.

### 3. Supply-chain assurance, exception, and independent-challenge package

- **Request and owner:** Supplier assurance/change notices, subcontractor flow-down evidence, approved exception/risk records, assessment observations, remediation plans, management decisions, closure/retest evidence, and independent-review workpapers from procurement, programme, security, risk, and audit owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one supplier concern, exception, or observation to its protected evidence reference, accountable decision-maker, mitigation, expiry, and independent challenge/retest. This cannot approve a supplier, accept defence risk, close an exception, or replace formal assessment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag overdue reviews and draft factual questions from authorized metadata. Humans approve supplier acceptance, exceptions, resources, closure, and MOD-facing statements. Review routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness challenge.
