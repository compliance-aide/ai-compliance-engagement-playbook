# UK Def Stan 05-138 Issue 4 defence-supplier engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Ministry of Defence standard text, Cyber Security Model content, questionnaire content, a certification, or a compliance claim. Confirm the current standard through the [UK MOD Def Stan 05-138 Issue 4 publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cyber-security-for-defence-suppliers-def-stan-05-138-issue-4).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a contract-aware supplier cybersecurity record: relevant contracts and subcontractors, authority-provided scope and risk-profile decisions, information and service boundaries, supplier flowdown evidence, security operating records, assurance-readiness evidence, remediation, approved improvement plans, and contract or material-change events. Keep customer or MOD determinations, certification, and submission actions separate from internal preparation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Contract and security owners maintain evidence; subcontract-management owners preserve flowdown and supplier records; legal and security leadership interpret contract obligations; independent reviewers test traceability; MOD and other authorized assessors retain independent decision rights. AI may organize approved evidence, identify gaps, and draft nonbinding questions, but cannot determine a contract risk profile, complete or submit a Supplier Assurance Questionnaire, certify a supplier, accept contractual risk, approve a Cyber Improvement Plan, or claim compliance. Review at contract award, renewal, subcontract, material service, incident, and annual engagement checkpoints.
