# TSA rail cybersecurity directive engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not TSA directive text, a determination of coverage, or an operational-security assessment. Confirm current applicability and directive version through the [TSA rail cybersecurity directive notice](https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/tsa-security-directive-1580_82-2022-01c-and-memo-508c.pdf).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an owner-approved record of covered rail operations, critical cyber-system scope, plans and exercises, evidence of operational safeguards, material changes, incidents, testing, remedial actions, and official communications.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Rail owner/operators and accountable security leaders determine coverage and operational priorities; safety and operations teams retain evidence; independent reviewers test documentation and exercise traceability. AI organizes approved records and prepares non-sensitive questions, but cannot determine directive applicability, approve an operational plan, perform safety actions, or submit material to TSA. Review after material system or threat changes and retain human decisions for every external representation.
