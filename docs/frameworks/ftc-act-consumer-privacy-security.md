# FTC Act consumer privacy and security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not FTC Act text, legal advice, an enforcement prediction, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [FTC Act record](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/federal-trade-commission-act), the FTC’s [privacy and security materials](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security), and qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain an evidence-backed record connecting consumer-facing statements and material data practices to accountable owners, product and service behavior, privacy and security representations, supporting operating evidence, customer-facing changes, incidents, complaints, exceptions, and remediation. Treat the engagement as a consistency and evidence-management activity; it does not determine whether an act or practice is lawful, unfair, deceptive, or subject to enforcement.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, legal, privacy, security, product, marketing, customer support, data, risk, and incident-response owners retain accountable decisions. Legal counsel determines applicable obligations and interpretation; product and marketing owners approve representations; security and data owners preserve operational evidence; and incident authorities decide escalation and communications. Independent reviewers sample material public statements and associated operating evidence, challenge stale or unsupported claims, trace change approvals, and escalate uncertainty rather than reach legal conclusions.

AI may inventory authorized statements and evidence, flag mismatches or aging reviews, draft questions, prepare change-impact workpapers, and organize complaint or incident metadata. AI cannot interpret the law, decide whether conduct is unfair or deceptive, approve public claims, communicate with regulators or consumers, make notification decisions, alter production systems, accept risk, or claim compliance. Reassess before material representation, product, data-practice, incident, complaint, supplier, or legal changes; perform quarterly reviews and an annual independent readiness review.
