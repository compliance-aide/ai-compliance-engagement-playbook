# FTC Act consumer privacy and security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not FTC Act text, legal advice, an enforcement prediction, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [FTC Act record](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/federal-trade-commission-act), the FTC’s [privacy and security materials](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security), and qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain an evidence-backed record connecting consumer-facing statements and material data practices to accountable owners, product and service behavior, privacy and security representations, supporting operating evidence, customer-facing changes, incidents, complaints, exceptions, and remediation. Treat the engagement as a consistency and evidence-management activity; it does not determine whether an act or practice is lawful, unfair, deceptive, or subject to enforcement.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, legal, privacy, security, product, marketing, customer support, data, risk, and incident-response owners retain accountable decisions. Legal counsel determines applicable obligations and interpretation; product and marketing owners approve representations; security and data owners preserve operational evidence; and incident authorities decide escalation and communications. Independent reviewers sample material public statements and associated operating evidence, challenge stale or unsupported claims, trace change approvals, and escalate uncertainty rather than reach legal conclusions.

AI may inventory authorized statements and evidence, flag mismatches or aging reviews, draft questions, prepare change-impact workpapers, and organize complaint or incident metadata. AI cannot interpret the law, decide whether conduct is unfair or deceptive, approve public claims, communicate with regulators or consumers, make notification decisions, alter production systems, accept risk, or claim compliance. Reassess before material representation, product, data-practice, incident, complaint, supplier, or legal changes; perform quarterly reviews and an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Consult the [FTC Act record](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/federal-trade-commission-act) and official [FTC privacy and security business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security), along with qualified counsel; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not supply legal interpretation, predict enforcement, or determine whether a practice is unfair or deceptive.

### 1. Consumer representation and data-practice inventory package

- **Request and owner:** Product, marketing, privacy, and legal owners provide an approved inventory of consumer-facing statements, notices, consent journeys where applicable, material data uses/sharing, product flows, and owner-approved change history.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected public statement to its approved source, the described product/data behavior, review authority, and evidence reference. This supports evidence-backed consistency review; it cannot determine truthfulness in law, consumer understanding, or legal sufficiency.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare authorized statements with supplied product/data records and flag discrepancies for human review. Legal and product owners decide wording and representations. Refresh before a material claim, notice, product, data-use, or partner change.

### 2. Security and service-provider practice package

- **Request and owner:** Security, engineering, privacy, and vendor owners provide the scoped data/system inventory, risk and change records, selected security-operation evidence, provider responsibility records, and exception decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect evidence provenance and trace a selected data flow or provider relationship to an owner, risk/change record, and follow-up. This supports scoped diligence; it cannot prove security, determine reasonable practice, or establish provider performance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized evidence and surface stale or unowned records. Humans choose safeguards, approve vendors, and accept risk. Refresh after an incident, material vulnerability, supplier change, or meaningful data-practice change.

### 3. Complaint, incident, and corrective-action package

- **Request and owner:** Customer-support, incident, legal, privacy, and executive owners provide complaint themes, incident timelines, decisions, communications approvals, corrective-action plans, and management-review records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected complaint or incident from intake through triage, authority, decision, action owner, and follow-up. This supports accountable response evidence; it cannot determine a notification duty, legal exposure, consumer harm, or closure adequacy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may cluster authorized metadata and prepare a redacted review packet; it cannot contact consumers or regulators or decide communications. Authorized humans decide escalation, notifications, remedies, and closure. Refresh on a material complaint trend, incident, regulator contact, or annual review.
