# ISO 9001:2015 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Check the [ISO current record](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html) and pending-version status.

## Engagement focus

Maintain customer feedback, process performance, nonconformity, improvement, supplier, audit, and management-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management owns quality decisions; independent auditors verify primary evidence and AI provenance. AI flags process drift and drafts analysis, but cannot close findings or certify. Review monthly, quarterly, and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use an authorized copy of ISO 9001:2015 and the [ISO current record](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO content is licensed. This plan is original operational guidance, does not reproduce standard text, and does not make a certification or conformity claim.

### 1. Process, customer, and quality-objective record

- **Request and owner:** Process map and owners, customer/contract requirement inputs, quality objectives and measures, scope/exclusion rationale, process interactions, and approved change records from quality and business-process leaders.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected customer requirement or objective to its process owner, performance measure, source record, and change decision. This can support a transparent QMS planning view; it cannot determine certification scope or prove every requirement is understood.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved process metadata and flag unowned measures or stale mappings. Management approves QMS scope, objectives, and changes. Refresh after a customer, product/service, process, organizational, or supplier change.

### 2. Operational-control, competence, and supplier evidence

- **Request and owner:** Controlled procedures/records, operational performance data, competence/training records, supplier evaluation and performance evidence, nonconforming-output handling records, and document-control history from process and quality owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a human-approved selection for version, responsible performer, time window, supplier/process link, result, and exception. This can support that selected operational controls have retrievable records; it cannot prove consistent product/service quality or full conformity.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index evidence and identify expired approvals, missing records, or performance drift; it may not change controlled documents, qualify suppliers, or release outputs. Recollect after material supplier/process changes, performance deterioration, or periodic review.

### 3. Audit, corrective-action, and management-review record

- **Request and owner:** Internal-audit plan/results, customer-feedback and performance analysis, nonconformity/corrective-action records, effectiveness checks, management-review decisions, and improvement actions from quality leadership.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected finding or improvement action from evidence through root cause, owner, correction, effectiveness check, and management decision. This can support disciplined improvement governance; it cannot close a finding, accept residual risk, or certify the QMS.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft source-linked trend analysis and surface overdue actions. Humans approve findings, corrective-action closure, resources, and external claims. Review monthly for operations, internally on the approved audit schedule, and annually in management review.
