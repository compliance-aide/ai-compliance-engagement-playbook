# NIST SP 800-157 derived-PIV-credential engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST credential content, identity-proofing instructions, credential issuance authority, a cryptographic approval, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-157](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/157/final) and the organization’s approved identity, security, privacy, legal, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed lifecycle for approved derived PIV credentials and their relationship to authoritative PIV credentials, credential issuers, devices or token form factors, relying systems, and access use cases. Maintain traceability from eligibility and sponsorship through proof of control, issuance, activation, use, lifecycle events, suspension or revocation, recovery, and records retention. An issuer record, credential status, successful authentication, or device report is evidence to assess; it does not prove identity proofing, credential validity, correct key custody, appropriate access, or compliance.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, identity and access management, security, PIV issuer liaison, credential-management, endpoint or device management, privacy, legal, relying-system owners, procurement, supplier-management, incident-response, change-management, and records-management roles. Operators maintain in-scope population and credential records, source-PIV relationship evidence, eligibility and proof-of-control records, issuer and relying-system approvals, credential lifecycle and device inventory, authentication and anomaly records, revocation and recovery evidence, exceptions, and supplier assurance. Reconcile active credential populations, source relationships, devices, and relying systems quarterly; review lifecycle events, lost or compromised credential handling, privileged access, exceptions, issuer changes, and unresolved findings at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material identity, device, system, issuer, or policy changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples source-PIV-to-derived-credential-to-access traceability; auditors test evidence without proofing an identity, issuing or revoking credentials, accessing private keys, granting access, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied lifecycle, relationship, device, access, and review evidence, identify missing ownership or stale reviews, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot proof an identity, issue, activate, use, suspend, revoke, or recover a credential, access private keys, grant access, make personnel decisions, approve an exception, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
