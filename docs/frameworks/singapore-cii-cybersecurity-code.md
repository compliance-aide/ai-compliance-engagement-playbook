# Singapore CII cybersecurity code — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Singapore legal advice or a regulatory representation. Confirm the current code and notices through the [Cyber Security Agency of Singapore](https://www.csa.gov.sg/legislation/codes-of-practice/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain critical-infrastructure scope, owner and authority records, risk assessments, security operations evidence, incident decision records, audit materials, suppliers, exercises, corrective actions, and regulator-interaction history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Designated infrastructure owners and legal authorities make compliance and reporting decisions; qualified auditors reach conclusions independently. AI coordinates evidence and flags missing records, but cannot determine CII status, report an incident, or make a representation. Exercise and review annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
