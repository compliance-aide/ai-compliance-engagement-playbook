# UK NCSC Active Cyber Defence engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCSC service instructions, an eligibility decision, a command to alter a protective service, a certification, or a compliance claim. Confirm current availability, eligibility, and service guidance through [NCSC Active Cyber Defence](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/section/active-cyber-defence/introduction) and the organization’s approved security, legal, privacy, procurement, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed adoption and assurance cycle for suitable Active Cyber Defence services that helps reduce exposure to common, high-volume cyber harm. Keep service scope, eligibility, organizational assets, accountable owners, onboarding records, alerts, outcomes, exceptions, service changes, supplier dependencies, and remediation decisions traceable. Treat a service notification or a successful check as useful operational evidence, not proof that all risk is managed, that a service is applicable, or that any organization is compliant.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, security, technology, network or domain, messaging, vulnerability-management, incident-response, privacy, legal, procurement, supplier-management, service-owner, and records-management roles. Operators maintain an approved inventory of eligible services and covered assets, enrollment and access records, alert-routing and escalation procedures, investigation and remediation records, exception decisions, change approvals, service-status evidence, and metric definitions. Reconcile asset and service coverage quarterly; review alerts, unresolved remediations, eligibility changes, and operating procedures at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material service, supplier, domain, network, or threat changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples coverage, alert handling, changes, and closure evidence; auditors test the evidence trail without enrolling the organization, changing DNS, email, web, endpoint, or network protections, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied service and remediation evidence, compare approved asset inventories with documented coverage, identify missing owners or overdue follow-up, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot determine eligibility, enroll or operate a service, alter protective controls, approve remediation or exceptions, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
