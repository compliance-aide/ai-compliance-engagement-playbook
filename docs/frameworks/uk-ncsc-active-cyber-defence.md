# UK NCSC Active Cyber Defence engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCSC service instructions, an eligibility decision, a command to alter a protective service, a certification, or a compliance claim. Confirm current availability, eligibility, and service guidance through [NCSC Active Cyber Defence](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/section/active-cyber-defence/introduction) and the organization’s approved security, legal, privacy, procurement, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed adoption and assurance cycle for suitable Active Cyber Defence services that helps reduce exposure to common, high-volume cyber harm. Keep service scope, eligibility, organizational assets, accountable owners, onboarding records, alerts, outcomes, exceptions, service changes, supplier dependencies, and remediation decisions traceable. Treat a service notification or a successful check as useful operational evidence, not proof that all risk is managed, that a service is applicable, or that any organization is compliant.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, security, technology, network or domain, messaging, vulnerability-management, incident-response, privacy, legal, procurement, supplier-management, service-owner, and records-management roles. Operators maintain an approved inventory of eligible services and covered assets, enrollment and access records, alert-routing and escalation procedures, investigation and remediation records, exception decisions, change approvals, service-status evidence, and metric definitions. Reconcile asset and service coverage quarterly; review alerts, unresolved remediations, eligibility changes, and operating procedures at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material service, supplier, domain, network, or threat changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples coverage, alert handling, changes, and closure evidence; auditors test the evidence trail without enrolling the organization, changing DNS, email, web, endpoint, or network protections, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied service and remediation evidence, compare approved asset inventories with documented coverage, identify missing owners or overdue follow-up, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot determine eligibility, enroll or operate a service, alter protective controls, approve remediation or exceptions, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** At engagement start, record retrieval date, service-status check, eligibility assumptions, and applicable use terms for the official [NCSC Active Cyber Defence introduction](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/section/active-cyber-defence/introduction). Source status is current when this official material is available. This is original planning guidance; a named human confirms eligibility, authority, and lawful operational scope.

### 1. Eligibility, asset-scope, and service-enrolment package

- **Request and owner:** Security, domain/network, messaging, asset, service, privacy, legal, and records owners provide approved eligibility decision, service/asset inventory, domain and address ownership evidence, accountable contacts, enrollment records, and exclusions or exceptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected covered asset or service from authoritative inventory through eligibility decision, named owner, and documented enrollment or stated exclusion. This can test scope traceability; it cannot determine eligibility, prove service coverage is complete, or authorize enrollment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied inventories and highlight unmatched owners or dates. Humans validate ownership, decide eligibility and enrollment, and approve exclusions. Refresh after asset, domain, network, contact, organizational, or service change.

### 2. Alert, protective-action, and remediation package

- **Request and owner:** Security operations, network, messaging, vulnerability, service, and incident owners provide alert-routing records, selected service notifications, triage tickets, investigation notes, remediation/change approvals, closure validation, and aging metrics for the review period.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a sampled notification or service finding from receipt to accountable triage, approved remediation or documented exception, and closure check using read-only records. This can support an observation about handling of that sample; it cannot confirm a notification's accuracy, establish compromise, or prove all alerts were handled correctly.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize case evidence and flag overdue follow-up. Humans decide severity, authorize protective changes, direct response, and accept exceptions. Recollect after a material alert, remediation failure, recurring pattern, or service change.

### 3. Service-change, supplier, and management-assurance package

- **Request and owner:** Service, procurement, supplier, security, privacy, legal, risk, and executive owners provide service-status notices, integration/dependency records, supplier responsibilities, change-impact assessments, exception register, outcome measures, and annual management-review evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a material service or supplier change from notice through impact assessment, owner decision, updated procedure or scope record, and follow-up metric. This can assess change governance evidence; it cannot guarantee service availability, validate supplier performance, or establish compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize changes and flag missing impact or review records. Humans approve service strategy, supplier decisions, risk treatment, and communications. Review quarterly and after eligibility, supplier, operational, or threat changes.
