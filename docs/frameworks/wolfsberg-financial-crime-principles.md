# Wolfsberg financial-crime principles — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Wolfsberg content, law, or endorsement. Check the [Wolfsberg resource library](https://wolfsberg-group.org/resources).

## Engagement focus

Define voluntary use case and local-law boundary; maintain governance, data, monitoring, investigation, third-party, outcome, automation, override, and remediation evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans make alert, risk, customer, and regulatory decisions; independent audit challenges effectiveness. AI supports evidence and planning, but cannot autonomously disposition alerts or assert compliance. Review quarterly and annually.
