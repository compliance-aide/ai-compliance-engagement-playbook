# ISO/IEC 27039:2015 intrusion detection and prevention engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a security-event determination, a response authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27039:2015 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/56889.html).

## Engagement focus

Run a year-round assurance record for intrusion detection and prevention capability. Maintain a service-and-environment inventory that connects monitoring objectives, protected assets, data sources, coverage assumptions, detection logic ownership, alert-routing paths, tuning decisions, test cases, operational exceptions, incident handoffs, retention, and review results. Distinguish documented coverage from observed effectiveness; a telemetry feed or an alert volume alone is not evidence that relevant threats are detected or safely handled.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Security operations, platform, network, endpoint, cloud, application, privacy, legal, risk, and service owners retain decisions in their authority. System owners confirm protected scope and dependencies; detection owners maintain documented logic and tuning rationale; response authorities approve containment or disruptive actions; and incident leaders decide escalations. Independent reviewers sample the record from declared objective through source availability, detection behavior, alert handling, tests, exceptions, and closure, then challenge blind spots and unsubstantiated effectiveness claims.

AI may correlate authorized alerts with asset and change records, identify stale tuning reviews, draft evidence requests, summarize analyst notes, and prepare non-authoritative test workpapers. AI cannot enable blocking, alter detection rules, suppress alerts, determine that an event is benign or malicious, initiate containment, access systems beyond assigned permissions, make a notification decision, or claim compliance. Keep humans accountable for triage and response. Review source coverage and material changes continuously, perform quarterly detection and exception reviews, conduct scheduled exercises, and complete an annual independent readiness review.
