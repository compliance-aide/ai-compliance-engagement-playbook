# ISO/IEC 27007:2020 ISMS audit programme — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or an audit opinion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/77802.html), including its revision status; do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain audit universe, risk-based plan, competence and independence records, scope decisions, sampling rationale, working papers, findings, corrective actions, quality review, and follow-up evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Audit leadership assigns qualified people and protects independence; independent reviewers reach conclusions themselves. AI may create traceability and prompt for gaps, but cannot select conclusions, issue findings, approve an audit plan, or alter evidence. Plan annually, perform throughout the year, and evaluate the programme annually.
