# ISO/IEC 27400:2022 IoT security and privacy engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a device-security determination, a privacy conclusion, a product approval, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27400:2022 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/44373.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an IoT solution engagement record that connects devices, services, interfaces, data flows, accountable product and service owners, security and privacy evidence, supplier dependencies, lifecycle changes, exceptions, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Use AI only to organize authorized records and prepare questions; accountable people decide design, deployment, privacy, and risk outcomes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product, engineering, security, privacy, operations, legal, risk, procurement, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; device and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test asset and data-flow traceability, lifecycle readiness, privacy boundaries, and exception handling. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify missing lifecycle records, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot approve a device or service, determine security or privacy sufficiency, make data-use decisions, accept risk, alter production configurations, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review continuously as devices and services change, conduct quarterly operating reviews, and reassess annually and before material supplier, interface, data, incident, privacy, or architecture changes.
