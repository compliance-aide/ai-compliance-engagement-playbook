# ISO 37002:2021 whistleblowing management — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content, legal advice, or a case conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/65035.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain reporting channels, protected-access boundaries, intake records, triage authority, impartiality safeguards, investigation handoffs, retaliation-risk handling, case closure, and program-improvement evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized ethics, legal, and investigation leaders decide case handling; independent reviewers assess programme evidence. AI manages non-sensitive case metadata and identifies missing records, but cannot investigate allegations, identify reporters, or decide outcomes. Test channels quarterly and review annually.
