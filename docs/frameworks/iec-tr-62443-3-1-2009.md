# IEC TR 62443-3-1:2009 IACS technology-assessment engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a technology recommendation, a safety decision, a product selection, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC TR 62443-3-1:2009 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/7031).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a technology-assessment record that connects industrial system context, accountable owners, operational and safety constraints, candidate technology categories, approved evaluation evidence, supplier representations, deployment assumptions, exception decisions, remediation, and independent review. The engagement record should support careful analysis of industrial cybersecurity technology without letting an AI-generated comparison become a procurement, engineering, or assurance conclusion.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, maintenance, security, procurement, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; system and evidence owners preserve authorized evaluation records; independent reviewers test traceability, conflict handling, and safety-aware escalation. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify unresolved evaluation questions, draft comparison criteria, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot recommend or select a product, determine suitability, approve an architecture, decide safety or operational impacts, accept risk, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Revisit technology assumptions annually and before material system, supplier, vulnerability, safety, incident, lifecycle, or architecture changes.
