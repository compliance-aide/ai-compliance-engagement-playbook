# TX-RAMP Level 1 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Texas DIR criteria, a certification claim, or an authorization decision. Confirm current scope and tier requirements through [TX-RAMP eligibility and requirements](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/texas-risk-and-authorization-management-program-tx-ramp/tx-ramp-eligibility).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a cloud-service scope record, agency and provider responsibilities, data classification decisions, evidence, material changes, assessment materials, conditions, and continuous-monitoring history appropriate to the approved tier.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Agency and provider owners determine scope; operating teams maintain evidence; independent reviewers test traceability; Texas DIR controls the certification process. AI prepares read-only evidence maps and gap questions, but cannot select a tier, certify a service, or submit a request. Reassess scope at data, service, or architecture change and retain human approvals.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [TX-RAMP eligibility and requirements page](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/texas-risk-and-authorization-management-program-tx-ramp/tx-ramp-eligibility), checked 2026-07-31, together with agency-approved scope and authorization records. This is original readiness planning, not DIR criteria, an assessment result, or a certification decision. Agency and provider officials retain all tier, authorization, and submission decisions.

### 1. Service and agency-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Provider product, security, privacy, and service owners, with the agency sponsor, supply the proposed service boundary, tenant/environment inventory, data-classification references, customer responsibility allocation, subservice dependencies, named custodians, and approved exclusions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one in-scope service component from its agency purpose through owner, data category, environment, and responsibility record. This supports evidence readiness; it cannot determine TX-RAMP eligibility, the proper tier, or the sufficiency of a boundary.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may map supplied metadata and flag a missing owner, environment, or responsibility. Named agency and provider officials decide scope. Refresh for a customer, data, hosting, architecture, or dependency change and at annual planning.

### 2. Level-1 operating-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Service operations, security, engineering, and supplier owners provide approved policy/process references, access and change-management records, vulnerability and incident workflow records, training/ownership evidence, supplier commitments, exception log, and remediation tracker for the selected period.
- **Validate and limit:** For a small, pre-approved sample, trace a request, change, security event, or exception to its accountable owner, time-stamped record, review/approval, and corrective action. This checks record linkage, not technical effectiveness, control conformance, or authorization readiness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index supplied non-sensitive evidence and identify missing links or stale records. Humans authorize sampling, determine exceptions, approve remediation, and make risk decisions. Refresh after an incident, material finding, process change, or supplier change.

### 3. Assessment-readiness and change package

- **Request and owner:** Agency liaison, provider security lead, legal, records, and executive owners provide the authorized engagement timeline, evidence register, source-version log, material-change record, condition/action tracker, retention constraints, and external-communication approval record.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one proposed evidence package or material change from request through custodian, provenance, authorized review, follow-up owner, and retention boundary. This supports preparation only; it cannot submit materials, represent the agency, or certify the service.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft an internal readiness queue and flag unresolved dependencies. Human agency/provider authorities approve all external communications and submissions. Refresh after DIR source changes, assessment scheduling, material changes, or annual renewal.
