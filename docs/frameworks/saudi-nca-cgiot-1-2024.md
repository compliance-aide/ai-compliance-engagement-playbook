# Saudi NCA Cybersecurity Guidelines for Internet of Things engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCA guideline text, a product-safety determination, a regulatory interpretation, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority Internet of Things cybersecurity guidelines](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/guidelines-list/cglot/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an IoT cybersecurity engagement record that connects device and service inventory, intended operating context, accountable product and service owners, supplier and software dependencies, authorized evidence, security events, remediation, and independently reviewable assurance. Keep safety, product design, and regulatory decisions with accountable human owners rather than converting collected evidence into an automated approval.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, product, engineering, security, privacy, safety, and legal authorities retain accountable decisions; device, platform, and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify stale evidence, surface dependency changes, and draft review questions, but cannot make product-safety or regulatory determinations, approve a product release or supplier, accept risk, alter production services, issue an assessment, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material device, software, data, connectivity, supplier, safety, incident, or regulatory changes.
