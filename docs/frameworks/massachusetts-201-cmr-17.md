# Massachusetts 201 CMR 17.00 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Massachusetts legal advice or a regulatory conclusion. Confirm current text through the [Massachusetts regulation record](https://www.mass.gov/regulations/201-CMR-1700-standards-for-the-protection-of-personal-information-of-residents-of-the-commonwealth).

## Engagement focus

Maintain personal-information scope, written-security-program evidence, asset and access records, vendor safeguards, workforce training, incident records, encryption and lifecycle decisions, exceptions, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal, privacy, security, and accountable business owners make applicability and incident decisions; independent reviewers test evidence. AI inventories approved artifacts and identifies gaps, but cannot make legal determinations, approve safeguards, or notify authorities. Review quarterly and annually.
