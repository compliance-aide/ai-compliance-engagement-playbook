# ISO/IEC 27014:2020 information-security governance — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a governance determination. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/74046.html) for current revision status; do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain governing-body direction, security objectives, decision authorities, reporting, investment and risk decisions, performance signals, escalation records, and review outcomes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

The governing body and management retain responsibility for direction and oversight; independent reviewers test the decision trail. AI synthesizes approved reporting and highlights unanswered questions, but cannot set strategy or accept risk. Report quarterly and review governance annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
