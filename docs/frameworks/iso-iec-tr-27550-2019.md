# ISO/IEC TR 27550:2019 privacy-engineering engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a privacy impact determination, a system-design approval, a legal conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC TR 27550:2019 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72024.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a privacy-engineering record that connects system lifecycle stages, accountable product and privacy owners, data and purpose assumptions, engineering and risk evidence, design decisions, exceptions, change history, incidents, remediation, and independent review. AI may help organize authorized records and questions, but privacy judgments and system decisions stay with accountable people.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product, engineering, privacy, security, legal, operations, risk, and business owners retain accountable decisions; lifecycle and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test data and purpose traceability, design-decision evidence, exception treatment, and escalation paths. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify unresolved questions, draft workpapers, and prepare non-authoritative status views, but cannot determine lawful processing, approve a privacy design, decide necessity or proportionality, accept risk, alter production systems, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review at design gates and material changes, conduct quarterly evidence reviews, and complete an annual privacy-engineering readiness review.
