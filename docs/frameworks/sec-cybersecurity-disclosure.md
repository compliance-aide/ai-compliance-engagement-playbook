# SEC cybersecurity disclosure readiness — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a disclosure decision. See the [SEC final-rule resource](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2023/07/s7-09-22).

## Engagement focus

Confirm filing applicability with authorized counsel, then maintain a board-owned disclosure operating model. Preserve incident decision timelines, materiality-analysis inputs, governance evidence, draft-review approvals, and annual refresh decisions. Test escalation timing and consistency between operations and drafts.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management and counsel own materiality and filing decisions. Independent reviewers test traceability, timing, and controls over disclosure preparation. AI can reconcile records and flag inconsistent drafts; it cannot decide materiality, provide legal advice, sign or submit filings, or communicate external conclusions.
