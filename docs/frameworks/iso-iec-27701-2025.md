# ISO/IEC 27701:2025 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for privacy management, not legal advice or an ISO claim. Use
> authorized material and check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/27701).

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing inventory, declared roles, jurisdictions, ownership, and
decision rights. Gate new/changed processing, vendors, products, data categories,
retention, sharing, transfers, and incidents. Reconcile inventory, notices,
contracts, operations, risk decisions, and action progress on a defined cadence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own processing reality and remediation. Independent reviewers test
consistency and evidence without replacing counsel. AI tracks evidence provenance,
aging, and conflicts and drafts clear questions; it cannot make legal determinations
or authorize processing. Annually review scope, risk, governance, and roadmap.
