# IEC PAS 62443-2-2:2025 IACS security protection scheme engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, an industrial safety decision, a security-protection determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC PAS 62443-2-2:2025 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/63886).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an industrial security protection-scheme record that connects protected IACS scope, accountable asset-owner and operations roles, safety and process constraints, technical, physical, and process measures, evidence, supplier dependencies, validation, maintenance, remediation, and independent review. Preserve the distinction between AI-assisted documentation support and human accountability for safety, security design, operational continuity, and acceptable risk.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, maintenance, security, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; asset and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and safety-aware readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify evidence gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot decide scheme scope, validate a protection outcome, determine safety or risk acceptability, approve a supplier or maintenance change, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material system, protection measure, process, supplier, safety, incident, maintenance, or regulatory changes.
