# IEC PAS 62443-2-2:2025 IACS security protection scheme engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, validation of protection or acceptance of industrial risk.

## Source and applicability

The [IEC publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/63886), checked 2026-09-04, describes guidance for developing, validating, operating and maintaining a Security Protection Scheme comprising technical, physical and process measures. It supports asset owners managing cyber risks to operating IACS using other parts of the 62443 series. Full licensed guidance, referenced requirements and evaluation rules remain unverified for this draft.

Confirm the Publicly Available Specification's current status, exact edition and contractual use. Do not treat the publisher stability date as a facility certification deadline. Obtain permission before processing restricted standard content. This workflow organizes evidence and accountable decisions; it does not reproduce a licensed methodology.

## Engagement focus

Connect each protected industrial system and risk scenario to the combined safeguards on which protection depends. Distinguish a documented scheme, installed measures, approved validation and continuing operational effectiveness.

## Roles

The asset owner approves scope and residual-risk decisions. Operations, safety, engineering, physical-security, cybersecurity and maintenance owners approve their measures and operating constraints. Suppliers provide bounded evidence for their responsibilities. An independent reviewer challenges combined protection and failure assumptions. AI may organize permitted records and draft questions; it cannot validate protection, approve measures, accept risk, initiate scans, change OT or make safety decisions.

## Before starting

Record facility, IACS population, operating modes, process consequences, interfaces, owners and relevant risk decisions. Obtain approved design and operating records plus authorized source access. Identify safety constraints, evidence permissions and escalation contacts. No discovery traffic, industrial login or physical protection change follows from this documentation review.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Define protected scope.** Reconcile every scoped IACS, dependency, interface and responsible party. Identify shared utilities, physical access and maintenance arrangements. Preserve unknowns and exclusions for owner review.
2. **Establish the risk basis.** Qualified owners identify scenarios, consequences, assumptions and approved protection objectives. Link them to relevant risk/design decisions and source requirements. Do not infer acceptable risk from the mere existence of controls.
3. **Describe combined measures.** Record each technical, physical and process measure, its owner, purpose, operating conditions and dependencies. Trace how measures work together and identify gaps between organizational responsibilities. Several measures relying on one service may fail together.
4. **Approve validation plans.** Qualified authorities define expected results, scenarios, representative environments, limitations and acceptance decisions. Include relevant degraded conditions and human handoffs. Any live activity requires separate operational/safety approval, stop criteria and recovery planning.
5. **Examine implementation and results.** Compare approved design with actual records and permitted observations. Preserve failed, incomplete and conflicting evidence. A successful isolated measure does not prove that the combined scheme meets its objective.
6. **Operate and maintain the scheme.** Assign monitoring, physical controls, procedure execution, maintenance and supplier responsibilities. Track temporary bypasses, expired decisions and changed dependencies. Verify restoration after maintenance through authorized evidence; ticket closure alone is insufficient.
7. **Review and improve.** Reassess affected risks after incidents, failed validation or change. Assign corrective actions, obtain risk/change decisions and retest. Preserve the old baseline and explain revisions. Obtain named approval for any external representation of protection.

## Evidence and test plan

### 1. Scope and protection rationale package

Asset and risk owners provide the complete scoped IACS register, scenarios, objectives, measure register and responsibility decisions. Reconcile every protected system with its dependencies and evidence. Do not silently omit difficult legacy systems.

### 2. Implementation and validation package

Engineering, safety and security owners provide approved configurations, physical-control records, procedures, validation plans, results and limitations. Trace each protection claim through all necessary measures. Preserve all evidence supplied for assessment without trimming or sampling. Full population accounting is not proof of exhaustive testing.

### 3. Operation and maintenance package

Operations, maintenance and suppliers provide inspection records, bypass approvals, monitoring evidence, incident timelines, restoration checks and corrective-action results. Record whether a measure is designed, installed, available and effective as separate assertions. Keep unverified supplier actions visible.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Two measures depend on the same failed service: reopen every affected protection claim rather than counting them as independent protection.
- Physical or procedural measure lacks an accountable owner: escalate the gap and retain its uncertain effectiveness.
- Temporary bypass persists after maintenance: record incomplete restoration and request authorized correction and verification.
- Validation creates operational or safety risk: stop the proposed activity and obtain qualified approval of a safer method or explicit testing limitation.
- Scheme documentation is current but operating records contradict it: preserve the contradiction and revise the claim, not the evidence.

Use [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) assertions: supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable and not_tested. These statuses are not security levels, validation approvals or risk acceptance.

## Cadence and renewal

Set review and maintenance intervals through approved risk decisions and governing obligations. Reopen affected work after changes in systems, measures, people, suppliers, incidents or source requirements. Do not invent an annual certification cycle.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the scope, protection rationale, combined-measure dependencies, validation coverage, operating responsibilities and open actions with owners and dates.
