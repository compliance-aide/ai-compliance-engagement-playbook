# NISPOM / 32 CFR Part 117 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not federal security guidance, clearance advice, or an authorization decision. Confirm current requirements at the [eCFR Part 117 record](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-A/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-117).

## Engagement focus

Maintain facility and contract scope, personnel and system boundaries, security roles, training records, classified-information handling evidence, incident records, subcontractor dependencies, approvals, and inspection readiness.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Cleared facility and government authorities control access, classifications, reporting, and authorization decisions; independent reviewers test evidence. AI cannot receive classified information and may only organize approved non-sensitive metadata. Review continuously and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [eCFR text for 32 CFR Part 117](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-A/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-117), applicable contract/security requirements, and designated government and facility-security authorities; checked 2026-07-31. This is original operational guidance, not clearance, classification, safeguarding, reporting, or authorization advice.

### 1. Facility, contract, personnel, and system boundary

- **Request and owner:** Request the approved facility and contract scope, security organization chart, authorized personnel and access-status metadata, system/location boundary, subcontractor responsibilities, and governing authority contacts from the facility security officer, contract, HR, and system owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected non-classified metadata record to an approved scope, responsible owner, access boundary, and related contract or facility record. This supports readiness traceability; it cannot determine eligibility, grant access, classify information, or approve a system.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved non-sensitive metadata only and flag missing ownership. Government and cleared-facility authorities retain access, classification, and authorization decisions. Refresh after a contract, facility, person, system, subcontractor, or authority change.

### 2. Safeguarding, training, and security-operation record

- **Request and owner:** Request non-classified evidence indexes for security training, visitor/access administration, system-security coordination, inspections, maintenance or change approvals, and corrective actions from facility security, training, and system owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Select an allowed metadata entry and trace it to its owner, date, approved record location, exception, and remedial action. This supports that a documented process generated evidence; it cannot expose classified material or prove suitability of a safeguard.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify incomplete indexes or review dates but may not receive classified data, administer access, change a system, or close a security exception. Recollect after a security event, inspection, personnel change, or material operating change.

### 3. Event, reporting, and inspection-readiness workpaper

- **Request and owner:** Request sanitized event metadata, authorized escalation/reporting decisions, inspection findings, correspondence references, corrective-action status, retention record, and annual readiness calendar from facility security, legal, and leadership owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected event or finding to factual metadata, an authorized decision-maker, approved follow-up, and closure evidence. This supports internal accountability; it cannot report an event, make a government representation, accept risk, or certify readiness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble a restricted-access workpaper from approved metadata and surface aging actions. Authorized humans decide reporting, remediation, exceptions, and closure. Revisit annually and after an incident, inspection, contract change, or government direction.
