# ISO/IEC 30111:2019 vulnerability handling — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a remediation commitment. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/69725.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain report intake, validation evidence, severity and affected-product decisions, remediation ownership, release traceability, customer communications, exceptions, and closure verification.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Security and product leaders own prioritization and remediation choices; independent reviewers test closure evidence. AI normalizes reports and links release artifacts, but cannot set severity, approve a fix, or close a case. Review queues continuously and programme performance annually.
