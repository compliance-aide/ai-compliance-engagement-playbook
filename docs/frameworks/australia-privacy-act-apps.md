# Australian Privacy Act / APPs — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Australian legal advice, an individual
> rights decision or an authorisation to disclose personal information.

## Source and applicability

Use the [OAIC APP guidelines](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines).
Checked 2026-09-04: the index records an update on 13 May 2026. Counsel must confirm
entity status, applicable Privacy Act provisions, exemptions and other regimes.
The guidelines cover collection, notices, use/disclosure, overseas disclosure,
security, access and correction; an entity's privacy policy is not the whole scope.

[APP 12 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-12-app-12-access-to-personal-information)
distinguishes agencies' 30-calendar-day response duty from organisations'
reasonable-period duty, for which OAIC generally expects no more than 30 calendar
days. Do not conflate APP access with Freedom of Information Act processing.

## Engagement focus

Trace personal-information handling from collection to disposal and demonstrate
approved access/correction and exception routes. Keep purpose, recipient and
retention decisions connected to actual operation. Unknown applicability or a
security pass must not become a privacy-compliance conclusion.

## Roles

Business/data owners supply processing facts. Privacy and Australian counsel
resolve legal scope, rights, disclosures and exceptions. Technology/records owners
perform approved operations. Independent reviewers challenge evidence. AI may
reconcile authorised metadata, calculate dates under approved rules and draft
workpapers; it cannot decide a person's request, legal grounds, breach notice,
transfer approval or risk acceptance.

## Before starting

Obtain entity/applicability decisions, processing/data-flow inventory, notice
versions, recipient/country list, request procedures, retention/hold rules,
incident contacts and evidence restrictions. Record agency versus organisation
status and the applicable clock before configuring response tasks. Use the
[agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md).

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Confirm scope and duties | Privacy/counsel identify entity role, data/process scope and legal exceptions. | Source-backed applicability register; no exemption inferred solely from company size. |
| 2. Map collection and flows | Owners identify solicited/unsolicited intake, purposes, systems, recipients and retention; AI reconciles metadata. | Processing map and unresolved routes tied to accountable owners. |
| 3. Reconcile decisions and notices | Privacy compares collection notices, use/disclosure decisions, overseas handling and supplier instructions with actual processing. | Decision/version references for each material activity; conflicts stay open. |
| 4. Plan rights cases | Privacy defines access and correction paths, identity handling, refusal review, timing and permitted response method. | Approved fictional cases for success, missing identity and exception/escalation. |
| 5. Exercise operations | Authorised QA operators execute cases and retention/hold checks; AI records observation and readback. | Actual records and outputs match the approved decision without exposing unrelated people. |
| 6. Handle gaps and events | Owners triage complaints, suspected exposure and adverse tests; counsel controls legal decisions. | Timely escalation, preserved facts, corrective actions and retest criteria. |
| 7. Renew | Privacy reviews changes and reviewer verifies corrections; governance decides unresolved matters. | Updated inventory/notices, workpapers, decisions and next review queue. |

These are original operating steps, not legal decision rules or a complete APP
assessment method.

## Evidence and test plan

| Request and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collection/flow map from business/privacy | Trace a selected collection through notice, purpose, storage and recipient. | Actual activity and approved records agree. | An unmapped overseas route remains a gap. |
| Rights procedures/cases from privacy | Exercise access and correction separately with approved timing and identity cases. | Correct role-specific route, decision and output are observed. | A generic timer or excessive identity request may fail the approved method. |
| Retention/security records from operations | Trace a fictional eligible disposal and a held record, plus approved access checks. | Actions match authority and actual state can be read back. | A policy does not prove deletion, protection or lawful retention. |
| Supplier/event decisions from privacy/security | Follow an event through evidence, legal review, corrective action and retest. | Facts and decisions remain separate and accountable. | Incident detection alone does not decide notifiability. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Unknown identity or conflicting requests use the approved privacy escalation
route; the AI does not invent verification documents. A potential incident goes
to the incident/privacy lead promptly under the separate Notifiable Data Breaches
process where applicable. Do not treat an APP access deadline as a breach deadline.
Overseas recipients and new purposes require appropriate review before the affected
disclosure. A refusal must follow the authorised decision route and remain visible;
missing evidence cannot be converted to an exception.

## Cadence and renewal

Use separately approved access, correction, complaint and incident clocks with
trigger/timezone and escalation owner. See
[APP 13 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-13-app-13-correction-of-personal-information)
for correction rather than assuming access rules are identical. Quarterly
inventory/supplier and annual governance reviews are planning defaults. Reassess
before new collection, purpose, recipient, retention or material legal changes.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope/obligation decisions, processing and recipient map, notice versions,
protected evidence, QA receipts, findings/retests, legal decisions and next queue.
Name unresolved flows, untested paths and pending responses. No actual rights
response or regulator notification is complete without authority and readback.
