# Australian Privacy Act / APPs — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Australian legal advice or a privacy conclusion. Confirm current application through the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner APP guidelines](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines).

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing inventory, purpose and notice records, access and correction requests, sharing and cross-border decisions, complaint records, incident evidence, retention decisions, vendor oversight, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, legal, security, and business owners determine application and individual-rights responses; independent reviewers test evidence. AI maps approved artifacts and drafts questions, but cannot interpret law, decide rights, notify regulators, or make representations. Review quarterly and annually.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [OAIC APP guidelines](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines) and qualified Australian review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide entity status, an APP exception, overseas-disclosure legality, breach notification, or an access/correction outcome.

### 1. Personal-information flow, purpose, and sharing record

- **Request and owner:** Entity/applicability inputs, personal-information collection/purpose inventory, notice/policy versions, systems, recipient/vendor and overseas-disclosure record, from privacy, product, and vendor owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected collection to the notice, system, purpose, owner, recipient, and cross-border record. This can support a traceable fact base; it cannot determine whether an APP applies, an exception, or a disclosure's legal status.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved inventory fields and flag changed flows. Privacy/legal humans approve decisions. Refresh for a new collection, purpose, recipient, overseas disclosure, or material vendor change.

### 2. Access, correction, retention, and security-operation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Access/correction intake and response records, retention/disposal schedule/execution evidence, security and access-control records, complaint register, and staff guidance from privacy, records, security, and operations owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace an approved request or disposal selection to source record, timing, owner, and escalation while minimizing personal information. This can support observable process operation; it cannot decide the appropriate response or prove safeguards effective in every case.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare redacted traceability and flag overdue internal milestones; it cannot decide a request or access personal information beyond authorization. Refresh after a request trend, process change, complaint, or incident.

### 3. Vendor, incident, and governance-review record

- **Request and owner:** Vendor oversight/change records, incident or exercise timeline, remediation/retest, exception approvals, and quarterly/annual governance decisions from accountable owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected vendor or incident to source evidence, authority, limitation, and corrective action. This can support accountable follow-through; it cannot determine notification/reportability or approve risk acceptance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index records and flag expired actions. Humans approve notifications, transfers, risk, and public statements. Revisit after incident, vendor, or regulatory change and annually.
