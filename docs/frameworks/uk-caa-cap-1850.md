# UK CAA CAP 1850 Cyber Assessment Framework for Aviation engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CAP 1850 framework text, a safety assessment, an aviation-authority determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [UK Civil Aviation Authority CAP 1850 publication](https://www.caa.co.uk/data-and-publications/publications/documents/content/cap1850/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an aviation cyber-assurance record that connects critical-system scope, safety and operational context, accountable aviation and technology owners, evidence of operating safeguards, supplier dependencies, security events, corrective work, and independently reviewable readiness. Preserve the distinction between cyber-assurance support and decisions that affect aviation safety, security, airworthiness, or operations.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Accountable aviation leaders, safety and security owners, and designated cyber-responsible managers retain decision rights; system and supplier owners preserve operational evidence; independent reviewers test scope, traceability, and readiness. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify stale records, draft questions, and prepare review packets, but cannot establish the critical-system scope, decide safety or operational acceptability, make a regulatory determination, assign assessment outcomes, approve a flight or service change, or claim compliance. Review annually and before a material aviation-system, service, supplier, safety, security, incident, or regulatory change.
