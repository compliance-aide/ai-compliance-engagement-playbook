# UL 2900-1 licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not UL standard text, evaluation criteria, or a certification conclusion. Confirm applicable program terms through the [UL 2900 standards overview](https://www.ul.com/insights/medical-device-cybersecurity-standards-and-services).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a connected-product lifecycle record: intended use, product and software boundaries, security ownership, design and release evidence, vulnerability handling, supplier dependencies, testing artifacts, customer communications, and change history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product and engineering owners define intended use and release decisions; security and quality teams retain evidence; authorized evaluators apply any licensed criteria; independent reviewers test internal traceability. AI may organize organization-owned lifecycle evidence and draft questions, but cannot access or reproduce licensed criteria, perform product testing, decide safety, or claim certification. Reassess at design and release changes and retain human approvals for every product statement.
