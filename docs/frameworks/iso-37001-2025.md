# ISO 37001:2025 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content, investigation advice, or certification. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/37001?lang=1).

## Engagement focus

Maintain integrity-risk, third-party oversight, conflict/disclosure, due-diligence, escalation, and remediation evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Human investigators and compliance owners determine allegations and dispositions; independent reviewers sample evidence. AI triages red flags with provenance, but cannot make allegations, findings, or investigation conclusions. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
