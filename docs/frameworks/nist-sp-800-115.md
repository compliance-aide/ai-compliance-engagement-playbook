# NIST SP 800-115 — security-testing engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST content or authorization to test. Confirm status at the [NIST publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain test objectives, authorization, scope, rules of engagement, data-handling boundary, methods, findings evidence, severity rationale, remediation tracking, and retest records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

System and test owners authorize work; qualified testers operate within agreed boundaries; independent reviewers challenge results. AI organizes artifacts and drafts questions, but cannot initiate testing, access systems, or declare findings. Plan annually and test after material change.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [NIST SP 800-115 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final), recording version and applicable use terms. This original plan does not reproduce test procedures, authorize testing, or establish a finding.

### 1. Authorization, scope, and safety package

- **Request and owner:** System owners, security leadership, legal/privacy where applicable, and test leads provide signed authorization, system and data scope, rules of engagement, timing, contacts, prohibited actions, data-handling requirements, and stop/escalation conditions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a test activity sample to its named owner, authorized target, time window, method boundary, and stop authority. This cannot grant authorization, expand scope, or substitute for a legal or operational decision.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare supplied plans for missing fields. Humans authorize, amend, pause, or terminate tests. Renew before each engagement and after material scope or incident change.

### 2. Execution and evidence-integrity package

- **Request and owner:** Qualified testers and evidence custodians provide test plan, tester qualifications, time-stamped activity records, collected artifacts, integrity/custody records, sensitive-data handling evidence, and deviation/escalation records.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample stated activity to approved scope, attributable tester, contemporaneous record, evidence location, and deviation handling. This does not independently reproduce results, access targets, or validate every technical claim.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized artifacts and flag unsupported assertions. Humans control tools, access, collection, and sensitive-data handling. Recollect after a deviation, safety event, or evidence-integrity concern.

### 3. Finding, remediation, and retest package

- **Request and owner:** Test, system, risk, remediation, and independent-review owners provide finding records, evidence references, severity rationale, management responses, remediation tickets, risk decisions, retest authorization/results, and review workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace sampled finding from evidence to human validation, accountable remediation owner, due date, decision, and authorized retest evidence. This cannot determine severity, accept risk, close a finding, or make an audit conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft challenge questions and flag overdue remediation. Humans validate findings, decide risk, and authorize retests; independent reviewers challenge evidence and closure. Review after each test cycle and quarterly for aging items.
