# Argentina personal-data protection — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Argentine legal advice, a rights decision
> or authorization to register, disclose, transfer or delete personal data.

## Source and applicability

Use the [updated official Law 25.326 record](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-25326-64790/actualizacion)
and [AAIP controller guidance](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/responsables/obligaciones).
The law covers data quality, consent, security, confidentiality, transfers and
individual rights. Articles 14 and 16 address different request paths; counsel
must confirm their respective clocks and exceptions. The [AAIP rights guide](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/derechos)
states 10 calendar days for an access response and 5 working days for
rectification/update/suppression after receipt; counsel confirms the applicable
case, exceptions and working-day calendar before those clocks are configured.
AAIP provides separate registration and international-transfer guidance. Do not treat an EU transfer
decision or a proposed Argentine reform as the governing rule for this workflow.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a database and processing record that connects purpose, collection,
recipients, security, retention and rights handling to approved decisions. Show
that the actual workflow can find the affected records and carry out an approved
response without exposing another person's data.

## Roles

Business/database owners identify processing facts; privacy and Argentine counsel
resolve legal roles, consent, registration, rights and transfer conditions.
Records/technology teams perform approved changes. Independent reviewers test
traceability and adverse cases. AI may reconcile permitted metadata and draft
workpapers; it cannot verify identity conclusively, choose legal grounds, decide
a request outcome, submit a registration or approve a transfer.

## Before starting

Obtain the charter, database/processing inventory, counsel-approved scope and
obligations, notices/consent versions, recipient and country list, request-intake
procedures, retention/hold decisions and reviewer. Preserve source section and
clock basis in the obligation register, including calendar versus working days.
Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md); a missing legal rule goes to counsel
while authorized inventory work continues.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Define database scope and roles | Business/privacy owners identify responsible entities, purposes, data categories and reporting/service relationships; counsel resolves applicability. | Approved role/scope record and registration decision or open question. |
| 2. Reconcile the lifecycle | AI traces permitted metadata from collection through use, recipients, storage, retention and disposal; owners confirm the records. | Data-flow and database register with every unexplained route listed. |
| 3. Check purpose and recipient decisions | Privacy/counsel compare approved collection notices, consent or other applicable grounds, supplier instructions and international-transfer decisions. | Decision references tied to each relevant activity and destination; no unsupported legal inference by AI. |
| 4. Prepare distinct rights paths | Privacy defines access, correction/update and suppression handling, identity checks, legal holds, clocks and escalation. | Approved route-specific case plans with trigger and deadline calculation basis. |
| 5. Exercise rights and correction | Operators run fictional cases in QA; AI records intake, search coverage, decision handoff, authorized change and readback. | Same-subject records are found; another person's data is withheld; affected downstream records are addressed under the approved rule. |
| 6. Review security and exceptions | Owners supply access, incident, complaint and retention evidence; independent reviewer checks a defined selection. | Workpapers identify missing records, contradictory handling and open decisions. |
| 7. Correct, retest and renew | Owners implement authorized corrections and reviewer retests; privacy refreshes register and recipient/transfer decisions on change. | Verified correction history, remaining questions and scheduled next checks. |

The steps are an original operating sequence. The legal register supplies the
actual obligations; this table is not a legal interpretation or official form.

## Evidence and test plan

| Request and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Database and purpose register from business/privacy | Trace a selected dataset to collection, purpose, owner, recipients and retention decision. | Records reconcile and decisions address the same activity. | Inventory completeness does not prove a lawful purpose. |
| Request cases from privacy | Exercise access and correction/suppression separately using fictional records and approved clocks. | Routing, time calculation, decision and output match the case plan. | A generic one-month timer must not replace the applicable Argentine rule. |
| Correction/retention records from operations | Read back the authorized action and any required downstream follow-up; test a held-record branch. | Actual record state matches the approved decision. | Ticket closure alone does not prove a correction or permitted deletion. |
| Recipient/transfer register from procurement and counsel | Match actual recipient, destination and service to the approved decision and current source reference. | Scope and conditions are explicit and still applicable. | A vendor contract alone cannot establish transfer legality. |

## Failure branches and decisions

An unverified identity pauses the response through the approved privacy route;
it does not authorize the AI to demand excess identity documents. Conflicting
rights, retention and third-party interests go to counsel. Missing downstream
records remain an evidence gap after a local correction. New countries or
recipients require a fresh transfer decision before the affected disclosure.
Incident facts go to the approved incident/legal route; do not import a GDPR
notification clock without an applicable source. Draft translations and proposed
laws must be labeled and never substituted for the operative Spanish record.

## Cadence and renewal

Quarterly database/recipient reconciliation and annual governance review are
planning defaults. Triage requests and incidents when received, with the approved
route-specific clocks. Reassess before new purposes, data types, recipients,
international routes or material legal/system changes. Assign an AAIP source-watch
owner and retain the version underlying each legal decision.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope and registration decisions, lifecycle/recipient register, approved
request rules, QA receipts, protected evidence index, incident/complaint references,
findings and retests. List unresolved transfers, missing databases and pending
legal decisions. No registration or individual response is complete without its
separate authorized action and readback.
