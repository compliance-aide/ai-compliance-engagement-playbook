# Argentina personal-data protection — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Confirm current sources at [Argentina’s official legal record](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-25326-64790/actualizacion).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a data-set register for purpose, source, use, recipient, retention, and access; operate rights workflows; review databases, vendors, transfers, accuracy, retention, and access evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators remediate stale holdings; independent reviewers trace records through lifecycle and rights cases. AI flags inconsistent evidence, but cannot resolve identity checks, determine legal basis, authorize disclosures, or decide exceptions. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use [Argentina's official legal record for Law 25,326](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-25326-64790/actualizacion) and qualified Argentine privacy counsel; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not reproduce legal text or decide database registration, data-controller role, consent, sensitive-data handling, transfer conditions, or a data-subject outcome.

### 1. Database, purpose, and data-lifecycle register

- **Request and owner:** Database/data-set register connecting purpose, collection source, data categories, subjects, systems, owner, recipients, retention, and disposal; notice/collection artifacts; and any counsel-owned registration assessment inputs.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected dataset from collection source to use, recipient, retention rule, and accountable owner using approved metadata. This supports a factual lifecycle record; it cannot decide registration, lawful collection, consent, or purpose compatibility.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may find missing inventory relationships and aging retention reviews. Privacy/legal humans approve classifications and decisions. Refresh after a new dataset, purpose, data source, recipient, or material system change.

### 2. Rights, accuracy, and access-control operations

- **Request and owner:** Access/rectification/suppression request procedures and redacted logs, identity-verification steps, data-quality correction records, access reviews, and retention/disposal execution evidence owned by privacy, operations, and records teams.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a redacted request or correction case to intake, identity decision authority, source record, action, escalation, and closure. This supports workflow traceability; it cannot decide identity, a rights outcome, exception, or legal deadline.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may issue internal completeness prompts but cannot communicate decisions to individuals. Recollect after a request trend, collection-channel change, failed trace, or records-process change.

### 3. Recipient, international, and incident governance

- **Request and owner:** Recipient/vendor register, contract/instruction evidence, cross-border review inputs, security incident timeline, complaint/escalation records, management review, and remediation/retest log.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected recipient or incident to owner, stated purpose, decision authority, security evidence provenance, corrective action, and review date. This supports an accountable record; it cannot approve a disclosure/transfer or determine incident, authority, or regulator duties.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale recipient records and draft a redacted review packet. Legal/privacy and incident owners approve transfers, communications, and risk decisions. Refresh on a recipient, transfer, incident, or annual scope review.
