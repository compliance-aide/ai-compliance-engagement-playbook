# New Zealand HISO 10029.2:2023 health information security for micro to small organizations engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not HISO framework text, clinical advice, a self-assessment result, or a compliance claim. Confirm current materials through the [Health New Zealand HISO 10029.2:2023 guidance](https://www.tewhatuora.govt.nz/assets/Our-health-system/Digital-health/Health-information-standards/HISO-10029-2-2023-Health-Information-Security-Framework-Guidance-for-Micro-to-Small-Organisations.pdf).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a proportionate health-information security record that connects the small organization’s services, health-information assets, accountable owners, essential operating evidence, suppliers, security events, corrective work, and an independently reviewable annual record. Keep the engagement usable for small teams without pretending that a simplified evidence process resolves clinical, safety, privacy, or legal decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business, clinical-safety, privacy, and security authorities retain accountable decisions; service and supplier owners preserve evidence; an independent reviewer challenges completeness and traceability. AI may organize authorized records, identify missing evidence, prepare reminders, and draft questions, but cannot access health records without authorization, make clinical or safety decisions, approve a supplier, determine legal or notification obligations, alter systems, accept risk, score the organization, or claim compliance. Review annually and before a material service, patient-data, supplier, system, incident, or regulatory change.
