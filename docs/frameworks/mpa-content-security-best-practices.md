# MPA Content Security Best Practices engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not MPA or Trusted Partner Network assessment content, a completed assessment, registry status, or a certification claim. Confirm the current program and any applicable materials through the [Motion Picture Association resource page](https://www.motionpictures.org/advocacy/defending-creators/additional-resources/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a defensible content-protection operating record for each production, post-production, localization, marketing, distribution, and cloud service workflow. Tie content classification, authorized people and partners, physical and digital locations, transfer paths, access decisions, vendor commitments, incidents, change decisions, and content-return or destruction actions to the specific engagement and release window.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Content owners define access and release decisions; production and vendor owners maintain operational evidence; security teams coordinate access, monitoring, and incident response; legal and rights holders decide contractual and intellectual-property matters; independent reviewers test evidence traceability. AI may inventory approved records, flag incomplete custody evidence, and prepare review questions, but cannot access unreleased content without authorization, make rights decisions, complete an MPA or TPN assessment, submit information, or represent registry or certification status. Reassess before a material workflow, supplier, location, platform, release-window, or incident change, with named human approval for every external statement.

### 1. Title, asset, workflow, and release-window boundary

- **Request and owner:** Approved title/asset classification, production/post/localization/marketing/distribution workflow map, approved locations/platforms, release-window record, authorized partner roster, and named content, production, security, and legal/rights owners. Cover the defined title/asset population and engagement dates without putting unreleased content in the repository.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected asset/workflow entries to an accountable owner, approved handling location/platform, release-window context, and authorized partner boundary. Use identifiers or sanitized metadata rather than content. This supports a bounded custody and workflow record; it cannot establish ownership, rights, program applicability, or protection of all copies.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved asset, partner, and workflow metadata and flag missing custody/owner links. Rights holders and production leaders decide classification, access, and scope. Trigger on a new title, supplier, location, distribution path, release-window shift, or material workflow change.

### 2. Authorized access, transfer, and partner-accountability trace

- **Request and owner:** Access authorization/recertification records, identity/role inventories, secure-transfer or delivery references, visitor/physical-access records where applicable, supplier onboarding/contractual-security references, offboarding/return/destruction attestations, and responsible production, IT, security, and supplier owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an authorized person or partner from request/approval through access or transfer authorization, period review, and offboarding/return record where applicable. Do not expose credentials, distribution secrets, contract terms, or unreleased material. This supports an accountable selected trace; it cannot prove every access was authorized, transfers were secure, or suppliers met contractual duties.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved metadata and flag absent owners, dates, or offboarding links. Humans decide access, transfer, partner approval, contractual remedies, and exceptions. Trigger on onboarding/offboarding, access anomaly, supplier change, new transfer path, or periodic recertification.

### 3. Leak/incident response, recovery, and release learning record

- **Request and owner:** Sanitized incident/leak register, preservation and investigation references, containment/revocation decisions, partner/risk/legal communication approvals, recovery/remediation evidence, post-incident review, and closure records from content protection, security response, legal, production, and rights owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected incidents or release-window concerns to detection, accountable decision makers, containment/recovery action, legal/rights escalation, and human closure. Preserve investigative and unreleased content outside public documentation. This supports a chronological response trail; it cannot determine infringement, legal reporting duties, damage, attribution, or effectiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize sanitized records and flag missing decision/closure links. Humans decide incident classification, legal action, external communications, risk acceptance, and closure. Trigger on suspected loss, unauthorized access/distribution, material supplier event, release incident, or annual review.
