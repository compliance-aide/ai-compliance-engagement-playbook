# MPA Content Security Best Practices engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not MPA or Trusted Partner Network assessment content, a completed assessment, registry status, or a certification claim. Confirm the current program and any applicable materials through the [Motion Picture Association resource page](https://www.motionpictures.org/advocacy/defending-creators/additional-resources/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a defensible content-protection operating record for each production, post-production, localization, marketing, distribution, and cloud service workflow. Tie content classification, authorized people and partners, physical and digital locations, transfer paths, access decisions, vendor commitments, incidents, change decisions, and content-return or destruction actions to the specific engagement and release window.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Content owners define access and release decisions; production and vendor owners maintain operational evidence; security teams coordinate access, monitoring, and incident response; legal and rights holders decide contractual and intellectual-property matters; independent reviewers test evidence traceability. AI may inventory approved records, flag incomplete custody evidence, and prepare review questions, but cannot access unreleased content without authorization, make rights decisions, complete an MPA or TPN assessment, submit information, or represent registry or certification status. Reassess before a material workflow, supplier, location, platform, release-window, or incident change, with named human approval for every external statement.
