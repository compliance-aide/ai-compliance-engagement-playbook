# ISO/SAE 21434:2021 — automotive cybersecurity engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO/SAE content or certification advice. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/70918.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain product, program, supplier, cybersecurity-case, risk, change, vulnerability, incident, and field-support evidence from concept through retirement.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Engineering owners approve design and risk; independent reviewers test evidence trails. AI links versioned artifacts and gaps, but cannot approve decisions or replace assessors. Review monthly, quarterly, and annually.
