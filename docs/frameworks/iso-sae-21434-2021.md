# ISO/SAE 21434:2021 automotive cybersecurity engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO/SAE standard text, an automotive cybersecurity assessment, or a conformity claim. Confirm status and edition through the [ISO/SAE 21434:2021 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/70918.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a lifecycle record for road-vehicle electrical and electronic systems: product intent, cybersecurity governance, assets and interfaces, supplier responsibilities, risk decisions, engineering work products, verification evidence, production and service changes, vulnerability reports, field events, and retirement decisions. Preserve the links between vehicle, component, software version, responsible organization, and customer or regulatory commitments.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Vehicle and product leaders own product intent and lifecycle decisions; engineering maintains development and change evidence; cybersecurity leads coordinate risk and vulnerability work; suppliers supply approved evidence; independent reviewers challenge traceability across the supply chain. AI may organize approved evidence, compare versions, identify missing provenance, and draft review questions, but cannot perform engineering verification, accept residual risk, authorize a vehicle or software release, contact regulators, or claim compliance. Reassess at material design, supplier, production, field, vulnerability, or retirement changes, with accountable human approval for every release and external statement.
