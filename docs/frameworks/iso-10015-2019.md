# ISO 10015:2019 competence management — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, a competence certification or an employment decision.

## Source and applicability

Confirm scope and edition at the [official ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/69459.html). Indexed publisher content checked 2026-09-04 identifies the 2019 second edition, confirmed in 2025, as guidance for competence management and people development. It does not modify the requirements of other standards.

The full licensed standard was not reviewed. Confirm permitted use before accessing protected ISO material for AI work; do not put ISO text in prompts without permission. These original instructions are not an ISO-prescribed rating scale, course requirement or assessment method. Qualified owners must establish applicable role, regulatory and professional requirements.

## Engagement focus

Connect the work that must be performed to the required competence, available evidence, development plan and observed effectiveness. Course attendance, a quiz result, practical performance and authorization to work are separate records. None should silently stand in for the others.

## Roles

Functional managers define task requirements and supervision. HR and learning owners govern development, fairness and protected records. Qualified assessors make competence judgments using approved criteria; legal or professional authorities resolve mandatory qualifications. An independent reviewer tests program traceability and evidence quality. AI may reconcile permitted metadata and organize aggregate gaps, but cannot rate people, make employment decisions, authorize unsupervised work, infer protected characteristics or access restricted HR records.

## Before starting

Record organizational objectives, processes, role populations, locations, contractors, relevant changes and review period. Identify program owners, approved evidence access and escalation routes. Obtain role/task definitions, development plans, completion records, authorized assessment references and applicable qualification requirements. Keep identifiable performance records private; public workpapers must contain no employee evaluations or sensitive personal information.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Define task-based needs.** Link each scoped role and task to its business or regulatory driver, operating context and accountable owner. Specify the observable performance expected and conditions that matter. Avoid vague labels such as expert without criteria. Have qualified humans approve requirements before comparing records; job title alone does not establish capability needs.
2. **Reconcile the complete known population.** Compare role assignments, contractor rosters and learning systems through approved metadata. Account for required roles, assigned people, development actions, assessment evidence and exceptions. Preserve unknown coverage. Never silently sample, trim or cap evidence; restricted records remain in their authorized system with safe references.
3. **Identify gaps with human assessment.** Assemble existing qualifications, relevant experience, performance observations and record limitations for the qualified assessor. Distinguish absent evidence from demonstrated shortfall. Check relevance to the current task, equipment, procedure and conditions. AI flags missing links; it does not convert attendance or a historical credential into a competence rating.
4. **Approve a development plan.** Link each gap to an objective, suitable learning or supervised practice, responsible manager, resources, timing and evaluation method. Identify prerequisites and any interim supervision or task restrictions decided by authorized humans. Consider process, tool or instruction defects as alternatives to a training-only explanation. Obtain required approval for investment and changes.
5. **Verify delivery and record integrity.** Reconcile planned participation with actual completion, content version, provider evidence and exceptions. Record inaccessible training, changed assignments and interrupted learning honestly. Preserve corrections and source history. Completion supports a delivery claim only; it does not automatically authorize the learner to perform the task.
6. **Evaluate effectiveness in context.** Have qualified assessors review evidence against the pre-approved criteria after an appropriate opportunity to apply learning. Record task conditions, procedure version, observed results, limitations and the human decision. Keep individual assessment separate from aggregate program trends. If expected improvement is absent, investigate method, environment, workload and tools as well as development needs.
7. **Close actions and renew the baseline.** Link remaining gaps to further action, supervision, responsible owner and follow-up date. Preserve the original evidence and assessment history. Reassess requirements after changed work rather than automatically carrying a prior result forward. Management reviews program effectiveness and resources; any employment action follows the separate authorized HR process.

## Evidence and test plan

These three packages preserve the prior tailored plan and expand selected records to full known scoped population accounting.

| Package | Minimum records | Reviewer challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role needs and development plans | Tasks, context, drivers, approved criteria, role population, gaps, plans and interim arrangements | Does the plan address the actual work and the human-reviewed need? |
| Completion and protected records | Assigned population, content version, delivery/completion references, access controls, exceptions and corrections | Is completion attributable without exposing restricted records or implying competence? |
| Effectiveness and governance | Assessment-method approval, private observations, human decisions, aggregate trends, follow-up and review actions | Is the conclusion supported for the relevant task and conditions? |

Apply the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) statuses `supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` and `not_tested` to program-evidence assertions, not automated ratings of people. Split completion, demonstrated capability and work authorization. Complete population accounting does not mean every person/task combination has been evaluated.

Fictional author desk case: a training dashboard records course completion, but the approved practical evaluation has not occurred. The completion record can be supported while the evaluation remains not tested and unsupervised-work authorization remains unverified. Route the next step to the qualified assessor and manager; do not label the person incompetent or automatically grant access.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Missing evidence: obtain authorized source confirmation; do not treat missing records as a negative employment finding.
- Conflicting assessment or outdated criteria: preserve both records and obtain qualified review before relying on either result.
- Repeated process errors despite completed training: investigate the process and tools rather than assigning the same course automatically.
- An assessment exposes a safety or legal concern: promptly route it to the designated authority for supervision and work decisions; AI does not independently change duties.
- Protected information appears in an aggregate export: stop further disclosure and use the approved privacy-handling route without copying the contents into an issue.

## Cadence and renewal

Use applicable qualification rules, task risk and owner-approved plans to set review intervals. Reopen after role, process, technology, procedure or legal changes and observed effectiveness problems. This guide invents no blanket quarterly or annual ISO mandate. Track credential expiry, assessment and development due dates separately.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the role/plan index, protected evidence references, qualified decisions, program findings and owned next actions. State limitations without exposing individual assessments. Independent source, skeptical and rights review remain pending before publication. Author desk cases are not real workforce evaluations, independent review or cross-model usability tests.
