# ISO 10015:2019 competence management — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or an employment decision. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/69459.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain role and competence needs, evidence of capability, learning plans, completion records, effectiveness review, workforce-access boundaries, exceptions, and improvement actions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Managers and HR make competence and employment decisions; independent reviewers test programme evidence. AI aggregates permitted records and identifies gaps, but cannot rate people, make employment decisions, or access restricted HR data. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm edition status at the [ISO 10015:2019 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/69459.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO content is licensed. This original plan uses high-level artifact categories and does not reproduce ISO requirements or make a competence or employment determination.

### 1. Role need and learning-plan package

- **Request and owner:** HR, functional leaders, and learning owners provide role/skill matrices, business or regulatory drivers, approved learning plans, accountable managers, access boundaries, and documented exceptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected role need to a business driver, named owner, and approved development plan. This supports a governed planning record; it cannot determine whether any individual is competent or whether a role design is sufficient.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved matrices and flag absent owners or stale plans using permitted metadata. Humans define role needs and approve learning investment. Refresh after role, process, technology, or regulatory changes.

### 2. Completion and protected-record package

- **Request and owner:** Learning and HR custodians provide appropriately minimized completion/attendance evidence, provider records, accommodations/access controls, completion exceptions, and retention rules.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a privacy-safe sample for linkage between the approved plan, assigned population, completion evidence, and exception route. This supports record integrity for the sample; it cannot judge learning quality or expose protected employee information.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize permitted aggregate status and flag missing evidence; it may not score people, make employment decisions, or access restricted records. Recollect after an access change, training-platform migration, or material gap.

### 3. Effectiveness and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Functional managers provide approved effectiveness indicators, non-punitive feedback, process-performance observations, remediation plans, leadership review materials, and exception decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected programme issue to a human-reviewed indicator, improvement action, owner, and follow-up. This supports an improvement trail; it cannot prove individual capability or justify discipline.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize aggregate evidence and flag overdue follow-up. Humans interpret results, approve exceptions, and decide any employment action. Review quarterly and renew annually.
