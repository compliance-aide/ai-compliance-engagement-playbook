# NIST SP 800-122 — PII confidentiality engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a legal substitute or certification. See [NIST SP 800-122](https://www.nist.gov/publications/guide-protecting-confidentiality-personally-identifiable-information-pii-0).

## Engagement focus

Maintain PII inventory, classification, context, usage, access, location, minimization, de-identification, mobile/offsite, training, vendor, incident, and remediation evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans assign impact and notification decisions; independent reviewers test samples. AI can index evidence, but cannot approve de-identification or make breach decisions. Review quarterly and annually.
