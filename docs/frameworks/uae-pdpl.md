# UAE federal personal-data law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not UAE legal advice. Check the [UAE legislation portal](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972) plus free-zone and sector rules.

## Engagement focus

Maintain entity/location, processing, notice, owner, vendor, rights, breach, transfer, change, and board-risk evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans and counsel decide scope, exemptions, transfers, and communications; independent reviewers test evidence. AI may support authorized inventory and evidence organization, but cannot determine legal scope, approve transfers, decide notification or communication obligations, access personal data without authorization, or make a compliance claim. Review quarterly and annually.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [UAE legislation portal](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972), applicable implementing, sector, and free-zone rules, and qualified UAE privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide federal versus free-zone or sector scope, controller/processor role, consent or another legal condition, DPO requirement, transfer conditions, breach-reporting duty, or an individual's outcome.

### 1. Entity, jurisdiction, processing, and transfer map

- **Request and owner:** Entity and operating-location register; human-reviewed jurisdiction/sector/free-zone applicability inputs; processing-purpose/data-category/system map; controller/processor/vendor register; notice records; transfer/location map; and retention/disposal decisions from privacy, legal, product, and procurement owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processing route to entity, operating context, system, purpose, owner, vendor, stated location/transfer, and retention. This supports factual scoping; it cannot decide governing regime, legal condition, role, or transfer legality.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized inventory and flag an unowned location, supplier, or flow. UAE-qualified humans decide scope and legal treatment. Refresh for new entity/location, sector/free-zone context, purpose, vendor, transfer, or system change.

### 2. Notice, rights, security, and incident-operation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Notice/version history, data-subject request and identity-verification procedures/logs, access/security/change evidence, processor instructions, incident exercise/event timeline, escalation contacts, and remediation records from privacy, security, and service owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted sample to source, period, owner, escalation, and follow-up while excluding personal data. This can demonstrate recorded workflow operation; it cannot decide a request, establish safeguard effectiveness, or determine notification obligations.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized artifacts and flag incomplete handoffs or internal dates. It cannot contact individuals, notify regulators, or make a legal conclusion. Recollect after incident, complaint trend, security/process change, or failed exercise.

### 3. Processor, exception, and leadership-review record

- **Request and owner:** Vendor due-diligence/change and contract/instruction evidence, DPO/governance ownership where applicable, exceptions and remediation/retest, management decisions, annual scope review, and source-change watch for federal, free-zone, and sector developments.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected vendor change, exception, or corrective action to human authority, facts, expiry, and retest. This can support governance traceability; it cannot approve risk, transfers, communications, or a compliance claim.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify stale approvals and assemble a redacted leadership packet. Humans approve risk, contracts, notifications, representations, and closure. Review annually and before material entity, jurisdiction, supplier, transfer, or regulatory change.
