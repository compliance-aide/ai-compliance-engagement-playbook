# ISO 22361:2022 — crisis-management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or emergency direction. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/50267.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain authority, escalation, roles, delegation, contacts, protected communications, decision logs, exercises, lessons, and corrective-action records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide severity and speak externally; independent reviewers test closure. AI drafts summaries and reconciles logs, but cannot activate response or issue statements. Exercise annually and review quarterly.
