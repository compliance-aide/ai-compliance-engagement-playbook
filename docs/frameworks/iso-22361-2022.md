# ISO 22361:2022 — crisis-management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or emergency direction. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/50267.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain authority, escalation, roles, delegation, contacts, protected communications, decision logs, exercises, lessons, and corrective-action records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide severity and speak externally; independent reviewers test closure. AI drafts summaries and reconciles logs, but cannot activate response or issue statements. Exercise annually and review quarterly.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm currency through the [ISO 22361:2022 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/50267.html) and use any licensed material only within its authorized terms; checked 2026-07-31. This plan is original operational guidance, not ISO text, emergency direction, or a claim of crisis-management capability.

### 1. Crisis authority and activation package

- **Request and owner:** Crisis leadership supplies approved authority/delegation records, activation criteria, role assignments, contact-maintenance evidence, and protected communication-channel arrangements.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected role from authority assignment through delegation and current contact verification. This supports readiness of the sampled governance record; it cannot determine severity, activate a response, or prove availability under crisis conditions.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag expired delegations or inconsistent rosters. Humans decide escalation and activation. Refresh after leadership, contact, supplier, or communications-platform change.

### 2. Scenario exercise and decision-log package

- **Request and owner:** Exercise owners provide a human-approved scenario, participation record, timed decision log, communications artifacts, injects, observations, and issues from a selected exercise.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a decision from scenario input to accountable decision-maker, communication path, observed outcome, and recorded lesson. This supports the tested exercise trail; it does not prove performance in an actual or different crisis.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize exercise chronology and draft questions; it may not direct participants, issue public statements, or characterize an event. Recollect after an exercise, an actual event, or a significant lesson.

### 3. Lessons, corrective action, and leadership review package

- **Request and owner:** Crisis and executive owners provide post-event or post-exercise lessons, corrective-action owners/dates, exception decisions, completion evidence, and leadership review minutes.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected lesson to an assigned action, due-date decision, closure evidence, and leadership challenge. This supports accountable follow-through; it cannot accept residual risk or establish that actions will work in future events.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag overdue actions and prepare an evidence packet. Humans approve priorities, resource commitments, risk decisions, and external disclosures. Review quarterly and after material events.
