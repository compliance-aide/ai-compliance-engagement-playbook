# Mexico private-sector personal-data protection engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Mexican legal text, a legal interpretation, a notification decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [official federal law record](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf) and qualified local counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a year-round record for personal-data processing in the private sector that connects processing purposes, accountable owners, data categories, notices and communications, individual-request handling, consent or other authorized bases as determined by counsel, service-provider arrangements, cross-border or onward-transfer decisions, safeguards, incidents, exceptions, and review dates. Treat the record as operating evidence; it does not resolve legal applicability or make a legal determination.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business, privacy, legal, security, product, data, customer operations, procurement, risk, and incident-response owners retain their decisions. Legal and privacy authorities determine scope and obligations; processing owners maintain authorized records; security and service owners preserve operating evidence; and incident authorities decide escalation and notification actions. Independent reviewers sample processing paths from stated purpose through notices, service-provider evidence, request outcomes, incident records, exceptions, and remediation, and escalate unresolved legal or factual uncertainty.

AI may organize authorized evidence, identify missing ownership or review dates, draft questions, compare documented records, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot decide legal applicability, infer valid consent or another basis, respond to an individual, approve a transfer, make a notification decision, change production processing, accept risk, or claim compliance. Minimize and authorize any data used with AI. Reassess before material processing, notice, recipient, supplier, incident, or legal changes; conduct quarterly reviews and an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [official federal law record](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf) and qualified local counsel; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not reproduce the law or decide notice content, consent, transfer conditions, rights outcomes, or notification duties.

### 1. Private-sector processing, notice, and retention trail

- **Request and owner:** Processing and data-category inventory, purpose/use register, privacy-notice versions and publication evidence, collection-channel records, retention/disposal schedule, and ownership assignments from privacy, product, marketing, records, and system owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one customer, employee, or service processing route from collection channel to notice version, stated purpose, system owner, retention event, and disposal evidence. This confirms recorded facts; it cannot decide legal applicability, notice sufficiency, or consent validity.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories and notice versions and flag missing ownership. Privacy/legal humans decide classifications and content. Refresh for a new collection channel, purpose, notice, or material data-category change.

### 2. Rights, service-provider, and transfer records

- **Request and owner:** Rights-request procedures and redacted logs, identity-verification controls, service-provider agreements/oversight, recipient register, cross-border/onward-transfer review inputs, and exception decisions from privacy, legal, procurement, and customer-operations owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted request or supplier change through receipt, authority, decision, communication, and follow-up. This supports accountable workflow evidence; it cannot decide a rights request or approve a transfer.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized evidence and prepare internal deadline prompts; it may not communicate externally or select a legal basis. Recollect after a new provider, recipient, transfer, or recurring request issue.

### 3. Safeguard, incident, and management-review evidence

- **Request and owner:** Security-operation records, supplier assurance evidence, incident exercise/event records, escalation inputs, remediation/retest, risk exceptions, and annual privacy review from security, incident, privacy, and executive owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an incident exercise or exception to facts, human authority, corrective action, expiry, and retest. This tests evidence continuity; it cannot prove safeguards effective or determine notification requirements.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale actions and assemble a redacted review packet. Humans decide incident escalation, risk acceptance, and closure. Revisit quarterly, annually, and after an incident or material legal change.
