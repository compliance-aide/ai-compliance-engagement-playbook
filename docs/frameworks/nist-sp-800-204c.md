# NIST SP 800-204C DevSecOps service-mesh engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST DevSecOps implementation guidance, a pipeline design, a deployment authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-204C](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/204/c/final) and the organization’s approved security, architecture, engineering, privacy, legal, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed engagement for DevSecOps implementation in an in-scope microservices and service-mesh environment. Connect service and code-type inventory to approved pipeline, infrastructure, policy, and observability decisions; source and build provenance; deployment and runtime evidence; access; monitoring; tests; supplier components; changes; incidents; and retirement. A pipeline record, code or policy extract, deployment artifact, test result, or audit log is evidence to assess; none alone proves a pipeline is secure, code is trustworthy, policies are enforced, a deployment is authorized, or compliance.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, security, architecture, platform and microservices engineering, build and release operations, identity and access-management operations, application and data owners, supplier-management, privacy and legal where relevant, change-management, incident-response, and records-management roles. Operators maintain scoped service and pipeline inventories, approved architecture and policy decisions, source and build provenance, infrastructure and observability traceability, deployment and configuration baselines, access and audit evidence, test and monitoring records, supplier materials, exceptions, incidents, and retirement evidence. Reconcile services, code types, pipelines, owners, policies, dependencies, and suppliers quarterly; review build, test, policy, deployment, or runtime failures, exceptions, incidents, and unresolved risks at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material architecture, pipeline, provider, threat, or publication changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples source-to-build-to-deployment-to-runtime-review traceability; auditors test supplied evidence without changing pipelines, granting access, deploying workloads, approving exceptions, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied source, pipeline, policy, deployment, monitoring, and review evidence, identify missing owners or stale decisions, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot design or change pipelines, grant access, deploy workloads, approve an exception, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
