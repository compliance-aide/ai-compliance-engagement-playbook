# Thailand PDPA — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Thai legal advice. Check the [ETDA PDPA law hub](https://www.etda.or.th/th/Useful-Resource/law/pdpa.aspx) and current notices.

## Engagement focus

Maintain Thailand processing, purpose, product, vendor, request, incident, transfer, notice, and accountable-executive evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide applicability, bases, notifications, and transfers; independent reviewers test samples. AI drafts logs and gaps, but cannot decide outcomes. Review quarterly and annually.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [ETDA PDPA law hub](https://www.etda.or.th/th/Useful-Resource/law/pdpa.aspx), applicable official notices, and qualified Thai privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide controller/processor role, legal basis, consent validity, sensitive-data treatment, cross-border conditions, DPO obligation, breach notification, or a data-subject outcome.

### 1. Thailand processing, basis-input, and transfer record

- **Request and owner:** Thailand-related processing inventory, purpose/category fields, collection notice/version evidence, human-approved basis/consent decision inputs where applicable, sensitive-data review inputs, controller/processor/vendor register, transfer map, and retention record from product, privacy, procurement, and operations owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected activity from collection to use/sharing through system, purpose, owner, notice, vendor/transfer entry, and retention record. This establishes a factual packet for review; it cannot determine legal basis, consent validity, role, or transfer compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized records and flag a changed collection channel or unowned vendor flow. Thai privacy/legal humans decide legal classifications. Refresh for new processing, sensitive-data use, purpose, supplier, transfer, or product change.

### 2. Rights, security, and breach-readiness evidence

- **Request and owner:** Data-subject request and identity-verification procedures/logs, access/security/change records, safeguards evidence, incident exercise/event timeline, escalation contacts, and corrective actions from privacy, security, and service owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted sample to source, timing, owner, escalation, and follow-up while minimizing personal data. This can support evidence of recorded operations; it cannot decide a request, establish security effectiveness, or decide notification to any party.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create an evidence index and highlight missing escalation fields; it cannot respond to individuals, notify authorities, or make a compliance claim. Recollect after incident, complaint trend, system/security change, or failed exercise.

### 3. DPO/governance, vendor, and renewal record

- **Request and owner:** DPO/governance records where applicable, vendor due-diligence/instructions, exception/risk decisions, remediation/retest, management review, annual scope review, and official-source change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception, vendor change, or remediation item to named human authority, facts, expiry, and retest. This can support accountable governance; it cannot accept risk, approve a transfer, or attest.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag overdue reviews and assemble a redacted management packet. Humans approve risk, communications, vendor decisions, and closure. Review annually and on material processing, supplier, transfer, or regulatory change.
