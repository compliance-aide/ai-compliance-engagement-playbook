# ISO 22316:2017 — organizational-resilience engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/50053.html) for revision status.

## Engagement focus

Maintain resilience context, executive ownership, value-delivery dependencies, change/risk signals, adaptation, decisions, improvement, and leadership-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

People set priorities and accept risk; independent reviewers test provenance. AI normalizes evidence and flags drift, but cannot certify. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm status and authorized edition through the [ISO 22316:2017 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/50053.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO material is licensed. This original plan uses evidence categories only and does not reproduce ISO content, decide risk acceptance, or make a resilience or certification claim.

### 1. Resilience context and dependency package

- **Request and owner:** Executive sponsors, service owners, and risk owners provide the operating context, critical value-delivery outcomes, dependencies, disruption assumptions, accountable roles, and approved resilience boundaries.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one priority outcome to its service dependencies, stated disruption assumption, and accountable decision owner. This tests whether the basis is visible; it cannot establish completeness of dependencies or adequacy of a resilience decision.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may map supplied dependencies and flag missing owners or stale assumptions. Humans define priorities, context, and risk tolerance. Refresh after a material strategy, supplier, operating-model, or disruption change.

### 2. Adaptation and decision package

- **Request and owner:** Business, risk, and operational leaders provide change signals, scenario or lessons-learned records, decisions, adaptation actions, implementation owners, due dates, and approved exceptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect one change signal from recognition through a human decision, assigned adaptation action, and status evidence. This supports accountable adaptation; it cannot judge whether the response is optimal or authorize a risk acceptance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize signals and surface unclosed actions, but may not prioritize investment, approve an exception, or close a decision. Recollect following a serious disruption, material change, or repeated missed action.

### 3. Leadership review and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Leadership provides review agendas, evidence summaries, decisions, action registers, resource commitments, escalation records, and follow-up evidence for prior decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one leadership decision to its evidence inputs, authority, action owner, and later follow-up. This supports governance traceability; it cannot prove organizational resilience or replace leadership judgment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a source-linked review packet and identify expired actions. Humans approve priorities, funding, risk acceptance, and external statements. Review quarterly and close the annual cycle with independent sampling.
