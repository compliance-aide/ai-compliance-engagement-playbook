# ISO 22316:2017 — organizational-resilience engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/50053.html) for revision status.

## Engagement focus

Maintain resilience context, executive ownership, value-delivery dependencies, change/risk signals, adaptation, decisions, improvement, and leadership-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

People set priorities and accept risk; independent reviewers test provenance. AI normalizes evidence and flags drift, but cannot certify. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
