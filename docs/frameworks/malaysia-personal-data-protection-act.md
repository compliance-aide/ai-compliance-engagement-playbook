# Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Malaysian statutory text or legal advice. Confirm current Act 709, amendments, and implementing materials through the [Personal Data Protection Commissioner](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/pdp-act-2010-en/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a lifecycle record of commercial personal-data processing: data purpose and ownership, processing and sharing decisions, notices, consent or other decision records, vendor evidence, registrations where applicable, incident handling, and change reviews.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business and privacy owners maintain the processing record; security and supplier owners provide operating evidence; Malaysian counsel confirms current scope and amendments; independent reviewers test traceability. AI indexes approved records, identifies stale owners or evidence, and drafts questions, but cannot interpret law, register an organization, decide individual rights, or submit incident notices. Review material processing changes before release and preserve human approvals.

### 1. Commercial processing, notice, and accountability package

- **Request and owner:** Business, privacy, product, data, and records owners provide a dated commercial-processing register; purposes; data categories; collection channels; systems; accountable owners; notice/version history; stated retention; disclosures; vendors/recipients; and amendment/scope questions for counsel.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processing activity to collection channel, documented purpose, data record, system, owner, notice, retention context, and recipient/vendor. This supports a factual planning record; it cannot determine commercial coverage, notice sufficiency, consent or other legal condition, registration duty, or permitted processing.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved inventories and release/provider metadata and flag changed flows or unowned evidence. Malaysian counsel and accountable humans decide scope and approvals. Refresh for a new purpose, collection channel, data category, product release, vendor, or material amendment/source update.

### 2. Individual request, security, and incident workflow package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, customer operations, security, and records owners provide approved request/complaint and identity-verification procedures, de-identified case metadata, access/security-operation references, retention/disposal execution evidence, incident/exercise timelines, communications approvals, and remediation records.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a de-identified request, access event, disposal event, or exercise through intake/trigger, owner, human decision or escalation, documented action, and follow-up. This can support traceability for a selected workflow; it cannot authenticate a person, decide a request, prove safeguards effective, or establish notification/reporting duties.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a restricted evidence index and flag missing dates or owner links; it may not access unapproved personal data, resolve requests, change systems, or submit reports. Humans and counsel approve responses and external communications. Recheck after incident, failed test, recurring complaint, material security change, or process change.

### 3. Vendor, registration-question, and governance-review package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, legal, privacy, security, and leadership owners provide processor/vendor register, contract and instruction references, assurance/change records, registration/applicability question log, exception and remediation records, management decisions, source-change monitoring, and retest evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected vendor change, registration question, or exception to the supporting facts, counsel review, accountable human decision, action owner, due date, and follow-up. This supports an accountable governance trail; it cannot decide registration, prove contract sufficiency or supplier performance, accept risk, or claim compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare source-linked, non-authoritative workpapers and flag stale review dates. Human legal, privacy, security, and executive authorities approve registration, contracts, risk acceptance, notifications, representations, and closure. Refresh after provider/subprocessor change, regulatory update, material incident, missed remediation target, or annual review.
