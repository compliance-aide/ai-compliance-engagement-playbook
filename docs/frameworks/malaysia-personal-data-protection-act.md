# Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Malaysian statutory text or legal advice. Confirm current Act 709, amendments, and implementing materials through the [Personal Data Protection Commissioner](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/pdp-act-2010-en/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a lifecycle record of commercial personal-data processing: data purpose and ownership, processing and sharing decisions, notices, consent or other decision records, vendor evidence, registrations where applicable, incident handling, and change reviews.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business and privacy owners maintain the processing record; security and supplier owners provide operating evidence; Malaysian counsel confirms current scope and amendments; independent reviewers test traceability. AI indexes approved records, identifies stale owners or evidence, and drafts questions, but cannot interpret law, register an organization, decide individual rights, or submit incident notices. Review material processing changes before release and preserve human approvals.
