# ISO/IEC 38507:2022 AI-governance engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO/IEC standard text, governance advice for a particular organization, or a certification claim. Confirm the current edition through the [ISO/IEC 38507:2022 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/56641.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a governing-body record for organizational AI use: intended outcomes, decision rights, accountable executives, policies, risk and assurance inputs, lifecycle oversight, stakeholder concerns, material incidents, supplier dependencies, performance and impact reporting, and decisions to continue, modify, suspend, or retire use. Keep board and executive decisions traceable to evidence without delegating governance judgment to an AI system.

## Roles and annual rhythm

The governing body and delegated executives set direction and make accountable decisions; management maintains implementation records; technical and business teams provide lifecycle evidence; risk, legal, privacy, safety, and internal-audit functions provide independent challenge. AI may consolidate approved reports, identify stale governance artifacts, and draft questions, but cannot act as a governing body, set policy, approve a use case, make rights or safety decisions, accept risk, or give an assurance conclusion. Review material AI uses at least annually and whenever purpose, capability, deployment context, impact, supplier, or incident information materially changes.
