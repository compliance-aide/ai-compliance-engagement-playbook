# TISAX — licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not VDA/ENX content, a score, or certification. Consult the [ENX participant handbook](https://portal.enx.com/handbook/tisax-participant-handbook.html).

## Engagement focus

Confirm customer-requested scope and locations with authorized parties; maintain information-flow, location, supplier, corrective-action, and readiness evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain evidence; assessors retain independent judgment. AI classifies records and gaps, but cannot self-score, choose level, or make/share results. Review quarterly and before assessment.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [ENX TISAX participant handbook](https://portal.enx.com/handbook/tisax-participant-handbook.html), customer requirements, current authorized program materials, and organization-approved scope records; checked 2026-07-31. This is original preparation guidance, not VDA/ENX material, an assessment scope, a label/score, or a certification conclusion. Do not reproduce protected program criteria or disclose assessment results without authorized approval.

### 1. Customer request, location, and information-flow package

- **Request and owner:** Customer-facing, program, security, privacy, facilities, product, and site owners provide customer-request reference, approved assessment objective/scope rationale, legal-entity and location inventory, information-flow and asset context, accountable owner matrix, and approved timing/assessor-contact record.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected requested location or information flow to its owner, source record, approved scope rationale, current location status, and review date. This supports bounded preparation; it cannot select the assessment objective, determine required level, or decide what an assessor will include.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved location and flow metadata and flag changes or missing ownership. Humans decide scope, assessor engagement, and customer commitments. Refresh after a customer request, location/legal-entity, product/data-flow, facilities, or ownership change.

### 2. Site evidence, supplier, and corrective-action package

- **Request and owner:** Site, security, privacy, HR, facilities, engineering, supplier-management, and remediation owners provide organization-owned evidence index, local implementation/change records, supplier dependency records, corrective-action register, accountable owners, milestones, exception decisions, and retest evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a location-specific observation or corrective action to a dated source reference, site owner, stated scope/limitation, supplier dependency where relevant, approval, action, and retest or review record. This can support evidence provenance and remediation governance; it cannot score implementation, determine conformity, or close an assessor finding.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag missing provenance, overdue milestones, and inconsistent site metadata. Humans validate evidence, decide supplier actions and exceptions, and approve closure. Recollect after failed testing, supplier/material change, incident, location change, or remediation delay.

### 3. Assessment-readiness, result-handling, and renewal package

- **Request and owner:** Program, legal, customer, executive, and independent-review owners provide readiness calendar, authorized assessor/portal-contact records, independent challenge notes, result-access authorization, customer-sharing approvals, improvement decisions, and renewal/change-trigger records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected readiness or result-handling decision to a named authority, approved source/location, limitation, review date, and follow-up. This can show controlled preparation and disclosure governance; it cannot publish, interpret, or represent a TISAX result.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may maintain a redacted readiness ledger and flag missing approvals. Humans retain assessment, sharing, risk, and renewal decisions; independent reviewers challenge assumptions. Review quarterly, before assessment, and after material customer, site, program, or result-handling changes.
