# TISAX — licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not VDA/ENX content, a score, or certification. Consult the [ENX participant handbook](https://portal.enx.com/handbook/tisax-participant-handbook.html).

## Engagement focus

Confirm customer-requested scope and locations with authorized parties; maintain information-flow, location, supplier, corrective-action, and readiness evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain evidence; assessors retain independent judgment. AI classifies records and gaps, but cannot self-score, choose level, or make/share results. Review quarterly and before assessment.
