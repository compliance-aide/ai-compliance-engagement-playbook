# NIST SP 800-172 Revision 3 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not contractual applicability or compliance.
> Check the [NIST publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/172/r3/final)
> with the authoritative customer, contract, and policy owner.

## Engagement focus

Establish the information scope, threat-informed protection objective,
accountable owners, and decision authority. Maintain an enhancement roadmap tied
to assets, dependencies, suppliers, and changes; curate evidence and exception
decisions; hold periodic governance challenge; and issue a management-reviewed
annual protection posture narrative.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own scope, implementation, supplier, incident, and evidence records.
Independent reviewers challenge unsupported closure claims. AI coordinates
discovery and change-impact prompts, but cannot declare applicability or
compliance. Run periodic governance reviews and monitor version changes.
