# ISO/IEC 29134:2023 privacy-impact assessment — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or legal privacy advice. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/86012.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing-change intake, data-flow evidence, affected parties, risk considerations, decision owners, mitigations, residual-risk approvals, report records, and review triggers.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, product, security, and legal owners determine applicability and outcomes; independent reviewers test the evidence trail. AI maps inputs and drafts questions, but cannot decide legal sufficiency or accept privacy risk. Review before material changes and annually.
