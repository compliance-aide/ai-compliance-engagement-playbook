# NIST SP 800-193 platform-firmware-resiliency engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST firmware-resiliency content, a platform design, a firmware authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-193](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/193/final) and the organization’s approved security, engineering, architecture, procurement, legal, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed lifecycle for in-scope platform firmware and associated data resilience. Connect scope and asset inventory to approved platform architecture, protection, detection, recovery, supplier and procurement decisions, deployment and configuration evidence, access, monitoring, tests and exercises, changes, incidents, remediation, and retirement. A firmware inventory, configuration extract, supplier statement, test result, recovery record, or audit log is evidence to assess; none alone proves a platform is resilient, firmware is authentic, recovery will succeed, or compliance.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, security, platform or hardware engineering, architecture, endpoint or infrastructure operations, asset owners, procurement, supplier-management, legal and privacy where relevant, change-management, incident-response, business continuity, and records-management roles. Operators maintain scoped platform and firmware inventories, approved architecture and procurement decisions, supplier and component traceability, configuration and access baselines, detection and recovery evidence, test and exercise records, exceptions, incidents, remediation, and retirement evidence. Reconcile platforms, firmware versions, owners, suppliers, and recovery readiness quarterly; review unauthorized-change alerts, configuration changes, supplier notices, exceptions, incidents, and unresolved risks at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material platform, firmware, provider, threat, or publication changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples inventory-to-protection-to-detection-to-recovery traceability; auditors test supplied evidence without modifying firmware, changing platform configuration, approving exceptions, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied inventory, supplier, configuration, monitoring, recovery, and review evidence, identify missing owners or stale decisions, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot modify firmware, select platform security architecture, change production configuration, authorize procurement or deployment, approve an exception, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
