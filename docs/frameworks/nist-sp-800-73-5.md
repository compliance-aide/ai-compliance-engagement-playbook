# NIST SP 800-73-5 PIV interfaces engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST interface specifications, smart-card or client-API implementation instructions, credential issuance authority, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-73-5 Part 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/73/pt1/5/final), Parts 2 and 3, and the organization’s approved identity, security, engineering, privacy, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed lifecycle for in-scope PIV card applications, data handling, interfaces, client integrations, devices, middleware, and relying systems. Maintain traceability from use case and system owner through approved interface and version baseline, test and interoperability evidence, deployment, access controls, changes, incident handling, exceptions, supplier dependencies, and lifecycle retirement. A successful interface test, card read, client log, or vendor statement is evidence to assess; it does not prove secure configuration, credential validity, approved access, interoperability in every environment, or compliance.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, identity and access management, security, endpoint or device management, platform engineering, application and relying-system owners, privacy, legal, procurement, supplier-management, change-management, incident-response, and records-management roles. Operators maintain an in-scope interface inventory, approved version and configuration baselines, device and middleware records, integration test results, deployment and change evidence, access and anomaly records, interoperability defects, exceptions, supplier assurances, and decommissioning records. Reconcile deployed interface versions, devices, middleware, and relying systems quarterly; review changes, failed authentication or interface events, supplier notices, exceptions, and unresolved risks at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material credential, device, application, provider, or policy changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples integration-to-deployment-to-change traceability; auditors test the evidence trail without issuing credentials, changing card or client configuration, granting access, approving exceptions, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied inventory, test, deployment, change, and review evidence, identify missing ownership or stale versions, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot implement or change a card interface or client API, issue or use a credential, grant access, access private keys, approve an exception, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
