# Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules system engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not program requirements, legal advice or certification. Draft pending independent source, skeptical and rights review.

## Source and applicability

Use the [Forum document catalog](https://www.globalcbpr.org/documents/) and [privacy certification page](https://www.globalcbpr.org/privacy-certifications/). Read 2026-09-04: the catalog labels one CBPR requirements set valid through March 31, 2027 and another effective April 1, 2027. Confirm the applicable assessment/transition instructions with the recognized Accountability Agent; do not mix editions or treat publication as immediate effect.

The certification page assigns certification authority to Forum-recognized Accountability Agents and distinguishes CBPR from the processor-focused PRP system. Confirm jurisdictional eligibility, entity role and the agent's current recognition for the actual program. Forum membership alone is not an organization's certification.

Only these overview/catalog pages were read for this draft. Full requirements, questionnaire, policies, transition rules, local law and rights clearance remain unverified. Obtain authorized source documents before making requirement-level conclusions; reference protected material rather than reproducing it here.

## Engagement focus

Prepare a complete, traceable record of the applicant's actual privacy practices within the proposed scope. Separate internal readiness, Accountability Agent review, issued certification, public listing and legal transfer analysis. A completed questionnaire does not establish any of the later states.

## Roles

Privacy, legal and business owners approve scope and legal interpretation. Product, security, records and provider owners supply operating evidence. The recognized Accountability Agent performs its program role; an internal independent reviewer challenges readiness and gaps without replacing it. Named management officials approve applications, contracts, transfers, remedies and public claims.

AI may reconcile authorized records, index evidence and draft questions/workpapers. AI cannot determine legal eligibility, approve transfers, select or engage an Accountability Agent, submit an application, issue a certification result, use certification marks or claim compliance. No personal-data access beyond explicit authorization is implied.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record applicant legal entity, headquarters, program/role, services, period, intended assessment date, owners, evidence access and approved purpose. Obtain current requirements and compatible questionnaire, agent recognition evidence and transition instructions. Unknown edition or role blocks dependent answers; independent processing-inventory work can continue.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Confirm the route.** Map entity, jurisdiction and role to the intended certification program and recognized agent. Record actual recognition sources and any unresolved eligibility questions. Output: route decision packet for authorized owners.
2. **Freeze the assessment baseline.** Identify requirements, questionnaire and policy versions with effective dates. Record any transition decision. Output: versioned source register; do not silently overwrite an earlier assessment when the catalog changes.
3. **Reconcile processing scope.** Inventory all included services, data categories, purposes, countries, recipients and roles. Compare product, procurement and data-flow records. Output: full scoped population with exclusions and unknowns assigned to owners.
4. **Build requirement evidence.** For every applicable item, link the approved policy, actual practice, test criterion and evidence period. Keep no-evidence answers visible; a drafted policy does not prove operation. Output: complete answer/evidence matrix without copied protected requirements.
5. **Test operational behavior.** Use authorized QA fixtures or supplied redacted case records to examine notice/choice, rights, retention, complaints and provider handoffs as relevant to verified requirements. Record expected and observed outcomes separately. Output: reproducible results and coverage gaps.
6. **Resolve and review gaps.** Assign each contradiction to an owner, remedy, approved decision and retest. Give the independent reviewer the complete packet and disagreements. Output: readiness findings that remain separate from the Accountability Agent's assessment.
7. **Prepare the handoff.** Freeze the exact package for authorized application submission. Track questions, revisions, decisions and receipts. Verify issued scope and current official status before preparing any public claim. Output: state-specific record, never an inferred certification.

## Evidence and test plan

### Processing, transfer and scope package

Restore the PR340 package with complete population tracking. Privacy, legal and business owners provide entity/service scope, data flows, purposes, categories, countries, recipient roles and decisions. Tie each transfer context to its legal analysis separately from certification. Do not extend one entity's scope to its affiliates, acquisitions or new services without evidence.

### Notice, choice, access and incident package

Privacy, operations and security owners supply versioned notices, choice behavior, rights/complaint cases, incident records and retention evidence. Trace every known scoped channel and retain coverage states. Test the actual downstream effect of a choice or correction, not just the UI acknowledgement. Record historical versions and populations; a current fix does not establish prior conformity. Keep request identity checks and response authority with the designated humans.

### Recipient oversight and certification-readiness package

Procurement, privacy and leadership supply recipient inventories, instructions/contracts, due diligence, operating evidence, remediation and review records. A recipient certificate supports only its verified program/entity/scope/status; it does not prove this particular transfer or integration is appropriate. Link every questionnaire answer to an owner and evidence. Track prepared, approved, submitted, under review and issued states independently, with current public status and mark permissions separately verified.

For each test record approved criterion, source/version, scope, period, fixture/evidence reference, expected result, actual result and limitations. Preserve the entire known population; risk-based test selection does not justify omitting untested services from the ledger. Keep personal data and confidential assessment material outside this public repository.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Conflicting editions: stop affected answers and obtain an authoritative transition decision; retain both versions and their dates.
- Only PRP evidence for a CBPR claim: mark the CBPR status claim inconclusive unless direct evidence resolves it. Program names are not interchangeable.
- Public claim extends beyond issued scope: mark the factual scope match not_supported, preserve the claim and route correction to its owner.
- Policy promise contradicted by QA behavior: retain a not_supported factual result and remediation owner; legal and certification conclusions stay with their authorities.
- Listing absent or inconsistent with supplied certificate: investigate the authoritative record; do not infer either certification or revocation solely from a failed search.

Fictional desk case: a questionnaire says all included services honor a preference, but one confirmed in-scope service continues the disallowed QA data flow. Complete implementation is not_supported; other services retain their own results. Submitting the questionnaire would not cure the contradiction.

## Cadence and renewal

Use verified program/agent requirements for renewal, monitoring and change notifications. Reopen affected work for scope, supplier, jurisdiction, incident or source changes. Schedule the known 2027 edition transition as a decision and evidence task; do not assume current certification automatically satisfies the future edition or expires on its effective date.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver route and edition decisions, full processing/recipient inventory, requirement/evidence matrix, test results, gaps, retests, reviewer disagreements and authorized package versions. Classify assertions supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable or not_tested with reasons. Keep readiness, certification, transfer legality and permission to publish distinct. No application, transfer or certification assessment occurred in authoring this guide.
