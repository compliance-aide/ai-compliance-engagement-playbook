# Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules system engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Global CBPR program requirements, legal advice, a certification assessment, or a compliance claim. Confirm current materials through the [Global CBPR Forum privacy certifications](https://www.globalcbpr.org/privacy-certifications/) and applicable jurisdictional law.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a cross-border privacy assurance record that connects processing purposes, personal-data categories, organizational and processor roles, transfer contexts, accountable privacy and business owners, supplier dependencies, evidence, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Keep certification preparation distinct from a legal determination, a data-transfer approval, or a claim of certification.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, privacy, legal, security, risk, and business authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve evidence; recognized accountability agents and independent reviewers perform their defined review roles. AI may organize authorized records, identify evidence gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine legal applicability, approve processing or transfers, select an accountability agent, assign an assessment result, access personal data without authorization, submit an application, or claim certification or compliance. Review annually and before material data, supplier, transfer, incident, jurisdiction, or certification-scope changes.
