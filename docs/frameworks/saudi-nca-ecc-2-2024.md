# Saudi NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls ECC 2-2024 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCA control text, an assessment, a regulatory submission, or a compliance claim. Confirm the current scope and edition through the [NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls page](https://nca.gov.sa/en/regulatory-documents/controls-list/ecc/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a scoped cybersecurity engagement record for the national entity: applicability decisions, accountable leadership, information and technology assets, service dependencies, operating evidence, incidents, assessment evidence, remediation, and material changes. Keep the current ECC edition, related NCA extensions, and any sector-specific regime separately identified; do not treat a broad control inventory as proof of implementation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Accountable entity leadership owns program decisions; cybersecurity and risk teams maintain operating evidence; system and service owners retain technical records; supplier owners retain third-party proof; independent reviewers test provenance and traceability; qualified Saudi counsel interprets applicability. AI may organize approved evidence, flag stale records, and draft review questions, but cannot decide scope, score compliance, perform an assessment, approve risk, submit to NCA, or claim conformity. Review the engagement annually and before material asset, service, supplier, operating-model, or incident changes.
