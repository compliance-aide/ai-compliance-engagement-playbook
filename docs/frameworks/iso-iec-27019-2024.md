# ISO/IEC 27019:2024 energy-utility engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO/IEC standard text, a safety assessment, or a certification conclusion. Confirm the licensed source through the [ISO/IEC 27019:2024 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/85056.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an energy-utility engagement record: service and process-control boundaries, accountable operations and security owners, dependencies, operating evidence, material changes, incidents, exercises, risk decisions, and remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Utility operations and safety owners determine operational priorities; security and engineering teams retain evidence; independent reviewers assess traceability. AI may organize approved evidence and draft non-operational questions, but cannot make safety decisions, change process-control systems, determine adequacy, or certify a program. Review after material service, supplier, or threat changes and preserve human approvals.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
