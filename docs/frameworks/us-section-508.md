# U.S. Revised Section 508 Standards — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an agency exception or compliance representation. See the [GSA Section 508 policy resource](https://www.section508.gov/manage/laws-and-policies/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain covered ICT, applicability, procurement/build scope, exception, vendor ACR/VPAT, testing, defect, remediation, escalation, and portfolio evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Human leads decide scope and exceptions; independent reviewers test evidence. AI assists inventory and issue dedupe, but cannot validate testing, rely solely on vendor statements, or make compliance claims. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [GSA Section 508 laws and policies resource](https://www.section508.gov/manage/laws-and-policies/), current acquisition instructions, and authorized accessibility/legal direction; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not reproduce standards, determine an exception, validate accessibility, or make a Section 508 compliance representation.

### 1. ICT inventory, scope, and exception package

- **Request and owner:** Accessibility, product, procurement, system, records, legal, and program owners provide the covered ICT inventory, user/context boundary, acquisition or build record, accountable owner, planned release/use, known accommodation/escalation route, and exception/undue-burden records where applicable.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one inventory item from intended use through accountable owner, delivery/acquisition record, stated scope, and exception status. This cannot determine legal coverage, approve an exception, or prove inventory completeness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may deduplicate authorized inventory records and flag missing owner, release, or review fields. Humans determine scope, accommodation, procurement, and exception decisions. Refresh after new ICT, material feature, procurement, audience, or ownership change.

### 2. Accessibility evaluation and remediation package

- **Request and owner:** Accessibility, development, quality, vendor, and support owners provide evaluation approach and results, representative assistive-technology/browser context, defect records, user-reported barriers, remediation approvals, retest references, and limitation disclosures.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one reported defect from evaluation or intake through reproducibility context, accountable remediation decision, change/retest record, limitation, and closure status. This cannot validate the full product, replace expert testing, or determine accessibility conformance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may group duplicate issue reports, index approved findings, and flag stale remediation records. Humans choose tests, validate results, prioritize fixes, and approve releases. Refresh after material UI/content/platform changes and before major procurement/release decisions.

### 3. Supplier assurance, reporting, and independent-review package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, accessibility, legal, vendor-management, program, and independent-review owners provide supplier accessibility claims/ACRs or equivalent records, contract requirements, evaluation limitations, exception decisions, remediation commitments, portfolio reports, and reviewer workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one supplier or portfolio statement to its source record, applicable item/version, human review, declared limitation, and follow-up action. This cannot rely solely on a supplier statement, approve procurement, or make a compliance claim.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied assurance artifacts and flag stale versions, missing limitations, or overdue actions. Authorized humans approve supplier decisions, public/internal statements, and closure. Review quarterly and during the annual independent review.
