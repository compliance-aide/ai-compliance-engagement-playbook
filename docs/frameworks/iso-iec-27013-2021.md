# ISO/IEC 27013:2021 integrated-management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a management-system integration approval, a certification conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27013:2021 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/78752.html) and its applicable updates.

## Engagement focus

Manage the connection between information-security and service-management work as a traceable operating model. Maintain an integration record that connects shared scope boundaries, accountable owners, common processes, distinct responsibilities, evidence sources, measurements, change and incident handoffs, audit observations, exceptions, and management decisions. Preserve clear boundaries where one program’s evidence supports another without treating it as automatic equivalence or duplicating assurance conclusions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Security, service-management, technology, operations, risk, compliance, internal-audit, privacy, legal, and executive owners retain their decisions. Program owners maintain their respective systems and agree documented handoffs; process owners preserve operating evidence; executive management approves objectives and resources; and certification authorities remain independent. Independent reviewers test selected shared processes across both systems for ownership, evidence provenance, change handling, measurement, exception governance, and conclusions that are supported within the applicable scope.

AI may map authorized records to shared and distinct process fields, surface missing handoffs, compare evidence references, draft workplans, and prepare non-authoritative review workpapers. AI cannot merge scopes, decide equivalence, approve objectives, certify a system, alter operational records, accept risk, or claim compliance. Reassess integration after material service, security, organizational, supplier, tool, incident, or scope changes; perform quarterly owner reviews and an annual independent readiness review.
