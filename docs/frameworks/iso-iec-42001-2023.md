# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for AI management, not a statement of trustworthy AI or an ISO
> certification claim. Use authorized material and check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001).

## Engagement focus

Maintain AI-system inventory, intended uses, stakeholders, lifecycle owner,
impact/risk authority, oversight, and withdrawal triggers. Before release or
material change, document purpose, dependencies, evaluation limits, human-use
boundaries, monitoring, incidents, exceptions, and approved decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators govern lifecycle decisions and corrective action. Independent reviewers
validate decision traceability and evidence. AI links inventory to evidence and
flags anomalies and aging decisions; it cannot self-approve, set risk appetite,
or independently review its own work. Reassess system scope and governance
effectiveness annually.
