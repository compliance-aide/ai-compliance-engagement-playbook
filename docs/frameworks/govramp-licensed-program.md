# GovRAMP — licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not program content or an authorization claim. Check the [GovRAMP security program](https://govramp.org/security-program) and current sponsor instructions.

## Engagement focus

Confirm offering/version, sponsor, status, impact category, boundary, evidence owner, risk, remediation, change, incident, monitoring, and assessment-readiness records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain service evidence; independent reviewers test the evidence while qualified third parties, PMO, and sponsors remain independent decision makers. AI identifies staleness and drafts nonbinding narratives, but cannot call a product authorized, submit/attest, or publish status.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [GovRAMP security program](https://govramp.org/security-program) and current sponsor instructions. Treat program material as licensed or restricted unless the publisher says otherwise; link rather than copy it. Retrieve status, offering category, sponsor process, and edition information for the engagement record before planning. This is original evidence-planning language, not a GovRAMP authorization method or program content.

### 1. Offering, sponsor, and authorization-status boundary

- **Request and owner:** Request the approved service/offering inventory, service-version identifiers, customer or sponsor relationship record, documented boundary and inherited-service inputs, program-status communication approval, and named offering, security, sponsor-liaison, and legal owners. Cover the offering and time period formally approved for the engagement, rather than every company service.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected represented offering from its catalog/release identifier through boundary inputs, accountable owner, sponsor-facing status source, and approved external wording. Reconcile dated status claims to an authoritative program or sponsor record without copying protected program material. This can support an accountable representation trail; it cannot establish authorization, impact categorization, procurement eligibility, or program conformance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories, status dates, and ownership metadata and flag conflicting representations. A named human confirms scope and external claims; program and sponsor authorities make their own decisions. Trigger on a new offering, material boundary/version change, sponsor change, or status communication.

### 2. Assessment, finding, and remediation lineage

- **Request and owner:** Obtain authorized assessment references, evidence-request registers, finding/remediation logs, risk-decision references, corrective-action owners and dates, and independent-review workpapers from security, engineering, operations, and program-management owners. Preserve only permitted identifiers and links for the defined assessment cycle.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a finding or readiness concern from intake through evidence owner, approved corrective action, retest/reference, and current disposition; inspect that independence and decision authority are recorded. This can support traceability of the organization’s response process; it cannot score an assessment, validate remediation effectiveness across the service, accept risk, or close a program finding.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize sanitized workpaper metadata, identify overdue actions, and draft questions. Humans determine risk treatment and remediation acceptance; assessors and program authorities retain assessment decisions. Trigger on an assessment request, new material finding, missed action date, or renewal preparation.

### 3. Operational change, incident, and monitoring record

- **Request and owner:** Request approved architecture/change records, material supplier and shared-service changes, access/monitoring summary references, incident and recovery decision records, continuous-monitoring calendar, and management-review minutes from engineering, operations, security, and supplier-management owners. Define the service boundary and reporting period before collection.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected material change or incident to affected offering scope, review/approval metadata, monitoring or follow-up record, customer/sponsor communication decision where applicable, and closure rationale. This can support a lifecycle evidence trail; it cannot prove monitoring coverage, incident absence, timeliness, or required notification compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved records and flag missing change-to-boundary links or stale reviews, but cannot operate production controls, report an incident, or decide notification obligations. Human owners make those decisions. Trigger on a material service, supplier, access, incident, or monitoring change and at annual planning.
