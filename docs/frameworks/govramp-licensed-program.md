# GovRAMP — licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not program content or an authorization claim. Check the [GovRAMP security program](https://govramp.org/security-program) and current sponsor instructions.

## Engagement focus

Confirm offering/version, sponsor, status, impact category, boundary, evidence owner, risk, remediation, change, incident, monitoring, and assessment-readiness records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain service evidence; independent reviewers test the evidence while qualified third parties, PMO, and sponsors remain independent decision makers. AI identifies staleness and drafts nonbinding narratives, but cannot call a product authorized, submit/attest, or publish status.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
