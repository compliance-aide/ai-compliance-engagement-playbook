# GovRAMP — licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not licensed program content, an assessment method or an authorization claim. Draft pending independent source, skeptical and rights review.

## Source and applicability

Use the [security program](https://govramp.org/security-program), [document library](https://govramp.org/document-library/) and [Program Participants directory](https://govramp.org/program-participants), together with current sponsor/buyer instructions. Preserve licensed or restricted documents in authorized engagement storage; do not reproduce them here.

The directory introduction read 2026-09-04 distinguishes verified and progressing offerings. Its Authorized Product List includes Core, Ready, Provisionally Authorized and Authorized statuses. Presence on that list therefore does not alone mean the offering's status is Authorized. The introduction distinguishes Core PMO validation from independent 3PAO assessments for the other listed verification routes and identifies ongoing monitoring.

Full current program policies, impact baselines, boundary/inheritance rules, monitoring requirements and sponsor procedures remain unverified in this draft. Confirm the exact pathway before prescribing required artifacts, assessors or deadlines. No individual product's current status is established by this guide.

## Engagement focus

Connect the exact offering and actual operating boundary to program evidence, findings, remediation and status representations. Keep readiness, third-party assessment, PMO review, sponsor decision, public status and buyer authorization distinct. A parent cloud's status cannot establish the application offering's status.

## Roles

Offering and security owners maintain scope and evidence. Engineering, operations and suppliers provide actual implementation records. A sponsor liaison tracks the selected pathway. Qualified assessors, PMO and applicable sponsoring authorities retain their defined decisions; an independent internal reviewer challenges readiness. Legal and business owners approve public representations and procurement interpretations.

AI may reconcile authorized metadata, identify stale or conflicting records and draft workpapers. AI cannot score an official assessment, categorize impact, accept risk, close program findings, submit attestations, authorize a product or publish status. Production changes and external submissions require their designated authority.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Obtain approved offering name, legal entity, version/build, regions, features, information types, intended status/impact level, pathway, owners and evidence permissions. Record each source version and missing sponsor/program decision. Do not assume a universal sponsor prerequisite for every readiness activity; verify the selected route.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Define offering and route.** Match the proposed service to its actual boundary, program pathway and buyer requirements. Record questions about impact or sponsor decisions for their owners. Output: approved scope/route register.
2. **Verify status representations.** Match any claim to the authoritative offering record, exact status, level, date and scope. Preserve official wording and distinguish ongoing assessment from issued status. Output: claim/source matrix; a directory section heading is not a product status.
3. **Reconcile dependencies.** Map all components, regions, integrations and shared services in scope. Document provider, inherited and customer responsibilities separately using authorized sources. Output: full boundary/inheritance register with uncovered dependencies retained.
4. **Build the assessment packet.** Reconcile every applicable evidence request with owner, current implementation, period, approved method and criterion. Output: complete evidence manifest; a submitted artifact is not a favorable assessment result.
5. **Track findings and retests.** Preserve assessor observations, provider responses, corrective actions, risk decisions and actual retest evidence. Output: finding lineage that distinguishes internal closure from program acceptance. Do not overwrite a failed test with an exception approval.
6. **Verify continuing operation.** Connect monitoring coverage, changes, incidents and recovery to the same boundary and current build. Route program/sponsor notifications according to verified requirements. Output: lifecycle register with exact reporting periods and missing coverage.
7. **Review and hand off.** Give the reviewer full evidence, gaps and disagreements. Prepare the authorized package and decision requests. After any approved external action, verify the corresponding decision or receipt independently. Output: separate submission, acceptance, status and buyer-decision records.

## Evidence and test plan

### Offering, sponsor and status-boundary package

Restore PR340's package with complete scoped-offering reconciliation. Offering, security and liaison owners provide catalog/release identifiers, boundary decisions, pathway/sponsor records and approved external wording. Match every claimed feature and region to actual scope. Preserve historical and current status dates; do not extend an old letter to changed services without a decision.

### Assessment, finding and remediation package

Security, engineering and program owners provide authorized assessment references, complete evidence-request inventory, findings, owners, action dates, risk decisions and retests. Retain every known finding and unresolved request rather than sampling away adverse entries. Separate source availability, actual test coverage and assessment disposition. A green internal dashboard cannot close an assessor's observation.

### Operational change, incident and monitoring package

Engineering and operations provide current architecture/build, supplier changes, monitoring asset coverage, failures, incident chronology, recovery results and approved communication decisions. Reconcile the full asset/service population to monitoring results; empty outputs do not establish incident absence or healthy operation. Track failed collection, excluded assets and unobserved periods explicitly. Verify actual deployed behavior after a change rather than relying on a merge or ticket.

Every test records criterion, authorized method, expected result, actual result, build/scope/time, evidence reference and limitation. Keep protected assessment packages, credentials and customer data out of the public repository. Complete population accounting is not a claim that every possible technical case was tested.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Offering appears on the APL with Ready status: preserve Ready; do not promote the wording to Authorized.
- Claim names a feature or region outside verified scope: mark the factual scope match not_supported and route correction to its owner.
- Inherited service is verified but customer controls lack evidence: retain the inheritance evidence and classify the customer portion separately as inconclusive or not_tested.
- Monitoring fails or misses assets: retain the failure and affected population; do not report zero findings as complete coverage.
- Source or status records conflict: seek authoritative resolution; an absent search result alone does not prove revocation or lack of participation.

Fictional desk case: an offering's official record says Ready, while its proposal says Authorized because it appears on the Authorized Product List. The listing fact is supported; the claimed status is not_supported. Buyer eligibility remains a separate decision under the actual solicitation.

## Cadence and renewal

Populate monitoring, reassessment and renewal dates from verified current pathway rules and award records. Keep operational review triggers for boundary, build, provider, incident and sponsor changes. Do not invent universal frequencies or assume continued listing proves every ongoing obligation was met. Recheck status at the time of each material representation.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope/route decisions, authoritative status references, complete boundary/dependency inventory, evidence manifest, finding lineage, monitoring gaps, reviewer disagreements and named next actions. Classify assertions supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable or not_tested. Preserve the distinction between internal readiness and program/buyer decisions. No actual program submission, assessment or authorization occurred through authoring this guide.
