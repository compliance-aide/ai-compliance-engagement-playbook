# IEC TS 62443-1-1:2009 IACS security concepts engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a terminology ruling, an industrial safety decision, a security-level determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC TS 62443-1-1:2009 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/7029).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a shared engagement glossary and boundary record for industrial security work. Connect systems, assets, interfaces, accountable owners, safety and process dependencies, decisions, evidence terms, unresolved ambiguities, and independent review so that engineering, operations, auditors, and AI systems are using a consistent working model without treating generated prose as an authoritative interpretation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, maintenance, security, risk, and supplier authorities retain ownership of terminology, architecture, safety, and risk decisions; system and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers challenge ambiguous scope, definitions, and traceability. AI may organize authorized vocabulary, surface inconsistent labels, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot establish a binding interpretation, set a security level, decide a safety or engineering outcome, accept risk, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Refresh the shared model annually and before material system, process, supplier, safety, incident, architecture, or governance changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [IEC TS 62443-1-1:2009 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/7029) and an authorized copy; checked 2026-07-31. IEC text is licensed. This original plan does not reproduce terminology, issue a binding interpretation, determine a security level, or make a safety, engineering, or compliance conclusion.

### 1. Working-boundary and vocabulary package

- **Request and owner:** Human-maintained system/asset boundary record, approved working glossary, interface labels, accountable owners, and a log of undefined or disputed terms, from OT engineering and governance owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected term or boundary label to its owner, context, date, and unresolved ambiguity. This supports shared evidence handling; it cannot establish an authoritative interpretation or complete architecture.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify inconsistent labels and draft questions. Humans resolve terms and scope. Refresh before material architecture, ownership, or governance changes.

### 2. Context and decision-trace package

- **Request and owner:** Process/safety dependency context, approved diagrams or inventories, decision records, assumptions, and references to authorized evidence, from operations, safety, and system owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected decision term to its stated context, human authority, and evidence reference. This supports traceability; it cannot decide safety impact, engineering outcome, or risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may map authorized references into a read-only workpaper; it cannot change records or infer approvals. Reopen after an incident, process change, or material supplier change.

### 3. Ambiguity escalation and independent challenge package

- **Request and owner:** Ambiguity/escalation register, human resolutions or deferrals, review dates, affected evidence links, and independent reviewer challenge notes.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected ambiguity to a responsible authority, documented disposition, limitation, and revisit date. This supports disciplined escalation; it cannot turn a draft resolution into a binding framework interpretation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag unowned or overdue entries. Independent reviewers test traceability and boundary clarity. Review annually and whenever a material interpretation question emerges.
