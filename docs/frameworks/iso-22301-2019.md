# ISO 22301:2019 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for continuity, not an ISO publication or recovery signoff. Use
> an authorized source and check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html)
> and current amendment/revision status.

## Engagement focus

Set services, dependencies, disruption assumptions, recovery objectives,
authorities, and stakeholder commitments. Refresh dependencies and recovery risks,
exercise realistic scenarios, retain plans and evidence, assign corrective actions,
and close with management resourcing and risk decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own recovery decisions, exercises, and fixes. Independent reviewers
test traceability between claims, evidence, decisions, and exercises. AI indexes
evidence, flags stale dependencies, and drafts scenario questions; it never
declares recovery capability or signs off. Review quarterly and close annually.
