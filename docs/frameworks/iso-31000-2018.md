# ISO 31000:2018 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Recheck the [ISO version record](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html) at engagement start.

## Engagement focus

Maintain an enterprise risk-decision register, significant-change reassessments, treatments, ownership, and governance evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management chooses appetite and treatments; independent reviewers test traceability. AI organizes risk inputs and flags stale owners, but cannot accept risk or certify. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm applicability through the [ISO 31000:2018 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html) and obtain licensed source material through authorized channels; checked 2026-07-31. ### 1. Context, risk input, and ownership package

- **Request and owner:** Risk and business owners supply scope/context records, objectives, stakeholder inputs, risk statements, assumptions, source evidence, ratings rationale, and accountable owner assignments.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected significant risk to its stated context, evidence input, owner, and recorded rationale. This supports decision traceability for the sample; it does not prove every risk has been identified or rated correctly.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile duplicate risks and flag missing owners. Humans set context and approve risk statements. Refresh after strategy, service, supplier, regulatory, or threat change.

### 2. Treatment, exception, and change package

- **Request and owner:** Control/process owners provide chosen treatments, implementation milestones, dependencies, exception requests, compensating measures, approvals, and change-impact reassessments.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected treatment or exception from rationale through decision authority, implementation evidence, expiry, and reassessment. This supports a controlled decision trail; it cannot accept risk or establish treatment effectiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag due dates and inconsistent status. Humans choose treatments, approve exceptions, and authorize operational changes. Recollect after material change or missed milestone.

### 3. Monitoring, challenge, and management-review package

- **Request and owner:** Risk owners provide indicators, review records, escalation logs, independent challenge notes, residual-risk decisions, and management-review outcomes.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected indicator breach to escalation, challenge, decision, and follow-up. This supports governance responsiveness; it cannot establish that monitoring detects all emerging risks or replace independent review.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize trends and identify stale reviews. Humans approve appetite, residual risk, resources, and public assertions. Review quarterly and annually.
