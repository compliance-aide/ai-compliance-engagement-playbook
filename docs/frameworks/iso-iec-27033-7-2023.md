# ISO/IEC 27033-7:2023 network virtualization security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a virtual-network approval. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80972.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain virtual-network scope, tenant and service boundaries, architecture decisions, configuration baselines, access records, change evidence, monitoring, test results, exceptions, and supplier responsibilities.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Platform and security owners approve architecture and production changes; independent reviewers sample segregation evidence. AI correlates configuration records and flags anomalies, but cannot change infrastructure or approve exceptions. Review changes continuously and assess annually.
