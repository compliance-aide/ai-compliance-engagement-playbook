# NIST SP 800-63C-4 — federation engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not federation-assurance advice. See [NIST SP 800-63C-4](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/63/c/4/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain CSP, relying party, trust link, assertion, attribute release, metadata, key, certificate, login, deprovisioning, incident, and offboarding evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans approve trust and attribute decisions; independent reviewers sample paths. AI reconciles changes, but cannot make federation conclusions. Review quarterly and annually.
