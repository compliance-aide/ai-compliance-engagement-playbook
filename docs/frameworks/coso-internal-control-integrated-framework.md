# COSO Internal Control—Integrated Framework engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not COSO framework content, a controls evaluation, or an effectiveness conclusion. Confirm current materials and permitted use through the [COSO internal-control guidance page](https://www.coso.org/guidance-on-ic/pages/default.aspx).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a traceable record of objectives, accountable owners, control activities, evidence of operation, change impacts, monitoring, deficiencies, remediation, and governance decisions across the reporting period.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management owns design and operation; process owners retain evidence; internal audit or independent reviewers evaluate the evidence trail; governance bodies decide remediation and risk matters. AI maps approved evidence to objectives, identifies stale ownership or gaps, and prepares questions, but cannot evaluate effectiveness, accept risk, or issue an audit conclusion. Perform periodic monitoring, preserve review results, and retain human judgments.
