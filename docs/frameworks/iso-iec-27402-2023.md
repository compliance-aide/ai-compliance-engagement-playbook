# ISO/IEC 27402:2023 IoT device-baseline engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a device-baseline determination, a product approval, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27402:2023 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80136.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a device-baseline evidence record connecting device identities, versions, interfaces, accountable product owners, security and privacy evidence, supplier dependencies, lifecycle records, exceptions, remediation, and independent review. Use AI to organize authorized evidence and identify open questions without treating its output as a release or baseline decision.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product, engineering, security, privacy, operations, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; device and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test inventory accuracy, lifecycle traceability, exception handling, and change readiness. AI may organize authorized evidence, flag stale versions, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot establish a baseline, approve a device, determine sufficiency, accept risk, alter production configurations, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review releases and material changes continuously, hold quarterly evidence reviews, and perform an annual baseline reassessment.
