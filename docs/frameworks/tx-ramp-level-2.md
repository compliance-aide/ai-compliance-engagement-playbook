# TX-RAMP Level 2 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Texas DIR criteria, a certification claim, or an authorization decision. Confirm current scope and tier requirements through [TX-RAMP eligibility and requirements](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/texas-risk-and-authorization-management-program-tx-ramp/tx-ramp-eligibility).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a cloud-service engagement record for the approved tier: agency and provider boundaries, data and system context, evidence custodians, assessment artifacts, material changes, supplier dependencies, monitoring, conditions, and remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Agency and provider owners decide scope and risk matters; service teams retain evidence; independent reviewers test the evidence trail; Texas DIR controls certification outcomes. AI organizes approved records, tracks changes and open actions, and drafts review questions, but cannot determine tier, assess conformance, or submit certification materials. Review continuously, reevaluate after material changes, and retain human approval for official communications.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [TX-RAMP eligibility and requirements page](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/texas-risk-and-authorization-management-program-tx-ramp/tx-ramp-eligibility), checked 2026-07-31, plus current agency-authorized scope, service, and assessment records. This original plan does not reproduce DIR criteria or decide certification, tier, or assessment outcome.

### 1. Authorized cloud-service scope package

- **Request and owner:** Agency sponsor, provider product/security/privacy owners, and system custodians supply the approved service description, environments and integrations, data/sensitivity categorization references, authorization boundary, agency-provider responsibility matrix, inventory links, and supplier/subprocessor map.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected service path from agency use case to component/environment, data boundary, accountable owner, and responsibility allocation. This supports a coherent evidence population; it cannot establish eligibility, authorization scope, or tier.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories and flag conflicting ownership or missing links. Agency and provider authorities approve scope and risk treatment. Refresh after a material architecture, tenant, data, integration, or supplier change.

### 2. Sustained security-operation package

- **Request and owner:** Security operations, engineering, HR, service delivery, and supplier owners provide approved operating procedures, asset/access/change records, monitoring and incident records, vulnerability/remediation evidence, secure-development/release artifacts, training/role records, and approved exceptions covering the selected period.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected evidence items from population and timestamp through owner, review, approved outcome, and any corrective action. This establishes documentary traceability, not the design or effectiveness of safeguards, assessment sufficiency, or certification status.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized evidence metadata and identify missing period coverage, record provenance, or overdue actions. Humans choose samples, interpret results, approve remediation, and accept risk. Refresh after an incident, control/process failure, release, or material supplier change.

### 3. Assessment and continuous-monitoring package

- **Request and owner:** Agency liaison, provider leadership, records, legal, and security owners provide approved assessment-planning records, evidence index, change log, monitoring/review cadence, finding and condition register, response owners, retention decisions, and submission authority evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected change, finding, or monitoring action to its source record, owner, escalation/approval, corrective action, and closure or re-review. This supports readiness and continuity; it cannot make an assessment finding, close a DIR condition, or submit materials.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may maintain a read-only internal action queue and source-change watch. Named humans approve all statements, submissions, closures, and risk decisions. Review on assessment planning, material changes, unresolved findings, DIR updates, and annually.
