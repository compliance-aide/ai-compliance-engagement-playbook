# TX-RAMP Level 2 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Texas DIR criteria, a certification claim, or an authorization decision. Confirm current scope and tier requirements through [TX-RAMP eligibility and requirements](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/texas-risk-and-authorization-management-program-tx-ramp/tx-ramp-eligibility).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a cloud-service engagement record for the approved tier: agency and provider boundaries, data and system context, evidence custodians, assessment artifacts, material changes, supplier dependencies, monitoring, conditions, and remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Agency and provider owners decide scope and risk matters; service teams retain evidence; independent reviewers test the evidence trail; Texas DIR controls certification outcomes. AI organizes approved records, tracks changes and open actions, and drafts review questions, but cannot determine tier, assess conformance, or submit certification materials. Review continuously, reevaluate after material changes, and retain human approval for official communications.
