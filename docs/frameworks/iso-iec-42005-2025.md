# ISO/IEC 42005:2025 AI system impact assessment engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, legal advice, an impact-assessment conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 42005:2025 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/42005).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an AI-system impact assessment record that connects intended and foreseeable uses, accountable business and system owners, affected people and groups, data and supplier dependencies, documented impact considerations, evidence, mitigations, change decisions, and independent review. Preserve a clear boundary between AI-assisted evidence organization and accountable human judgment about impacts, deployment, remediation, and acceptable risk.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, product, risk, legal, privacy, security, safety, and domain authorities retain accountable decisions; AI-system and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and assessment readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine impact significance, decide whether deployment is acceptable, approve mitigation or risk acceptance, make legal or safety determinations, alter production systems, access sensitive data without authorization, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material model, data, intended-use, affected-population, supplier, incident, or regulatory changes.
