# Australia Essential Eight — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an ASD assessment or maturity result. Check
> the [current Essential Eight source](https://www.cyber.gov.au/business-government/asds-cyber-security-frameworks/essential-eight/essential-eight-maturity-model)
> at engagement start.

## Engagement focus

Leadership selects a target resilience posture based on mission, threat, and
impact. Record a consistent boundary and deviation approach; have strategy
owners document safeguards and compensating measures; validate operational
evidence; review exceptions on a schedule; and test incident/recovery
assumptions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Leadership owns targets and exceptions. Reviewers assess evidence under their
defined mandate. AI maintains the goal, exception, and evidence ledger and
flags uneven maturity or unsupported conclusions; it never determines a formal
result. Recalibrate target, investment, and risk acceptance annually.
