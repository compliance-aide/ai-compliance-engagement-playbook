# NIST SP 800-39 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not risk acceptance. Consult the current
> [NIST risk-management source](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/39/final).

## Engagement focus

Establish organizational risk context and decision rights; align mission and
business objectives with system work; maintain a single risk picture and
escalation path; revisit material changes, risk appetite, and resource decisions;
and provide leadership a source-linked annual risk narrative.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own risk inputs, decisions, and evidence. Independent reviewers test
scope, consistency, conflicts, and traceability. AI organizes evidence and
surfaces stale or contradictory claims; it cannot accept risk or invent evidence.
Review material risk events as they arise and renew priorities annually.
