# Alaska Personal Information Protection Act engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Alaska statutory text, legal advice, a breach-notification decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current requirements through the [Alaska Department of Law consumer-protection page](https://www.law.alaska.gov/consumer/IDtheft.html) and qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a record of Alaska personal-information handling: covered records and systems, accountable owners, access and supplier relationships, retention and destruction operations, security-event evidence, investigation records, decision logs, and external communication approvals. Separate technical incident detection from the legal determination of whether an event triggers notice or another obligation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy and legal owners determine applicability and notification obligations; security leads preserve incident evidence; system and records owners maintain handling and retention evidence; communications owners approve external statements; independent reviewers test record traceability. AI may organize approved records, flag missing evidence, and draft nonbinding questions, but cannot determine whether an incident is legally reportable, send notices, contact affected people or authorities, approve disposal, or claim compliance. Review annually and when material data, system, supplier, retention, or incident circumstances change.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Alaska Department of Law consumer-protection page](https://www.law.alaska.gov/consumer/IDtheft.html) and qualified Alaska counsel; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not reproduce statute text or decide covered-information status, security reasonableness, breach notification, timing, content, or any regulator/consumer communication.

### 1. Alaska personal-information and custody inventory

- **Request and owner:** Data inventory identifying Alaska-resident personal-information routes, systems, owners, vendors, access groups, storage locations, retention schedules, and disposal controls, maintained by privacy, system, and records owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected collection-to-disposal route to system ownership, access evidence, vendor relationship, and destruction or retention record. This supports factual custody traceability; it cannot decide whether information is legally covered or whether safeguards are reasonable.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventory fields and flag unknown owners. Humans approve classifications and disposal exceptions. Refresh for a new data class, system, vendor, or retention change.

### 2. Safeguard and incident-investigation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Access-review results, security-control operation evidence, incident tickets/timeline, forensic preservation record, affected-data analysis inputs, containment/remediation evidence, and exercise results owned by security and incident leads.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected incident or exercise to detection, preservation, investigation authority, systems considered, containment, and corrective-action retest. This supports a documented investigation; it cannot establish compromise, harm, or legal notification duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize redacted incident facts and flag missing timestamps; it cannot determine reportability. Recollect after an incident, failed exercise, material control change, or recurring alert pattern.

### 3. Notification decision and supplier oversight record

- **Request and owner:** Counsel-owned notification decision log, approved communication templates/version history, supplier security/incident terms, vendor escalation records, management approvals, and post-incident lessons learned.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier event or decision to facts, counsel review, authorized approver, recipient list basis, communication approval, and remediation. This supports accountability; it cannot decide notice content, audience, deadline, or legal sufficiency.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft nonbinding questions and evidence indexes. Counsel and communications owners approve all legal decisions and outbound messages. Refresh after a supplier change, incident, or annual review.
