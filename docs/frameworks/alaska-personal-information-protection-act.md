# Alaska Personal Information Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, an incident-notification
> decision or a statement of compliance.

## Source and applicability

Start with the [Alaska Department of Law overview](https://www.law.alaska.gov/consumer/IDtheft.html)
and its link to the Personal Information Protection Act. The overview describes
breach notification, restrictions involving Social Security numbers, disposal,
credit freezes and identity-theft relief. These subjects have different actors
and triggers; do not treat them as one universal privacy-request workflow.

## Engagement focus

Trace personal-information custody, protection and disposal; maintain an incident
fact trail and a timely route for authorized legal decisions. Add credit-report
or identity-theft processes only if the scope owner confirms that organizational
role. Evidence that a process exists is not proof of legal sufficiency.

## Roles

Privacy and Alaska counsel decide applicability and legal response. Records,
system and supplier owners operate approved handling rules. Incident leadership
preserves and investigates facts; communication owners control approved notices.
Independent reviewers test traceability. AI may organize permitted metadata,
calculate dates under an approved rule and draft workpapers. It cannot decide
reportability, authorize destruction, issue notices or accept a legal exception.

## Before starting

Obtain entity/role scope, data and custody inventory, relevant resident/data
classification decisions, supplier contacts, retention/legal-hold rules, incident
escalation plan and current counsel-approved obligation register. If the legal
source is inaccessible, continue factual inventory and QA planning but do not
invent a notice clock or declare legal coverage. Follow the
[agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md).

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Confirm role and duties | Privacy/counsel identify the entity, information-handling roles and applicable statutory provisions. AI indexes decisions and unresolved questions. | Source-backed scope and obligation register; unresolved legal interpretation stays pending. |
| 2. Trace custody | Records/system owners map collection, storage, access, suppliers and disposal. AI reconciles locations and owners using metadata. | Custody map with unaccounted copies or recipients called out explicitly. |
| 3. Check operating safeguards | Owners provide approved access, handling and disposal rules plus operating records. Reviewer defines checks and coverage. | Evidence request/test plan tied to the covered systems and period. |
| 4. Exercise disposal and exception handling | Authorized operators use fictional QA records to demonstrate eligible disposal and a blocked legal-hold case. | Observable outcomes and retained decision trail; no destruction of real evidence. |
| 5. Exercise incident routing | Incident/legal owners run a fictional suspected-exposure case; AI records timestamps, source facts and escalation. | Investigation and decision roles receive the case; legal timing comes from the approved rule, not the exercise author. |
| 6. Resolve and retest gaps | Operators implement approved corrections; reviewer verifies the failed path again. | Finding, correction and retest remain linked; unresolved issues retain owners and dates. |
| 7. Review external decisions and renew | Counsel/communications owners review actual decision records where authorized; privacy owner updates scope after changes. | Protected decision/receipt index, open matters and next review queue. |

This is an original evidence-management sequence, not a legal determination or
complete reproduction of the Act's procedures.

## Evidence and test plan

| Request and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Custody and supplier map from records/system owners | Trace reviewer-selected data routes through collection, access, vendor custody and retention. | Owners and locations reconcile with operating records. | Unmapped copies leave completeness inconclusive. |
| Disposal and hold records from records/legal owners | Exercise a fictional eligible record and a record subject to an approved hold. | Disposal follows authorization; held data is preserved. | A schedule alone does not prove deletion or justify destroying evidence. |
| Incident timeline from security | Follow alert, preservation, investigation and escalation references with timestamps. | Facts remain distinguishable from hypotheses and legal decisions. | A tool alert alone does not determine compromise or notice duty. |
| Legal/communication records from counsel | Trace a decision to its facts, source, authorized approver and any permitted delivery receipt. | Decision scope and actual delivery state are explicit. | A draft notice or vendor email is not a completed notification. |

## Failure branches and decisions

An unverified source blocks legal rules, not authorized factual work. Missing
incident facts trigger a collection/escalation task, not a no-notice conclusion.
Conflicting retention and preservation directions go to counsel before disposal.
If the entity is not a credit-reporting actor, record the approved scope decision
rather than assigning unrelated freeze operations. Do not route a consumer's
identity-theft legal remedy to an AI decision. Escalate suspected exposure through
the current incident route immediately.

## Cadence and renewal

The owner may choose quarterly custody/supplier reviews and an annual exercise
as planning defaults. Review immediately after material data, supplier, retention
or incident changes. No numeric statutory notice deadline is asserted in this
draft while current section-level verification is outstanding; the approved
legal register must supply the clock, trigger, timezone and escalation owner.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver scope/obligation decisions, custody map, handling/disposal evidence,
exercise workpapers, protected incident and communication references, retest
results and the next queue. Name the unresolved statutory-source check and any
untested role-specific duties. This draft is not ready for legal reliance.
