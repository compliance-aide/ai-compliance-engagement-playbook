# NYDFS Part 504 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not AML/sanctions judgment or certification. Consult the [NYDFS transaction-monitoring resource](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/transaction_monitoring).

## Engagement focus

Maintain scope, filing ownership, program change, data quality, tuning, governance, testing, alert performance, training, independent testing, remediation, and certification-support evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own procedures and alerts; independent reviewers evaluate filing support without operating scenarios. AI triages evidence gaps, but cannot disposition alerts, override judgments, sign/submit certification, or conceal defects. Review quarterly and annually.
