# ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Check the [ISO base record](https://www.iso.org/standard/70636.html) and amendments.

## Engagement focus

Maintain service catalog, commitments, incident, change, problem, supplier, resilience, and service-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Service owners approve changes and communications; independent reviewers test records and outcomes. AI correlates tickets and trends, but cannot approve change or create certification claims. Review monthly, quarterly, and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm the edition and amendments using the [ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/70636.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO/IEC content is licensed. This plan uses original language and does not reproduce requirements, authorize service changes, or support a certification claim.

### 1. Service commitment and ownership package

- **Request and owner:** Service-management and service owners provide the current service catalog, service commitments, customer/owner assignments, service dependencies, supplier interfaces, and review cadence for a selected service.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected service commitment to an accountable owner, service description, dependency, and review record. This supports a transparent service boundary; it does not prove all commitments are achievable or all service relationships are captured.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile catalog metadata and flag unowned dependencies. Humans approve commitments and external communications. Refresh after a new, changed, or retired service or supplier.

### 2. Incident, change, and problem package

- **Request and owner:** Operations owners provide a linked sample of incident tickets, change decisions, approvals, implementation/recovery records, problem analysis, customer communications, and corrective actions.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect one service-affecting path from incident through authorized change or workaround, validation, and problem follow-up. This supports the sampled operating trail; it cannot prove service reliability or authorize a production change.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may correlate records and flag missing links. Humans assess impact, approve changes, and close operational decisions. Recollect after a major incident, failed change, or recurring problem.

### 3. Performance, supplier, and service-review package

- **Request and owner:** Service, supplier, and leadership owners provide performance measures, service-review records, supplier performance evidence, resilience issues, exceptions, and improvement decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected performance issue to measurement source, accountable discussion, supplier or service action, and follow-up review. This supports management attention; it does not establish target attainment or supplier conformance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize trends and flag overdue actions. Humans approve investments, exceptions, risk decisions, and customer statements. Review monthly or quarterly as the service risk warrants.
