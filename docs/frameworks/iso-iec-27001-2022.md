# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an ISO publication or a certification claim.
> Use an authorized copy and check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001)
> and applicable amendments before each milestone.

## Engagement focus

Define the information-security management boundary; refresh organizational
context and information-risk decisions; maintain applicability and rationale
records without recreating the standard; govern changes and suppliers; exercise
incident and continuity capability; review performance and corrective action.

## Roles

The organization runs its management system. An independent reviewer evaluates
the work under the applicable engagement rules. AI maintains traceability,
evidence schedules, and open-question logs; it cannot certify a management
system or determine certification readiness.

## Annual rhythm

Set objectives and risk priorities, then perform periodic operational and
evidence reviews. Trigger scope and risk review after significant business,
technology, supplier, incident, or legal changes. End each cycle with internal
review, leadership review, corrective-action follow-through, and improvement
planning.
