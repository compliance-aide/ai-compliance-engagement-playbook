# IEC 31010:2019 risk-assessment techniques engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC/ISO text, a prescribed technique or an accepted risk decision.

## Source and applicability

The [ISO publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72140.html) and [IEC publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/59809) describe the second edition as guidance for selecting and applying risk-assessment techniques, including planning and checking their use. Publisher records checked 2026-09-04. Full licensed technique descriptions and current applicable sector requirements remain unverified. Obtain authorized access before relying on detailed method instructions; do not reproduce protected tables or technique descriptions here.

Record the applicable edition, decision context and governing requirements. A publisher review or stability date is not an organizational certification expiry. This workflow organizes assessment work; it does not make IEC 31010 a certification scheme or replace the organization's risk-management process.

## Engagement focus

Connect the question being decided to a suitable approved technique, credible inputs, reproducible analysis, limitations and an accountable decision. Treat a numerical result as an analytical output, not automatic authorization to accept risk.

## Roles

The sponsor approves objectives and scope. Qualified assessment and subject-matter owners select the technique, criteria and assumptions. The risk owner decides treatment and acceptance within authority. An independent reviewer challenges method fit, data quality and conclusions. AI may organize permitted evidence, draft candidate-method comparisons and reproduce approved calculations; it cannot approve a technique, invent likelihoods or impacts, accept risk, authorize changes or claim compliance.

## Before starting

Record decision question, alternatives, scope, affected people/assets/processes, time horizon, stakeholders, source versions, owner and due date. Identify mandatory constraints and available evidence. Define allowed tools, confidentiality and record retention. Obtain authorized method instructions and approve calculations before execution. Keep sensitive source material outside this public repository.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Frame the question.** State the decision and required output, including units or qualitative categories where relevant. Identify what is excluded and why. Resolve vague objectives before choosing a method.
2. **Reconcile evidence.** Inventory all scoped sources and scenarios, retaining provenance, periods, limitations and conflicting records. Mark missing information explicitly. Zero, unknown and not applicable are different states.
3. **Approve method selection.** Compare candidate techniques against the question, available knowledge, dependencies, expertise and required output. Record why the selected approach fits and what it cannot establish. A familiar spreadsheet is not sufficient selection rationale. The accountable owner approves the method and criteria before results are interpreted.
4. **Apply the method reproducibly.** Preserve input versions, formulas or procedural steps, assumptions, participant judgments and outputs. Check units, time bases and aggregation rules. Do not combine ordinal scores as physical quantities or assume independent events without a justified model. Escalate incompatible inputs to the method owner.
5. **Challenge the result.** Verify calculations against the approved method and assess whether the method answers the intended question. Test sensitivity to material assumptions where appropriate. Preserve disagreements and adverse scenarios; a precise number does not erase uncertain inputs. Separate computational correctness from model validity.
6. **Make and communicate the decision.** Present alternatives, results, limits and unresolved questions to the authorized owner. Record treatment, acceptance or further-analysis decisions with rationale and approval. Link resulting work to controlled action records; assessment completion is not treatment completion.
7. **Follow up and reassess.** Verify action evidence and actual effectiveness against approved objectives. Reopen the assessment when circumstances, sources or assumptions change. Keep original results and the reason for revisions so another reviewer can reconstruct the decision history.

## Evidence and test plan

Maintain complete accounting of scoped assessments, sources, assumptions, results and actions. Preserve all evidence supplied for assessment without silent trimming, sampling or caps. State actual analytical coverage separately from inventory completeness.

### 1. Assessment mandate and context package

- **Request and owner:** The assessment sponsor and risk owner provide the decision to be informed, scope, affected assets or activities, accountable roles, timing, information sources, and excluded assumptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Reconcile every scoped assessment and trace each to an authorized purpose, owner, boundary, and dated inputs. This tests that its mandate is knowable; it does not decide whether the scope is sufficient or every stakeholder was included.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved inputs and flag absent scope or ownership fields. Humans approve scope and decision use. Refresh for a material context, objective, stakeholder, or information-source change.

### 2. Method-selection and assumptions package

- **Request and owner:** Assessment and subject-matter owners provide the method-selection rationale, relevant capabilities/limitations, assumptions, uncertainty notes, participant competence records, and authorization of the chosen approach.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect each scoped selection record for a visible connection between purpose, available information, limitations, assumptions, and accountable authorization. This supports reviewable rationale; it cannot establish that a method is objectively best or validate expert judgment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare required fields and draft challenge questions. It cannot choose a technique, infer missing evidence, calculate unapproved ratings, or approve a rationale. Revisit after a material uncertainty, evidence gap, or decision change.

### 3. Results, treatment-interface, and review package

- **Request and owner:** Assessment, risk-treatment, and records owners provide authorized results, evidence references, communicated limitations, resulting treatment/escalation records, open assumptions, and independent review observations.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace every reported result to its evidence, limitation statement, recipient, treatment or escalation interface, and follow-up owner. This tests traceability; it cannot accept risk, prove effectiveness, or substitute for a decision authority.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compile an authorized briefing and flag unowned follow-up. Humans approve results, treatment, residual risk, and external communications. Review at approved intervals and after a material decision, incident, or evidence update.


## Failure branches and decisions

- No authorized method instructions: continue context and evidence organization; defer dependent analysis until the owner supplies them.
- Missing input: record uncertainty and assess its decision impact; never substitute a convenient estimate without expert approval.
- Correct arithmetic but unsuitable model: retain the calculation result and reject the unsupported decision claim.
- Different methods disagree: preserve both outputs and their assumptions; do not average incompatible results to manufacture agreement.
- Proposed acceptance violates a mandatory constraint: escalate rather than treating a low score as permission.
- Treatment is implemented but follow-up fails: keep effectiveness unresolved or failed and assign corrective action.

Use [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) assertions: supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable and not_tested. These evidence statuses are separate from risk ratings and approval states.

## Cadence and renewal

Use approved risk-based review intervals and event triggers. Reassess after changes in objectives, dependencies, operating conditions, evidence or incidents. Do not invent universal quarterly reviews or annual recertification requirements.

Deliver the mandate, evidence inventory, approved method rationale, reproducible workpapers, uncertainty register, reviewer challenges, authorized decisions and open actions with owners and dates. Full licensed-source, independent skeptical and rights reviews remain pending for this draft.
