# IEC 31010:2019 risk-assessment techniques engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC standard content or a risk conclusion. Confirm the licensed publication through the [IEC 31010:2019 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72140.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a decision trail for risk assessments: decision context, chosen method, assumptions, evidence inputs, participants, results, limitations, risk owners, actions, and re-evaluation triggers.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Accountable decision-makers choose methods and accept risk; risk practitioners coordinate documented assessments; subject-matter experts validate inputs; independent reviewers test consistency and traceability. AI can structure approved evidence, identify missing assumptions, and draft questions, but cannot select methods, determine risk acceptability, or make safety decisions. Reassess after material change and preserve human rationale for methodology and decisions.
