# IEC 31010:2019 risk-assessment techniques engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC or ISO text, a required-method determination, a risk decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC 31010:2019 publication record](https://www.iso.org/cms/%20render/live/en/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/07/21/72140.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a risk-assessment method record that connects decision context, accountable owners, purpose and scope, information sources, method-selection rationale, limitations, assumptions, authorized results, treatment decisions, evidence retention, and independent review. Use AI to make assessment work traceable and comparable across the year without turning a suggested method or generated synthesis into an accepted risk judgment.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business, risk, security, privacy, safety, legal, and service owners retain accountable decisions; assessment owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, method rationale, assumptions, evidence traceability, and conclusion boundaries. AI may organize authorized inputs, identify missing assumptions, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot choose or approve a method, determine likelihood or impact, accept risk, authorize treatment, alter systems, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Revisit material assessments when context changes, perform quarterly risk-record reviews, and conduct an annual method-governance review.
