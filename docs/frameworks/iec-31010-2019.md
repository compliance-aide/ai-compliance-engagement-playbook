# IEC 31010:2019 risk-assessment techniques engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC or ISO text, a required-method determination, a risk decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC 31010:2019 publication record](https://www.iso.org/cms/%20render/live/en/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/07/21/72140.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a risk-assessment method record that connects decision context, accountable owners, purpose and scope, information sources, method-selection rationale, limitations, assumptions, authorized results, treatment decisions, evidence retention, and independent review. Use AI to make assessment work traceable and comparable across the year without turning a suggested method or generated synthesis into an accepted risk judgment.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business, risk, security, privacy, safety, legal, and service owners retain accountable decisions; assessment owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, method rationale, assumptions, evidence traceability, and conclusion boundaries. AI may organize authorized inputs, identify missing assumptions, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot choose or approve a method, determine likelihood or impact, accept risk, authorize treatment, alter systems, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Revisit material assessments when context changes, perform quarterly risk-record reviews, and conduct an annual method-governance review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm the applicable edition through the [ISO publication record for IEC 31010:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/72140.html), checked 2026-07-31, and use authorized material only. The publication is licensed. This original plan does not reproduce it, mandate a technique, or make a risk or conformity conclusion.

### 1. Assessment mandate and context package

- **Request and owner:** The assessment sponsor and risk owner provide the decision to be informed, scope, affected assets or activities, accountable roles, timing, information sources, and excluded assumptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected assessment to an authorized purpose, owner, boundary, and dated inputs. This tests that its mandate is knowable; it does not decide whether the scope is sufficient or every stakeholder was included.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved inputs and flag absent scope or ownership fields. Humans approve scope and decision use. Refresh for a material context, objective, stakeholder, or information-source change.

### 2. Method-selection and assumptions package

- **Request and owner:** Assessment and subject-matter owners provide the method-selection rationale, relevant capabilities/limitations, assumptions, uncertainty notes, participant competence records, and authorization of the chosen approach.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect one selection record for a visible connection between purpose, available information, limitations, assumptions, and accountable authorization. This supports reviewable rationale; it cannot establish that a method is objectively best or validate expert judgment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare required fields and draft challenge questions. It cannot choose a technique, infer missing evidence, calculate unapproved ratings, or approve a rationale. Revisit after a material uncertainty, evidence gap, or decision change.

### 3. Results, treatment-interface, and review package

- **Request and owner:** Assessment, risk-treatment, and records owners provide authorized results, evidence references, communicated limitations, resulting treatment/escalation records, open assumptions, and independent review observations.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one reported result to its evidence, limitation statement, recipient, treatment or escalation interface, and follow-up owner. This tests traceability; it cannot accept risk, prove effectiveness, or substitute for a decision authority.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compile an authorized briefing and flag unowned follow-up. Humans approve results, treatment, residual risk, and external communications. Review quarterly and after a material decision, incident, or evidence update.
