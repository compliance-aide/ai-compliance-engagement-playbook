# NIST SP 800-234 high-performance-computing overlay engagement guide

> This is original operational guidance, not NIST overlay content, a control selection, a system authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-234](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/234/final) and the organization's approved mission, security, architecture, privacy, legal, operational, and risk decisions.

## Engagement focus

Manage a year-round engagement for applying and maintaining a high-performance-computing security overlay in the actual mission and system context. Connect the in-scope HPC environment, performance requirements, workloads, data, architecture, inherited services, applicable security expectations, local tailoring rationale, implementation evidence, assessment approach, exceptions, and improvement work. Treat an overlay as a structured tailoring aid, not as a substitute for accountable scoping, control decisions, evidence, or authorization.

Keep the rationale behind tailoring visible, particularly where performance, specialized hardware, research activity, user environments, or operational constraints influence the decision. A control matrix, architecture diagram, configuration record, assessment result, system plan, waiver, or performance test is evidence to evaluate in context—not automatic proof that a requirement applies, has been satisfied, or is acceptable for a mission. Preserve decisions, assumptions, unresolved gaps, compensating measures, and reassessment triggers.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Mission, HPC operations, security, architecture, risk, system, data, privacy, legal, export-control, performance, supplier, assessment, and records owners establish scope and make accountable decisions. Operators maintain overlay applicability and tailoring records, system and workload inventories, inherited-service evidence, implementation and assessment artifacts, performance-impact records, exceptions, remediation plans, and change history. Reconcile scope, workloads, architecture, inherited dependencies, tailoring decisions, and evidence freshness at least quarterly; reassess before major platform or mission changes; and conduct an annual management review.

Independent reviewers sample a tailored security expectation from scope and rationale through implementation, evidence, and corrective action. Auditors assess supplied records without choosing controls, tailoring requirements, changing cluster settings, accepting risk, authorizing the system, or issuing an attestation. AI may organize evidence and tailoring records, identify missing rationale or stale assessments, draft workpapers, compare supplied records with approved decisions, and prepare review questions. AI cannot select or tailor binding controls, approve compensating measures, alter HPC systems, accept risk, authorize operations, make legal conclusions, or attest to conformance.
