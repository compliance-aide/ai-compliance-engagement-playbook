# SOC 2 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an AICPA publication, CPA report, or “SOC 2
> certification.” Use authorized [AICPA materials](https://us.aicpa.org/interestareas/frc/assuranceadvisoryservices/socforserviceorganizations.html)
> and a qualified CPA firm for any examination.

## Engagement focus

Establish service boundary and system-narrative ownership; identify customer
commitments and relevant risk outcomes; maintain governance and risk records;
operate and evidence applicable service practices; coordinate vendor evidence;
plan an observation period; reconcile changes, incidents, and exceptions; and
prepare organization-owned materials for independent review.

## Roles

The service organization operates and describes its system. The CPA service
auditor determines scope, performs the examination, and issues its report. AI may
trace authorized evidence to organization-authored claims, draft non-authoritative notes,
and flag inconsistencies; it cannot determine sufficiency, select scope, issue a report, or claim certification.

## Annual rhythm

Confirm scope, commitments, and vendors; maintain evidence throughout the
observation period; inspect material changes and incidents; resolve exceptions;
support independent review; and carry findings into the next operating cycle.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use authorized [AICPA SOC materials](https://us.aicpa.org/interestareas/frc/assuranceadvisoryservices/socforserviceorganizations.html); checked 2026-07-31. AICPA materials may have use restrictions. This original plan does not reproduce criteria, select an examination scope, or represent a SOC report or certification.

### 1. System boundary and commitment record

- **Request and owner:** System narrative inputs, service and data-flow inventory, customer commitments, subservice-provider records, and accountable-owner approvals from service, product, security, and vendor-management leaders.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected customer commitment through the described service boundary, data flow, owner, and supplier allocation. This can support a transparent system-description workpaper; it cannot determine the applicable criteria or prove the description is complete.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized inventories and flag undocumented dependencies. Management approves the system description and scope. Refresh after a material service, data, customer-commitment, or supplier change.

### 2. Operating-practice and exception evidence

- **Request and owner:** Time-bounded access, change, monitoring, incident, availability, confidentiality, and vendor-oversight records relevant to the organization’s approved objectives, held by the respective operational owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a human-approved selection for source, date, population, system linkage, exception, and follow-up. This can support that selected declared practices have observable records; it cannot prove continuous operation, conclude effectiveness, or replace CPA testing.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may build a read-only evidence index and identify missing periods or owners; it may not alter controls or conclude on exceptions. Recollect after an incident, failed control activity, material change, or observation-period rollover.

### 3. Observation-period governance and audit-support record

- **Request and owner:** Risk and change decisions, exception/remediation log, management representations or approvals, evidence-retention record, and CPA-request tracker from management and the engagement lead.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception to its owner, evidence, decision authority, remediation, and retest status. This can support accountable preparation for an examination; it cannot issue a report, accept risk, or bind the service auditor.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft traceability questions and surface stale decisions. Humans approve representations, risk decisions, and external communications. Review throughout the observation period and before independent examination.
