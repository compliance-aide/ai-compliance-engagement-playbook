# SOC 2 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an AICPA publication, CPA report, or “SOC 2
> certification.” Use authorized [AICPA materials](https://us.aicpa.org/interestareas/frc/assuranceadvisoryservices/socforserviceorganizations.html)
> and a qualified CPA firm for any examination.

## Engagement focus

Establish service boundary and system-narrative ownership; identify customer
commitments and relevant risk outcomes; maintain governance and risk records;
operate and evidence applicable service practices; coordinate vendor evidence;
plan an observation period; reconcile changes, incidents, and exceptions; and
prepare organization-owned materials for independent review.

## Roles

The service organization operates and describes its system. The CPA service
auditor determines scope, performs the examination, and issues its report. AI may
trace authorized evidence to organization-authored claims, draft non-authoritative notes,
and flag inconsistencies; it cannot determine sufficiency, select scope, issue a report, or claim certification.

## Annual rhythm

Confirm scope, commitments, and vendors; maintain evidence throughout the
observation period; inspect material changes and incidents; resolve exceptions;
support independent review; and carry findings into the next operating cycle.
