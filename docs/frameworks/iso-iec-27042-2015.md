# ISO/IEC 27042:2015 digital-evidence analysis — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content, legal advice, or an evidentiary conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/44406.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain authorized analysis scope, evidence provenance, method selection, analyst competence, reproducibility records, interpretations, quality review, case decisions, and closure documentation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized investigators and counsel make analytical and disclosure decisions; independent reviewers test traceability. AI organizes approved metadata and flags gaps, but cannot access evidence, interpret facts, or conclude on an investigation. Exercise annually and review each case.
