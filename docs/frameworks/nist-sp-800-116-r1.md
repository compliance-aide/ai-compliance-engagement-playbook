# NIST SP 800-116 Revision 1 PIV facility-access engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST physical-access instructions, a facility-risk determination, an access decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-116 Revision 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/116/r1/final) and the organization’s approved physical-security, identity, legal, privacy, safety, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed lifecycle for PIV-enabled facility access across in-scope sites, zones, readers, physical access control systems, credential populations, visitor or exception processes, and suppliers. Maintain traceability from facility and access-use scope through risk-informed design, approved authentication and access decisions, device and integration baseline, issuance and revocation dependencies, event monitoring, exception handling, incident response, and review evidence. A successful badge read, physical-access log, or vendor statement is evidence to assess; it does not prove a person is authorized, a facility is adequately protected, an access decision is correct, or compliance.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, physical security or facilities, identity and access management, security, safety, legal, privacy, human resources or sponsor function, site owners, endpoint or device management, procurement, supplier-management, incident-response, and records-management roles. Operators maintain in-scope facility and zone records, approved access and risk decisions, PACS and reader inventories, credential and integration evidence, access-event and anomaly records, visitor and exception decisions, incident and recovery records, supplier assurance, and decommissioning evidence. Reconcile facility scope, zones, systems, and credential populations quarterly; review access anomalies, device and integration changes, exceptions, visitor processes, supplier notices, and unresolved risks at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material facility, system, population, safety, threat, or policy changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples facility-to-access-decision-to-event traceability; auditors test evidence without granting, changing, or revoking physical access, directing a facility operation, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied facility, access, event, exception, and review evidence, identify missing ownership or stale reviews, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot grant, deny, change, or revoke physical access, operate a PACS or reader, determine a person’s authorization, make a safety or legal decision, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
