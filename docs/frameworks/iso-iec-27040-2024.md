# ISO/IEC 27040:2024 storage security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a storage-security conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80194.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain storage inventory, data categories, lifecycle and media records, access decisions, encryption/key-management dependencies, disposal evidence, recovery tests, suppliers, exceptions, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Data, platform, and security owners approve lifecycle and risk decisions; independent reviewers test evidence integrity. AI identifies incomplete inventories and conflicting records, but cannot access data, destroy media, or accept risk. Review quarterly and annually.

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
