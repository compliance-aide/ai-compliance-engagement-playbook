# Japan APPI — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Japanese legal advice or a regulatory conclusion. Confirm current material at the [Personal Information Protection Commission](https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a data, process, vendor, purpose, notice, rights, retention, security, and overseas-transfer decision register. Exercise incident escalation and sample a live processing route through approved evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Owners operate the evidence; independent reviewers test traceability. AI can classify evidence and draft questions, but cannot interpret Japanese law, decide applicability, release data, or make an audit conclusion. Review quarterly and refresh scope annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
