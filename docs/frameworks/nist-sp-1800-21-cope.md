# NIST SP 1800-21 COPE mobile-device engagement guide

> This is original operational guidance, not NIST practice-guide content, a device-management configuration, a privacy conclusion, a compliance claim, or professional advice. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 1800-21](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1800/21/final) and the organization's approved security, privacy, legal, human-resources, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Manage a year-round engagement for corporate-owned, personally enabled mobile devices. Connect device inventory, ownership, enrollment, permitted personal use, enterprise data boundaries, identities, applications, network access, management configuration, monitoring, support, exceptions, offboarding, and evidence. Make the boundary between organizational control and employee privacy explicit; a management-tool signal is evidence for human review, not automatic authority to inspect, wipe, suspend, or discipline.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Security, mobility, identity, privacy, legal, HR, business, service desk, procurement, and records owners establish scope and make accountable decisions. Operators maintain enrollment, configuration, access, application, support, incident, exception, and retirement records. Reconcile ownership, enrollment, management posture, and high-risk access quarterly; review material device or policy changes promptly; and conduct an annual privacy and mobile-governance review.

Independent reviewers sample a device from assignment through approved configuration, access, support or exception, and offboarding review. Auditors evaluate supplied evidence without changing devices, accessing personal content, approving employee actions, making privacy or legal conclusions, accepting risk, or issuing an attestation. AI may organize records, flag stale ownership or evidence, draft workpapers, and prepare review questions. AI cannot change device settings, inspect personal content, authorize wiping, decide personnel action, accept risk, or attest to conformance.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Retain the retrieved version and applicable use terms for the official [NIST SP 1800-21 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1800/21/final) with approved security, privacy, legal, HR, and operational direction. This is original evidence-planning guidance; it does not reproduce the practice guide, authorize device action, or make a conformance claim.

### 1. COPE scope and privacy-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Mobility, HR, privacy, legal, identity, and business owners provide device assignment and enrollment inventories, approved personal-use boundary, enterprise-data boundary, owner records, and exception register.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one selected device from assignment through enrollment, stated management scope, enterprise-data boundary, and accountable owner. This tests supplied-record traceability, not the propriety of monitoring or personal-content access.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify missing owners or stale enrollment evidence. Humans decide employee action, privacy interpretation, and device scope. Refresh after policy, device, employment, identity, or management-platform change.

### 2. Access, configuration, and support package

- **Request and owner:** Mobility, identity, security, service-desk, and application owners provide approved management baseline, configuration references, access-path records, support tickets, changes, and non-sensitive monitoring summaries.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one enrolled device’s approved baseline, configuration/change record, access path, and support or exception record. This cannot change a device, prove all settings are effective, or authorize a wipe.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied metadata and flag missing change or review links. Humans configure devices, grant access, and approve exceptions. Recollect after a material configuration, access, support, or incident change.

### 3. Offboarding, exceptions, and review package

- **Request and owner:** Mobility, HR, privacy, legal, records, risk, and independent-review owners provide offboarding workflow evidence, exception approvals, incident follow-up, retention decisions, corrective actions, and review workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one exit, exception, or incident follow-up to human authority, recorded limitation, due date, and disposition. This supports governance visibility; it cannot accept risk or establish legal compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag expired exceptions and overdue actions. Humans approve exceptions, retention decisions, risk treatment, and external statements. Review quarterly and after a material privacy, HR, incident, or supplier change.
