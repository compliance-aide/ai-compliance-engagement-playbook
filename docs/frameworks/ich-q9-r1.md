# ICH Q9(R1) quality-risk engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ICH content or a risk decision. See the [ICH Q9(R1) final document](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_Q9%28R1%29_Guideline_Step4_2025_0115.pdf).

## Engagement focus

Maintain risk context, accountable decision-maker, evidence date, uncertainty, trigger, outcome, supply implication, change history, and open-assumption records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Qualified humans set criteria and approve decisions; independent reviewers test provenance. AI detects stale risks and drafts evidence packs, but cannot accept risk or judge effectiveness. Review quarterly and annually.
