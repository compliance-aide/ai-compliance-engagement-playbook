# BSI Standard 200-2 IT-Grundschutz methodology engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not BSI methodology or a certification result. Confirm the current source and its applicability through the [BSI Standard 200-2 publication](https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/EN/BSI/Grundschutz/International/bsi-standard-2002_en_pdf.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a living protection-methodology record: organizational scope, service and asset structure, selected approach, security objectives, implemented work, exceptions, risk decisions, evidence of operation, and improvement actions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management establishes the desired protection level and accepts risk; security leadership coordinates the program; service owners maintain current evidence; independent reviewers test that claims have support. AI creates evidence inventories, follows change impacts, and drafts targeted review questions, but cannot select a protection approach, judge sufficiency, or issue certification claims. Refresh the engagement plan with material organizational or technical change, conduct periodic evidence reviews, and record human approvals.
