# Global Privacy Recognition for Processors system engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Global PRP program requirements, legal advice, a certification assessment, or a compliance claim. Confirm current materials through the [Global CBPR Forum privacy certifications](https://www.globalcbpr.org/privacy-certifications/) and applicable jurisdictional law.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a processor-focused privacy assurance record that connects documented customer processing instructions, personal-data processing context, accountable processor and customer contacts, suppliers and subprocessors, evidence, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Preserve the separation between supporting a processor’s evidence lifecycle and deciding a controller’s legal duties, contractual directions, or certification outcome.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Processor executives, privacy, legal, security, risk, and service authorities retain accountable decisions; customer and supplier owners preserve relevant records; recognized accountability agents and independent reviewers perform their defined review roles. AI may organize authorized evidence, flag incomplete processor records, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine legal applicability, change customer instructions, approve a subprocessor or transfer, select an accountability agent, assign an assessment result, access personal data without authorization, submit an application, or claim certification or compliance. Review annually and before material customer, processing, subprocessor, transfer, incident, jurisdiction, or certification-scope changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [Global CBPR Forum privacy-certifications information](https://www.globalcbpr.org/privacy-certifications/) and authorized processor engagement records; checked 2026-07-31. Confirm program process, customer-contract obligations, and applicable law with qualified privacy counsel. This is original processor evidence-planning guidance, not program text, a controller analysis, a legal conclusion, or a certification determination.

### 1. Customer-instruction and processing-scope record

- **Request and owner:** Customer agreements and documented instructions, service/data-processing inventory, personal-data categories, processing locations, customer contacts, service owners, and scope-change approvals from account, privacy, legal, and service-delivery owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a human-selected customer service or processing activity to the documented instruction, service scope, accountable contacts, location context, and review date. This supports bounded traceability; it cannot interpret an instruction, decide controller duties, or establish complete processing coverage.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized metadata and flag absent instructions, owner conflicts, or stale scope reviews. Humans approve scope, interpret customer instructions, and decide legal applicability. Refresh after a new customer service, data category, location, material instruction, or contract change.

### 2. Subprocessor, transfer, and security-operation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Subprocessor/service-provider inventory, approval and contractual records, processing-location/transfer context, due-diligence and assurance evidence, security incident records, access-review evidence, and corrective-action tracking from procurement, security, privacy, and supplier owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected subprocessor or incident to approval/oversight context, owner, action, customer-notice decision, and closure evidence. This can demonstrate a selected evidence trail; it cannot authorize a subprocessor, determine transfer legality, or decide notification duties.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized evidence and flag missing approvals, review dates, or remediation links; it may not approve a supplier, change processing, access personal data without authorization, or communicate with a customer. Humans approve supplier and communication decisions. Recollect after a supplier change, incident, failed assurance result, or material access/architecture change.

### 3. Processor-accountability and review workpaper

- **Request and owner:** Customer inquiry/complaint handling records, audit or assessment inputs where authorized, exception/remediation register, management-review decisions, retention/deletion evidence, and independent-review workpapers from privacy, service, legal, leadership, and review owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception, inquiry, or review observation to its source record, accountable decision, due date, customer-facing constraint, and retest. This supports review readiness; it cannot settle a customer dispute, accept risk on a customer’s behalf, or replace an accountability-agent assessment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare non-authoritative challenge questions and a factual evidence register. Humans decide customer responses, residual risk, certification path, closure, and any submission. Review annually and after a material customer, audit, complaint, retention, program, or jurisdiction change.
