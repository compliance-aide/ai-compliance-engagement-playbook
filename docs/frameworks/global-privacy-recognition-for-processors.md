# Global Privacy Recognition for Processors system engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not program text, legal advice or certification. Draft pending independent source, skeptical and rights review.

## Source and applicability

Use the [Forum catalog](https://www.globalcbpr.org/documents/), [certification overview](https://www.globalcbpr.org/privacy-certifications/) and [linked PRP requirements document](https://www.globalcbpr.org/wp-content/uploads/Global-PRP-Program-Requirements_Final.pdf). The six-page document read for this draft groups its 18 questions under security safeguards and accountability measures. It addresses controller instructions, incident/request routing, disposal/return, employees and subprocessors. Certification authority belongs to Forum-recognized Accountability Agents.

Confirm current jurisdictional eligibility, program recognition, questionnaire and policy versions. The catalog's CBPR edition transition is not automatically a PRP transition. Full companion policies, applicable contracts and law, source-status reconciliation and rights clearance remain pending. Do not copy the questionnaire or assessment criteria into this public guide; keep authorized references in engagement records.

## Engagement focus

Show whether the processor's actual services follow the approved controller instructions across every scoped system and subprocessor. Keep internal readiness, contract performance, legal obligations and certification status distinct. Processor certification does not certify its customers or authorize new processing purposes.

## Roles

Processor leadership owns program accountability. Privacy, legal and service owners approve instruction interpretation, scope and escalation. Controller contacts provide authorized instructions; procurement and security owners maintain downstream evidence. An independent reviewer challenges readiness. The recognized Accountability Agent performs its certification role. Named officials approve applications, contracts, customer communications and public claims.

AI may reconcile authorized metadata, compare evidence and draft workpapers. AI cannot change customer instructions, approve a subprocessor or transfer, decide controller duties, access personal data without authorization, submit applications, communicate externally or assign certification results.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record applicant entity, program, services, customer population, locations, period, source versions, owners and permitted evidence access. Obtain approved contracts/instructions, scope, recognized-agent route and current requirements. Conflicting instructions block dependent operations; continue independent inventory reconciliation.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Confirm program and scope.** Verify entity/role, jurisdiction and agent recognition for PRP. Identify the applicable source versions and assessment period. Output: route register with unresolved questions assigned; a CBPR claim is not PRP evidence by default.
2. **Reconcile instruction coverage.** Link each customer/service/data route to the controlling instruction, version, purpose, locations and accountable contacts. Retain missing or conflicting instructions. Output: complete instruction-to-service matrix.
3. **Map downstream processing.** Trace every scoped subprocessor and relevant further dependency to data, purpose, contractual mechanism, controller-notice/approval requirements and actual use. Output: downstream register; distinguish notice from approval when the contract requires both.
4. **Bind requirements to evidence.** For each program item, record the approved policy, implementation evidence, expected behavior and owner. Separate PRP criteria from additional contractual or local-law requirements. Output: complete answer/evidence matrix with all gaps visible.
5. **Test actual operation.** Use authorized QA or redacted historical records to examine instruction enforcement, security, requests, incident routing and return/disposal. Record actual behavior and time coverage, not just procedure existence. Output: case results with explicit untested routes.
6. **Review corrective actions.** Trace each failed test, complaint or assessment observation to its owner, decision, remedy and retest. Preserve constraints requiring controller input. Output: readiness packet and independent-review disagreements, without assuming customer risk acceptance.
7. **Prepare the certification handoff.** Freeze the approved application package and track versions, questions, decisions and receipts. Verify the issued entity/service scope and current authoritative status before public claims. Output: separate prepared, submitted, reviewed and issued states.

## Evidence and test plan

### Customer instruction and processing-scope package

Restore PR340's package with full scoped population tracking. Account, legal and service owners supply contracts, instructions, data categories, purposes, locations and change history. Trace instructions to actual configuration and job behavior. An unsigned draft, sales promise or internal assumption cannot substitute for an authorized customer instruction. Preserve historical versions and effective dates.

### Subprocessor, transfer and security package

Procurement, security and privacy owners supply downstream inventories, contracts/mechanisms, notices or approvals where required, safeguards, access evidence, incidents and corrective actions. Check each declared dependency against actual processing. Treat assurance reports and certificates as bounded evidence; do not require every provider to possess a certificate merely because certification appears as one possible mechanism in program questions. Verify the method actually selected and its coverage with the assessor.

### Processor accountability and review package

Service, privacy and leadership owners supply request/complaint routing, training, legal-demand escalation, controller-contact records, disposal/return results and management decisions. Preserve receipt, acknowledgement, action and completion as distinct events. Check current escalation contacts, fallback ownership and unresolved cases. Legal restrictions on communications require the designated authority's decision, not an automated notification.

For every case record criterion/source, approved expectation, customer/service scope, evidence date, authorized method, observed outcome, limitation and owner. Keep all known services and dependencies in the population; disclose technical testing gaps rather than silently sampling them away. No private customer data or protected program text belongs in this repository.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Instruction conflicts with deployed behavior: mark the factual match not_supported; route interpretation and correction to the authorized owners.
- Controller instruction missing: mark its authority claim inconclusive and stop dependent changes; do not create a new purpose to complete the task.
- Processor sends a deletion request but lacks downstream readback: distinguish instruction delivery from unverified completion, retaining every affected dependency.
- Direct individual request arrives: follow the approved controller-routing procedure; AI must not adjudicate entitlement or disclose data.
- Certification evidence covers another entity/service: keep the exact scope mismatch visible; do not extend the certificate to a new customer or affiliate.

Fictional desk case: a processor's primary store is cleared following an approved QA disposal instruction, but a scheduled subprocessor export still contains the fixture. Primary-store disposal is supported; the full scoped disposal criterion is not_supported. No real personal-data operation was performed.

## Cadence and renewal

Record verified program/agent renewal and monitoring requirements separately from contractual deadlines and internal reviews. Reopen affected work after customer instructions, locations, subprocessors, incidents, source versions or scope change. Do not invent a universal annual deadline or import CBPR transition dates into PRP without authority.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the route/source register, full instruction and downstream inventories, requirement/evidence matrix, tests, gaps, retests, reviewer disagreements and named decisions. Classify assertions supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable or not_tested with reasons. Distinguish readiness, contractual outcomes, transfer legality and certification. Applications, customer communications and public claims require explicit authority and separate receipts.
