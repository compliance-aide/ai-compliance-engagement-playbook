# ISO 50001:2018 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or energy-savings certification. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/69426.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain sites, significant uses, assets, meter quality, approved baselines, drivers, improvement projects, and evidence lineage.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Facilities owners validate readings; energy leaders approve assumptions; independent reviewers reperform samples. AI reconciles data and trends, but cannot select methods, certify savings, or approve investment. Review monthly, quarterly, and annually.
