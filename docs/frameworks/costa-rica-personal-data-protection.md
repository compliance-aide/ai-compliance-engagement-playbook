# Costa Rica personal-data-protection engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Costa Rican law or a legal conclusion. Confirm current law, regulations, and authority guidance through [Prodhab’s legislation page](https://www.prodhab.go.cr/acercade/normativa/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an accountable processing record: data purposes, owner decisions, collection and sharing evidence, database records where applicable, service-provider handling, individual requests, incidents, and changes.

## Roles

Privacy and business owners maintain processing evidence; security and supplier owners provide operating records; Costa Rican counsel confirms scope and legal duties; independent reviewers test traceability. AI organizes approved artifacts and drafts gap questions, but cannot interpret legal requirements, register databases, decide individual-rights outcomes, or submit complaints or notifications. Review records at material processing change and through a recurring annual cycle with human approval.

## Source and applicability

Start with Law 8968 in the [official legal information system](https://pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?nValor1=1&nValor2=70975&param1=NRTC), its current implementing regulation and Prodhab guidance. Record the actual text/version retrieved; a search extract or a page containing navigation alone is not a complete legal source. The official indexed Article 7 identifies access, correction/deletion and consent to assignment, with a five-business-day resolution period from receipt. Counsel must verify the current text and counting rules before configuring deadlines. Do not import another jurisdiction's request timetable.

Identify public/private databases, manual and automated records, purposes, recipients, providers and geographic flows. Counsel decides scope, exclusions, sensitive-data restrictions, consent exceptions, registration and transfer duties. An internal database label, public availability of some fields or a signed vendor contract does not by itself resolve those questions.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Assign Costa Rican legal, privacy, support, data, security and procurement owners. Use synthetic request records and approved metadata; keep personal records and verification documents in authorized systems. Create separate work items for each processing purpose/database, rights case, provider flow and unresolved legal determination. Avoid treating a generic privacy policy as proof of operational compliance.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and accountable owner | Output and exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Resolve sources and scope | Counsel reviews current law, regulation and authority guidance against actual activities. | Dated source register and approved applicability decisions; missing texts remain explicit dependencies. |
| 2. Map collection and use | Data owners inventory databases, purposes, categories, collection channels, recipients and retention. | Complete flow register with owner and evidence for each processing path, including manual records and exports. |
| 3. Verify notice and permission design | Privacy and legal owners approve the information supplied before collection and the applicable permission or exception. | Versioned purpose/notice/permission matrix; changed purposes and disclosures receive separate review. |
| 4. Test collection boundaries | Authorized testers exercise synthetic granted, refused, withdrawn and missing-permission states where applicable. | Observed interface and backend behavior linked to approved expectations; no real consent decisions made by AI. |
| 5. Exercise individual requests | Support traces intake, safe authority verification, retrieval, human decision, correction/deletion and response. | Original receipt time, counsel-approved deadline, execution evidence and escalation; verification work does not silently restart the clock. |
| 6. Verify handling and transfers | Security, records and vendor owners trace access, confidentiality, disposal and recipient handling. | Safeguard evidence and approved transfer/registration determinations; contract presence is separate from actual recipient behavior. |
| 7. Resolve failures | Owners remediate approved gaps and reviewers retest; counsel considers historical consequences and incident duties. | Corrective action evidence, remaining affected records and unresolved decisions; a repaired workflow does not erase prior processing. |
| 8. Review and renew | Independent reviewers challenge coverage; management approves representations and the monitoring plan. | Complete handoff with named owners, open decisions and change triggers; no automated legal conclusion or filing. |

## Failure branches and decisions

- **Request entered late:** retain the actual receipt time and queue-entry time separately; escalate against the approved clock instead of substituting today's date.
- **Identity or authority uncertain:** use the approved verification route, disclose no records prematurely and escalate time pressure. AI must not decide identity sufficiency.
- **Consent record lacks a notice version:** preserve the missing evidence and refer the affected processing to privacy/legal owners; never reconstruct permission from current wording.
- **New export or provider absent from the register:** trace fields, purpose and recipient; reopen permission, transfer and safeguard review before relying on old approvals.
- **Database registration status unknown:** prepare the factual decision packet for counsel; do not infer exemption or register a database automatically.
- **Deletion incomplete or contested:** identify remaining copies and legal retention questions; record a human decision and tested execution rather than marking a successful job as complete erasure.

### 1. Processing, database, and accountability record

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, business, product, data, and records owners provide a dated register of processing activities and databases; purposes; data categories; collection channels; systems; owners; recipients/service providers; retention/disposal context; policy/notice versions; and scope questions for counsel.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected activity or database to its collection route, purpose record, system, owner, recipient/provider, notice reference, and review date. This supports a factual accountability record; it cannot decide controller obligations, database registration, consent, applicability, or lawful processing.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved records and identify absent owners, unsupported purposes, or changed recipients. Costa Rican counsel and designated human owners decide scope and actions. Refresh for a new data use, database, collection channel, provider, data transfer, or material product change.

### 2. Notice, request, and safeguards workflow package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, customer-operations, security, and records owners provide approved notice/version history, rights-request and identity-verification procedures, de-identified case logs, access/security-operation references, retention/disposal execution evidence, incident/exercise records, and corrective-action tracking.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a de-identified request, retention event, or incident exercise through intake, assigned owner, documented human decision, system action, escalation, and follow-up. This can evidence recorded workflow operation; it cannot decide an individual's rights result, verify identity, establish security effectiveness, or determine notification/reporting duties.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized metadata and flag missing timestamps or ownership; it cannot resolve requests, contact people, change records, or submit a notice. Humans and counsel approve decisions and communications. Recheck after an incident, complaint trend, policy/interface change, or failed workflow test.

### 3. Provider, exception, and annual legal-review package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, legal, privacy, security, and leadership owners provide provider/data-recipient records, contract/instruction references, assurance inputs, exception and remediation logs, source-change watch, legal-review record, management decisions, and retest evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected provider change, exception, or remediation item to the factual record, accountable human authority, limitation, due date, and retest/follow-up. This supports reviewable governance; it cannot prove provider performance, accept risk, determine legal sufficiency, or issue an audit conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a non-authoritative evidence packet and flag stale approvals. Human legal, privacy, security, and executive authorities approve contracts, risk treatment, registrations, filings, external statements, and closure. Refresh after a provider change, incident, legal-source update, or annual review.

## Cadence and renewal

Use an annual program review as an engagement convention, supplemented by applicable legal deadlines and material-change review. Reopen affected records after new collection, purpose, database, recipient, transfer, security incident or legal-source change. Monitor overdue requests and missing evidence on a frequency that supports the approved response clock.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the source/scope register, processing and recipient inventory, notice/permission mappings, request-clock records, synthetic test results, safeguard evidence and legal decisions. State what was prepared, executed, verified and approved. Give each unresolved item an owner, next action and required evidence; a future agent must be able to continue without earlier chat. No framework completion claim while source or operational coverage remains unknown.
