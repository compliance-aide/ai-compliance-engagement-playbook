# Costa Rica personal-data-protection engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Costa Rican law or a legal conclusion. Confirm current law, regulations, and authority guidance through [Prodhab’s legislation page](https://www.prodhab.go.cr/acercade/normativa/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an accountable processing record: data purposes, owner decisions, collection and sharing evidence, database records where applicable, service-provider handling, individual requests, incidents, and changes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy and business owners maintain processing evidence; security and supplier owners provide operating records; Costa Rican counsel confirms scope and legal duties; independent reviewers test traceability. AI organizes approved artifacts and drafts gap questions, but cannot interpret legal requirements, register databases, decide individual-rights outcomes, or submit complaints or notifications. Review records at material processing change and through a recurring annual cycle with human approval.
