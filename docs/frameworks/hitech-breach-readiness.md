# HITECH breach readiness — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for a statutory overlay, not a breach determination. Use [HHS OCR breach resources](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/breach-notification/index.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain scope and business-associate inventory, risk decisions, incident-to-breach decision records, notification rehearsal, vendor reporting paths, and corrective actions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized privacy and legal owners decide breach status and notices. Independent reviewers sample risk decisions, timelines, notices, and evidence lineage. AI can flag missing approvals and timing but cannot determine breach status, process PHI in unapproved systems, or submit reports.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
