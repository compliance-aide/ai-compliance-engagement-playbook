# HITECH breach readiness — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a breach determination, legal advice or a notification. Draft pending independent source, skeptical and rights review.

## Source and applicability

Use [HHS OCR breach resources](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/breach-notification/index.html) and current 45 CFR 164.400–414 with qualified counsel. The HHS overview read 2026-09-04 has a 2013 review date; full current regulation, amendments, applicable state law, contracts and encryption/destruction guidance remain unverified.

HHS distinguishes individual, Secretary, media and business-associate notification routes. Its Secretary threshold is 500 or more affected individuals; its media threshold is more than 500 residents of a State or jurisdiction. Fewer-than-500 Secretary reporting does not postpone individual notice. Individual notice is due without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days after discovery; this is an outer limit, not a waiting period. Confirm all clocks and discovery rules against binding sources before execution.

HHS describes a presumption following an impermissible use/disclosure, exceptions and a fact-based assessment addressing information, recipient, actual acquisition/viewing and mitigation. Counsel owns the determination. An incident severity score is not a substitute. Separate HIPAA and FTC health-breach routes rather than assuming identical coverage.

## Engagement focus

Prepare an evidence-backed event decision and notification workflow that preserves uncertainty, accountable deadlines and delivery records. Keep suspected event, legal breach decision, approved notice, attempted delivery and verified completion distinct. Investigation and decision preparation must not silently stop incident response or applicable clocks.

## Roles

Privacy/legal officials decide applicability, breach status, exceptions and notices. Incident command owns containment and fact collection. Data and vendor owners reconcile affected populations and associate relationships. Communications owners prepare approved channels. An independent reviewer challenges chronology, coverage and evidence. Named executives approve decisions where required.

AI may organize redacted facts, reconcile counts and calculate reminders from approved trigger inputs. AI cannot determine breach status, access PHI in unapproved systems, approve exceptions, contact individuals/media/regulators or submit reports. Legal and incident authority remain with designated humans.

## Before starting

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record event/exercise identity, entity roles, known discovery facts, responsible officials, approved evidence locations and communications boundary. Obtain the current notification matrix and associate agreements. Missing facts remain explicit; prompt escalation does not require a completed investigation.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Preserve intake and chronology.** Retain original reports, discovery observations, awareness by relevant parties, affected systems and preservation decisions. Output: attributed timeline with unknowns; reassignment to another team does not reset dates.
2. **Resolve entity/data scope.** Privacy/legal owners identify regulated parties, PHI and applicable notification regimes. Reconcile all known affected systems and associates. Output: role/scope register, not an automatic breach conclusion.
3. **Assemble decision evidence.** Link facts and limitations to the authorized analysis, exception or notification decision. Preserve contrary evidence and revisions. Output: signed or otherwise authorized determination with its factual basis.
4. **Reconcile recipients and clocks.** Count distinct individuals and jurisdiction populations using approved methods, retaining duplicates and exclusions in an audit trail. Record each route's source, trigger, deadline, responsible party and uncertainty. Output: complete notification matrix; count uncertainty is escalated rather than rounded below a threshold.
5. **Prepare notices and delivery plans.** Bind content, recipient population, channel, approvals and any substitute-notice path to the applicable requirement. Output: reviewable versioned package. Test using synthetic recipients only; never send rehearsal notices to actual affected people.
6. **Verify authorized execution.** When separately authorized, record sent/submitted versions, timestamps, recipient counts, acknowledgements, rejected deliveries and follow-up. Output: route-specific receipts. An upload screen or draft press release is not completed notification.
7. **Review and improve.** Reconcile all obligations, late/failed actions, incident remediation and reassessment of new facts. Give independent reviewers full gaps and disagreements. Output: bounded closure or open-action packet with named owners.

## Evidence and test plan

### Regulated-data, entity and associate boundary package

Restore PR340's package with full event population reconciliation. Privacy, security and vendor owners provide role decisions, affected services, associate contacts and evidence references. Track upstream/downstream reporting responsibilities separately. A vendor's assurance that it notified someone does not establish which covered entity, individuals or authority received what.

### Risk and notification-decision package

Legal, privacy and incident owners provide fact versions, authorized analysis, recipient calculations, clock sources and decisions. Distinguish the event-specific compromise assessment from the broader Security Rule risk analysis. Keep conflicting evidence and non-notification rationale reviewable. A generic “encrypted” label cannot settle notification treatment without the applicable method/key facts and legal review.

### Associate readiness, exercises and correction package

Vendor, communications and governance owners supply contacts, contracts, authorized drills, delivery-failure procedures, action owners and retests. Cover the full associate and communication-channel population in scope. Record missing contact information and substitute-route decisions explicitly. A tabletop demonstrates decision discussion; it does not prove delivery capacity or live provider response.

Each case records criterion/source, population, approved expected outcome, evidence date, actual outcome, limitation and owner. Keep PHI, individual contact details and restricted legal advice outside this public repository. Do not trim, sample or cap evidence used to establish affected-person counts or notification coverage.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Incomplete facts or count: preserve uncertainty, bound what is known and promptly escalate deadline/route implications; do not wait silently for final forensics.
- New facts undermine the prior determination: retain the original record and reopen the decision with its owner; do not overwrite history.
- Business associate reports late: preserve both discovery and receipt facts for legal review; do not start every clock at the customer's ticket creation.
- Delivery fails: distinguish attempted dispatch from completion, assign follow-up and retain evidence for required substitute routes.
- Small-event Secretary schedule selected: independently verify individual and other applicable obligations; it is not a global postponement.

Fictional desk case: 499 people are affected and the Secretary report is placed on the approved annual schedule. The individual-notice task is mistakenly deferred to that same schedule. The Secretary scheduling decision does not support individual-notice timing; the latter needs immediate separate review against its actual trigger and deadline. No real notice was sent.

## Cadence and renewal

Maintain event-driven escalation and source-backed clocks. Exercise owner-approved scenarios after associate, channel, personnel or legal changes and failed drills. Do not invent universal annual certification. Reconcile new facts and delivery exceptions until every required route has a supported disposition.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver timeline, role/scope decisions, full recipient accounting, authorized determination, route/deadline matrix, approved versions, receipts, failures, remediation and reviewer disagreements. Classify factual assertions supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable or not_tested. Keep legal decisions and task states separate. No actual breach determination, notification or live response occurred in authoring this guide.
