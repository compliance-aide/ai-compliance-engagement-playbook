# Indonesia Personal Data Protection Law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Indonesian legal advice, a transfer approval or a regulatory conclusion.

## Source and applicability

Start with the [official BPK record for Law 27 of 2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uuno-27-tahun-2022) and [Komdigi legal text](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/832/t/crc32/). The BPK record was read on 2026-09-04; it lists the law as in force and flags constitutional decision 151/PUU-XXII/2024 affecting Article 53(1)(b). Have counsel verify that decision before using an old checklist for appointment criteria. An empty related-rules field on another index does not prove there are no subsequent decisions or implementing measures.

The full statutory text, judgment and current implementing/sector rules have not been reconciled for this draft. The Komdigi text could not be retrieved beyond its initial portion during the author check. Qualified Indonesian legal review must establish current coverage, duties, deadlines, authority and official submission routes before execution. Do not convert a source-access gap into an exemption or rely on a historical transition period without checking its end date.

## Engagement focus

Connect each processing purpose to accountable roles, approved authority, actual data flows, individual requests, security evidence, transfer decisions and lifecycle outcomes. A completed inventory is evidence of scope management, not proof that processing is lawful. Maintain factual findings separately from counsel's determinations.

## Roles

Legal owns applicability, processing grounds, rights outcomes, transfers, incident duties and exceptions. Privacy owns the register and request process. Product and service owners supply operational facts; security preserves incident evidence; procurement obtains processor and recipient evidence; records owners implement approved retention. An independent reviewer challenges source interpretation and end-to-end results. AI may reconcile approved metadata and draft workpapers, but cannot choose legal grounds, approve transfers, decide consent validity, notify regulators, send individual responses or accept risk.

## Before starting

Record entities, services, jurisdictional facts, purposes, data categories, subject groups, systems, recipients, processor relationships and review period. Identify legal/privacy and incident contacts with backups. Obtain approved access to contracts, notices, consent references, logs, safeguards, requests and deletion evidence. Preserve personal data and legal advice in the approved private record system; use only non-sensitive references in this repository.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Establish the source baseline and scope.** Create a provision-level register naming source, version, effective period, relevant judgment, interpretation owner and unresolved questions. Have legal approve the engagement boundary and current duties. Source uncertainty blocks only dependent conclusions; continue authorized inventory work with gaps visible.
2. **Reconcile the complete known processing population.** Compare business, system, vendor and data inventories. Give each purpose and route a stable ID. Map collection, transformation, storage, access, disclosure, return and destruction, including onward processing and remote access. Record missing stores or unknown destinations explicitly; never silently sample, trim or cap evidence.
3. **Resolve authority and higher-risk conditions.** Attach the legal-owner decision and supporting facts to each purpose, role and claimed exception. Record applicable notice/consent versions and special-data considerations. Route impact-assessment and data-protection-officer appointment questions to counsel using current sources, including the Article 53 judgment. A vendor's claim or policy label is not the organization's legal determination.
4. **Validate individual-request execution.** Map each request type to approved identity/authority checks, receipt clock, affected systems, decision owner and response rule. Reconcile every available scoped request with actions and communications. Preserve original receipt time through reassignment. Separate action approved, action executed and response delivered; an automated acknowledgment closes none of them.
5. **Review processors and transfers.** Compare approved instructions, contracts and destinations with actual configuration and logs. Link each cross-border route to its legal decision, conditions and review trigger. Examine onward recipients and remote support access rather than just the hosting region. Do not approve a transfer because a supplier has a security certificate or standard contract. Escalate unsupported routes and retain the actual observed facts.
6. **Review safeguards, incidents and lifecycle outcomes.** Link protection measures and detection evidence to the scoped data. For an event, preserve the factual timeline, affected population, uncertainty and evidence provenance; obtain counsel's reporting decision and approved timing rule promptly. Do not import a deadline from GDPR or a neighboring country's law. Reconcile retention and authorized erasure across processors, backups and failed jobs; resolve preservation conflicts before destruction.
7. **Verify remediation and prepare decisions.** Define expected outcomes for authorized synthetic QA before running it. Capture exact configuration, actual result, deviations, owner and retest evidence. Reconcile all scoped findings and unresolved obligations. Named humans approve legal conclusions, external communications and closure; retain open gaps with next actions rather than issuing an unconditional readiness claim.

## Evidence and test plan

The following packages preserve the earlier tailored plan while expanding selected-record checks to full known scoped population accounting.

| Package | Minimum records | Reviewer challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Processing and accountability | Data/purpose register, roles, category rationale, notices, authority references, recipients and retention decisions | Does each actual use have an owner and a current reviewed rationale? |
| Individuals, security and incidents | Private request references, original timelines, identity checks, system actions, incident evidence, approved clocks and response receipts | Can another reviewer distinguish facts, decisions and observed outcomes? |
| Processors, transfers and governance | Instructions, actual destinations, contracts, transfer decisions, appointment/impact-assessment review, exceptions and retests | Do actual routes match approved conditions, including onward access? |

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) statuses `supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` and `not_tested` for individual assertions. Keep task state separate. Split compound conclusions; known failures remain failures even when other evidence is incomplete. Full population accounting does not imply every technical configuration has been tested.

Fictional author desk case: the approved register lists domestic hosting, but an authorized support log identifies access by a foreign support team. Domestic storage does not establish that no cross-border processing occurs. Preserve the access evidence, reopen the route's legal review and assign the vendor owner to resolve the discrepancy. AI does not decide the transfer's legality or independently disable production access.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Conflicting source records: record both versions and obtain legal reconciliation; do not select whichever permits easier closure.
- Unverified processor instructions or destination: flag the affected route and assign a supplier action; a missing response is not approval.
- Missing incident logs: preserve uncertainty and escalate; absence of records is not evidence that no exposure occurred.
- Destruction conflicts with a preservation direction: pause the destructive step and obtain prompt legal direction while maintaining due-date visibility.
- Response or notification fails: keep the communication task open with retry or alternate-route ownership; a draft or send attempt is not delivery.

## Cadence and renewal

Use owner-approved review intervals and actual source-specific duties. Reopen after a purpose, data category, recipient, processor, transfer, retention, authority, judgment, request or incident change. Do not invent blanket quarterly or annual obligations. Track individual deadlines independently of the governance review calendar.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the source register, scoped processing map, private evidence index, decisions, verification results and unresolved actions to named owners. Independent source, skeptical and rights review remain pending before publication. Author desk cases are not legal opinions, real assessments or cross-model usability trials.
