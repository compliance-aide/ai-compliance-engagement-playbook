# Indonesia Personal Data Protection Law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Indonesian legal advice or a regulatory conclusion. Confirm current law and decisions through the [official legal record](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022.12UUD).

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing inventory, personal-data categories, legal-basis rationale, controller and processor relationships, transfer records, data-subject requests, security events, retention and deletion evidence, risk decisions, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, legal, security, and service owners determine applicable obligations and response decisions; independent reviewers sample evidence. AI maps approved records and flags gaps, but cannot interpret law, approve transfers, notify regulators, or make legal representations. Review quarterly and annually.
