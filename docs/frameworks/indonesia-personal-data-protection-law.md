# Indonesia Personal Data Protection Law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Indonesian legal advice or a regulatory conclusion. Confirm current law and decisions through the [official legal record](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022.12UUD).

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing inventory, personal-data categories, legal-basis rationale, controller and processor relationships, transfer records, data-subject requests, security events, retention and deletion evidence, risk decisions, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, legal, security, and service owners determine applicable obligations and response decisions; independent reviewers sample evidence. AI maps approved records and flags gaps, but cannot interpret law, approve transfers, notify regulators, or make legal representations. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm current material through the [official legal record](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022.12UUD) and qualified Indonesian legal review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide data-controller/processor status, lawful basis, consent, transfer conditions, a rights request, or regulatory notification.

### 1. Data processing and accountability record

- **Request and owner:** Privacy and service owners maintain a data-processing register for categories, sensitive data, purpose, systems, owner, collection source, approved legal/consent rationale, retention, recipients, processors, and cross-border route.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected records to source system, owner, documented purpose, notice/consent artifact where applicable, recipient, and retention execution. This supports factual traceability; it cannot interpret statutory conditions or validate the rationale.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved register entries and flag incomplete ownership. Privacy/legal humans approve purpose, status, and change decisions. Refresh for a new data use, sensitive-data handling, recipient, processor, transfer, or retention change.

### 2. Individual, security, and incident record

- **Request and owner:** Redacted request/complaint files, identity-verification procedure, access and security-operation evidence, incident investigation/exercise timeline, vulnerability/remediation inputs, and data deletion/quality records.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample cases to intake, authorized reviewer, affected service, action, escalation, and closure without placing personal data in public workpapers. This cannot decide an individual outcome, incident impact, safeguards, or reportability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved evidence and identify missing review dates; it cannot determine notification or send communications. Recollect after a request, complaint, incident, security failure, or material control change.

### 3. Processor, transfer, and governance record

- **Request and owner:** Processor instruction/contract register, transfer review inputs, vendor due diligence, risk/exception decisions, management review, corrective-action/retest, and legal/regulatory change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected third-party or transfer records to a human decision, evidence supplied, approved instruction, expiry, and corrective action. This supports governance review; it cannot approve a transfer, accept risk, or make a representation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may produce a redacted packet and flag expiring records. Privacy, legal, security, and supplier owners approve actions. Review annually and on supplier, transfer, or regulatory change.
