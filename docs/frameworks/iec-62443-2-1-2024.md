# IEC 62443-2-1:2024 IACS asset-owner security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC content or an industrial-security conclusion. Check the [IEC record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/62883); do not place IEC text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain IACS asset scope, owner policies, legacy-system decisions, security-program evidence, risk and compensating-measure records, incidents, suppliers, tests, exceptions, and improvement plans.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Asset owners and safety/security leaders determine operational and risk decisions; independent reviewers test evidence. AI organizes approved records and flags gaps, but cannot alter industrial systems, approve compensating measures, or accept risk. Review continuously and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm the applicable IEC 62443-2-1:2024 publication through the [IEC record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/62883), checked 2026-07-31, and obtain authorized material only. IEC content is licensed. This original plan does not reproduce the standard, authorize IACS changes, or claim industrial-security conformity.

### 1. IACS asset boundary and ownership package

- **Request and owner:** Asset, operations, safety, and security owners provide the approved IACS inventory, zone/conduit or equivalent boundary decisions where used, criticality, asset/accountable owner, connection map references, and stated exclusions.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a material asset or connection and trace it to its operational owner, security owner, boundary record, and approved scope. This tests whether the engagement boundary is reviewable; it cannot establish a safe configuration or complete discovery.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may correlate approved inventories and identify missing ownership or stale maps. Humans approve scope and safety/operational decisions. Refresh before a new connection, ownership change, major modernization, or boundary change.

### 2. Security-program and risk-treatment package

- **Request and owner:** Program and risk owners provide approved policies/procedures, risk and threat records, compensating-measure rationale, exception records, assigned treatment actions, and authorized residual-risk decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected material risk or exception from owner and evidence through mitigation, decision authority, and follow-up. This supports accountable documentation; it cannot validate a compensating measure, accept risk, or assess safe operation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized workpapers and flag expired exceptions. It must not access control systems, choose safeguards, approve a measure, or accept risk. Reopen after a vulnerability, incident, audit observation, or change in operational conditions.

### 3. Operations, incident, and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Operations, incident-response, supplier, and maintenance owners provide access/change records, maintenance windows, incident and recovery records, supplier interfaces, exercise or review results, corrective actions, and closure decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected change, incident, or corrective action for owner, operational authorization, evidence trail, safety consideration, and follow-up. This tests documentation of a sample; it cannot prove continuous monitoring, system resilience, or recovery success.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a cross-record timeline and surface overdue actions without operating IACS. Humans authorize operational changes, incident actions, closures, and communications. Review at least annually and after a material incident, supplier change, or failed exercise.
