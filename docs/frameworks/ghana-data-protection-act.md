# Ghana Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, a registration determination or a compliance assertion. Draft pending independent source, skeptical and rights review.

## Source and applicability

Use the [Commission document collection](https://dataprotection.org.gh/documents/), its [Act 843 PDF](https://dataprotection.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Data-Protection-Act-2012-Act-843.pdf), and current [registration guidance](https://dataprotection.org.gh/registration/). The document collection separately lists a draft bill; do not treat a draft as enacted law.

Selected Act passages read for this draft include sections 29–33. They address processor authority/confidentiality, written processor contracts and security, security-compromise notification, access and correction. Section 31 uses a reasonably-practicable notification standard after discovery; do not import GDPR's numerical deadline. Have Ghanaian counsel verify the complete trigger, recipients, exceptions, current amendments and applicable guidance.

Source limitation: only selected statutory passages and indexed Commission registration/document guidance were reviewed. Full text, current legal status, registration conditions, rights deadlines and republication rights remain for qualified review. Below are original evidence procedures, not a substitute for those determinations.

## Engagement focus

Connect every scoped processing route to its purpose, accountable party, collection practices, recipients, retention, rights handling, security and registration decisions. Separate factual operating evidence from legal conclusions and regulator acceptance. A certificate does not prove each processing practice complies.

## Roles

Privacy and business owners own the processing register. System, records and procurement owners supply implementation and processor evidence. Ghanaian counsel decides applicability and legal interpretation. Named registration, rights and incident officials approve filings, outcomes and communications. An independent reviewer challenges completeness and factual conclusions.

AI may reconcile authorized inventories, flag discrepancies and draft workpapers. AI cannot determine lawful processing, identify a requester conclusively, approve rights outcomes, submit filings, notify individuals or regulators, accept risk or claim compliance. Keep real personal data and confidential records in approved restricted systems.

## Before starting

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record entity, relevant Ghana connection, controller/processor role questions, period, source versions, owners and permitted actions. Obtain the complete scoped processing inventory, registration records, approved rights procedures, incident route and evidence-access limits. Missing applicability or authority blocks dependent actions; continue independent fact collection.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Establish scope and sources.** Counsel identifies applicable obligations, exemptions and current source versions; owners enumerate all relevant systems, channels and providers. Output: scoped obligation register with unresolved questions assigned.
2. **Trace processing.** Map each route from collection through use, disclosure, retention and disposal. Record purpose, categories, notices, permissions/other approved justification, recipients and actual system behavior. Output: complete processing register with unknown routes retained.
3. **Reconcile registration.** Match entity identity and declared activities to official records, certificate/status evidence and any applicable update or renewal decision. Output: registration evidence packet distinguishing prepared, submitted, acknowledged and confirmed states. A payment or application receipt is not confirmation of registration.
4. **Check safeguards and processors.** Compare approved instructions and contracts with actual access, data flows and security evidence. Include foreign-provider questions for counsel. Output: responsibility matrix and coverage gaps; contract language alone cannot prove implementation.
5. **Test rights and retention routes.** In authorized QA or redacted records, trace intake, identity-check decision, population search, approved outcome, system action and response. Include downstream correction, preferences and disposal where applicable. Output: case results with missing systems and unobserved recipients explicit.
6. **Trace security events.** Preserve discovery facts, affected population, uncertainty, authority decisions, restoration actions and notification evidence. Escalate promptly against the verified Ghana-specific trigger. Output: incident decision packet; drafting a notice is not sending it, and sending is not confirmed receipt.
7. **Review and hand off.** Reconcile changes and remediation against every affected route. Give the independent reviewer full evidence and disagreements. Output: bounded conclusions, open actions, owners and approval requests; no blanket compliance claim.

## Evidence and test plan

### Processing, purpose and registration package

Privacy, business and product owners supply the processing register, collection channels, notices, recipients, retention context and registration evidence. Reconcile the full scope against system and provider records. Preserve entity-name conflicts, undeclared changes and source uncertainties. Record the exact scope and date of any official status rather than extending it to affiliates or later practices.

### Rights, retention and direct-contact package

Privacy, records and customer operations supply approved procedures, de-identified case indexes, preference records, correction/disposal outcomes and escalation logs. For each case preserve original receipt time, approved identity outcome, searched systems, factual results, exceptions, response authority and closure evidence. Do not reset a clock when a request moves teams. Verify relevant downstream action separately from the controller's instruction. Do not infer deletion from an empty screen or preference enforcement from a saved checkbox.

### Processor, security-event and governance package

Procurement, security and leadership supply provider instructions/contracts, access and safeguard records, incident timelines, management decisions and remediation/retest evidence. Trace every known scoped provider and event to an owner. Distinguish restoration, containment, notification and prevention of recurrence. A closed ticket does not prove each outcome; record untested parts explicitly.

Each test identifies its criterion, approved expected result, authorized method, evidence source/date, affected population and limitation. Full inventory coverage does not imply every technical case was tested. Preserve all known gaps without silent sampling or caps.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Registration evidence missing or mismatched: mark the factual status claim inconclusive and escalate applicability/operating decisions to counsel and the accountable official.
- Confirmed data route absent from declared inventory: mark inventory completeness not_supported, retain the route and reopen affected notices, registration and security questions.
- Correction instructed but recipient action unknown: distinguish sent instruction from unverified downstream result; do not close the latter as supported.
- Incident notification criteria uncertain: promptly route known facts and source gaps to the named authority; do not wait for perfect forensic certainty to escalate.
- Rights outcome disputed: preserve the request, evidence and authorized decision path; AI must not adjudicate the dispute or erase its history.

Fictional desk case: the controller corrects a QA record and instructs a processor, but no processor readback exists. Local correction is supported; downstream correction remains inconclusive. The instruction does not establish the processor's action. No actual personal-data operation was performed.

## Cadence and renewal

Record source-backed registration/renewal and request requirements after qualified verification. Set additional operational reviews according to risk and owner policy. Reopen affected work after new purposes, systems, recipients, source changes, complaints, incidents or failed tests. Do not invent a universal quarterly or annual statutory review cycle.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver source/applicability questions, complete processing and provider inventories, registration-state evidence, rights/retention tests, incident decisions, gaps, remediation owners and reviewer disagreements. Classify each assertion supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable or not_tested with its reason. Keep factual status, task completion and human authority separate. Filings and communications require explicit authorization and readback receipts.
