# Ghana Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the [Ghana Data Protection Commission](https://dataprotection.org.gh/what-we-do/) and applicable guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain data, process, vendor, collection, use, disclosure, retention, rights, incident, registration-review, and remediation evidence; revalidate after product changes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators execute processes; independent reviewers test selected flows and records. AI classifies evidence and discrepancies, but cannot authenticate compliance, make filings, or resolve individual requests. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Ghana Data Protection Commission](https://dataprotection.org.gh/what-we-do/) official material, checked 2026-07-31. Source status, current consolidated text, registration scope, and republication rights require qualified Ghanaian legal review. This is original planning guidance, not legal text or a filing determination.

### 1. Processing, purpose, and registration-question package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, business, product, and records owners provide a dated processing register, purpose and collection-channel records, data categories, accountable roles, systems, recipients, retention context, notices, and a registration/applicability question log.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one declared processing route from collection to the accountable owner, system, documented purpose, notice, recipient, and review date. This supports an evidence trail; it cannot determine lawful processing, registration, notice sufficiency, or compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved inventories and flag missing owners or stale reviews. Ghanaian counsel and named humans decide scope, filings, and releases. Refresh for a new collection, purpose, system, recipient, or source change.

### 2. Individual-rights, retention, and direct-contact package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy and customer-operations owners provide approved request and identity-check procedures, de-identified case indexes, communication/marketing preference records, correction or deletion workflow evidence, retention/disposal records, training, and escalation logs.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one redacted request, preference, or disposal event through intake, human authority, source record, system action, response approval, and closure. This supports workflow traceability; it cannot identify a requester, decide an outcome, or establish statutory timing.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a restricted sample index and identify stalled cases; it cannot communicate with individuals or alter records. Humans approve outcomes. Recollect after complaint trends, workflow failures, or a new contact channel.

### 3. Processor, security-event, and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, security, privacy, and leadership owners provide supplier registers, instruction/assurance references, access and incident-exercise records, change reviews, exception/remediation logs, and management decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier change, incident exercise, or remediation to factual inputs, decision authority, action owner, and retest. This supports governed oversight; it cannot prove safeguards effective, decide notification, accept risk, or assess supplier compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved evidence and flag overdue actions. Humans decide contracts, notifications, risk acceptance, and closure. Refresh after a processor change, incident, failed test, or annual review.
