# Ghana Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the [Ghana Data Protection Commission](https://dataprotection.org.gh/what-we-do/) and applicable guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain data, process, vendor, collection, use, disclosure, retention, rights, incident, registration-review, and remediation evidence; revalidate after product changes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators execute processes; independent reviewers test selected flows and records. AI classifies evidence and discrepancies, but cannot authenticate compliance, make filings, or resolve individual requests. Review quarterly and annually.
