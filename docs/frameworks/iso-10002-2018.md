# ISO 10002:2018 complaints handling — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a customer-remedy decision. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71580.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain complaint channels, intake records, categorization, owners, response commitments, privacy and escalation boundaries, investigations, resolutions, trend analysis, and improvement actions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Customer, legal, product, and service leaders determine responses and remedies; independent reviewers test the evidence chain. AI classifies non-sensitive themes and drafts questions, but cannot promise remedies, decide disputes, or send customer communications. Monitor continuously and review annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm the current edition at the [ISO 10002:2018 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71580.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO material is licensed. This is original guidance using evidence categories, not ISO clauses, a complaint decision, or a conformity claim.

### 1. Intake, ownership, and response-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Customer-service and legal owners provide approved intake channels, sampled case records, category definitions, ownership/escalation assignments, communication commitments, and privacy or dispute-routing boundaries.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected case from receipt through ownership and customer response record, including any escalation. This supports auditable handling flow; it cannot decide whether a remedy was fair, timely in every circumstance, or legally sufficient.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may group de-identified themes and flag missing owners; it may not contact a complainant, promise a remedy, or decide a dispute. Refresh when channels, service commitments, privacy rules, or escalation routes change.

### 2. Investigation, resolution, and exception package

- **Request and owner:** Case owners provide investigation references, factual support, resolution rationale, approval records, open-case aging, exception/escalation logs, and restricted-access handling records.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected resolved or overdue case for linkage between the reported issue, investigation record, human decision, response, and retained evidence. This supports traceability; it cannot validate the underlying facts or authorize closure.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied records and identify aging patterns within approved access limits. Humans approve outcomes, external communications, exceptions, and closure. Recollect after a material incident, regulatory inquiry, or repeated overdue trend.

### 3. Trend, improvement, and management-review package

- **Request and owner:** Service, product, and leadership owners provide period trend analyses, recurring-cause investigations, improvement actions, effectiveness follow-up, decision minutes, and unresolved-risk records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one recurring theme to its evidence basis, accountable improvement action, due date, and management review. This supports a visible improvement loop; it cannot prove customer satisfaction or that recurrence is eliminated.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a source-linked trend packet and flag unclosed actions. Humans prioritize changes, accept residual risk, and approve public statements. Review quarterly and close the annual cycle with independent sampling.
