# ISO 10002:2018 complaints handling — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, a conformity claim or an authorized customer-remedy decision.

## Source and applicability

The [official ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71580.html), checked 2026-09-04, describes guidance for internal handling of product/service complaints, covering the process lifecycle and improvement. It lists the 2018 edition as current following 2023 confirmation. Employment disputes and disputes referred outside the organization are outside the stated scope; route them to the appropriate owner without dropping the customer's issue.

The publisher displays restrictions on using ISO content in AI systems. Do not put licensed text into prompts or retrieve further protected material without confirmed permission. This original workflow uses operational evidence categories; the full standard has not been reviewed. A qualified authorized reviewer must confirm source accuracy, permitted use and applicability before publication or conformity assessment.

## Engagement focus

Make every complaint traceable from receipt to investigation, authorized response, implemented remedy and a defensible closure record. Maintain improvement work separately from individual case resolution. A closed ticket does not prove customer satisfaction or that a recurring defect has been eliminated.

## Roles

The complaint-process owner sets channels, service commitments, resources and escalation routes. Case owners collect facts and coordinate action; product/service owners investigate; legal and specialist owners handle regulated, safety, privacy or dispute matters. Named authorized officials approve remedies and communications. An independent reviewer challenges fairness of process, traceability and unsupported conclusions. AI may organize de-identified themes and draft internal questions but cannot promise refunds, accept liability, decide disputes, send customer messages or approve closure.

## Before starting

Record products/services, customer groups, languages, channels, locations, period and accountable owners. Obtain approved procedures, commitments, category definitions, private complaint records and permissions. Confirm how urgent concerns reach the responsible team and how complainants can use an accessible alternative when a channel fails. Do not place customer identities, attachments or sensitive allegations in public workpapers.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Check channel readiness and scope.** Reconcile all known intake routes, including complaints received through sales, support or other teams. Assign a receiving owner, handoff route, backup and approved response commitment to each. Test only with authorized synthetic cases. An advertised form that does not create a retrievable case is a failed channel, even if its confirmation page displays success.
2. **Register and preserve the complaint.** Give each case a stable ID, original receipt time, source channel, reported issue, desired outcome and evidence reference. Preserve the complainant's account separately from staff interpretation. Link duplicate contacts without erasing chronology or distinct allegations. Record missing information and request it through authorized staff rather than inventing facts.
3. **Triage and assign ownership.** Apply approved urgency and escalation criteria. Safety, security, privacy and legal concerns may need parallel specialist action; do not wait for routine complaint resolution. Record owner, deadlines, conflicts of interest and any special handling. Reassignment retains the original clock. Explain disputed or out-of-scope routing in the private record, with a receiving owner and follow-up.
4. **Investigate the specific issue.** Compare the complaint with relevant service records and authorized evidence. Track each allegation, supporting and conflicting facts, unresolved questions and investigation actions. Separate observed facts from causal hypotheses. A generic policy does not establish what happened in this case. Exit with a reasoned finding or an explicit evidence limitation, not an AI confidence score alone.
5. **Approve and implement the response.** Link proposed explanation and remedy to the findings, delegated authority and any legal review. Capture approval before communication or expenditure. Keep offered, accepted, executed and verified outcomes distinct. Track promised follow-up and failed delivery. An authorized communicator sends the response; AI drafting does not imply permission to contact the customer.
6. **Decide closure and escalation.** Reconcile every allegation, promised action, response and unresolved point against approved closure criteria. Record customer feedback or lack of response accurately. Silence is not agreement. If the customer rejects the outcome or new facts emerge, record the applicable internal review or external dispute route and preserve the history. Do not reopen only to reset performance metrics.
7. **Use complaints to improve the process.** Analyze the complete known period population with category definitions, duplicates, exposure measures, open cases and exclusions visible. Link recurring themes to an accountable improvement action, due date and effectiveness check. Review resources, inaccessible channels and overdue cases as well as product defects. Reduced complaint volume alone cannot establish better service if fewer customers could report problems.

## Evidence and test plan

The three packages below preserve the earlier tailored plan and expand selected cases to full known population accounting. Individual technical tests remain bounded and explicitly described.

| Package | Minimum records | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake, ownership and response boundary | Channel register, complete known case index, receipt times, owners, commitments, escalation and access rules | Can every received complaint be found and assigned without losing its clock? |
| Investigation, resolution and exceptions | Allegation/fact links, conflicting evidence, remedy authority, communications, execution, open actions and closure rationale | Does the decision follow from the evidence, and was the promised action completed? |
| Trends, improvement and management review | Population definition, calculation rules, themes, causal evidence, changes, follow-up and unresolved risks | Does the improvement claim survive channel, volume and case-mix changes? |

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) statuses `supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` and `not_tested` for individual assertions. Keep case status separate from evidence conclusions. Preserve all available scoped evidence without silent sampling, trimming or caps; represent restricted material by approved private references rather than copying it into an unapproved tool.

Fictional author desk case: a case closes after a refund is approved, but the payment record shows it failed. The remedy-executed assertion is not supported. Keep fulfillment open, assign the authorized payments owner and retain the original complaint timeline. Do not issue another payment without checking the actual transaction state and authority.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Missing or conflicting records: preserve uncertainty, assign an investigation action and avoid unsupported blame or dismissal.
- Owner is implicated in the allegation: route review through the approved impartial escalation path.
- Channel outage or lost handoff: record the failure, recover cases from authorized sources and reconcile the affected period before declaring intake complete.
- Complaint contains urgent harm or sensitive information: invoke the designated specialist route and restrict access without making the complaint disappear.

## Cadence and renewal

Set review frequency from service commitments, risk, case volume and applicable obligations; do not invent quarterly or annual ISO mandates. Monitor case deadlines independently of management reviews. Reassess after channel, product, staffing, policy or escalation changes and when complaints recur after a claimed fix.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the channel/case index, private evidence references, outstanding actions, closure decisions and improvement results to named owners. Report unresolved complaints and failed promises explicitly. Independent source, skeptical and rights review remain pending before guide publication. Author desk checks are not customer research, independent review or cross-model usability trials.
