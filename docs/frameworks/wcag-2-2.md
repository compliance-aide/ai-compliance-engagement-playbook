# WCAG 2.2 — accessibility engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a W3C conformance claim. Consult the [WCAG 2.2 Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain product/content inventory, target level, acceptance, automated and human evaluation, findings, user feedback, remediation, retest, and unresolved-barrier evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans validate with assistive technology and approve claims; independent reviewers sample results. AI triages issues and drafts suggestions, but cannot make conformance claims. Test continuously, review quarterly, and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
