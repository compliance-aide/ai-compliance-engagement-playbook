> Original operational guidance, not a management conclusion or auditor report.

## Engagement focus

Set the reporting boundary, materiality/risk method, accountable control owners,
and external-auditor coordination rules. Capture financial-process and technology
changes, evaluate their effects on management's documented rationale, remediate
deficiencies, retain evidence, and carry lessons into the next fiscal cycle.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management owns evaluation, remediation, and its report. Independent auditors
perform separately required work. AI organizes controlled documentation, change
tracking, and evidence lineage; it cannot set materiality, conclude effectiveness,
classify a deficiency, sign certifications, or access MNPI without controls.
