# ISO/IEC 27551:2021 unlinkable-authentication engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, an authentication design, an identity decision, an access decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27551:2021 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72018.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an attribute-based authentication engagement record connecting service scope, accountable owners, authorized design and privacy evidence, relying-party and supplier dependencies, assurance assumptions, exceptions, lifecycle changes, incidents, remediation, and independent review. AI supports evidence organization only; it does not verify identity or make authentication outcomes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Identity, security, privacy, product, legal, risk, and service owners retain accountable decisions; evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, assurance assumptions, privacy boundaries, traceability, and exceptions. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot authenticate an entity, verify attributes, grant or revoke access, establish an assurance level, accept risk, alter systems, initiate changes, or claim compliance. Review material design and supplier changes continuously, conduct quarterly evidence reviews, and reassess annually.
