# MHRA GxP data-integrity engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not MHRA guidance or an investigation conclusion. Consult the [MHRA data-integrity guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/guidance-on-gxp-data-integrity).

## Engagement focus

Maintain GxP data lifecycle, outsourced flows, criticality, lineage, anomalous changes, training, investigations, CAPA, provenance, and closure evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans approve investigations and CAPA closure; independent reviewers use frozen sample ledgers. AI finds anomalies and gaps, but cannot accept risk or reach conclusions. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
