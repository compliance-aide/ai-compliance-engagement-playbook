# ISO 13485:2016 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Confirm version status at the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/59752.html); do not input ISO material to AI tools.

## Engagement focus

Define QMS, device, and regulatory scope; maintain design-to-postmarket lifecycle evidence, supplier/change/CAPA rhythms, risk and feedback signals, internal audit records, management review, and external-audit readiness.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Quality and product leaders own decisions and release evidence. Independent reviewers test selected trails and independence. AI uses only organization-owned approved materials and cannot make safety, regulatory, release, CAPA-closure, or certification conclusions. Review lifecycle signals quarterly and management evidence annually.

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
