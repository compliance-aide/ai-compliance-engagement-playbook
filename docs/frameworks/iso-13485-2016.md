# ISO 13485:2016 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or certification advice. Confirm version status at the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/59752.html); do not input ISO material to AI tools.

## Engagement focus

Define QMS, device, and regulatory scope; maintain design-to-postmarket lifecycle evidence, supplier/change/CAPA rhythms, risk and feedback signals, internal audit records, management review, and external-audit readiness.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Quality and product leaders own decisions and release evidence. Independent reviewers test selected trails and independence. AI uses only organization-owned approved materials and cannot make safety, regulatory, release, CAPA-closure, or certification conclusions. Review lifecycle signals quarterly and management evidence annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Check the current status through the [ISO 13485:2016 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/59752.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO content is licensed. This is original evidence-planning guidance, not medical-device requirements, regulatory advice, safety assessment, certification advice, or a release decision.

### 1. Device/QMS scope and lifecycle-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Quality, regulatory, and product owners provide device/family scope, applicable-market assumptions, QMS boundary, lifecycle ownership, approved change routes, supplier interfaces, and management decision records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected product or change boundary to an accountable owner and approved governance record. This supports scope traceability; it cannot establish regulatory applicability, safety, or market authorization.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile organization-owned inventories and flag missing ownership. Humans decide scope, regulatory strategy, changes, and release. Refresh after a device, intended-use, supplier, market, or material process change.

### 2. Design, production, and feedback traceability package

- **Request and owner:** Quality and product teams provide selected lifecycle records, approved specifications/reviews, production or service trace references, supplier-quality evidence, complaint/feedback intake, and controlled change history.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect one approved trace from a lifecycle decision through linked controlled records and feedback disposition. This supports completeness of the selected trail; it cannot validate design adequacy, product safety, or production quality.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved, non-sensitive records and flag broken links; it may not alter controlled records or make technical, clinical, or safety conclusions. Recollect after material feedback, supplier failure, or process deviation.

### 3. Issue, corrective-action, and management oversight package

- **Request and owner:** Quality leadership provides nonconformance/issue records, investigation summaries, corrective-action plans, verification evidence, internal-audit outputs, management-review inputs, and unresolved-risk decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected issue to human investigation, approved action, accountable owner, due date, and follow-up evidence. This supports governed follow-through; it cannot close a corrective action, accept safety risk, or prove effectiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize evidence and highlight aging actions. Humans approve actions, risk decisions, closure, external communications, and certification statements. Review lifecycle signals quarterly and conduct annual management review.
