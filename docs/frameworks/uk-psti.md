# UK PSTI product-security regime — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a product-compliance statement. Check the [UK PSTI regime guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-product-security-and-telecommunications-infrastructure-product-security-regime).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a UK consumer-connectable product catalogue, role map, release and support ownership, product decisions, vulnerability handling, support-period, contact, supply-chain, and statement-distribution evidence by model/version.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product owners maintain records; independent reviewers sample catalogue, release, and post-release practice. AI connects product/build/support evidence and flags gaps, but cannot declare a product compliant, choose exceptions, or issue statements. Review releases continuously and catalogue annually.
