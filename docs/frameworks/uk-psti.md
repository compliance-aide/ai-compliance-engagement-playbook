# UK PSTI product-security regime — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a product-compliance statement. Check the [UK PSTI regime guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-product-security-and-telecommunications-infrastructure-product-security-regime).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a UK consumer-connectable product catalogue, role map, release and support ownership, product decisions, vulnerability handling, support-period, contact, supply-chain, and statement-distribution evidence by model/version.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product owners maintain records; independent reviewers sample catalogue, release, and post-release practice. AI connects product/build/support evidence and flags gaps, but cannot declare a product compliant, choose exceptions, or issue statements. Review releases continuously and catalogue annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [UK PSTI regime guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-product-security-and-telecommunications-infrastructure-product-security-regime), checked 2026-07-31, with current official guidance and counsel. This is original evidence-planning language; it does not reproduce legal text, decide product scope, issue a statement, or assert UK compliance. Product, legal, security, and authorized business owners retain all role, scope, release, exception, and external-representation decisions.

### 1. UK product catalogue and accountable-role record

- **Request and owner:** Product, legal, commercial, supply-chain, and security owners provide consumer-connectable product/model/version catalogue entries, UK distribution context, responsible-role map, supplier and service dependencies, support owner, and dated scope/role decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected catalogue entry to its model/version, accountable product and support owners, distribution context, dependency record, and review date. This supports traceability; it cannot determine legal scope, establish a responsible role, or prove catalogue completeness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied catalogue and ownership metadata and flag version or owner conflicts. Humans decide scope and roles. Refresh before a UK launch, product/version change, supplier/channel change, or support ownership change.

### 2. Vulnerability, support, and release-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Engineering, security, support, and supplier owners provide vulnerability-intake and response records, security-test references, release/change approvals, update delivery evidence, support-period information, customer security-contact records, and relevant supplier commitments.
- **Validate and limit:** Follow a selected vulnerability or material release from intake through accountable triage, update or disposition decision, product/version record, and customer-support communication. This supports a selected lifecycle trail; it cannot prove security, guarantee remediation, or approve release.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied records and flag missing support dates, remediation owners, or version links. Humans approve release, remediation, support commitments, and disclosures. Recollect after a vulnerability, release, support-period change, material supplier issue, or incident.

### 3. Statement control, exception, and independent review workpaper

- **Request and owner:** Product, legal, communications, records, and assurance owners provide controlled statement drafts and versions, signer authority, product/version references, distribution records, exception rationale, review observations, remediation actions, and retest or follow-up records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected external statement or exception to supporting records, accountable approval, product/version scope, expiry or follow-up date, and independent challenge notes. This supports disciplined representation and remediation; it cannot issue a statement, accept risk, or replace independent review.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare an access-controlled evidence index and flag statements or exceptions lacking support. Humans approve external claims, exceptions, closure, and escalation. Review before distribution, at each material release, and annually across the active catalogue.
