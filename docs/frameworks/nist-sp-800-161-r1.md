# NIST SP 800-161 Revision 1 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not supplier certification. Check the current
> [NIST supply-chain publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/161/r1/upd1/final)
> before each cycle.

## Engagement focus

Treat suppliers, components, services, and dependencies as a governed business
process. Map material dependencies, distinguish verified facts from supplier
assertions, define escalation thresholds, review lifecycle exposure, collect
proportionate assurance, and exercise a meaningful disruption scenario.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Procurement, security, and service teams own dependency records and mitigation.
Independent reviewers test inventory and criticality logic. AI organizes evidence,
flags concentration and stale assertions, and prepares renewal questions; it
never infers supplier compliance from a logo or questionnaire. Reassess the
portfolio annually and before critical renewals.
