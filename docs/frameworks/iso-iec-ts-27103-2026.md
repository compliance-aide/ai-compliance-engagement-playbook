# ISO/IEC TS 27103:2026 cybersecurity-framework engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a framework mapping, a control equivalence decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC TS 27103:2026 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/27103).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a standards-integration record that connects the organization’s cybersecurity framework, accountable governance owners, selected standards and sources, scope and applicability rationale, authorized evidence, implementation dependencies, exceptions, decisions, refresh triggers, and independent review. This is a governance and traceability tool—not an implied crosswalk or equivalence conclusion.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Security, risk, privacy, engineering, legal, audit, and business owners retain accountable choices; framework and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test source currency, scope rationale, traceability, and conclusion boundaries. AI may organize authorized source references, identify stale or missing evidence, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot create an authoritative mapping, declare equivalence, select or approve standards, determine applicability, accept risk, alter systems, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Reassess annually and before material framework, regulatory, technology, supplier, incident, or organizational changes.
