# ISO/IEC 27555:2021 PII-deletion engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a legal retention rule, a deletion instruction, a data-destruction authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27555:2021 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71673.html), which identifies a revision in development.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a PII-deletion readiness record that connects data categories, accountable owners, retention and deletion-rule sources, legal and contractual holds, system and supplier dependencies, authorized deletion evidence, exceptions, incidents, remediation, and independent review. AI can organize authorized evidence and flag contradictions, but it must not cause or authorize data destruction.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, legal, records-management, security, operations, product, and service owners retain accountable decisions; data and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test rule traceability, hold handling, deletion evidence, supplier dependencies, and escalation paths. AI may organize authorized records, identify stale rules or missing evidence, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot set retention or deletion rules, decide a legal hold, authorize or execute deletion, alter production data, accept risk, initiate changes, or claim compliance. Review rules and evidence quarterly, reassess annually, and reopen the record before material legal, contractual, system, supplier, data, or incident changes.
