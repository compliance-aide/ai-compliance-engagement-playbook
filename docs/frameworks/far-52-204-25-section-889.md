# FAR 52.204-25 / Section 889 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a contractual representation. Confirm current text at the [Acquisition.gov clause record](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.204-25?searchTerms=section+889).

## Source and applicability

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Source check, 2026-09-04: read the
complete [official November 2021 clause](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.204-25),
displayed under FAC 2026-01. Paragraph (b) distinguishes provision to Government
from entity use, including use outside Federal performance. Paragraph (d) sets
initial reporting within one business day of identification/notification and
follow-up within ten business days of the initial submission. Paragraph (e)
excludes (b)(2) from the prescribed flowdown. Verify the incorporated award,
deviations, current reporting route and related representation provisions before
applying these anchors to an actual contract.


## Engagement focus

Maintain solicitation and contract scope, product and service inventory, supplier attestations, due-diligence evidence, exception and waiver records, incident/escalation decisions, disclosures, and renewal reviews.

## Before starting

Define the solicitation/award, entity boundary, review period, inventory sources,
service dependencies and named legal/contract decision owners. Record separate
supply and use assertions in [work items](../../templates/work-item.md), including
criteria, evidence location and unresolved source questions. Keep procurement,
network and confidential supplier evidence in its authorized repository.

Reconcile the inquiry plan with the incorporated definition of reasonable inquiry.
Do not turn this guide into a mandatory forensic audit of every component. Record
what sources the approved inquiry actually covers and what remains unknown;
never trim evidence bound for an assessment scorer.

## Ordered workflow


1. Obtain the solicitation/award, incorporated clauses, modifications and any
   approved exception or waiver. Keep the applicable version and decision owner
   visible. A supplier certificate cannot establish which contract text applies.
2. Build separate inventories for equipment/services supplied under the contract
   and equipment/services used by the contractor within the applicable inquiry
   boundary. Do not limit the latter automatically to Government-funded projects.
   Reconcile procurement, network, facilities and service-owner records.
3. Identify producer/provider, exact model, service, location, intended use and
   supporting records. Preserve unknown OEM, subsidiary or affiliate relationships
   for resolution; reseller branding alone does not establish manufacturer identity.
4. Have contracts/legal apply the clause's definitions, substantial/essential or
   critical-technology conditions and exceptions to the evidence. Record the basis
   of every disposition. Do not equate all China-origin goods with prohibited
   equipment or treat a marketing claim of “889 compliant” as a complete analysis.
5. For each waiver or exception relied upon, link the authoritative approval or
   decision, covered entity/items, conditions and period. Check its exact scope
   against the proposed use. A waiver request is not a granted waiver; a granted
   waiver for one case does not automatically cover another.
6. On potentially reportable discovery, preserve the identification or notification
   time and evidence, escalate immediately to the authorized contract owner, and
   prepare the required factual packet. Record the initial and follow-up clocks
   separately with approved business-day computation. Do not wait for complete
   mitigation evidence before escalating the initial reporting decision.
7. Verify current recipients and authorized submission procedure, including affected
   orders where relevant. Preserve draft, approval, actual submission and receipt
   as different records. Do not file a government report or update representations
   during this guide-development task.
8. Reconcile required subcontract language to executed instruments and preserve
   lower-tier discovery/escalation paths. Do not copy the prime entity-use paragraph
   into the mandatory flowdown merely because it appears in the prime clause;
   obtain legal review of additional independently negotiated requirements.
9. Track remediation, replacement, further available facts and prevention changes.
   Verify actual installed/service state after authorized corrections and preserve
   earlier findings. A purchase order for replacement does not prove removal of
   the affected equipment or completion of reporting.

## Failure branches and decisions

 an unresolved OEM leaves the classification `inconclusive`;
a missing inquiry source is `not_tested`, not evidence of no covered use. A verified
inventory omission makes the inventory-completeness assertion `not_supported`.
Reportability and any exception remain separate human decisions.

**Fictional desk case:** the supplied-product inventory is clear, but facilities
records identify an unresolved camera model used in the contractor's office.
The supplied-product result cannot establish the entity-use result. If a supplier
later identifies the OEM, preserve that new evidence and its receipt time for
legal/contract review; do not backdate knowledge or restart a clock without a
supported decision. No actual contractor, prohibited item or report is represented.

## Roles

Procurement, legal, supply-chain, and authorized contract officials determine applicability and representations; independent reviewers test evidence. AI compiles approved records and flags missing artifacts, but cannot determine coverage, certify, disclose, or submit. Recheck at the approved solicitation and change triggers below.


## Evidence and test plan

The three packages below retain PR #340's useful requests with complete coverage
of the approved inquiry population. Earlier review does not approve this revision.

### 1. Solicitation, award, and representation-scope package

- **Request and owner:** Contracts and procurement owners provide solicitation/award versions, amendment history, clause applicability and representation decision records, bidder/offeror scope, deadlines, named approvers, and any agency-directed instructions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace each in-scope representation or applicability record to the relevant contract artifact, version/date, owner, approval authority, and stated scope. This tests record linkage; it cannot decide covered use/equipment/services or make a representation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare supplied versions, calendar deadlines, and flag missing authorities. Humans interpret the award and authorize all representations. Refresh upon solicitation, amendment, award, renewal, or direction change.

### 2. Product, service, and supplier due-diligence package

- **Request and owner:** Supply-chain, asset, IT, and vendor-management owners provide scoped product/service inventories, supplier identity and due-diligence records, procurement records, supporting attestations where authorized, lifecycle/change information, and unresolved-item register.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace each in-scope inventory item to its supplier/source record, acquisition/use context, owner, diligence evidence, limitation, and disposition date. This does not independently determine manufacturer ownership, coverage, or eligibility.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventory and supplier data, flag gaps or outdated diligence, and prepare questions. Humans investigate, decide disposition, and approve supplier actions. Refresh after new procurement, supplier change, or inventory discovery.

### 3. Exception, escalation, and disclosure-readiness package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, contracts, procurement, security, and executive owners provide exception/waiver records, escalation paths, incident or discovery log, disclosure decision evidence, authorized contacts, corrective-action tracking, and periodic management review.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace each in-scope exception or discovery through source record, responsible authority, timing, decision rationale, approved communication path, remediation owner, and follow-up. This cannot determine reportability, request a waiver, or submit a disclosure.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale exceptions, incomplete ownership, and approaching deadlines. Authorized humans decide waiver, disclosure, and external communication; independent reviewers challenge the trail. Refresh after a discovery, exception change, or solicitation/award event.

### Identity, scope and change reconciliation

Use stable item/service identifiers to join inventory, purchasing and supplier
records. Preserve aliases and the evidence establishing each match. Do not merge
records solely because vendors have similar names, and do not treat a change of
reseller as proof that the underlying producer changed.

For each supplier statement, record its date, issuer, addressed entity, products,
services and limitations. Compare those boundaries to the actual inquiry. A
statement limited to newly sold equipment does not establish the status of all
installed equipment or managed services. Ask the authorized owner to resolve
unknown identities and conflicting evidence instead of choosing the newest claim
without examining what it covers.

For an approved replacement, reconcile the old item's removal, the new item's
identity and the actual service path. A closed purchasing ticket can coexist
with the old device still operating. Use authorized read-only records or a
QA-named synthetic exercise; do not disconnect infrastructure or test real
reporting portals as part of this guide refresh.

Maintain reporting evidence separately from technical remediation. An approved
replacement cannot establish that required communications were submitted, and a
submission receipt cannot prove the affected use ended. Each needs its own
criterion, observation, owner and disposition.

## Cadence and renewal

Revisit before representations and after relevant amendments, procurement,
provider changes, new inventory evidence or waiver/exception changes. Use a
documented internal recurrence for inventory/diligence checks rather than claiming
a universal annual deadline. Active discovery/reporting clocks are independent
of the next scheduled review.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the incorporated-clause record, separate supply/use populations, inquiry
sources, item/provider identity evidence, exceptions/waivers, unresolved questions,
reporting decisions/receipts and remediation verification. Reconcile every scoped
item to an owned disposition; unknowns cannot be counted as clear results.

State the exact assertion supported by each artifact. The packet is not a signed
representation, coverage determination or Government acceptance. Independent
source, skeptical, rights and publication reviews remain pending. Award-specific
deviations and cross-model execution are unverified; structural tests do not
prove them.
