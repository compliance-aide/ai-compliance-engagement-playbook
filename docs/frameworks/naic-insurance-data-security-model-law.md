# NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NAIC model-law text, state law advice, or an enforcement conclusion. Confirm adoption and local duties through the [NAIC cybersecurity overview](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/cybersecurity) and the insurer's domiciliary-state requirements.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a year-round insurer record of information-security governance, data and system context, risk assessment, supplier oversight, cybersecurity-event handling, investigations, notices, decisions, and remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Licensed-entity leadership determines applicable state obligations; security and privacy owners maintain evidence; legal counsel directs notice decisions; independent reviewers test the engagement record. AI organizes approved artifacts, tracks state-specific questions and deadlines, and prepares review packets, but cannot determine legal applicability, make a notification decision, or communicate with regulators or affected persons. Recheck state adoption and organizational scope periodically and obtain human approval for all incident communications.
