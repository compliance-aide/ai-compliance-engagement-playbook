# NIST SP 800-124 Revision 2 mobile-device security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST text, a device-management approval, a production action, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [NIST SP 800-124 Revision 2 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/124/r2/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a mobile-device security engagement record that connects device populations, ownership models, business purposes, management boundaries, approved configurations, data access, enrollment and offboarding evidence, monitoring, incidents, exceptions, and disposal or reuse actions. Separate organization-managed and personally owned device scenarios, and document the actual administrative and privacy boundaries rather than assuming a policy applies uniformly.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Endpoint, security, identity, privacy, legal, people, procurement, service, risk, and business owners retain accountable decisions. Endpoint owners maintain authorized management records; identity owners govern access; privacy and legal authorities decide acceptable handling boundaries; service owners approve user needs; and incident leaders decide response actions. Independent reviewers sample devices or cohorts from ownership and enrollment through configuration evidence, access, monitoring, offboarding, and exceptions, challenging unsupported management and coverage claims.

AI may organize authorized inventory and evidence metadata, identify stale enrollment or exception records, compare approved configuration attestations, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot enroll, lock, wipe, track, or reconfigure a device; grant access; inspect personal content; decide ownership or privacy obligations; initiate incident actions; accept risk; or claim compliance. Reassess before material device, management, application, identity, data, ownership, incident, or legal changes; review exceptions quarterly and complete an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [NIST SP 800-124 Revision 2 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/124/r2/final) at engagement start and record the version retrieved and applicable use terms. This is original engagement guidance, not reproduced NIST text, a device-management approval, a production action, or a compliance claim.

### 1. Device population, ownership, and approved-management-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Endpoint, identity, service, people, procurement, privacy, legal, security, and business owners provide device/cohort inventory, ownership model, approved business purpose, enrollment eligibility, management boundary, data/access category, accountable owner, and documented exceptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected device or cohort from ownership model and approved purpose through management eligibility, accountable owner, privacy/handling boundary, and current exception status. This can assess documented governance; it cannot determine ownership, consent, legal obligations, or authorize device management.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized inventory metadata and flag stale ownership, enrollment, or exception records. Humans decide ownership, management scope, privacy boundaries, and exceptions. Refresh after device, ownership, workforce, application, identity, data, legal, or procurement change.

### 2. Enrollment, configuration, access, and monitoring package

- **Request and owner:** Endpoint, security, identity, application, service, and incident owners provide authorized enrollment/offboarding records, approved configuration attestations, access-role evidence, encryption or update status evidence where applicable, monitoring/alerting records, and incident handoffs.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a selected device or cohort through authorized enrollment, approved configuration evidence, access assignment/review, monitoring signal, and offboarding or incident handoff where applicable. This cannot enroll, configure, lock, wipe, track, or access personal content, nor prove all device settings are effective.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied attestations and flag mismatched owner, enrollment, or exception evidence. It cannot alter a device, grant access, or launch a response. Authorized humans administer devices, approve access, and direct incident action. Recollect after configuration drift, access change, compromise, or management-platform change.

### 3. Exception, privacy, disposal, and independent-challenge package

- **Request and owner:** Endpoint, privacy, legal, records, asset-management, risk, service, and independent-review owners provide exception approvals, compensating-action records, privacy review artifacts, offboarding/disposal or reuse records, management review, corrective actions, and retest evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception, offboarding, or disposal/reuse record to authority, boundary/limitation, accountable action, completion/retest evidence, and current decision status. This cannot accept risk, decide lawful handling, approve disposal, or certify mobile security.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag expiring exceptions and prepare redacted review packets. Humans approve risk, privacy/legal determinations, disposition, closure, and external statements. Review quarterly and after material device, incident, privacy/legal, ownership, or management changes.
