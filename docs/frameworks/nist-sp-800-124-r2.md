# NIST SP 800-124 Revision 2 mobile-device security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST text, a device-management approval, a production action, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [NIST SP 800-124 Revision 2 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/124/r2/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a mobile-device security engagement record that connects device populations, ownership models, business purposes, management boundaries, approved configurations, data access, enrollment and offboarding evidence, monitoring, incidents, exceptions, and disposal or reuse actions. Separate organization-managed and personally owned device scenarios, and document the actual administrative and privacy boundaries rather than assuming a policy applies uniformly.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Endpoint, security, identity, privacy, legal, people, procurement, service, risk, and business owners retain accountable decisions. Endpoint owners maintain authorized management records; identity owners govern access; privacy and legal authorities decide acceptable handling boundaries; service owners approve user needs; and incident leaders decide response actions. Independent reviewers sample devices or cohorts from ownership and enrollment through configuration evidence, access, monitoring, offboarding, and exceptions, challenging unsupported management and coverage claims.

AI may organize authorized inventory and evidence metadata, identify stale enrollment or exception records, compare approved configuration attestations, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot enroll, lock, wipe, track, or reconfigure a device; grant access; inspect personal content; decide ownership or privacy obligations; initiate incident actions; accept risk; or claim compliance. Reassess before material device, management, application, identity, data, ownership, incident, or legal changes; review exceptions quarterly and complete an annual independent readiness review.
