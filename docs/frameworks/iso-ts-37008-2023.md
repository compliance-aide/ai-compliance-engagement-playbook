# ISO/TS 37008:2023 internal investigations — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content, legal advice, or an investigative conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/74094.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain investigation authority, scope decisions, investigator independence, evidence custody, interviews and analysis records, findings review, remediation decisions, reporting approvals, and closure evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized legal, ethics, and investigation leaders control case decisions; independent reviewers test integrity. AI organizes approved, non-sensitive metadata and flags chronology gaps, but cannot interview people, access protected evidence, make findings, or disclose results. Review each case and programme annually.
