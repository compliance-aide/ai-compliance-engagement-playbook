# NIST SP 800-160 Volume 1 Revision 1 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a design approval. Consult the [NIST
> systems-security-engineering source](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/160/v1/r1/final).

## Engagement focus

Set trustworthiness objectives and stakeholder priorities; integrate security
and resilience into architecture, acquisition, change, and retirement; maintain
assumptions, design rationale, dependencies, and assurance claims; conduct
lifecycle and major-change reviews; and close the year with an investment roadmap.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain architecture, lifecycle, supplier, change, and assurance
records. Independent reviewers challenge assumptions and cross-lifecycle gaps.
AI synthesizes approved lifecycle records and identifies conflicts; it cannot
approve designs or represent engineering judgment as fact.
