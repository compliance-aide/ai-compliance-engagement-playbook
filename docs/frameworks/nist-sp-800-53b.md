# NIST SP 800-53B baseline-selection engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST control or baseline content and not an authorization conclusion. Confirm the current publication through [NIST SP 800-53B](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/b/upd1/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a decision record for system-impact assumptions, baseline-selection rationale, tailoring or overlay decisions, privacy considerations, implementation evidence, assessment results, conditions, and change history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

System and risk owners establish system context; security and privacy teams maintain decision and evidence records; independent assessors test traceability; authorizing officials make authorization decisions. AI organizes approved evidence, highlights inconsistent or aging decisions, and drafts reviewer questions, but cannot select a baseline, tailor safeguards, assess effectiveness, or authorize operation. Revisit the record after material changes and retain dated human approvals.

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
