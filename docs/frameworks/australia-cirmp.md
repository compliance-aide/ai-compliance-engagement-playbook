# Australia CIRMP — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a statutory scope or reporting conclusion. Check the [critical-infrastructure rules](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2023L00112/latest).

## Engagement focus

Establish in-scope assets and accountable owner; maintain dependencies, all-hazards risk-program, treatment, exercise, incident, and annual-report evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own inventories and treatment; independent reviewers test material scenarios to records. AI flags stale ownership and drafts workpapers, but cannot decide scope, approve the program, or submit reports. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [Security of Critical Infrastructure (Critical Infrastructure Risk Management Program) Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2023L00112/latest) and qualified Australian legal review; checked 2026-07-31. This plan is original operational guidance, does not reproduce legislation, and cannot determine asset scope, regulated status, reportability, or statutory compliance. Authorized legal and responsible-entity decision-makers retain those determinations.

### 1. Asset scope and accountable-risk-program package

- **Request and owner:** Asset/service inventory, ownership and responsible-entity inputs, scope rationale, critical dependencies, all-hazards risk-program charter, and accountable-role records from asset, legal, risk, and executive owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected asset/service from inventory through scope rationale and accountable owner to the program record, preserving uncertainties. This can support a transparent scope workpaper; it cannot decide regulated critical-infrastructure status or legal applicability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved metadata and flag missing owners. Humans confirm scope and approve the program. Refresh after ownership, asset, service, dependency, or legal/regulatory change.

### 2. Hazard, treatment, and exercise package

- **Request and owner:** Risk scenarios and analysis inputs, treatment records, protective-security/continuity dependencies, exercise designs/results, incident records, and remediation/retest evidence from risk, operations, and security owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a sampled hazard through risk input, approved treatment, scenario exercise or incident observation, and follow-up record. This supports evidence linkage for the sampled path; it cannot establish that risks are complete, treatments sufficient, or an exercise predictive of live events.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index evidence and flag overdue reviews; it may not rate risk, choose treatment, or run incident response. Recollect after material hazards, exercises, incidents, or control changes.

### 3. Annual assurance and reporting-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Annual program-review inputs, responsible-entity approvals, exceptions, source-change log, independent-review observations, and any report-preparation evidence from legal, risk, and executive owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception or program-review statement to its source, authority, limitation, and remediation date. This can support accountable review; it cannot authorize, sign, or submit a report.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft a source-linked, non-submission workpaper. Authorized humans approve statements, risk decisions, and any submission. Revisit annually and before a material representation.
