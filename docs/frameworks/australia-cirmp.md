# Australia CIRMP — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a statutory scope or reporting conclusion. Check the [critical-infrastructure rules](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2023L00112/latest).

## Engagement focus

Establish in-scope assets and accountable owner; maintain dependencies, all-hazards risk-program, treatment, exercise, incident, and annual-report evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own inventories and treatment; independent reviewers test material scenarios to records. AI flags stale ownership and drafts workpapers, but cannot decide scope, approve the program, or submit reports. Review quarterly and annually.
