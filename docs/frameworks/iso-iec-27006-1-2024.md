# ISO/IEC 27006-1:2024 assurance-provider readiness — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a certification decision. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/82908.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

For assurance providers, maintain competence, impartiality safeguards, engagement planning, reviewer assignments, working papers, technical review, decision separation, complaints, and corrective-action evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Qualified people retain assessment and certification authority; independent quality personnel test the assurance process. AI organizes workpapers and highlights inconsistencies, but cannot assess, certify, or make impartiality judgments. Sample engagements quarterly and review the system annually.

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
