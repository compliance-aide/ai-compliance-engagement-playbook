# Singapore Cyber Trust mark engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Cyber Trust certification criteria, a certification outcome, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [Cyber Security Agency of Singapore Cyber Trust mark program](https://www.csa.gov.sg/our-programmes/support-for-enterprises/sg-cyber-safe-programme/certification-for-the-cyber-trust-mark/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a risk-based cybersecurity assurance record that connects organizational context, accountable owners, technical and operational evidence, cloud, operational technology, and AI scope where applicable, supplier dependencies, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Keep certification preparation distinct from a claim of certification or an automated conclusion about organizational readiness.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, security, risk, privacy, technology, and business authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and readiness. AI may organize authorized records, flag stale evidence, prepare review packets, and draft questions, but cannot determine the applicable tier, assign an assessment result, select or approve a certification body, accept risk, make a certification claim, alter production systems, or submit an application. Review annually and before material organization, cloud, OT, AI, supplier, system, incident, or certification-scope changes.
