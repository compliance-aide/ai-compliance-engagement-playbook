# Singapore Cyber Trust mark engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Cyber Trust certification criteria, a certification outcome, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [Cyber Security Agency of Singapore Cyber Trust mark program](https://www.csa.gov.sg/our-programmes/support-for-enterprises/sg-cyber-safe-programme/certification-for-the-cyber-trust-mark/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a risk-based cybersecurity assurance record that connects organizational context, accountable owners, technical and operational evidence, cloud, operational technology, and AI scope where applicable, supplier dependencies, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Keep certification preparation distinct from a claim of certification or an automated conclusion about organizational readiness.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive, security, risk, privacy, technology, and business authorities retain accountable decisions; system and supplier owners preserve evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and readiness. AI may organize authorized records, flag stale evidence, prepare review packets, and draft questions, but cannot determine the applicable tier, assign an assessment result, select or approve a certification body, accept risk, make a certification claim, alter production systems, or submit an application. Review annually and before material organization, cloud, OT, AI, supplier, system, incident, or certification-scope changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [CSA Cyber Trust mark program](https://www.csa.gov.sg/our-programmes/support-for-enterprises/sg-cyber-safe-programme/certification-for-the-cyber-trust-mark/) as the official program source; checked 2026-07-31. The current criteria edition, tier applicability, assessment method, and redistribution rights must be confirmed with CSA materials and a qualified Singapore adviser. This original plan neither reproduces criteria nor decides certification eligibility or outcome.

### 1. Scope, risk, and accountable-security package

- **Request and owner:** Executive, security, risk, technology, and business owners provide approved organization and service scope; asset, cloud, OT, and AI-use inventories; risk and treatment records; accountable roles; policy references; and certification-scope questions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected scoped service or material risk to its owner, asset/service record, risk decision, treatment action, and review date. This supports a bounded evidence trail; it cannot determine a tier, validate control effectiveness, or establish certification readiness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories and flag unowned assets or stale treatments. Accountable humans decide scope and risk. Refresh after material service, cloud, OT, AI, ownership, or risk changes.

### 2. Technical operation, incident, and remediation package

- **Request and owner:** Security and operations owners provide approved configuration/change references, monitoring and access-operation evidence, vulnerability/remediation records, incident or exercise timelines, supplier dependencies, and retest results.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected remediation or exercise from triggering evidence through assigned owner, approved change, validation record, and follow-up. This supports observable operation; it cannot prove broad effectiveness, approve a production change, or decide external reporting.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized evidence and flag overdue actions; it cannot scan unapproved systems, alter configurations, or communicate externally. Recollect after an incident, failed exercise, material weakness, supplier change, or missed remediation date.

### 3. Assurance, supplier, and certification-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Security, procurement, legal, assurance, and executive owners provide supplier assurance/change evidence, exception register, independent-review observations, management decisions, source-change watch, and approved certification-application decision records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception, supplier change, or readiness finding to facts, human authority, expiry, remediation, and retest. This supports accountable governance; it cannot accept risk, select a certification body, submit an application, or claim certification.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare non-authoritative workpapers and flag stale decisions. Named humans approve exceptions, contracts, representations, and closure. Review annually and after material scope, source, supplier, or incident change.
