# NYDFS Part 500 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a regulatory certification. Confirm current obligations at the [NYDFS cybersecurity resource center](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/cybersecurity).

## Engagement focus

Have an authorized owner determine coverage or exemption status. Maintain an annual calendar for governance, risk assessment, evidence collection, third-party oversight, exercises, incidents, approvals, and required notifications or certifications. Preserve versioned evidence and escalation records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Program owners keep the operating record; counsel and authorized executives make coverage, materiality, certification, and submission decisions. Independent reviewers test completeness, timing, and conflicts. AI can run the calendar and prepare review packs; it cannot make legal judgments, sign certifications, manage an incident, or submit to NYDFS.

### 1. Applicability, governance, and risk-program record

- **Request and owner:** A coverage/exemption decision record, named qualified security leadership and board/management oversight artifacts, asset/data boundary, and current risk-assessment method/results from counsel, security leadership, risk, and governance owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a sample program decision or risk treatment to its approver, input evidence, date, and follow-up. This supports that governance evidence exists; it cannot decide legal coverage, establish qualified-person status, or judge risk adequacy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved inventories and flag stale approvals; humans determine scope, risk, and governance action. Refresh for acquisitions, material systems/data changes, leadership change, significant risk change, or annual review.

### 2. Control-operation, resilience, and third-party record

- **Request and owner:** Time-bounded identity/access, vulnerability/remediation, logging/monitoring, secure-development or change, business-continuity/incident-exercise, and third-party risk records from operational and vendor owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample declared populations and trace each item to a source record, owner, result, exception, and remediation. This can show observable process operation; it cannot prove every safeguard is effective, a vendor is secure, or an incident is reportable.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile indexes and flag missing time periods; humans approve risk acceptance, remediation closure, and incident actions. Recollect after an incident, failed exercise, material supplier change, or material control failure.

### 3. Filing-support and evidence-preservation record

- **Request and owner:** Annual certification/acknowledgement preparation records, executive review evidence, exception/remediation register, and retained support for any regulator communication, owned by compliance, counsel, security leadership, and authorized signatories.
- **Validate and limit:** Check that the proposed filing pack identifies scope, signer, source period, exceptions, and approval chain; reconcile a limited selection to retained source evidence. This supports readiness of a human review pack, not the truth of a certification or authority to submit it.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble a redacted index and identify missing approvals. Only authorized humans decide statement content and submit. Refresh on rule/source change, new material exception, or before any filing deadline.
