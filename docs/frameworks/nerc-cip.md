# NERC CIP — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a NERC or FERC determination. Confirm the applicable effective standards in the [NERC catalog](https://www.nerc.com/standards/reliability-standards).

## Engagement focus

Establish the registered entity, jurisdiction, and bulk-electric-system scope for the engagement date. Maintain accountable ownership, asset and change evidence, exercises, incident and recovery records, third-party dependencies, and dated exception decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain truthful operational evidence. Independent reviewers sample provenance, timelines, and exceptions against then-effective requirements. AI may organize authorized evidence and flag conflicts; it cannot decide legal applicability, change operational technology, certify compliance, or submit material to regulators. Review operational signals quarterly and scope annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the effective-standard records in the [NERC Reliability Standards catalog](https://www.nerc.com/standards/reliability-standards), applicable regulator notices, and the entity's registered-entity and regional authority records; checked 2026-07-31. This original guide does not reproduce standards or determine registration, applicability, or compliance.

### 1. Registered entity, asset, and responsibility scope

- **Request and owner:** Request the registered-entity/jurisdiction record, approved engagement scope, asset and system inventory, criticality or classification decisions made by authorized personnel, ownership ledger, and third-party responsibility map from compliance, operations, asset, and legal owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected in-scope asset or service from the approved boundary to its owner, inventory record, operating location, and provider responsibility. This supports a time-bounded scope trail; it cannot decide BES status, requirement applicability, or registration.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories and identify missing owners; accountable personnel retain technical and regulatory decisions. Refresh after an asset, topology, entity, provider, jurisdiction, or classification change.

### 2. Operational security, change, and recovery evidence

- **Request and owner:** Request read-only operational records for access, configuration/change control, monitoring, vulnerability or remediation tracking, recovery exercises, incident response, and approved exceptions from operations, security, engineering, and recovery owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Select a change, alert, or recovery exercise and trace it to the declared scope, dated source record, accountable reviewer, corrective action, and retest where applicable. This supports evidence of a recorded operating process; it cannot prove continuous effectiveness or authorize an operational change.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved evidence and highlight timeline conflicts; it may not connect to or change operational technology. Human owners approve remediation and exception disposition. Recollect after a material change, incident, exercise result, or control failure.

### 3. Compliance decision, event, and annual review record

- **Request and owner:** Request compliance calendar, effective-version watch, self-assessment workpapers, event/notification decision records, audit or inquiry correspondence where applicable, corrective-action register, and management-review minutes from compliance, legal, and leadership owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected assertion, event, or exception to contemporaneous facts, authorized human decision, required follow-up, and closure record. This supports governance accountability; it cannot make a regulator submission, determine reportability, or certify compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a non-destructive evidence index and escalate aging actions. Authorized humans make reporting, risk, and closure decisions. Revisit annually and after a standards update, regulator inquiry, event, or material exception.
