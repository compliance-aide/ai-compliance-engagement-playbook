# IEC 62443 — industrial cybersecurity engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC content or conformity advice. Confirm role and edition at the [IEC catalog](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/62883).

## Engagement focus

Maintain industrial asset/system/supplier boundary, approved architecture inventory, risk register, remote-access, recovery, vulnerability, maintenance, and service evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Asset owners make safety-aware decisions; independent reviewers test lineage and open risks. AI inventories evidence and flags drift, but cannot change OT, accept risk, or certify. Review quarterly and annually.
