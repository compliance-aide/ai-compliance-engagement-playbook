# EU AI Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal classification or conformity advice. Check current dates at the [European Commission AI regulatory framework](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai).

## Engagement focus

Maintain EU-facing AI inventory, declared role, use, human intervention, dependencies, risk and release evidence, monitoring, incidents, transparency, and retrieval readiness.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain inventory and change signals; independent reviewers sample classification, releases, monitoring, and traceability. AI organizes evidence and gaps, but cannot decide classification, sign conformity conclusions, submit filings, or override escalation. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
