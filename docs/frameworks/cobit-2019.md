# COBIT 2019 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISACA content or a governance assessment.
> Consult authorized [COBIT materials](https://www.isaca.org/store2/product/CB19FGM)
> and applicable licensing terms.

## Engagement focus

Establish decision rights, stakeholder outcomes, risk appetite, material I&T
services, and governance calendar. Document decision and evidence pathways;
compile performance signals, overdue actions, and management statements;
periodically review whether I&T produces intended value within risk and resource
constraints; and refresh the governance design each year.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management owns transparent decision records and approved actions. Independent
reviewers test decision traceability and escalation effectiveness. AI identifies
missing owners, aged commitments, and conflicting metrics and drafts agenda
questions; it cannot determine strategy or an assurance conclusion.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use authorized current COBIT 2019 materials from the [ISACA COBIT store record](https://www.isaca.org/store2/product/CB19FGM); reviewed 2026-07-31. COBIT content is licensed. This original plan does not reproduce objectives, models, mappings, or assessment methods; a named governance owner confirms authorized access and permitted use before any engagement.

### 1. Governance boundary and decision-rights record

- **Request and owner:** Approved enterprise objectives, I&T service boundary, stakeholder and decision-rights map, committee charter/calendar, risk appetite inputs, and named business, technology, risk, and governance owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected material I&T decision from stated outcome and accountable authority to the dated approval and escalation path. This supports transparent governance traceability; it cannot establish that governance is effective, complete, or aligned to every COBIT concept.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved metadata and flag missing owners, decisions, or review dates. Human governance authorities approve scope, priorities, and risk acceptance. Refresh after a material strategy, service, ownership, authority, or risk-appetite change.

### 2. I&T value, risk, and resource operating record

- **Request and owner:** Approved outcome measures, service performance records, material risk/action register, investment or capacity decisions, dependency/supplier records, and management-review inputs from service, finance, risk, and technology owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected measure or overdue action back to its source record, accountable owner, decision, and follow-up. This can support a bounded account of management oversight; it cannot prove value realization, risk reduction, or resource sufficiency.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved measures and flag stale actions or conflicting metadata; it cannot set targets, allocate resources, alter services, or close risks. Humans approve decisions and remediation. Refresh after a missed material measure, major investment, supplier change, incident, or service change.

### 3. Governance review and challenge workpaper

- **Request and owner:** Meeting packs/minutes, exception and escalation records, corrective-action status, independent challenge notes, and annual design-refresh decisions from governance leadership and independent reviewers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected escalation or exception to its decision authority, rationale, expiry or follow-up, and independent challenge. This supports accountable review; it cannot issue an assurance conclusion or substitute for a licensed assessment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare source-linked, non-authoritative workpapers and flag unlinked decisions. Humans approve external statements, residual-risk decisions, and closure. Review quarterly and complete a human-approved annual governance refresh.
