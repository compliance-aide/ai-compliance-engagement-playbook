# COBIT 2019 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISACA content or a governance assessment.
> Consult authorized [COBIT materials](https://www.isaca.org/store2/product/CB19FGM)
> and applicable licensing terms.

## Engagement focus

Establish decision rights, stakeholder outcomes, risk appetite, material I&T
services, and governance calendar. Document decision and evidence pathways;
compile performance signals, overdue actions, and management statements;
periodically review whether I&T produces intended value within risk and resource
constraints; and refresh the governance design each year.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management owns transparent decision records and approved actions. Independent
reviewers test decision traceability and escalation effectiveness. AI identifies
missing owners, aged commitments, and conflicting metrics and drafts agenda
questions; it cannot determine strategy or an assurance conclusion.
