# ISO/IEC 29100:2024 privacy-framework engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO/IEC standard text, legal advice, or a certification claim. Confirm the current edition through the [ISO/IEC 29100:2024 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/85938.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a consistent privacy operating record across technology and service lifecycles: processing purposes, people and organizational roles, personal-data categories, data flows, service and supplier dependencies, design decisions, access and retention decisions, incident handling, rights requests, and material changes. Connect each record to the applicable law, contract, internal policy, and accountable human decision without treating the framework as a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal analysis.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy leadership defines governance and escalates legal questions; business and product owners maintain purpose and lifecycle evidence; engineering and operations document system behavior; legal counsel determines jurisdictional obligations; independent reviewers test traceability. AI may organize approved records, identify missing owners and inconsistent data-flow evidence, and draft review questions, but cannot determine lawful processing, respond to an individual, approve a transfer, accept privacy risk, or make certification or compliance claims. Review the record at least annually and whenever a purpose, data flow, supplier, jurisdiction, or material system behavior changes.
