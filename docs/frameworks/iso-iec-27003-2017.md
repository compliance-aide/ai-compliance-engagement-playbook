# ISO/IEC 27003:2017 ISMS implementation — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a certification conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/63417.html) for current revision status; do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain implementation scope, leadership decisions, process owners, risk and treatment records, operating evidence, assurance results, change decisions, corrective actions, and improvement history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management sets direction and accountable owners operate the program; independent reviewers evaluate evidence separately. AI connects approved records and drafts questions, but cannot interpret requirements or certify. Review operating cadence quarterly and system direction annually.

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
