# NIST SP 800-207 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an architecture approval. Consult the
> [NIST zero trust publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final)
> and approved architecture decisions before acting.

## Engagement focus

Catalogue high-value resources and real access journeys, including privileged,
third-party, and emergency access. Document architectural intent and phased
priorities; assess whether relevant identity, device, resource, session, and
telemetry signals are reliable; validate important journeys safely; and track
coverage, exceptions, enforcement failures, and roadmap progress.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Identity, endpoint, network, application, and business owners own actual access
paths and approved changes. Independent reviewers distinguish design intent from operating
evidence. AI may reconcile authorized inventories, flag orphaned exceptions, and
draft evidence requests; it cannot grant access, change enforcement, bypass
human approval, accept risk, or approve an architecture decision. Refresh priorities after access failures or major change.
