# California data-breach notification — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, a breach determination or a notice.

## Source and applicability

Use [Civil Code 1798.82](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1798.82.)
and [Attorney General reporting guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/databreach/reporting).
Checked 2026-09-04: the statute records SB 446 amendments effective January 1, 2026.
For covered owners/licensees, resident disclosure is due within 30 calendar days
of discovery or notification, subject to the statutory delay provisions. A separate
AG sample-notice submission applies above 500 California residents and is due
within 15 calendar days of notifying affected consumers. Maintainers have a separate
immediate owner/licensee notification route. Counsel must resolve entity role,
trigger, covered information and applicable delays; encryption alone does not
settle applicability when keys or credentials may also be compromised.

Agency obligations use a separate statutory route; do not automatically apply this
business section to every public body. See the
[author record](../refresh-reviews/california-data-breach-notification.md).

## Engagement focus

Produce a timely, source-backed decision and notification packet from preserved
incident facts. Separate incident occurrence, discovery, notification, legal review,
resident delivery and AG submission timestamps. A prepared notice is not delivery.

## Roles

Incident command coordinates investigation; system/data owners supply facts;
privacy/records maintain population and evidence. California counsel decides legal
scope, timing, notice content and delays. Authorised communications/submitter owners
approve and deliver exact artifacts. Independent reviewers challenge the trail.
AI may reconcile permitted metadata and calculate approved clocks; it cannot decide
reportability, invoke delays, send notices, accept risk or make a compliance claim.

## Before starting

For readiness, obtain entity/data roles, incident contacts, current sources,
approved clocks, preservation procedures and fictional cases. For an actual event,
escalate promptly to incident command/legal while preserving authorised evidence;
do not wait for this checklist to finish. Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md).

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Establish facts and role | Incident/legal identify owner, licensee or maintainer, systems and initial timestamps. | Provenance-backed fact log with uncertainty; immediate routes considered promptly. |
| 2. Reconcile population | Data/privacy owners document affected information, California residency methodology and deduplication. | Population record with assumptions and unresolved individuals; no arbitrary cap. |
| 3. Decide duties and clocks | Counsel applies current law and records applicable triggers, deadlines and any supported delay. | Separate resident, owner/licensee and regulator decisions; no automatic delay for an open investigation. |
| 4. Prepare notices | Communications drafts against current content/method requirements; AI checks approved facts and missing fields. | Exact draft, intended audience and unresolved legal questions. |
| 5. Review distribution | Legal/communications verify recipient source, safe delivery route, approvals and AG sample privacy. | Approved artifact/version and authorised delivery plan. |
| 6. Deliver when authorised | Designated operator sends/submits and independently records receipt or failure. | Delivery and AG submission states remain distinct; failures escalate under the active clock. |
| 7. Reconcile and improve | Owners address returned notices, new facts and corrective actions; reviewer retests readiness gaps. | Follow-up queue, decisions and preserved evidence/history. |

## Evidence and test plan

| Evidence and custodian | Check | Expected observation | Failure or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Timeline/role register, incident/legal | Exercise a maintainer case separately from owner/licensee disclosure. | Correct approved route and trigger. | A single generic timer cannot cover every role. |
| Population method, privacy | Reconcile source counts, residency, duplicate handling and uncertain records. | Count and scope are reproducible. | Missing records cannot be dropped to avoid a threshold. |
| Fictional notice packet, communications | Check facts, current statutory content/method requirements and approved audience. | Draft is understandable and traceable. | A prior notice template may be outdated. |
| QA delivery ledger, submitter | Simulate resident notice and AG sample submission without external sends. | Independent statuses and receipts can be distinguished. | A tabletop cannot establish actual delivery. |

## Failure branches and decisions

Conflicting discovery dates go immediately to incident/legal with both sources.
Potential key compromise remains a factual question, not an automatic exemption.
Unsupported delay requests remain unapproved. Missing recipient details require a
legal method decision; AI cannot invent substitute notice. Remove personal details
from any public-facing sample under approved review. After interruption check prior
delivery before retrying and preserve original clocks. Other jurisdictions and
sector duties remain separately scoped.

## Cadence and renewal

Track active-event deadlines continuously through the approved incident process.
Quarterly contact/source checks and annual fictional exercises are planning defaults.
Reassess after system, data, provider or legal changes, and retest failed readiness
steps before claiming correction. Preserve evidence according to approved holds.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver facts/population methodology, legal decisions and clocks, approved notices,
actual delivery/submission receipts where authorised, failures and next actions.
Independent source/legal, engagement and skeptical reviews and named human
publication approval remain pending. Original prose and links only; no statutory
forms, private incident data or personal evidence reproduced.
