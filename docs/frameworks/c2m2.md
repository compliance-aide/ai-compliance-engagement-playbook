# Cybersecurity Capability Maturity Model (C2M2) — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a DOE maturity rating. Check the
> [DOE C2M2 source](https://www.energy.gov/ceser/cybersecurity-capability-maturity-model-c2m2)
> before each cycle.

## Engagement focus

Choose a self-evaluation boundary and accountable subject-matter owners. Use
business and operational risk to define desired capability outcomes; collect
operating narratives rather than document-only checklists; challenge whether
work is repeatable; then fund and sequence verified improvements.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management owns targets and accepted risk. Independent reviewers retain
judgment on capability claims. AI may organize interview and evidence themes,
track provenance and confidence, and identify dependencies; it cannot assign
a maturity result, accept risk, approve targets, or alter systems. Run periodic health reviews and a year-end current-versus-
target review.
