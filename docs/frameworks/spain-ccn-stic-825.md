# Spain CCN-STIC 825 ENS and ISO 27001 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CCN-STIC guide text, mapping content, a conformity evaluation, or a certification claim. Confirm the current document through the [CCN-CERT CCN-STIC 825 publication](https://www.ccn-cert.cni.es/en/series-ccn-stic/guias/series-ccn-stic/800-guia-esquema-nacional-de-seguridad/7122-ccn-stic-825-national-security-framework-27001-certifications/file.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a separate evidence record for the organization’s National Security Framework context, its information-security management activities, scope decisions, approved evidence reuse, assessment observations, remediation, and certification-related communications. Treat comparison and reuse as an evidence-management task; do not assert equivalence between frameworks, certifications, or a particular organization’s obligations.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Spanish legal and security leadership determine applicability and scope; ISMS and ENS owners maintain their respective evidence; assurance providers and certification bodies retain independent evaluation authority; independent reviewers challenge traceability and reuse rationale. AI may inventory approved evidence, identify missing provenance, and draft questions, but cannot create a mapping, declare equivalence, determine ENS applicability, certify, approve a statement of conformity, or submit externally. Review whenever scope, system, evidence, certification status, or applicable regulatory context materially changes and document annual renewal readiness.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Retain the official [CCN-CERT CCN-STIC 825 publication page](https://www.ccn-cert.cni.es/en/series-ccn-stic/guias/series-ccn-stic/800-guia-esquema-nacional-de-seguridad/7122-ccn-stic-825-national-security-framework-27001-certifications/file.html), retrieved 2026-07-31, and confirm document status, edition, availability, and permitted use with CCN-CERT and authorized users before relying on it. This is original evidence planning, not CCN-STIC text, a reusable mapping, a certification method, or an assertion that ENS and ISO 27001 obligations or results are equivalent.

### 1. Separate ENS and ISMS scope-and-provenance package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain the human-approved ENS service/system boundary, ISMS scope statement, organization/unit context, accountable owners, current source/version register, evidence repository location, and approved evidence-use rationale from ENS, ISMS, legal, and records owners. Identify whether each artifact is organization-owned, supplier-provided, assessor-held, or otherwise restricted.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected scope assertion back to its accountable owner, source/version record, system or organizational boundary, and access/use condition. Maintain ENS and ISMS records as distinct evidence sets even when the same artifact is referenced. This supports provenance and controlled evidence handling; it cannot determine applicability, authenticate a certificate, or establish framework equivalence.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved metadata for missing owner, version, source, or rights fields. Humans decide scope and permitted reuse; legal and licensing owners resolve restrictions. Trigger on scope expansion, source revision, certificate/status change, supplier change, or annual renewal.

### 2. Controlled evidence-reference and difference record

- **Request and owner:** Obtain approved evidence references, artifact date/version, stated purpose, owner attestation where appropriate, access restrictions, known gaps, and human-authored rationale for considering each item in the engagement from ENS and ISMS evidence owners. Do not request or publish proprietary mappings, control text, or certification-body workpapers without permission.
- **Validate and limit:** Select a referenced artifact and trace it to its original repository, owner, time period, stated purpose, and documented limitation; interview the owner when provenance or reuse is unclear. This supports a bounded evidence-reference trail; it cannot show that one framework requirement is met by another artifact or that a management system operates effectively.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may build an index of approved references and flag evidence used outside its recorded boundary. Humans decide whether a reference is usable and document differences or unresolved questions. Trigger on new evidence reuse, changed artifact version, rights restriction, contrary review finding, or annual evidence refresh.

### 3. Independent assessment, communication, and renewal package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain assessor/certification communication references where authorized, independent-review observations, management responses, remediation records, public-claim approvals, and renewal planning records from assurance, legal, communications, ENS, and ISMS leadership. Keep certification-body decisions and confidential assessment material access controlled.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected external or internal statement to the approved source, exact scope, decision authority, date, and any open limitation or remediation. Challenge unsupported claims of alignment, equivalence, certification, or reuse. This supports responsible communications; it cannot issue a certificate, endorse a conformity claim, or close an independent finding.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify stale approval links and assemble a non-authoritative review packet. Independent reviewers retain skeptical challenge; named humans approve communications, remediation closure, and renewal decisions. Trigger on a planned external statement, assessment finding, certification event, material scope change, source update, or annual renewal.
