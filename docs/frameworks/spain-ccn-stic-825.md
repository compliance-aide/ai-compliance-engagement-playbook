# Spain CCN-STIC 825 ENS and ISO 27001 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CCN-STIC guide text, mapping content, a conformity evaluation, or a certification claim. Confirm the current document through the [CCN-CERT CCN-STIC 825 publication](https://www.ccn-cert.cni.es/en/series-ccn-stic/guias/series-ccn-stic/800-guia-esquema-nacional-de-seguridad/7122-ccn-stic-825-national-security-framework-27001-certifications/file.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a separate evidence record for the organization’s National Security Framework context, its information-security management activities, scope decisions, approved evidence reuse, assessment observations, remediation, and certification-related communications. Treat comparison and reuse as an evidence-management task; do not assert equivalence between frameworks, certifications, or a particular organization’s obligations.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Spanish legal and security leadership determine applicability and scope; ISMS and ENS owners maintain their respective evidence; assurance providers and certification bodies retain independent evaluation authority; independent reviewers challenge traceability and reuse rationale. AI may inventory approved evidence, identify missing provenance, and draft questions, but cannot create a mapping, declare equivalence, determine ENS applicability, certify, approve a statement of conformity, or submit externally. Review whenever scope, system, evidence, certification status, or applicable regulatory context materially changes and document annual renewal readiness.
