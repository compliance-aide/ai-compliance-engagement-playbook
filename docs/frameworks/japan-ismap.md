# Japan ISMAP cloud-service readiness — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Japanese Government content or a registration decision. Confirm current rules and service-list status through the [official ISMAP portal](https://www.ismap.go.jp/csm?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010005).

## Engagement focus

Maintain cloud-service scope, service architecture, security evidence, assessment and assurance records, change history, customer and government-facing claims, exceptions, remediation, and registration-application approvals.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Accountable cloud, security, legal, and authorized submission owners make readiness and registration decisions; independent assessors conclude separately. AI organizes approved evidence and flags gaps, but cannot assess, register a service, or make claims. Review releases continuously and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [official ISMAP portal](https://www.ismap.go.jp/csm?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010005) for current registration and program materials; checked 2026-07-31. The current rules, applicable service scope, assessment evidence, submission procedure, and republication rights require confirmation through official materials and qualified Japanese advisers. This original plan does not reproduce government content or decide registration.

### 1. Cloud-service scope, architecture, and responsibility package

- **Request and owner:** Cloud, product, security, privacy, and service owners provide service/version and tenant scope, architecture/data-flow records, shared-responsibility model, asset and dependency inventory, geographic/service-provider context, accountable roles, and material-change history.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected customer-facing service component to its version, architecture record, owner, dependency, and stated responsibility. This supports a factual readiness packet; it cannot determine ISMAP scope, control applicability, or registration eligibility.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved architecture and inventory metadata and flag changed dependencies. Humans decide service scope and representations. Refresh for releases, material architecture, region, provider, customer, or ownership changes.

### 2. Security operation, assurance, and change-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Security, engineering, and operations owners provide approved policy/control references, access and monitoring operations, secure-development/change records, vulnerability and incident evidence, test/assurance outputs, remediation, and retest records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected change, finding, or incident from source evidence through accountable review, remediation, retest, and closure. This supports operational traceability; it cannot validate all controls, substitute for an assessor, or authorize production changes.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized evidence and flag stale tests or missing links; it cannot alter services, assess conformity, or generate a passing conclusion. Recollect after a material incident, failed test, significant release, or remediation miss.

### 3. Submission, supplier, and claim-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, procurement, cloud, security, assurance, and authorized-submission owners provide supplier assurance/change records, exceptions, management decisions, claim review, source-change watch, and approved submission authority records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier change, exception, or readiness claim to facts, human authority, expiry, review, and corrective action. This supports accountable governance; it cannot submit an application, select an assessor, or state registration.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare non-authoritative workpapers and flag stale approvals. Named humans approve contracts, exceptions, submissions, and public claims. Refresh before submission and after source, supplier, scope, or material service changes.
