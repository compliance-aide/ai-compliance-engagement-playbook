# Qatar personal-data privacy — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Qatari legal advice. Consult the [official legal portal](https://www.almeezan.qa/LawPage.aspx?id=7121&language=ar) and current authority guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain controller/processor, privacy-by-design, processing, risk, vendor, transfer, rights, incident, change, and exercise evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide legal conditions and reports; independent reviewers test workflows. AI cannot make rights outcomes or file reports. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
