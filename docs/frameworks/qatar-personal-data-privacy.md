# Qatar personal-data privacy — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Qatari legal advice. Consult the [official legal portal](https://www.almeezan.qa/LawPage.aspx?id=7121&language=ar) and current authority guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain controller/processor, privacy-by-design, processing, risk, vendor, transfer, rights, incident, change, and exercise evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide legal conditions and reports; independent reviewers test workflows. AI cannot make rights outcomes or file reports. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm current material through the [official legal portal](https://www.almeezan.qa/LawPage.aspx?id=7121&language=ar) and qualified Qatari privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide controller/processor status, processing conditions, consent, transfer treatment, a rights outcome, or a regulator report.

### 1. Controller, processing, and design record

- **Request and owner:** Privacy and service owners maintain a processing register with controllers/processors, data categories, purpose, collection channel, systems, owners, notices/approved legal rationale, privacy-by-design review inputs, retention, recipients, and transfer context.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected processing routes to source, system, owner, stated purpose, review record, recipient, and retention execution. This supports evidence lineage; it cannot decide a legal condition, consent, role, or design adequacy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved register fields and identify unowned flows. Privacy/legal humans approve status and design decisions. Refresh before a new purpose, collection route, processor, recipient, transfer, or material system change.

### 2. Rights, security, and incident-readiness record

- **Request and owner:** Redacted rights/complaint log, identity-check procedure, security-operation evidence, risk-assessment inputs where used, incident/exercise timeline, corrective actions, and relevant workforce training records.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a rights case and an incident exercise to intake, authorized reviewer, source system, escalation, action, and closure. This cannot decide identity, a rights result, security sufficiency, reportability, or notification.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved, redacted evidence and flag missing reviews; it cannot contact individuals or authorities. Recollect after a complaint, incident, failed test, security change, or major release.

### 3. Vendor, transfer, and governance record

- **Request and owner:** Processor/vendor register, contract/instruction evidence, transfer-review inputs, exception/risk decisions, management review, remediation/retest, and authority-guidance watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processor or transfer route to human authority, current contract/instructions, operating evidence, expiry, and corrective action. This supports skeptical review; it cannot approve a vendor, transfer, exception, or compliance assertion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a redacted renewal packet and flag expiring records. Privacy, legal, security, and supplier owners approve decisions. Review annually and on vendor, transfer, or regulatory change.
