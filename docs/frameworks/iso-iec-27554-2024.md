# ISO/IEC 27554:2024 identity-risk engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, an identity-risk conclusion, an identity-proofing decision, an access decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27554:2024 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71672.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an identity-risk assessment record that connects identity-dependent processes and services, accountable owners, decision context, assumptions, authorized evidence, risk scenarios, treatment decisions, exceptions, lifecycle changes, and independent review. Treat AI as an evidence and question-preparation assistant only; it does not determine identity trust, access, or risk outcomes.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Identity, security, privacy, legal, risk, product, and service owners retain accountable decisions; assessment owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test context, assumption quality, evidence traceability, exception treatment, and conclusion boundaries. AI may organize authorized inputs, identify unresolved questions, draft workpapers, and prepare non-authoritative status views, but cannot verify identity, set an assurance level, grant or revoke access, determine risk, accept risk, alter systems, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review material identity-process changes continuously, conduct quarterly risk reviews, and perform an annual independent readiness review.
