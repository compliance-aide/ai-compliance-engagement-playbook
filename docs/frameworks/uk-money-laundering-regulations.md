# UK Money Laundering Regulations — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or reporting direction. Check the [live UK regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/692/contents) and supervisor guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain regulated-status, supervisor, service, risk assessment, CDD/EDD, ownership, monitoring, escalation, training, record, outsourcing, and amendment evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide diligence, suspicion, reporting, and customer actions; independent reviewers test cases. AI compares versions and evidence, but cannot make customer or legal decisions. Review quarterly and annually.
