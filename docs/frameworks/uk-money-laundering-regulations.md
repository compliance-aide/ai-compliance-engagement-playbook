# UK Money Laundering Regulations — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or reporting direction. Check the [live UK regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/692/contents) and supervisor guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain regulated-status, supervisor, service, risk assessment, CDD/EDD, ownership, monitoring, escalation, training, record, outsourcing, and amendment evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide diligence, suspicion, reporting, and customer actions; independent reviewers test cases. AI compares versions and evidence, but cannot make customer or legal decisions. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Record retrieval date, applicable supervisor guidance, and use conditions for the [live UK regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/692/contents). This is original evidence-planning language, not legal advice, customer due-diligence direction, a suspicion decision, a report, or a compliance conclusion. Named MLRO, legal, compliance, business, and authorized reporting owners retain all customer, legal, and external-reporting decisions.

### 1. Regulated-status, governance, and risk-assessment package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, compliance, MLRO, executive, business, HR, and records owners provide regulated-activity and supervisor records, MLRO/role appointments, enterprise risk assessment, policy approvals, service/channel inventory, training plan, outsourcing map, and periodic review evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected business service or channel to recorded regulated-status rationale, accountable role, risk assessment, and review date. This supports governed evidence; it cannot interpret law, determine regulated status, or prove risk assessment completeness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied service, owner, and review metadata and flag stale entries. Humans decide legal scope, governance, risk appetite, and policy approval. Refresh after product, jurisdiction, supervisor, ownership, or risk change.

### 2. Customer due-diligence, ownership, and monitoring package

- **Request and owner:** Compliance operations, MLRO, business, onboarding, data, and records owners provide selected onboarding files, customer-risk records, verification-source references, ownership/control assessment evidence, EDD decisions, screening/monitoring logs, periodic review records, and quality-control workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Follow a selected customer file from intake through human diligence and risk decisions, ownership evidence, monitoring/review, and controlled record retention. This supports a sampled process trail; it cannot decide identity, beneficial ownership, customer risk, or suitability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index documents, compare required fields, and flag missing dates or owners. Humans make all diligence, EDD, onboarding, monitoring, and customer-action decisions. Recollect after trigger event, risk change, ownership change, or periodic review.

### 3. Escalation, reporting-boundary, and independent-assurance package

- **Request and owner:** MLRO, legal, compliance, investigations, records, HR, executive, and assurance owners provide escalation procedures, selected case decision records, access-controlled handling logs, reporting-authority records where lawfully retained, training completion, independent testing, remediation actions, and management-review evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected escalation through restricted handling, named human decision, authorized next step, retention control, and independent challenge/follow-up. This supports controlled governance; it cannot create or submit a report, determine suspicion, direct customer action, or disclose protected case information.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a restricted evidence index and flag overdue corrective actions. Humans decide suspicion, reporting, customer restrictions, legal advice, and risk acceptance. Review quarterly and after escalation, supervisor change, test failure, or material control issue.
