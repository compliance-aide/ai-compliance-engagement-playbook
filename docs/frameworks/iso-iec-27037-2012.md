# ISO/IEC 27037:2012 digital-evidence handling engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a determination of evidential value, a legal conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27037:2012 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/44381.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a defensible operating record for identifying, collecting, acquiring, transferring, and preserving potential digital evidence. Each case or exercised scenario should connect authority to act, scope, source and custodian, time basis, handling personnel, method and tooling version, integrity records, storage location, access history, transfers, deviations, retention, and release or disposition decision. The goal is traceability and reproducibility of handling, not an automatic judgment that collected material is admissible or proves a claim.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Incident response, forensics, security, technology, legal, privacy, records management, and risk owners retain their decisions. Authorized handlers conduct approved collection activities; custodians confirm system context; evidence stewards preserve records and access histories; legal and privacy owners determine handling constraints; and accountable authorities decide use, disclosure, retention, and disposition. Independent reviewers select completed cases and exercises to test authority, continuity of custody, integrity evidence, restricted access, exception treatment, and the clarity of limitations.

AI may index authorized metadata, detect missing custody fields, compare timestamps and version records, draft evidence requests, and produce non-authoritative review workpapers. AI cannot access or collect evidence, modify systems, copy or delete material, infer authenticity, decide evidential value, decide legal handling, approve disclosure, or claim compliance. Keep case content outside AI inputs unless that use is specifically authorized and safeguarded. Exercise handling workflows periodically, review exceptions quarterly, and conduct an annual independent readiness review.
