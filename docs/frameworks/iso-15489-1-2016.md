# ISO 15489-1:2016 — records-management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or records-disposition advice. Consult the [ISO committee record](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc46sc11/home/projects/published/iso-15489-records-management.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain record-producing scope, holds, repositories, owners, taxonomy, retention, deletion, migration, access, exception, and immutable evidence records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans approve classification, holds, destruction, and findings; independent reviewers test evidence chain. AI detects metadata gaps and drafts samples, but cannot dispose of records. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Consult the [ISO 15489 records-management page](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc46sc11/home/projects/published/iso-15489-records-management.html) and an authorized edition as needed; checked 2026-07-31. ISO material is licensed. This original plan identifies records-management artifacts only; it does not reproduce ISO content or authorize retention, hold, deletion, or legal conclusions.

### 1. Record scope, ownership, and classification package

- **Request and owner:** Records, legal, and system owners provide in-scope repositories, record-producing processes, accountable custodians, classification/taxonomy decisions, metadata rules, access roles, and documented exclusions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected record class to its producing process, repository, owner, and approved classification decision. This supports defined custody; it cannot prove all records have been discovered or that classification is legally correct.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare inventories and identify missing metadata using approved access. Humans approve classifications and scope. Refresh after a new system, acquisition, process change, or repository migration.

### 2. Retention, hold, and disposition-decision package

- **Request and owner:** Records and legal custodians provide retention schedules, legal/operational hold records, exception approvals, disposition authorizations, audit trails, and access-restricted decision evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected retention or hold decision for owner, authority, effective scope, repository reach, and preserved audit trail. This supports decision traceability; it cannot determine legal obligations or authorize destruction.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag conflicting metadata or approaching review dates; it may not alter holds, retention, access, or deletion state. Recollect after a hold notice, law/policy change, or material exception.

### 3. Preservation, migration, and oversight package

- **Request and owner:** Repository and records owners provide integrity/preservation records, migration plans and outcomes, access reviews, loss/incident records, issue remediation, and annual governance-review materials.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected migration or preservation event to approval, integrity checks, exceptions, and remediation. This supports a controlled record of the event; it cannot prove long-term readability or the completeness of an entire repository.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a read-only evidence index and flag stale reviews. Humans approve remediation, exceptions, access, and disposition. Review quarterly and renew scope annually.
