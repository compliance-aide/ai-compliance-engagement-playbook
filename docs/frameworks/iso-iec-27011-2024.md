# ISO/IEC 27011:2024 telecommunications security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a network-security determination, an operational approval, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27011:2024 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80584.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a telecom-sector security engagement record that connects services, network boundaries, supporting systems, customer and partner interfaces, accountable owners, operational dependencies, approved safeguards, monitoring evidence, resilience and incident records, supplier evidence, exceptions, and improvement decisions. Keep service claims linked to observed operating evidence and scope assumptions, including outsourced or shared-network boundaries.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Telecommunications service, network, security operations, engineering, privacy, legal, procurement, resilience, risk, and customer-facing owners retain accountable decisions. Service owners define the in-scope offering; network and operations teams preserve authorized technical evidence; security leadership governs risk treatment; and business authorities decide customer, regulatory, and material operational actions. Independent reviewers sample service paths from scope through dependencies, operational evidence, incidents, supplier obligations, exceptions, and remediation to identify unsupported boundary or coverage claims.

AI may organize authorized service and evidence inventories, identify stale dependencies, compare change records, draft evidence requests, and prepare non-authoritative audit workpapers. AI cannot change network configuration, enable or disable services, initiate scans, decide service availability or customer impact, approve supplier or risk decisions, interpret telecommunications obligations, or claim compliance. Reassess on material network, service, supplier, architecture, incident, or regulatory changes; perform quarterly operating reviews and an annual independent readiness review.
