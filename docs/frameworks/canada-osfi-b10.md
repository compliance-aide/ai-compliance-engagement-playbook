# Canada OSFI Guideline B-10 — third-party risk engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not OSFI content or a supplier-risk conclusion. Confirm current guidance at the [OSFI third-party risk record](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/third-party-risk-management-guideline).

## Engagement focus

Maintain vendor inventory, criticality decisions, due-diligence evidence, contractual governance, subcontractor visibility, concentration and exit considerations, continuity evidence, monitoring, exceptions, and remediation history.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business, procurement, risk, legal, and technology owners make supplier and risk decisions; independent reviewers challenge the evidence. AI organizes approved vendor artifacts and detects stale records, but cannot select suppliers, approve contracts, or accept risk. Review critical suppliers quarterly and annually.
