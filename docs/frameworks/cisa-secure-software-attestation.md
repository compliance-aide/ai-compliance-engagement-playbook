# CISA secure-software attestation readiness — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an attestation, legal conclusion, or authorization to submit. Consult the [CISA attestation resource](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/secure-software-development-attestation-form) and the applicable solicitation.

## Engagement focus

Maintain the requested-product boundary, responsible parties, development and supply-chain evidence, exception analysis, release records, supporting artifacts, and customer communication history.

## Roles

Authorized organizational representatives control representations and submissions; independent reviewers challenge the evidence package. AI creates an evidence inventory and drafts gaps, but cannot complete, sign, transmit, or characterize an attestation. Reassess each request and annually.

## Source and applicability

Use the current requesting agency instructions and exact form version, not a draft form found by filename. CISA's resource describes online and email submission options, while [Interior's agency instructions](https://www.doi.gov/pam/secure-software-development-attestation) direct users away from RSAA. Resolve the recipient-specific route before submission; a general CISA link is not enough. Verify current governing policy, product scope, signer requirements and any exception path. Detailed current form and legal applicability review remain pending; no universal deadline or portal availability is asserted here.

## Before starting

Apply the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Obtain the actual request, contract/solicitation, requested form, product/version/release inventory, responsible producer, signer authority and evidence custodians. Reuse valid read-only authorization. Keep evidence preparation separate from making a binding representation or government submission.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Resolve the request | Procurement/legal owner confirms current authority, requested scope, form and recipient. | Source-pinned request register with due date and open interpretations. No generic portal assumption. |
| 2. Reconcile software boundary | Product/release owners map offered software, versions, release dates and producer responsibilities. | Complete covered population, exclusions with authorized rationale and evidence needs. A product-level packet does not prove company-wide practices. |
| 3. Map every representation | Qualified reviewer maps each requested assertion to accountable process owners and evidence. | Full assertion register with supported, unsupported and unresolved states. A framework crosswalk alone is not proof. |
| 4. Gather operating evidence | Engineering/security owners supply actual development, build, dependency, testing and vulnerability-handling records. | Source-linked evidence with periods and release coverage; written policy alone does not demonstrate ongoing practice. |
| 5. Resolve gaps | Owners remediate deficiencies; legal/procurement evaluates any agency-allowed alternative path. | Observed correction or explicit unresolved decision. An internal exception is not agency acceptance. |
| 6. Prepare review packet | AI organizes factual workpapers; authorized representatives control final form assertions. | Exact proposed scope, evidence index, gaps and signer decision packet. No inferred affirmative answers. |
| 7. Independently challenge | Reviewer checks coverage, contradictory evidence, signer authority and recipient instructions. | Findings resolved or retained for the signer; incomplete evidence cannot be hidden in an attachment. |
| 8. Execute only with authority | Named representative signs; authorized submitter uses the confirmed route after approval. | Separate signed, transmitted, received and accepted states with actual receipts. Upload success is not buyer acceptance. |

## Evidence and test plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [CISA secure-software development attestation resource](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/secure-software-development-attestation-form), the requesting agency's current solicitation, and organization-approved legal direction; prior locator snapshot 2026-07-31; current request/form verification required. This original plan does not reproduce the form, interpret a solicitation, determine an exception, complete an attestation, or authorize submission. Authorized human representatives confirm source currency, scope, and use conditions.

### 1. Solicitation, offered-software, and signer-authority package

- **Request and owner:** Capture the applicable solicitation/request, offered software and version list, delivery/hosting boundary, organizational role, known exceptions, responsible executives, proposed signer authority, and legal/procurement interpretation record.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected offered product/version to the request, delivery boundary, accountable organization, and documented signer authorization. This can expose mismatched product scope or missing authority; it cannot interpret procurement law, decide eligibility, or authorize a representation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized request/product metadata and flag missing version, scope, or authority fields. Humans resolve solicitation applicability, exception posture, and signer authority. Refresh for each request, material offer change, or signer/organizational-role change.

### 2. Secure-development and software-supply-chain evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Engineering, security, release, and supplier owners provide development-process references, source/build provenance pointers, release approvals, dependency/supplier records, security-test references, vulnerability/remediation records, and approved deviations relevant to the offered software.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one offered release from source/build or supplier evidence through security/release record, exception approval if applicable, and retained provenance. This can test a bounded evidence chain; it cannot validate all code, determine conformance, or replace the requested attestation analysis.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a read-only evidence index and identify absent provenance, dates, or owners. Humans judge sufficiency, approve deviations, and decide remediation/release actions. Refresh after material code, build, dependency, supplier, vulnerability, or release changes.

### 3. Exception, representation, and submission-readiness package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, procurement, executive, security, and independent-review owners provide exception analysis, supporting citations, unresolved-gap register, management decisions, reviewer challenge notes, proposed representation, and submission approval trail.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception or proposed statement to its evidence, human authority, limitation, decision date, and required follow-up. This can show whether readiness governance is documented; it cannot conclude that an exception is permitted, sign, transmit, or bind the organization.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft discrepancy questions and flag stale evidence; it cannot complete forms or make representations. Humans approve legal/procurement posture, final language, signature, and transmission. Recheck before every submission and annually for baseline readiness.


## Failure branches and decisions

Missing signer authority blocks signature, not evidence organization. Conflicting product scope requires reconciliation before assertions. Portal failure preserves exact error and routes to the request owner; do not send through an unapproved alternative. Missing or contradictory evidence remains visible. Preserve all authorized scorer-bound evidence without trimming, sampling or capping; any approved inspection selection must state its limits. On interruption, save form/source versions, covered releases, unresolved assertions and next safe action.

## Cadence and renewal

Recheck for every request and material software, process, ownership or authority change. Annual readiness review is an internal choice, not an assumed attestation expiry or renewal rule. Track any actual agency conditions separately.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver request/scope records, complete assertion/evidence register, gap decisions, reviewer findings, signer authority and exact approved artifact. Record submission and acceptance only when observed. Independent source and skeptical review and named human approval remain necessary for final representations; readiness is not attestation.
