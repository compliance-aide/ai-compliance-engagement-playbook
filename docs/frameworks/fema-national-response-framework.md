# FEMA National Response Framework — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not government authority or an emergency directive. Consult the [FEMA National Response Framework](https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/national-preparedness/frameworks/response).

## Engagement focus

Where applicable, maintain authorities, coordination, mutual-aid, public-private contacts, dependencies, information-sharing, escalation, exercise, and improvement evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized officials activate structures and submit reports; independent reviewers test jurisdictional approvals. AI supports awareness and task tracking, but cannot represent government authority. Exercise periodically and review annually.
