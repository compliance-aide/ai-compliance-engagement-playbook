# FEMA National Response Framework — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not government authority or an emergency directive. Consult the [FEMA National Response Framework](https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/national-preparedness/frameworks/response).

## Engagement focus

Where applicable, maintain authorities, coordination, mutual-aid, public-private contacts, dependencies, information-sharing, escalation, exercise, and improvement evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized officials activate structures and submit reports; independent reviewers test jurisdictional approvals. AI supports awareness and task tracking, but cannot represent government authority. Exercise periodically and review annually.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [FEMA National Response Framework](https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/national-preparedness/frameworks/response), applicable jurisdictional authorities, plans, and approved organization records; checked 2026-07-31. This is original operational guidance, not FEMA text, an emergency directive, or a claim of government authority. Authorized public officials and designated organizational leaders decide activation, jurisdictional coordination, public communications, and submissions.

### 1. Authority, role, and coordination-scope package

- **Request and owner:** Emergency-management, legal, executive, and continuity owners provide applicable authority references, approved emergency/continuity plans, role and contact roster, coordination and mutual-aid agreements, jurisdiction/dependency assumptions, and approval history.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one selected role, coordination relationship, or authority reference to its source, accountable official, current contact, scope limitation, and review date. This checks preparation records; it cannot activate a structure, validate legal authority, or direct a response.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved contact and plan metadata and flag stale owners or expired agreements. Humans decide authority and activation. Refresh after organizational, jurisdictional, partner, or plan change.

### 2. Readiness, exercise, and information-sharing package

- **Request and owner:** Operations, continuity, communications, information-security, and partner-management owners provide readiness checklists, exercise objectives/scenarios, participant records, information-sharing protocols, dependency inventories, exercise observations, and approved lessons learned.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a selected exercise or readiness item to its stated objective, participants, evidence source, observed result, limitation, improvement owner, and target date. This does not prove real-incident performance or authorize release of protected information.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may track tasks, summarize approved observations, and flag unassigned improvements. Humans approve scenarios, information sharing, and lesson adoption. Refresh after an exercise, material dependency change, or information-sharing incident.

### 3. Activation, escalation, and improvement-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Authorized incident leaders, legal, communications, and executive owners provide escalation criteria, decision/activation logs, situation-report governance, approved public/private coordination records, demobilization or recovery decisions, corrective-action register, and management review.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one selected activation scenario or improvement item to authority, timing, factual record, communication approval, limitation, assigned owner, and closure review. This supports accountability review; it cannot decide activation, issue public direction, or represent a government entity.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag missing decision times, aging corrective actions, and stale escalation contacts. Authorized humans make activation and external-communication decisions; independent reviewers challenge the record. Refresh after activation, exercise finding, material event, or annual governance review.
