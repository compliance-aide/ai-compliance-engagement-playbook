# Nigeria Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Confirm current material through the [Nigeria Data Protection Commission](https://ndpc.gov.ng/resources/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain data-use, service, authority, rights, supplier, transfer, security, incident, exception, and remediation evidence; exercise requests and incidents.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain records and verified workflow evidence; independent reviewers challenge system-to-evidence claims. AI flags stale records and drafts questions, but cannot decide applicability, approve transfers or responses, or represent the organization to NDPC. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Nigeria Data Protection Commission resources](https://ndpc.gov.ng/resources/); checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide controller/processor roles, lawful processing, registration, transfer conditions, request outcomes, or regulatory notification duty.

### 1. Data-use, purpose, and retention accountability record

- **Request and owner:** Processing inventory, data-flow map, purpose and authority rationale inputs, privacy communication records, controller/processor role assessment inputs, retention/disposal schedule, and accountable-owner register from privacy, legal, product, and records owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one processing route to its owner, purpose record, systems, communication artifact, retention event, and disposition evidence. This verifies recorded operations; it cannot make a legal determination or validate the authority rationale.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved inventories and identify missing review dates. Privacy/legal humans decide classifications and remediation. Refresh after a new purpose, dataset, system, or material processing change.

### 2. Individual, vendor, and transfer workflow evidence

- **Request and owner:** Rights-request procedures and redacted logs, identity-verification method, processor/vendor due diligence and oversight, data-sharing register, transfer review inputs, and approvals/escalations from privacy, procurement, legal, and customer-service owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted request, vendor onboarding, or data-sharing change through facts, owner, human decision, communication, and follow-up. This supports operational traceability; it cannot decide a request or approve a transfer.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare an internal evidence index and overdue-review prompts; it may not contact a data subject, sign a contract, or represent the organization to NDPC. Recollect after a new vendor, transfer route, or request-process change.

### 3. Security incident and remediation oversight

- **Request and owner:** Safeguard-operation evidence, incident exercise/event timeline, escalation inputs, risk/exception log, corrective actions, retests, and management-review minutes from security, incident, privacy, and executive owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an incident or overdue remediation to its detection, authority, action, expiry, and retest evidence. This does not prove safeguards effective or determine notification duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may surface stale actions and draft non-authoritative questions. Named humans approve risk, notifications, and closure. Revisit quarterly, annually, and after an incident or source change.
