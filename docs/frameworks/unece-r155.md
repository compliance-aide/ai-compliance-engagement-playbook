# UNECE R155 — vehicle cybersecurity engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not type approval or legal advice. Confirm applicable market and amendment at the [UNECE regulation source](https://unece.org/transport/documents/2021/03/standards/un-regulation-no-155-cyber-security-and-cyber-security).

## Engagement focus

Maintain vehicle type, market, governance, risk, field-monitoring, release, and type-approval evidence; reassess post-production issues quarterly.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Qualified humans approve market actions; independent reviewers test retrieval and traceability. AI tracks evidence and rule changes, but cannot determine applicability, sign declarations, or submit type-approval materials.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [UNECE Regulation No. 155 source](https://unece.org/transport/documents/2021/03/standards/un-regulation-no-155-cyber-security-and-cyber-security), checked 2026-07-31, and have homologation, legal, and product authorities confirm applicable market, series, and amendment. This is original evidence planning, not regulation text, legal advice, a cybersecurity-management judgment, or a type-approval claim.

### 1. Vehicle-type, market, and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Vehicle program, cybersecurity governance, homologation, legal, and quality owners provide vehicle-type/version and market records, program roles, approved cybersecurity governance references, supplier responsibility boundaries, material-change rules, and decision/exception records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected type or variant to market assumption, accountable program and cybersecurity owners, supplier boundary, and approved decision record. This supports traceability; it cannot establish market applicability, type-approval eligibility, or legal sufficiency.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize sanitized program metadata and flag unlinked variants or owners. Qualified humans decide market scope and governance. Refresh before a new market, vehicle variant, ownership change, or material architecture change.

### 2. Cyber-risk and engineering-lifecycle package

- **Request and owner:** Engineering, product security, safety, supplier, and quality owners provide approved asset/interface and dependency references, risk-management artifacts, threat/vulnerability intake records, design/change and verification records, supplier evidence links, exceptions, and remediation actions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected cyber-risk or engineering change from source/affected boundary through accountable owner, approved treatment, verification reference, and open-action status. This does not prove risk completeness, vulnerability absence, product safety, or cybersecurity effectiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may correlate approved records and flag missing ownership, decision dates, or remediation links; it cannot rank risk, approve a design, or accept safety/cyber risk. Human authorities decide treatment. Refresh after a vulnerability, design/software change, supplier change, or test result.

### 3. Field monitoring and approval-readiness package

- **Request and owner:** Post-production, incident response, customer support, quality, legal, homologation, and executive owners provide monitoring inputs, reported-event triage records, escalation/playbook references, corrective-action and recall/change decisions, evidence register, and authorized external-communication records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected field signal or exercise to intake, accountable assessment owner, decision/escalation record, action, and closure/review evidence. This supports lifecycle traceability; it cannot determine reporting duties, authorize a recall, or make a declaration.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify stale follow-ups from approved records while preserving data-minimization restrictions. Humans decide notifications, filings, field actions, and declarations. Refresh after a significant field event, campaign/update, regulation change, or quarterly monitoring review.
