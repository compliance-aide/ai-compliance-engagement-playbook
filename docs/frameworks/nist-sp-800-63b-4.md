# NIST SP 800-63B-4 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an identity-service approval. Check the
> [NIST digital identity publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/63/b/4/final)
> and relevant privacy, accessibility, relying-party, and policy context.

## Engagement focus

Identify covered identity journeys and accountable owners; govern authenticator
lifecycle and recovery; maintain exceptions, fraud signals, accessibility and
privacy impacts, and supplier change; periodically review design and operating
evidence; and produce an annual residual-identity-risk briefing.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain service inventory, changes, user-impact information, and
evidence. Independent reviewers evaluate rationale and evidence coherence. AI may
trace authorized claims to evidence and flag staleness; it cannot make
access, identity, or exception decisions. Review operational signals throughout
the year and watch for source revisions.
