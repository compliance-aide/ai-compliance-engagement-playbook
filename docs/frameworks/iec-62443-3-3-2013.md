# IEC 62443-3-3:2013 IACS system security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a control selection, a security-level determination, a safety decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC 62443-3-3:2013 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/7033).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a system-security evidence record that connects the industrial system boundary, accountable operations and engineering owners, safety and process constraints, risk-informed design inputs, authorized technical evidence, exceptions, remediation, and independent review. Treat the record as a way to prepare a defensible engagement without converting an AI summary into an engineering or assurance conclusion.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, maintenance, security, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; system and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test traceability, evidence quality, and safety-aware readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify unresolved evidence questions, draft workpapers, and prepare non-authoritative status views, but cannot select or approve safeguards, determine security levels, decide a safety or engineering outcome, accept risk, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review the evidence set quarterly, reassess it annually, and reopen it before material system, process, supplier, safety, incident, or design changes.
