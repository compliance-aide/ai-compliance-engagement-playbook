# ISO/IEC 27043:2015 incident investigation — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content, legal advice, or an investigative conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/44407.html) for current status; do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain investigation authority, case intake, evidence preservation, chain-of-custody records, timeline, analysis decisions, external-coordination boundaries, findings, closure records, and lessons.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized investigators and counsel control investigative decisions; independent reviewers test case integrity. AI structures non-sensitive case records and flags chronology gaps, but cannot access evidence, reach conclusions, or make disclosure decisions. Exercise annually and review each case.
