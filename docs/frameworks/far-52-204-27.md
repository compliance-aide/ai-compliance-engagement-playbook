# FAR 52.204-27 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a contract-compliance conclusion. Confirm current clause use at the [Acquisition.gov clause guidance](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/4.2203?searchTerms=52.204-27).

## Engagement focus

Maintain covered-contract inventory, endpoint and managed-device records, application governance, authorized exceptions, communications, incident and remediation evidence, and contract-file traceability.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized contract, legal, security, and IT owners determine scope and compliance actions; independent reviewers test evidence. AI identifies inventory gaps and drafts questions, but cannot remove software, grant exceptions, or make contract representations. Review at award, change, and annually.
