# ISO/IEC 27099:2022 PKI trust-service engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, a certificate policy, a certificate-practice statement, a trust determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27099:2022 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/56590.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a PKI trust-service engagement record that connects the service boundary, accountable trust and security owners, certificate-policy and practice artifacts, lifecycle evidence, subscriber and relying-party obligations, supplier dependencies, exceptions, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Keep AI work limited to organizing authorized evidence and questions; never treat a generated statement as a trust-service decision or assurance conclusion.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Trust-service leadership, security, operations, legal, privacy, risk, and accountable certificate authorities retain decisions; policy and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, lifecycle traceability, exception handling, and assurance boundaries. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot issue, revoke, suspend, or approve certificates, establish policy or practice statements, determine trustworthiness, accept risk, alter production configuration, initiate changes, or claim compliance. Review lifecycle evidence continuously, conduct quarterly operating reviews, and reassess annually and before material policy, service, supplier, incident, cryptographic, or regulatory changes.
