# FFIEC Architecture, Infrastructure, and Operations — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not examiner guidance or a certification. Consult the [FFIEC AIO booklet release](https://www.ffiec.gov/news/press-releases/2021/pr-06-30).

## Engagement focus

Maintain architecture, infrastructure, provider, change, resilience, cyber, operational-risk, management-narrative, remediation, and examination-readiness evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators provide accurate diagrams and results; independent reviewers challenge support for the risk narrative. AI reconciles inventories and evidence age, but cannot represent institutions, decide safety-and-soundness, or make examiner claims. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [FFIEC AIO booklet release](https://www.ffiec.gov/news/press-releases/2021/pr-06-30) and the current booklet collection; checked 2026-07-31. This is original engagement-planning language, not examination guidance, a supervisory interpretation, or a statement about safety and soundness.

### 1. Architecture and critical-service accountability package

- **Request and owner:** Architecture, operations, and business-service owners provide current service maps, infrastructure/dependency inventories, criticality designations, hosted-service responsibilities, and approved architecture/change boundaries for the review period.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected critical services to the recorded components, dependencies, owners, and architecture approval. This supports a coherent operating narrative; it cannot prove inventory completeness, resilience, or supervisory acceptability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare authorized maps and inventories and flag orphaned dependencies. Human owners establish criticality and architecture decisions. Refresh after a material platform, outsourcing, acquisition, or service change.

### 2. Operations, change, and resilience package

- **Request and owner:** Operations and continuity owners provide time-bounded change approvals, monitoring and capacity records, backup/recovery exercise evidence, incident records, problem management, and service-level exception logs.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect source access and dates, then trace selected production changes or recovery exercises to approvals, outcomes, issues, and follow-up. This supports evidence of selected practices; it cannot prove uninterrupted operation or recovery under every scenario.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may build a non-destructive evidence index and flag overdue remediation. Humans approve production changes, risk treatment, and recovery conclusions. Recollect after an outage, failed exercise, material control failure, or annual cycle.

### 3. Third-party and governance narrative package

- **Request and owner:** Vendor-management, risk, and executive owners provide provider due-diligence records, contract/responsibility summaries, material findings, issue decisions, board or committee reporting, and examination-preparation ownership records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected provider issue to accountable ownership, escalation, decision record, and follow-up evidence. This supports governance traceability; it cannot assess vendor adequacy, conclude regulatory compliance, or bind the institution in an examination.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized provider evidence and identify stale reviews. Humans determine risk, contract actions, board reporting, and examiner communications. Refresh after a material provider change, adverse finding, or governance escalation.
