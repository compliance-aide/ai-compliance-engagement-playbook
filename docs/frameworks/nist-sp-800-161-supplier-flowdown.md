# NIST SP 800-161 supplier-flowdown engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST control content, a supplier rating, or a procurement decision. Confirm the current publication through [NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1 update](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/161/r1/upd1/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a lifecycle record for supplier and product-service supply-chain risk: supplier scope, risk assumptions, contractual obligations, evidence requests, dependency changes, due diligence, exceptions, findings, remediation, and accountable decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Procurement and business owners own supplier scope; security and risk teams maintain assessment records; suppliers provide their evidence; independent reviewers test traceability. AI maps approved evidence and contractual artifacts, flags stale supplier records, and drafts questions, but cannot make a supplier qualification decision, accept risk, or make procurement commitments. Reassess at onboarding, renewal, material supplier change, and scheduled risk review with human approval.
