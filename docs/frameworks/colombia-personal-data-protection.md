# Colombia personal-data protection — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the [Colombian data-protection authority hub](https://www.sic.gov.co/proteccion-de-datos-personales) for current guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain use and disclosure inventory, notices, authorizations, complaints, supplier oversight, registration-review, and public-claim evidence. Test requests and vendor evidence quarterly and reconcile public claims annually.

## Roles

Operators run intake and remediation; independent reviewers sample notice-to-system and closure records. AI organizes records and contradictions, but cannot decide authorization, submit registrations, or answer a data subject as the organization.

## Source and applicability

Start with [Law 1581 of 2012 in the official legal repository](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=49981), its implementing rules and current SIC instructions. Have Colombian privacy/legal owners identify applicable general and sector-specific regimes, entity roles, exclusions and special data categories. Do not assume that every Colombian database has the same registration duty or that a foreign privacy-law basis is interchangeable with Colombian authorization rules.

Articles 14 and 15 distinguish consultas from reclamos. Their procedures and clocks differ; identify the actual request rather than placing everything in a generic privacy ticket. Official indexed text confirms a ten-business-day consultation period and a fifteen-business-day complaint period with different starting wording. The complete extension, incomplete-request, referral and complaint-marking provisions must be verified before operational deadline calculation. This guide supplies no automatic deadline calculator.

## Before starting

Apply the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Record organization, processing boundary, responsible privacy owner, authorized tools and evidence locations. Use redacted request metadata and safe source pointers; keep identity documents and sensitive records in approved systems. Separate intake facts, counsel decisions, proposed actions and verified execution. Never contact a requester or change their records without the required authority.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and accountable owner | Output and exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Resolve applicability | Privacy/legal owner determines the applicable regime, roles and purpose-specific obligations. | Source-linked decisions with unresolved questions assigned; a website notice alone does not decide the legal position. |
| 2. Map the lifecycle | Business and system owners trace collection, use, disclosure, storage, retention and disposal across services and suppliers. | Complete processing inventory with owners and gaps; include actual integrations, not only declared uses. |
| 3. Reconcile authorization and notice | Owners link each use to the relevant notice version, authorization evidence or legally reviewed exception. | Purpose/channel/version trace with unsupported or conflicting uses visible for human resolution. |
| 4. Build full obligation coverage | Agent indexes the adopted legal and SIC requirements against processing activities and owners. | Requirement/evidence register including rights handling, security, supplier and registration-review duties; justified applicability decisions replace silent omissions. |
| 5. Handle request workpapers | Privacy team classifies consulta/reclamo, verifies authority and completeness using approved procedures, and records receipt and all subsequent events. | Human-checked business-day deadline and procedural actions based on the full applicable article; verification activity does not silently pause the clock. |
| 6. Test actual execution | Authorized operators and reviewers trace approved corrections, disclosures or disposal across relevant systems and recipients. | Dated observations and limitations; drafted responses and scheduled jobs remain distinct from completed actions. |
| 7. Resolve supplier and incident issues | Privacy/security owners evaluate recipient roles, instructions, cross-border arrangements, incidents and registration obligations. | Documented legal decisions, remediation and reporting preparation; no assumed transfer approval or regulator filing. |
| 8. Review and sustain | Independent reviewers challenge lifecycle coverage and closure; accountable owners approve communications and actions. | Evidence-backed closure, verified delivery or filing receipt where authorized, and next review dates. |

## Failure branches and decisions

- **Authorization record missing:** preserve the use and source facts, assign the legal decision and correction proposal; do not fabricate consent from continued use of a service.
- **Complaint incomplete or misdirected:** invoke the exact applicable procedure after legal verification; do not invent a universal pause, abandonment or referral rule.
- **One system corrected but replicas unchanged:** retain partial execution and identify remaining recipients or stores before closure.
- **Foreign vendor involved:** distinguish the actual data movement and parties' roles; a contract title alone does not resolve transfer or transmission conditions.
- **Registration not required:** retain that specific applicability decision separately from other protection duties; do not label the organization exempt from the law.

## Evidence and test plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Colombian data-protection authority hub](https://www.sic.gov.co/proteccion-de-datos-personales) and qualified Colombian privacy/legal review; prior snapshot dated 2026-07-31; revalidate before use. This original plan does not reproduce Colombian legal text or decide controller/processor status, authorization validity, database-registration duty, international-transfer conditions, data-subject outcome, or regulator filing.

### 1. Data-use, authorization, and notice package

- **Request and owner:** Personal-data inventory connecting collection source, data categories, subjects, purpose, use, systems, recipients, retention, owner, and notice/authorization artifacts; plus collection-channel and privacy-program records from privacy, product, operations, and records owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected data use from collection channel to stated purpose, notice/authorization record, system, recipient, retention setting, and accountable owner. This supports a factual lifecycle record; it cannot decide authorization validity, purpose compatibility, legal basis, or whether a notice is sufficient.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventory and collection metadata and flag missing relationships. Colombian legal/privacy humans decide classifications and authorization questions. Refresh for a new data source, purpose, collection channel, recipient, or material system change.

### 2. Data-subject, quality, and retention-operation package

- **Request and owner:** Data-subject request and complaint procedure; redacted intake, verification, response, and closure log; correction/quality workflow; access reviews; retention/disposal evidence; workforce training; and escalation records owned by privacy, customer operations, records, and security teams.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a redacted request, correction, or disposal event through intake, assigned human, verification/decision authority, source system, action, escalation, and closure. This can support operational traceability; it cannot establish identity, decide an exception or outcome, or prove statutory timing or complete lifecycle execution.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may generate de-identified sampling indexes and flag incomplete handoffs; it cannot answer a data subject or alter records. Authorized humans decide identity, requests, communications, and closure. Recheck after a request trend, quality failure, or retention-process change.

### 3. Supplier, registration-review, and incident-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Supplier/recipient register; contractual/instruction and due-diligence evidence; cross-border or transfer-review inputs; counsel-owned database-registration review inputs; incident/complaint timeline; management decisions; and remediation/retest records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier, registration-review input, transfer, complaint, or incident item to evidence provenance, accountable authority, limitation, corrective action, and review date. This supports an accountable governance record; it cannot approve a transfer, determine registration or notification duties, establish supplier performance, or make a compliance conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale supplier records and prepare an approved-metadata packet. Legal, privacy, security, and communications humans approve contracts, filings, external communications, risk decisions, and closure. Refresh after a supplier, transfer, incident, source update, or annual review.


## Cadence and renewal

Quarterly operational checks and annual public-claim review are engagement conventions. Derive statutory request, incident, registration and update dates from verified current sources, with the applicable local calendar and named owner. Reopen relevant work after purpose, authorization, recipient, system, legal-source or incident changes. Preserve original receipt timestamps and decision history when ownership changes.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the source/applicability record, processing and obligation registers, authorization/notice reconciliation, request timelines, execution evidence and unresolved legal decisions. State exactly what was drafted, approved, executed and delivered. Identify the next action, owner and evidence needed without prior chat history; a closed internal ticket does not prove a valid response or complete downstream correction.
