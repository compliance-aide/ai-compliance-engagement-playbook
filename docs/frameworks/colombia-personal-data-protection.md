# Colombia personal-data protection — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the [Colombian data-protection authority hub](https://www.sic.gov.co/proteccion-de-datos-personales) for current guidance.

## Engagement focus

Maintain use and disclosure inventory, notices, authorizations, complaints, supplier oversight, registration-review, and public-claim evidence. Test requests and vendor evidence quarterly and reconcile public claims annually.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators run intake and remediation; independent reviewers sample notice-to-system and closure records. AI organizes records and contradictions, but cannot decide authorization, submit registrations, or answer a data subject as the organization.
