# ISO 10004:2018 customer-satisfaction measurement — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a customer-satisfaction conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71582.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain measurement objectives, segment definitions, collection methods, response data, quality checks, analysis records, interpretation decisions, improvement actions, and management-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Customer and business owners approve measurement choices and interpretations; independent reviewers test data integrity. AI consolidates permitted data and flags anomalies, but cannot claim satisfaction, change methodology, or contact customers. Review trends quarterly and programme annually.
