# ISO 10004:2018 customer-satisfaction measurement — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a customer-satisfaction conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71582.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain measurement objectives, segment definitions, collection methods, response data, quality checks, analysis records, interpretation decisions, improvement actions, and management-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Customer and business owners approve measurement choices and interpretations; independent reviewers test data integrity. AI consolidates permitted data and flags anomalies, but cannot claim satisfaction, change methodology, or contact customers. Review trends quarterly and programme annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Check the applicable edition at the [ISO 10004:2018 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71582.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO content is licensed. This original plan requests organization-created evidence categories, not standard text, and makes no customer-satisfaction or conformity conclusion.

### 1. Measurement purpose, population, and method package

- **Request and owner:** Customer-insight and business owners provide approved measurement purpose, customer or journey population definitions, inclusion/exclusion logic, collection method, frequency, question governance, and privacy/contact permissions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected reported measure to its population definition, approved method, and period. This supports explainable measurement design; it cannot establish that the sample represents all customers or that a measure captures satisfaction accurately.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile documented populations and flag missing method approval. Humans approve methodology and outreach. Refresh after a product, segment, channel, consent, or methodology change.

### 2. Collection integrity and analysis lineage package

- **Request and owner:** Data and research owners provide collection extracts or immutable summaries, response-rate/calculation records, data-quality checks, transformation/version history, anomaly investigations, and access restrictions.
- **Validate and limit:** Reperform a small approved calculation from a selected frozen dataset or report input and inspect its lineage. This supports reproducibility of that result; it cannot authenticate every response, prove causation, or validate statistical inference.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved summaries, surface missing lineage, and draft anomaly questions; it may not alter data or publish a finding. Recollect after a material quality failure or reporting-definition change.

### 3. Interpretation, action, and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Business leaders provide interpretation memos, decision records, improvement backlog, owner/due-date evidence, follow-up measurement plans, and management-review materials.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected insight to its evidence, human interpretation, authorized action, and follow-up. This supports accountable use of feedback; it cannot prove an action caused a customer outcome.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble an evidence packet and flag overdue follow-ups. Humans decide interpretation, investment, customer communication, and closure. Review quarterly and renew scope/method annually.
