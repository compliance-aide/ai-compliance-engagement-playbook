# ISO 10004:2018 customer-satisfaction measurement — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, a conformity claim or proof that customers are satisfied.

## Source and applicability

Confirm the applicable edition and scope at the [official ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71582.html). The indexed publisher record was checked 2026-09-04 and identifies ISO 10004:2018 as guidance for monitoring and measuring customer satisfaction. The full licensed standard was not reviewed. Confirm permitted source use and obtain qualified review before clause-level assessment; do not place ISO text in AI prompts without permission.

The steps below are original measurement work instructions. They do not prescribe an ISO-required metric, survey instrument, sample size or statistical method. An authorized research owner must approve those choices for the question, population and intended decision.

## Engagement focus

Create a reproducible chain from a business question to the customer population, collection process, valid observations, calculation, interpretation and improvement decision. Separate satisfaction as reported by respondents from assumptions about all customers. A correct calculation can still answer the wrong question or reflect a biased collection process.

## Roles

The business owner defines the decision and approves action. Research/customer-insight owners approve instruments, population, methods and interpretation. Data owners preserve inputs and reproducible transformations; privacy/legal owners approve collection and contact permissions. An independent reviewer challenges data integrity and inference. AI may consolidate authorized data, execute approved calculations and draft anomaly questions, but cannot invent responses, change methods to improve a score, contact customers, approve spending or publish satisfaction claims.

## Before starting

Record the business question, product/service, customer journey, population, geography, period and decision owner. Obtain approved instruments, contact rules, datasets, prior methodology and access permissions. Keep personal responses in an approved private environment and public workpapers de-identified. Determine whether the available data can answer the question; missing observations are not neutral scores.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Define the decision and measurement contract.** Specify what will be measured, why, for whom and at what time. Record the unit of analysis: person, account, transaction or interaction. Define eligible population, exclusions, response window, metric formula, comparison period and limitations before inspecting results. Obtain method approval; do not silently substitute an easier available proxy.
2. **Validate collection design.** Record question wording, scale, language, order, channel, invitations, reminders and version. Check accessibility and whether the invitation frame omits important customer groups. Research owners justify any survey sampling and associated inference; it is separate from assessment evidence handling. Preserve all supplied evidence and account for every known collection route rather than silently trimming or capping it.
3. **Freeze inputs and reconcile coverage.** Retain approved immutable source references, extraction date, dataset version and provenance. Reconcile eligible, invited, delivered, responded, valid and analyzed populations with explicit definitions. Log duplicates, test entries, invalid values and incomplete responses with approved treatment and reasons. Retain originals and a transformation trail; do not delete unfavorable responses or overwrite them during cleaning.
4. **Reproduce the analysis.** Apply the approved formula and transformation version to the complete authorized analysis dataset. Record numerator, denominator, units, missing-value rules, weights and rounding. Calculate segment results using the approved definitions. A zero denominator produces an unavailable result, not zero satisfaction. Independently check meaningful calculations and trace reported numbers to inputs before interpretation.
5. **Challenge inference and comparability.** Examine response coverage, nonresponse, channel changes, repeated respondents and segment mix. Keep descriptive results separate from population estimates and causal claims. Compare like instruments and populations; where they differ, explain the effect or withhold the trend conclusion. Research owners approve statistical assumptions and uncertainty methods; AI confidence is not statistical uncertainty.
6. **Approve interpretation and action.** Link each proposed insight to its measure, evidence, limitation and alternative explanation. Compare with other approved feedback such as complaints without assuming the signals measure the same construct. Business owners choose an action, owner, due date and follow-up measure. Do not label an action successful merely because a metric improved after implementation.
7. **Verify follow-up and hand off.** Repeat measurement under a documented comparable design or disclose the changes. Track action execution separately from outcome evidence. Preserve original results, corrected versions and decisions. Deliver a reproducible packet that another authorized analyst can use, including unresolved data-quality and inference gaps.

## Evidence and test plan

These three packages preserve the earlier tailored plan while replacing selected-report checks with complete accounting of scoped measures and supplied evidence.

| Package | Minimum records | Reviewer challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose, population and method | Decision question, units, eligibility, instrument version, collection design, permissions and method approval | Does the measure address the intended question and population? |
| Collection integrity and analysis lineage | Frozen inputs, population reconciliation, exclusions, transformations, formulas and reproduced outputs | Can the result be rebuilt without undocumented choices? |
| Interpretation, action and governance | Supported insights, uncertainty, comparison limits, decisions, action evidence and follow-up | Does the conclusion stay within the evidence, and can improvement be distinguished from changed measurement? |

Apply the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) statuses `supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` and `not_tested` to individual assertions. Split arithmetic accuracy, representativeness and causal attribution into separate assertions. A supported arithmetic result does not make the other two supported. Preserve unfavorable findings and all available evidence even when public reporting requires authorized privacy protection.

Fictional author desk case: period A has 20 satisfied responses out of 40; period B has 18 out of 20. The respondent proportions are 50% and 90%, but B changed the invitation channel and excluded customers with failed transactions. The arithmetic can be supported while the claim of improved satisfaction across all customers remains unsupported by this comparison. Preserve the excluded population and obtain a qualified comparability review.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Raw data or transformations are missing: report what can be reproduced and mark the remaining results unverified; do not fabricate row-level inputs from a chart.
- Instrument or segment definitions changed: version the series and explain the break rather than joining it into a continuous trend without review.
- Duplicate or suspicious responses appear: apply documented treatment with retained originals and a visible reason, not an improvised score-improvement filter.
- A reported metric is wrong: issue a controlled correction through the authorized owner, retaining the prior version and affected decisions.
- A segment is too small for safe reporting or credible inference: obtain approved treatment, preserve private evidence and do not silently merge it into another segment.

## Cadence and renewal

Set collection and review frequency from the decision cycle, customer exposure and approved research plan; this guide invents no quarterly or annual ISO mandate. Reopen after product, population, instrument, channel, permission or methodology changes and material data-quality failures.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the measurement contract, private input references, reconciliation, reproducible analysis, approved interpretations and action status to named owners. Explicitly state what the results cannot establish. Independent source, skeptical and rights review remain pending before guide publication. Author desk calculations are not a real survey, independent review or cross-model usability test.
