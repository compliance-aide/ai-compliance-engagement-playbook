# Spain National Security Scheme (Royal Decree 311/2022) engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Spanish legal text, a legal interpretation, a certification decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current applicability through [Royal Decree 311/2022](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-7191), the [CCN ENS information page](https://ens.ccn.cni.es/es/que-es-el-ens), and qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a public-sector security-engagement record that connects in-scope services and systems, accountable roles, classification and categorization decisions made by authorized people, risk evidence, safeguards, supplier and service context, policy and governance records, assurance results, exceptions, remediation, and review dates. Retain sources, scope, uncertainty, and approval history for material statements. Connect the record to operating evidence so that an assessment can distinguish intended security arrangements from observed operation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized public-sector authorities, information, service, system, and security owners, risk, legal, privacy, procurement, operations, and suppliers retain accountable decisions. System owners maintain technical context; security owners coordinate evidence; authorized authorities approve policy, categorization, and risk decisions; and auditors evaluate evidence in their independent role. Independent reviewers sample services and systems through scope, ownership, risk, safeguards, evidence, provider obligations, assurance, findings, and closure, challenging unclear responsibilities, stale records, unsupported implementation claims, and unverified remediation.

AI may organize authorized, suitably handled metadata, identify missing ownership or review fields, compare approved records with retained evidence, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot determine legal scope, categorize a system, select safeguards, authorize a service, approve an exception, accept risk, make a certification claim, alter evidence, or claim compliance. Reassess before material service, system, data, supplier, authority, incident, or risk changes; review evidence routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Retain the official [Royal Decree 311/2022 record](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-7191) and the [CCN ENS information page](https://ens.ccn.cni.es/es/que-es-el-ens) as retrieved 2026-07-31. Treat the decree record and CCN page as official sources whose current applicability, amendments, and service boundary require authorized Spanish public-sector and legal confirmation at engagement start. ### 1. Service boundary and authorized categorization record

- **Request and owner:** Obtain the in-scope public-service and system inventory, service purpose, information/service/system/security-owner assignments, approved categorization decisions, dependency and provider context, and authorization records from the designated public authority and accountable owners. Cover the approved service population and material changes since the last authorized decision.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected service to its named authorities, system boundary, information and service context, categorization decision, date, and retained approval. Inspect access-controlled records without exporting sensitive system details. This supports accountability and decision traceability; it cannot determine ENS applicability, validate a categorization, or establish that all services are in scope.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventory metadata and flag missing owner, date, or decision links. Authorized human authorities decide categorization and scope; counsel resolves legal questions. Refresh before a new public service, material system or data change, supplier boundary change, or annual review.

### 2. Risk, safeguard-operation, and supplier evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain approved risk records, safeguard implementation references in original organizational language, operating records, technical-test references, provider/shared-responsibility records, exception decisions, and remediation evidence from security, system, operations, procurement, and supplier owners. Define the sampling population, evidence period, and any restricted evidence location before collection.
- **Validate and limit:** For a selected risk or dependency, inspect the chain from approved risk decision through assigned safeguard activity, protected operating evidence, provider responsibility, exception, and remediation/retest link. Confirm record integrity and reviewer access constraints. This supports a traceable engagement record; it cannot prove effective operation across the environment, supplier conformance, or ENS compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved metadata and identify stale risk, supplier, or retest links; it cannot select safeguards, modify systems, or approve exceptions. Human security and procurement owners approve treatment and escalation. Trigger on a material risk finding, incident, provider change, failed test, expired exception, or annual renewal.

### 3. Assurance, exception, and authority-review package

- **Request and owner:** Obtain independent-review workpapers or references, finding register, management responses, authorized risk acceptances, remediation ownership and due dates, retest results, and authority-review minutes from assurance, security leadership, and the authorized public authority. Preserve the review boundary and any unresolved disagreement.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected finding or exception from evidence reference through independent challenge, accountable decision, compensating action, expiry, and retest or approved closure rationale. Do not treat a policy, test output, or closure statement alone as operating effectiveness. This supports transparent governance; it cannot certify a system, replace an assessor, or accept residual risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a source-linked review queue and flag overdue or unowned actions. Independent reviewers challenge evidence; authorized humans approve risk and external statements. Trigger on an assessment result, material incident, authority change, overdue action, source change, or annual readiness cycle.
