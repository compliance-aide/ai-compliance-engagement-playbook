# CTPAT — supply-chain program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CBP criteria or a program submission. Consult [CBP CTPAT](https://www.cbp.gov/border-security/ports-entry/cargo-security/CTPAT).

## Engagement focus

Confirm entity category and participation status; maintain international map, partner verification, contract, annual profile-review, reassessment, validation-interview, and walkthrough evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain evidence; independent reviewers test claimed operation. AI flags partner and route gaps, but cannot fabricate submissions, certify, or submit to CBP. Review annually and on material events.
