## Engagement focus

Maintain legal/perimeter and materiality decisions, disclosure/data owners, evidence lineage, change, impact-risk-opportunity, remediation, assurance-readiness, and immutable reporting package.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Counsel and management decide applicability/materiality; independent providers assure. AI checks internal consistency and gaps, but cannot file, sign, or assert compliance. Review monthly, quarterly, and pre-publication.

### 1. Applicability and boundary evidence

- **Request and owner:** A dated record of the applicable organization, service, system, data, supplier, location, and applicable boundary, exclusions, inherited responsibilities, and accountable human owners, maintained by business, security, service, and supplier owners.
