# EU CSRD / ESRS — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice, an assurance opinion or authorization to publish a sustainability statement.

## Source and applicability

Use the [Commission's implementing/delegated acts register](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/regulation-and-supervision/financial-services-legislation/implementing-and-delegated-acts/corporate-sustainability-reporting-directive_en), applicable Official Journal acts, national law and qualified legal review. Record reporting entity/group, financial year, applicable scope and transition provisions, reporting basis and assurance route before requesting disclosures.

The Commission [announced revised ESRS adoption on 3 July 2026](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-adopts-revised-sustainability-reporting-standards-2026-07-03_en), alongside a voluntary standard. Its announcement identifies a two-month scrutiny period that may be extended by two months. Adoption alone therefore does not establish the applicable reporting basis for a particular year. Verify scrutiny outcome, publication and application provisions before replacing the prior standards or deleting datapoints. Source check: 2026-09-04; current scrutiny outcome remains unverified in this draft.

Keep CSRD legal scope, the applicable ESRS disclosure basis, voluntary reporting and separate sustainability due-diligence obligations distinct. A customer questionnaire does not establish statutory scope; a reduced statutory perimeter does not erase an existing contractual commitment without review.

## Engagement focus

Produce a traceable reporting package connecting perimeter, materiality decisions, impacts/risks/opportunities, disclosures, metrics and narrative to approved evidence. Preserve source lineage, estimation limits, comparatives, corrections and assurance questions.

## Roles

Legal owns applicability and reporting dates. Management owns materiality and the statement. Sustainability and finance own disclosure processes and reconciliation; operational owners supply data; IT maintains extraction and change records. Independent assurance providers perform their engagement. AI prepares inventories, calculations and consistency checks within approved authority; it cannot determine final materiality, issue assurance, sign or file the statement.

## Before starting

Follow the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md). Obtain entity/group structure, reporting period, approved legal basis, materiality method, disclosure inventory, data owners, systems, prior statements and assurance instructions. Store confidential supplier, worker and financial records in approved systems. Record missing inputs and decision owners rather than filling gaps with plausible values.

## Ordered workflow

| Step | Action and owner | Output and exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fix reporting basis | Legal and finance reconcile entity/group scope, year and applicable standards. | Dated approved basis and unresolved transitions; no unverified standard replacement. |
| 2. Record materiality | Management applies the approved method to impacts, risks and opportunities. | Evidence-backed decisions and challenges; unavailable data is not a materiality exclusion. |
| 3. Map disclosures | Reporting owners reconcile the complete applicable disclosure inventory. | Every item has an owner and reasoned disposition, including dependencies and approved omissions. |
| 4. Specify data | Data owners define units, boundary, period, method, estimates and source systems. | Repeatable calculation/extraction plan with acceptance criteria. |
| 5. Collect and reconcile | Custodians supply originals or authorized references; reviewers reconcile all expected inputs. | Complete coverage ledger, discrepancies and uncollected records retained. |
| 6. Calculate and challenge | Authorized analysts reproduce metrics and compare narrative with evidence. | Versioned computations, adverse findings, estimation uncertainty and corrected drafts. |
| 7. Review for assurance | Management and assurance teams resolve questions through their agreed process. | Reviewed statement and explicit open matters; agent checks do not substitute for assurance. |
| 8. Freeze and hand off | Authorized owner approves the exact package and publication route. | Immutable version, supporting evidence, approvals and next-period changes; no inferred filing. |

## Evidence and test plan

### 1. Perimeter and materiality

Legal/finance supply the source decision, entity list, financial year, consolidation boundary and transition rationale. Management supplies the materiality method, stakeholder inputs and decisions. Reconcile every in-scope entity and disclosure disposition; retain disagreements. This tests traceability, not legal scope or materiality correctness without qualified review. Reopen after acquisitions, changed activity or source changes.

### 2. Metrics and lineage

Data owners supply source identifiers, units, period, formula, conversion factors, estimates and approvals. Recalculate from preserved inputs and reconcile totals to the declared boundary. Keep zero, missing, estimated and excluded values distinct. A successful spreadsheet formula does not establish source accuracy or completeness. Reopen after source, factor, method or boundary changes.

### 3. Narrative and reporting package

Reporting owners supply the versioned statement, metric links, prior-year comparisons, corrections and assurance questions. Trace each material factual claim to evidence and check that narrative does not overstate targets as delivered outcomes. Freeze the approved package and compare the saved version with the reviewed artifact. Reopen on substantive edits; a prior approval does not cover changed figures automatically.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Source extract excludes a facility: retain the missing facility and assign collection; do not silently lower the total population.
- Missing data is entered as zero: restore its missing status and reassess affected metrics before using the total.
- Units or periods differ: normalize under an approved method and preserve the original values; do not add incomparable quantities.
- A conversion factor changes: record version, justification and effect on comparatives; route the reporting treatment for approval.
- An important topic lacks data: keep materiality and data availability separate; management resolves disclosure treatment.
- A tool fails before calculation: record `not_tested`; do not manufacture a metric.
- A policy describes a future target as achieved: separate commitment from demonstrated outcome and correct the draft.
- A revised standard is only adopted: verify its legal application before removing previously applicable disclosures.

## Worked handoff example

A fictional energy metric covers three sites. Two provide period-matched records; the third is blank but the workbook treats it as zero. The assertion that the metric includes valid inputs from all three sites is `not_supported`. The complete total remains `inconclusive` until the owner supplies data or an approved estimation treatment. Correct the missing-value handling, retain the original workbook and recalculate. Do not label the reduced total an efficiency improvement.

## Cadence and renewal

Align data collection and review with the financial/reporting calendar and assurance milestones. Reopen affected work after entity, method, source or materiality changes. Reconcile comparative periods and track source changes before the next cycle; a new template does not validate old data.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the approved basis, full disclosure ledger, materiality decisions, evidence and calculation index, corrected statement, unresolved limitations and exact approval/assurance records. Publication requires the named authorization and readback of the published artifact. Independent source, engagement, skeptical and rights review remain pending for this guide. No compliance or assurance claim follows from structural checks.
