# Canadian Program for Cyber Security Certification Level 1 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Government of Canada text, a certification determination, a contract-eligibility decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current applicability through the [Canadian Program for Cyber Security Certification Level 1 criteria](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting/cyber-security-certification-defence-suppliers-canada/cyber-security-certification-level1.html) and applicable contract authority.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a supplier-readiness record that connects contract and program scope, organization and system ownership, applicable criteria identified by authorized people, implementation evidence, assessment records, exceptions, corrective actions, management decisions, renewal dates, and official-source change monitoring. Keep eligibility, certification, and bid decisions separate from readiness evidence. Record what was observed, its source, the period covered, and unresolved uncertainty so that a reviewer can test the record without treating it as a self-certification.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Authorized business leaders, contract owners, security, technology, operations, procurement, legal, and evidence owners retain accountable decisions. Program owners coordinate the readiness record; system owners maintain operating evidence; authorized signatories make any declaration or submission; and auditors evaluate evidence in their independent role. Independent reviewers sample scope, evidence, assessment activity, corrective actions, and change records, challenging missing systems, unsupported implementation claims, stale evidence, and unapproved representations.

AI may organize authorized evidence metadata, identify missing owners or review fields, compare approved criteria records with retained artifacts, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot determine program applicability, assess or certify an organization, make a contract-eligibility decision, submit a declaration, accept risk, alter evidence, or claim compliance. Reassess before material contract, system, supplier, program, incident, or scope changes; review readiness routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Canadian Program for Cyber Security Certification Level 1 criteria](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting/cyber-security-certification-defence-suppliers-canada/cyber-security-certification-level1.html), the applicable contract, and authorized program direction; checked 2026-07-31. This plan requests original readiness evidence and does not reproduce program criteria, determine eligibility, create a certification result, or authorize a declaration.

### 1. Contract, organization, and system-scope package

- **Request and owner:** Contract/program applicability record, organization ownership chart, in-scope system/service inventory, supplier and shared-service register, evidence-owner roster, and scope-change log from contract, security, technology, and procurement owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected system or service to its accountable owner, contract/program scope assumption, supplier/shared-service dependency, and supporting inventory record. This supports a readiness fact base; it cannot decide contract eligibility or program applicability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventories and flag an unowned scope change. Contract/security humans approve scope and applicability decisions. Refresh before a bid/declaration and after a contract, system, supplier, or scope change.

### 2. Implemented-practice and operating-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Human-selected criterion mapping references, implementation descriptions in original language, configuration/change evidence, access/operations records, training or review evidence, and known-gap register from system and security owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected claimed practice to dated operational evidence, responsible owner, evidence location, limitation, and corrective action. This can show evidence availability; it cannot assess the organization, prove effectiveness, or conclude that any program criterion is satisfied.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved evidence metadata and identify stale or missing records. Human security and operations owners judge sufficiency and direct remediation. Recollect after material configuration, access, incident, or operating-model change.

### 3. Assessment, corrective-action, and declaration-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Independent-review workpapers, gap and corrective-action register, retest evidence, management-review decisions, source-change watch, declaration/submission authority record, and retained official receipts where applicable.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected gap or readiness assertion to source evidence, reviewer challenge, accountable decision, target date, retest, and unresolved limitation. This supports governed preparation; it cannot certify, accept risk, submit a declaration, or make a contract representation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag overdue actions and prepare a non-authoritative review packet. Authorized humans approve corrective-action closure, representations, declarations, and submissions. Conduct annual independent review and re-evaluate after program or contract changes.
