# NIST SP 800-76-2 PIV biometrics engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST biometric specifications, biometric collection instructions, identity-proofing advice, an authentication decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-76-2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/76/2/final) and the organization’s approved identity, security, privacy, legal, accessibility, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a governed lifecycle for in-scope PIV biometric collection, preparation, storage, authentication, device or modality use, accuracy monitoring, privacy handling, exceptions, and retirement. Maintain traceability from authorized use case through data source, collection event, approved system or device, quality and performance evidence, access controls, retention, disclosure, incident handling, and accountable owner. A successful biometric match, quality score, or device report is evidence to assess; it does not prove identity, authentication appropriateness, fairness, privacy compliance, or that an access decision is correct.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, identity and access management, security, privacy, legal, human resources or sponsor function, accessibility, physical-security or facility owners where relevant, endpoint or device management, procurement, supplier-management, incident-response, and records-management roles. Operators maintain an in-scope biometric-use inventory, approved purpose and modality records, collection and access evidence, device and system baselines, quality and performance reviews, privacy and retention artifacts, exception decisions, incident records, supplier assurance, and retirement evidence. Reconcile use cases, systems, device populations, and data stores quarterly; review performance, failures, access, retention, exceptions, supplier notices, and unresolved risks at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material system, modality, population, privacy, legal, accessibility, or threat changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples use-to-data-to-access-to-retention traceability; auditors test the evidence trail without collecting biometrics, determining identity, granting access, changing biometric settings, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied inventory, performance, privacy, retention, and review evidence, identify missing ownership or stale decisions, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot collect or process biometrics, determine identity, authenticate or deny a person, grant access, change biometric configuration, make a legal or fairness conclusion, accept risk, attest for management, or replace independent review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [NIST SP 800-76-2 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/76/2/final) and approved identity, security, privacy, legal, accessibility, and operational decisions; checked 2026-07-31. This is original evidence-planning language, not a biometric collection instruction, an identity-proofing procedure, an authentication decision, or a conformance claim. Authorized human roles decide biometric use, access, and legal/privacy treatment.

### 1. Biometric purpose, modality, and data-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Identity, privacy, legal, accessibility, security, and sponsor owners provide the approved use-case inventory, purpose and population records, modality/device/system boundaries, data-flow and storage references, collection authority records, retention/disclosure decisions, accessibility accommodations, and accountable owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one approved use case to its purpose, population, modality/device/system boundary, data-flow reference, privacy/retention decision, accessibility consideration, owner, and review date. This checks governance linkage; it cannot collect biometrics, determine identity, establish consent or legal authority, prove fairness, or validate a biometric match.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied non-biometric metadata and flag missing purpose, retention, or ownership links. Humans approve use, collection authority, data handling, accommodations, and changes; AI cannot receive, store, or process biometric samples. Refresh after a population, purpose, modality, system, privacy, legal, or accessibility change.

### 2. Device, quality, and authentication-operation package

- **Request and owner:** Physical security, endpoint/device management, identity operations, security, and vendor owners provide approved device/system baselines, calibration or maintenance references where maintained, quality/performance review summaries, authentication-failure categories, administrative-access controls, deployment/change records, and incident/escalation paths.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one device/system baseline, quality review, or controlled operational exercise through its approved configuration record, owner, performance summary, access boundary, change/maintenance record, and follow-up. This cannot operate a sensor, assess a biometric template, authenticate or deny a person, prove accuracy, or establish appropriate access decisions.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify stale baselines, missing review dates, or anomalous aggregate trends from approved summaries. Humans decide configuration, maintenance, authentication operations, investigation, remediation, and access; AI cannot alter biometric settings or make authentication decisions. Refresh after device, software, deployment, failure trend, or supplier change.

### 3. Retention, incident, exception, and retirement package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, records, legal, incident-response, supplier-management, and operations owners provide retention/disposal schedules, access/disclosure logs or summaries, incident records, exception/accommodation decisions, vendor notices, decommissioning evidence, corrective actions, and annual management-review records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected retention review, access/disclosure inquiry, incident, exception, or retirement event to its source record, accountable owner, decision path, corrective action, and closure/retention evidence. This cannot determine reporting duty, legal compliance, appropriate accommodation, deletion effectiveness, or residual risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize minimized records and flag overdue disposal, unresolved exceptions, or absent retirement owners. Humans decide notification, disclosure, exceptions, remediation, risk acceptance, and disposal verification. Review quarterly and after an incident, retention rule change, vendor notice, exception trend, or system retirement.
