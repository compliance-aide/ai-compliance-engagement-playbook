# ISO/IEC 27403:2024 smart-home IoT engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a smart-home design decision, a privacy determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27403:2024 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/78702.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a smart-home IoT engagement record connecting home devices and hubs, user and household data flows, accountable service owners, interfaces, supplier dependencies, security and privacy evidence, lifecycle changes, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Keep AI assistance confined to authorized evidence organization and question preparation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product, engineering, security, privacy, operations, legal, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; service and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test data-flow traceability, consumer-impact boundaries, lifecycle readiness, and exception handling. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot approve architecture or data uses, determine security or privacy sufficiency, accept risk, alter production configurations, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review material product and service changes continuously, conduct quarterly reviews, and reassess annually and before material interface, supplier, data, incident, or privacy changes.
