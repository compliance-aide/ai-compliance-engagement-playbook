# ISO/IEC 27035 incident management — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or an incident determination. Check the [ISO series records](https://www.iso.org/standard/78973.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain incident criteria, intake, triage, decision authority, investigation records, communications, evidence preservation, recovery actions, lessons, and recurring-risk records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Incident leaders make classifications, notification, and recovery decisions; independent reviewers test the case record. AI correlates timelines and drafts questions, but cannot decide reportability or direct containment. Exercise quarterly and review annually.

### 1. Intake, triage, and authority package

- **Request and owner:** Incident-response leadership provides intake channels, escalation criteria, on-call/decision roles, case records, classification rationale, and declared communications authorities.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected case from intake to human triage, evidence reference, accountable decision-maker, and escalation path. This supports case-record traceability; it cannot validate classification accuracy, reportability, or timeliness in every event.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may correlate timestamps and flag missing owners or milestones. Humans classify events and decide escalation. Refresh after a material incident, organizational change, or a recurring intake failure.

### 2. Investigation, containment, and recovery record package

- **Request and owner:** Incident, security, legal, and service owners provide evidence-preservation references, investigation timeline, containment/recovery approvals, operational actions, customer/partner handoffs, and restoration evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected incident from authorized action through preserved evidence, decision record, operational result, and known exception. This supports an auditable account of the sampled response; it cannot direct containment, establish root cause, or prove recovery outside the observed scope.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble a read-only timeline and identify missing records; it may not alter systems, decide legal obligations, or authorize recovery. Recollect after an incident, exercise, evidence-preservation failure, or material response change.

### 3. Lessons, corrective action, and executive oversight package

- **Request and owner:** Incident and executive owners provide post-incident reviews, recurring-theme analysis, corrective actions, risk/escalation decisions, retest records, and management-review minutes.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected lesson to a named owner, corrective action, target date, retest or verification evidence, and management follow-up. This supports accountable improvement; it cannot close a corrective action, accept risk, or replace independent review.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft a source-linked lessons packet and flag overdue actions. Humans approve risk decisions, closure, notifications, and external communications. Review quarterly and after significant events.
