# EU GDPR — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a determination of GDPR
> applicability. Review the [official regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng),
> national law, and regulator guidance with qualified privacy counsel.

## Engagement focus

Map purposes, data categories, roles, systems, locations, recipients, and
transfers. Maintain transparent notices and documented rationale for processing;
minimize collection and retention; operate rights-request and complaint
workflows; test security and breach response; govern processors and
subprocessors; assess higher-risk change early; and preserve accountable records.

## Roles

Controllers own processing purposes, means, and accountability. Processors act
under documented instructions and contractual terms. Privacy and legal
professionals decide consequential interpretations. Independent reviewers test
traceability and the evidence supporting those decisions. AI may help with authorized inventory,
evidence tracing, deadlines, plain-language drafting, and gap triage; it cannot
determine lawful basis, reportability, an individual's rights outcome, or approve a transfer or processing decision.

## Annual rhythm

Review processing and transfers; inspect notices, contracts, retention, and
vendor changes; test rights, incident, and security processes; refresh risk and
impact decisions after material change; and carry incident learnings into the
following cycle.
