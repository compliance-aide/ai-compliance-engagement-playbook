# Shared Assessments SIG licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for authorized users only, not SIG content, a supplier rating, or a due-diligence conclusion. Confirm entitlement and current terms through the [Shared Assessments SIG page](https://sharedassessments.org/sig/).

## Engagement focus

Verify license and version; maintain supplier scope, questionnaire custody, response provenance, evidence references, review status, exceptions, remediation, and decision records through the annual third-party lifecycle.

## Roles and annual rhythm

The licensee controls authorized SIG access and content; third-party risk owners set scope; suppliers provide responses; independent reviewers validate the supporting evidence. AI may organize organization-owned process evidence and track status, but cannot receive, copy, edit, map, score, or distribute licensed SIG questions or responses, or approve a supplier decision. Revalidate entitlement, scope, and supplier change triggers regularly, with human approval for supplier decisions.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Shared Assessments SIG page](https://sharedassessments.org/sig/), the licensee's current agreement, and authorized program materials; checked 2026-07-31. This is original third-party-risk process planning, not SIG content, a completed questionnaire, a supplier score, or a due-diligence conclusion. Keep licensed questions, response text, proprietary mappings, and supplier-confidential material in the authorized program environment.

### 1. Entitlement, supplier scope, and decision-authority package

- **Request and owner:** The license administrator, third-party risk, procurement, privacy, security, and business owners provide entitlement/version confirmation, authorized-user roster, supplier inventory, scope rationale, criticality/tiering decision record, intake/change triggers, and accountable decision roster.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier review to a current entitlement record, named scope owner, approved supplier identity and tier, decision authority, and review date. This can support process traceability; it cannot establish that SIG applies, determine supplier risk, or authorize access to licensed content.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index organization-owned metadata and flag missing ownership, stale reviews, or unrecorded supplier changes. Humans confirm entitlement, scope, supplier tier, and decisions. Refresh after license/version, supplier, service, data-use, ownership, or materiality change.

### 2. Response-provenance, evidence-reference, and exception package

- **Request and owner:** Authorized supplier-review and evidence-custodian owners provide questionnaire custody/location reference, response provenance and submission-status metadata, permitted evidence-reference index, reviewer notes, exception/risk-treatment records, remediation owner, milestone, and expiry records—without exporting licensed questions, answers, or proprietary mappings.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception or evidence reference to its authorized repository location, source owner, review date, documented limitation, human decision, and follow-up record. This can show controlled handling and accountable follow-up; it cannot validate a response, score a supplier, or conclude diligence is sufficient.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved metadata, dates, owners, and remediation status, but cannot ingest or transform licensed questionnaire content or supplier-confidential evidence outside authorization. Humans review source material and approve exceptions. Recollect after a material response change, evidence expiry, overdue action, or review challenge.

### 3. Lifecycle monitoring, remediation, and independent-challenge package

- **Request and owner:** Third-party risk, supplier-management, security, privacy, procurement, and independent-review owners provide periodic monitoring events, supplier-change notices, remediation updates, reassessment decisions, closure evidence, escalation records, and independent challenge results.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a supplier lifecycle event through change detection, accountable triage, documented decision, required action, deadline, and re-review or closure record. This supports evidence of lifecycle governance; it cannot certify supplier security, accept risk, or replace independent judgment.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag upcoming reviews and inconsistent status metadata. Humans decide escalation, acceptance, reassessment, and closure; independent reviewers challenge evidence sufficiency. Review at least annually and after supplier incidents, ownership/service/data changes, material findings, or program changes.
