# Shared Assessments SIG licensed-program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for authorized users only, not SIG content, a supplier rating, or a due-diligence conclusion. Confirm entitlement and current terms through the [Shared Assessments SIG page](https://sharedassessments.org/sig/).

## Engagement focus

Verify license and version; maintain supplier scope, questionnaire custody, response provenance, evidence references, review status, exceptions, remediation, and decision records through the annual third-party lifecycle.

## Roles and annual rhythm

The licensee controls authorized SIG access and content; third-party risk owners set scope; suppliers provide responses; independent reviewers validate the supporting evidence. AI may organize organization-owned process evidence and track status, but cannot receive, copy, edit, map, score, or distribute licensed SIG questions or responses, or approve a supplier decision. Revalidate entitlement, scope, and supplier change triggers regularly, with human approval for supplier decisions.
