# NIST SP 800-34 Revision 1 — contingency-planning engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an operational-recovery declaration. See [NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain system/service inventory, impact, dependency, recovery objectives, backups, alternate procedures, restoration, exception, and test evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans authorize recovery; independent reviewers sample immutable test lineage. AI correlates inventory and artifacts, but cannot declare recoverability or perform production recovery. Review quarterly and annually.
