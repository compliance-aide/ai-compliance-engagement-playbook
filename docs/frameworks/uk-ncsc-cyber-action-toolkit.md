# UK NCSC Cyber Action Toolkit engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NCSC toolkit content, a security certification, a technical configuration instruction, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [NCSC Cyber Action Toolkit](https://cybertoolkit.service.ncsc.gov.uk/) and the organization’s approved operational and risk decisions.

## Engagement focus

For sole traders, micro businesses, and small organizations, maintain a practical cyber-resilience record that turns prioritized actions into owned, verifiable work. Connect the organization’s services, accounts, devices, data, suppliers, and people to selected actions, completion evidence, exceptions, recovery readiness, and follow-up work. Treat toolkit progress as a learning and improvement aid, not as proof of security, certification, or a substitute for accountable risk decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign an accountable owner and, where available, technology, security, data, finance, supplier, and service-owner roles. Operators maintain a simple service and account inventory, chosen action plan, completion and validation evidence, access and recovery records, supplier contacts, exceptions, incident notes, and improvement backlog. Review priority actions and open exceptions monthly; exercise account recovery, incident contact, and essential-service continuity at least annually and after material device, service, provider, staffing, or threat changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples completed actions and recovery evidence; auditors test the evidence trail without configuring systems, accepting risk, deciding legal obligations, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied evidence, flag stale action records or missing owners, help summarize progress, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot configure systems, approve actions, accept risk or an exception, determine legal obligations, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** At engagement start, retain retrieval date, current-status check, and applicable use terms for the official [NCSC Cyber Action Toolkit](https://cybertoolkit.service.ncsc.gov.uk/). Source status is current when the official service is available. These are original evidence packages, not toolkit content, a configuration instruction, certification method, or claim that the selected actions fit every organization. A named human selects priorities and approves risk decisions.

### 1. Small-organization scope and priority-action package

- **Request and owner:** Accountable owner plus technology, security, service, data, and finance owners provide a concise services/accounts/devices inventory, nominated priorities, selected action plan, accountable owners, risk or exception log, and dates for review.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one selected priority from a stated business or service dependency through owner assignment, planned action, and documented rationale or exception. This can support a planning-traceability observation; it cannot decide priorities, establish applicability, or prove the organization is secure.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize supplied lists and flag missing owner/date combinations. Humans choose actions, set budget and scope, accept exceptions, and approve risk treatment. Refresh monthly and after a material service, device, provider, staffing, or threat change.

### 2. Account, device, and service-protection package

- **Request and owner:** Technology, service, account, device, and security owners provide approved access practices, account-recovery records, update/backup or protection evidence, configuration/change records, supplier-admin records, and time-bounded completion evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Select one essential account, device group, or service and inspect the supplied completion record, owner, recovery path, and a recent validation or change record without altering the environment. This can test the evidence for that sample; it cannot verify every configuration, guarantee recovery, or establish operating effectiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify stale validation or missing recovery-owner records. Humans configure systems, grant access, approve changes, and decide remediation. Recollect after an account, device, provider, recovery, or access change.

### 3. Incident, recovery, and supplier-continuity package

- **Request and owner:** Accountable owner, service, supplier, incident, finance, legal, privacy, and communications owners provide essential-contact list, incident notes, recovery test records, backup/restore or continuity evidence, supplier contacts/escalations, exceptions, and improvement backlog.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one test, incident, or service disruption from detection/contact through a responsible decision, recovery or corrective evidence, and follow-up action. This can support a readiness observation; it cannot direct an incident, establish legal notification duties, or prove future continuity.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft timelines and identify overdue follow-up. Humans direct incident handling, approve communications, invoke recovery, and accept risk. Review annually and after an incident, recovery failure, supplier change, or essential-service change.
