# GLBA Safeguards Rule — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or an FTC notification.
> Check the current [FTC Safeguards Rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314)
> and applicable regulator requirements.

## Engagement focus

Confirm regulatory applicability and customer-information scope; maintain a
written program charter, accountable oversight, system/service-provider
inventory, and current risk decisions; incorporate operational and supplier
change; and prepare governance reporting from approved facts and decision logs.

## Roles and annual rhythm

The program owner runs risk treatment and escalates events. Independent reviewers
test evidence coherence and governance reporting, without replacing management.
AI prepares evidence indexes and draft reports but cannot declare safeguards
adequate, approve exceptions, classify regulators, or make notifications. Review
material changes continuously and perform annual governance review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Verify the current [FTC Safeguards Rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314) and applicable regulator direction with qualified counsel; checked 2026-07-31. The regulation is public, but this is original evidence-planning language, not legal advice, a program determination, or an FTC/regulator notification decision.

### 1. Customer-information boundary and program-accountability package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, privacy, security, and business owners provide the human-approved applicability analysis, customer-information/data-flow inventory, covered-system and service inventory, written program ownership, qualified-individual designation, and material scope decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected information flow or service to its owner, boundary decision, program record, and source evidence. This supports a traceable program boundary; it cannot determine legal applicability, inventory completeness, or the adequacy of a program.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile authorized inventories and flag missing ownership. Qualified humans determine scope, program leadership, and legal interpretation. Refresh after a product, data, affiliate, system, or service-provider change.

### 2. Risk, safeguard-operation, and provider package

- **Request and owner:** Security and supplier-management owners provide risk assessment records, chosen-safeguard evidence, access/change/monitoring evidence for the scoped period, service-provider due-diligence records, contract/responsibility inputs, and exception decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect time coverage and access controls, then trace a selected risk or provider to accountable owner, evidence, decision, and follow-up. This supports selected-record governance; it cannot prove effectiveness, determine reasonable safeguards, or validate a supplier.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create an evidence inventory and flag stale assessments or unexpired exceptions; it may not select safeguards, approve a provider, or accept risk. Refresh after a material incident, test result, supplier change, or control failure.

### 3. Incident, reporting, and annual oversight package

- **Request and owner:** Incident, legal, executive, and program owners provide incident logs, investigation/decision records, communications and reporting approvals where applicable, remediation tracking, board/senior-governance reporting, and annual program-review evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected incident or governance issue to its authority, evidence, decision, remediation owner, and subsequent review. This supports oversight traceability; it cannot determine notification/reporting duties, approve communications, or conclude that safeguards are compliant.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a restricted workpaper and flag overdue actions. Authorized humans decide reporting, communications, exceptions, funding, and closure. Refresh after a covered incident, material management finding, regulatory change, or annual governance review.
