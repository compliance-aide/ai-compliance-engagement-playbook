# ISO/IEC 27009:2020 sector-specific ISMS application — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a sector-framework approval. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/73907.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

For teams creating or maintaining sector-specific security programs, maintain scope rationale, stakeholder inputs, source/version records, compatibility reviews, governance approvals, evidence of consultation, change history, and publication decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Qualified framework stewards and accountable governance bodies decide sector interpretation and publication; independent reviewers challenge compatibility evidence. AI tracks versions and drafts comparison questions, but cannot derive controls, establish sector requirements, or approve a framework. Review on source changes and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
