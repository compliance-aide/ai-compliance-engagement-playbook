# CIS Controls v8.1 Implementation Group 2 engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CIS Controls text, a safeguard selection, a security decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through CIS’s [Implementation Group 2 resource](https://www.cisecurity.org/controls/implementation-groups/ig2).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a scaled-security engagement record that connects enterprise scope, differentiated risk contexts, accountable technology and business owners, specialized operating evidence, prioritization decisions, exceptions, remediation, supplier dependencies, and independent review. Make the evidence process usable across more complex environments without converting AI-generated analysis into a decision about security design or implementation sufficiency.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business leadership, IT, security, privacy, risk, procurement, and service owners retain accountable decisions; evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, prioritization rationale, evidence traceability, and unresolved exceptions. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot choose an implementation group, select or approve safeguards, determine sufficiency, accept risk, alter production configuration, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review operating evidence quarterly, reassess organizational fit annually, and reopen the plan before material asset, service, threat, incident, regulatory, business, or technology changes.
