# IEC PAS 62443-1-6:2025 IIoT engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a connected-device architecture decision, a safety decision, a technology selection, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC PAS 62443-1-6:2025 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/102885).

## Engagement focus

Maintain an IIoT engagement record that connects connected assets and services, accountable owners, data and operational flows, supplier dependencies, safety and process constraints, lifecycle evidence, approved design decisions, exceptions, remediation, and independent review. Preserve a clear boundary between AI-assisted evidence organization and accountable decisions about industrial connectivity, architecture, safety, service continuity, and risk.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, security, product, privacy, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; asset and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test boundary traceability, lifecycle readiness, and safety-aware escalation. AI may organize authorized records, identify stale dependencies or evidence gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot approve a connected architecture, onboard a device or service, select safeguards, decide safety or operational impacts, accept risk, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans or changes, or claim compliance. Review continuously as assets and suppliers change, conduct quarterly operating reviews, and reassess annually and before material device, interface, supplier, safety, incident, or architecture changes.
