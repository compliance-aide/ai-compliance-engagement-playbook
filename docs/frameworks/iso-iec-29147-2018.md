# ISO/IEC 29147:2018 vulnerability disclosure — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a public disclosure decision. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72311.html) and current version status; do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain public intake routes, reporter communication, affected-product records, coordination owners, disclosure decisions, remediation communications, exceptions, timelines, and post-incident improvements.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product security and legal owners decide disclosure and communication; independent reviewers inspect responsiveness and evidence. AI groups duplicate reports and drafts status updates, but cannot disclose a vulnerability or make promises. Exercise intake quarterly and review annually.
