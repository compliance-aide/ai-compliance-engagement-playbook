# Peru personal-data protection — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the [Peruvian authority’s regulatory hub](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/anpd/normas-legales) for current materials.

## Engagement focus

Maintain data handling, rights, notice, consent, security-event, marketing, processor, transfer, and accountable-role evidence. Reassess workflows annually and refresh evidence quarterly after service or guidance change.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain services and processor records; independent reviewers trace workflows to configuration and contract proof. AI locates stale attestations and drafts audit packets, but cannot appoint roles, decide rights requests, make legal conclusions, or file externally.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm current material through the [Peruvian authority’s regulatory hub](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/anpd/normas-legales) and qualified Peruvian privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide consent, a processing condition, database registration, international transfer, a rights outcome, or external notification.

### 1. Data-use, notice, and consent record

- **Request and owner:** Privacy and business owners maintain a processing register of data uses, categories, collection sources, systems, purposes, notices, consent/authorization artifacts where used, accountable owner, recipients, retention, and database/registration assessment inputs.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace selected data uses to collection point, approved notice/version, system, owner, recipient, and retention/disposal record. This can substantiate factual lineage; it cannot determine notice sufficiency, consent validity, or registration obligations.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved inventory fields and identify stale artifacts. Privacy/legal humans decide status and scope. Refresh before a new data use, collection channel, recipient, marketing activity, or material release.

### 2. Rights, security, and event record

- **Request and owner:** Redacted rights/complaint log, identity-check procedure, correction/deletion evidence, access/security operations, incident/exercise timeline, remediation, and training record from service, privacy, and security owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an individual case and an incident exercise to source system, authorized decision maker, action, escalation, and closure. This cannot decide a rights request, authenticate an individual, assess security adequacy, or determine notification.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble redacted evidence and gap questions, not communicate externally. Recollect after complaint, incident, failed exercise, security change, or operational remediation.

### 3. Processor, transfer, and annual governance record

- **Request and owner:** Processor/vendor register, contract/instruction evidence, sharing and transfer review, exception/risk decisions, corrective-action/retest, management review, and regulatory-change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a sampled processor or transfer route to evidence, human authority, instruction/contract, expiry, and corrective action. This supports review; it cannot authorize a processor, transfer, or exception, or attest compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag expiring records and prepare a review packet. Privacy, legal, supplier, and management owners approve decisions. Review annually and after vendor, transfer, or regulatory change.
