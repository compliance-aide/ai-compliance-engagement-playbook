# APRA CPS 230 operational-risk management — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not APRA content or a prudential conclusion. Confirm current applicability at the [APRA CPS 230 record](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-230).

## Engagement focus

Maintain operational-risk inventory, critical operations, disruption scenarios, internal controls, monitoring, remediation, material-service-provider records, test evidence, exceptions, and governance decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Accountable executives and boards retain operational-risk decisions; independent reviewers assess evidence separately. AI coordinates approved records and flags stale dependencies, but cannot classify critical operations, accept risk, or make regulatory representations. Test significant scenarios and review annually.
