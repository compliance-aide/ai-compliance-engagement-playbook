# California CCPA/CPRA — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not California legal advice. Consult current
> [California regulations](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/) and qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Confirm applicability and roles, maintain a data/use/disclosure map and
consumer-notice/choice workflow, monitor rights and vendor-change evidence,
assess current regulatory requirements before material change, exercise incident
and request escalation, and track management-approved remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators verify facts and implement approved notices and contract positions.
Independent reviewers test evidence integrity and escalation governance. AI
assembles review packets and detects stale notices; it cannot decide applicability,
consumer outcomes, data classifications, or filings. Review quarterly and after
regulatory or business changes.
