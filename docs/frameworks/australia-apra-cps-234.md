# APRA CPS 234 information-security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not APRA text, a prudential determination, incident-notification decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current applicability through [APRA CPS 234](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-234) and qualified regulatory counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain an information-security accountability record that connects information assets, material dependencies, accountable owners, threat and vulnerability evidence, safeguards, assurance and testing records, incidents, decision paths, remediation, external reporting decisions, and review dates. Preserve asset criticality and sensitivity decisions with their sources and limitations. Make assurance evidence traceable to the assets and safeguards it supports, while distinguishing observation, inference, and approved conclusion.

## Roles and annual rhythm

The board, senior management, security, risk, technology, privacy, operations, procurement, and information owners retain accountable decisions. Information owners maintain asset context; security owners coordinate assurance; incident authorities make escalation and reporting decisions; and auditors evaluate evidence in their independent role. Independent reviewers sample paths from asset understanding through safeguards, testing, observations, governance review, remediation, and retest, challenging unsupported capability claims, stale evidence, blind spots, and unverified closure.

AI may organize authorized asset and evidence metadata, flag aging tests or missing ownership fields, compare approved expectations with retained records, draft factual questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot classify an asset, determine safeguard effectiveness, decide materiality, direct reporting, approve a risk decision, alter source evidence, or claim compliance. Reassess before material asset, threat, supplier, system, incident, or regulatory changes; review security evidence routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the current [APRA CPS 234 page](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-234) and applicable organization records; checked 2026-07-31. APRA is the authoritative source for current prudential material. This original plan does not reproduce the standard, assess materiality, decide notification, or make a prudential compliance claim; accountable security, risk, legal, and management owners decide those matters.

### 1. Information-asset accountability package

- **Request and owner:** Asset/information register, business purpose, sensitivity/criticality inputs, accountable owner, system and provider relationships, and lifecycle/change records from information, technology, and procurement owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected information asset from inventory to accountable owner, supporting system/provider, and current review record; record unresolved boundary questions. This supports asset-evidence traceability, not an asset classification or completeness conclusion.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify missing approved fields and reconcile record links. Humans classify information and approve ownership decisions. Refresh after an acquisition, new data use, provider, system change, or material threat change.

### 2. Control, assurance, and incident-operation package

- **Request and owner:** Approved safeguard descriptions, configuration/test evidence, vulnerability and assurance records, incident exercise/timeline, escalation decisions, and remediation/retest records from security and operations owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected safeguard from its asset context through time-bounded test or observation evidence to an issue/remediation record. This can support a factual observation for the sampled period; it cannot establish control effectiveness, materiality, or notification duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized evidence and flag age or absent ownership; it may not alter systems or direct incident handling. Recollect after incidents, significant findings, supplier changes, or evidence-period rollover.

### 3. Security governance and assurance package

- **Request and owner:** Management/board review inputs, security-risk decisions, exceptions, independent assurance results, remediation status, and regulatory/source-change log from accountable management and risk owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception or assurance observation to authority, limitation, due date, and retest. This supports governance traceability; it cannot accept risk or substitute for independent assurance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a redacted, read-only review packet. Humans approve risk, reporting, funding, and closure. Revisit annually and after material risk, regulatory, or assurance change.
