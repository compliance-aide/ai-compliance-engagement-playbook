# APRA CPS 234 information security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not APRA content or a prudential conclusion. Confirm current text through the [APRA CPS 234 publication](https://www.apra.gov.au/system/files/cps_234_july_2019_for_public_release.pdf).

## Engagement focus

Maintain information-asset records, security capability evidence, threat and vulnerability decisions, incident readiness, testing records, third-party security evidence, weaknesses, remediation, and accountable approvals.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Information-security and accountable executives decide program priorities and incident responses; independent reviewers test evidence. AI indexes approved records and drafts gap questions, but cannot assess security adequacy, approve controls, or report to APRA. Monitor continuously and review annually.
