# NIST SP 800-218A — federal software-provider engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST content or a federal attestation. Check the [NIST publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/a/final); use the procuring agency's current terms for any submission.

## Engagement focus

Maintain product scope, supplier relationships, secure-development evidence, exception decisions, release traceability, remediation history, and the records supporting any customer-requested representation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Engineering and product leaders validate operating facts; authorized officers decide whether any representation is appropriate. AI assembles traceability and identifies missing evidence, but cannot attest, sign, or submit. Review releases continuously and reassess annually.
