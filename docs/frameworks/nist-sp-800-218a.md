# NIST SP 800-218A federal software-provider engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST content, legal advice, a federal attestation, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [NIST SP 800-218A publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/a/final), and use the procuring agency's current terms for any submission.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a federal software-provider engagement record that connects product scope, accountable product and supplier owners, secure-development evidence, authorized exception decisions, release traceability, vulnerability and remediation history, customer-requested representations, and independently reviewable workpapers. Preserve the distinction between organizing evidence and deciding whether an authorized representative may make a submission or representation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Engineering, product, security, legal, and authorized business officials retain accountable decisions; product and supplier owners preserve lifecycle evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify missing evidence, correlate approved product facts, and draft non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine contract applicability, make an attestation decision, sign or submit a representation, accept risk, approve a release or supplier, alter production services, or claim compliance. Review releases continuously, conduct a formal annual review, and reassess before material product, supplier, vulnerability, contract, or regulatory changes.
