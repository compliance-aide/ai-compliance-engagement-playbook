# NIST SP 800-82 Revision 3 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not operational-technology safety approval.
> Consult the [NIST OT security publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final)
> with site operations and safety leadership.

## Engagement focus

Start with safety, physical-process, and operating constraints. Maintain an
OT-aware asset and architecture record; prioritize risk by physical consequence;
evaluate safeguards only through safe, authorized methods; review maintenance,
remote support, recovery, and vendor access; and exercise non-disruptive
scenarios.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, maintenance, and IT own operational truth
and may halt work that threatens safe operation. Independent reviewers make unassessed areas
explicit. AI may analyze approved records for staleness, prepare interview and
tabletop material, and flag missing safety approvals; it cannot control OT,
initiate scans, alter configuration, or make a safety determination. Review throughout the year with annual
operations-and-safety signoff.
