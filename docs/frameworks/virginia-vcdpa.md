# Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the live [Virginia Code CDPA chapter](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title59.1/chapter53/) at planning time.

## Engagement focus

Counsel records changes; operators refresh data, purpose, recipient, retention, sensitive-data, rights, appeals, notices, consent, opt-out, assessment, and processor evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy owners operate workflows; independent reviewers trace disclosures to systems and remediation. AI reconciles inventories and drafts test plans, but cannot decide applicability, authenticate requesters, approve risk, or submit anything. Review quarterly and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
