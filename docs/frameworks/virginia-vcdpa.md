# Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the live [Virginia Code CDPA chapter](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title59.1/chapter53/) at planning time.

## Engagement focus

Counsel records changes; operators refresh data, purpose, recipient, retention, sensitive-data, rights, appeals, notices, consent, opt-out, assessment, and processor evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy owners operate workflows; independent reviewers trace disclosures to systems and remediation. AI reconciles inventories and drafts test plans, but cannot decide applicability, authenticate requesters, approve risk, or submit anything. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the live [Virginia Code CDPA chapter](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title59.1/chapter53/) and qualified Virginia privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide applicability, controller/processor status, a processing basis, consent, exemption, or consumer-request outcome.

### 1. Processing, purpose, and recipient package

- **Request and owner:** Processing inventory, data-category and sensitive-data flags, purpose/retention record, system and recipient/processor map, privacy-notice versions, and approved change records from privacy, product, data, and procurement owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processing activity to its system, documented purpose, data categories, recipient/processor entry, notice version, and owner. This supports a factual inventory; it cannot decide scope, legal status, or whether a disclosure is permitted.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved metadata and identify gaps between inventory and vendor records. Privacy/legal humans approve classifications and material changes. Refresh for a new purpose, sensitive-data flow, recipient, processor, or product change.

### 2. Consent, rights, and appeal-operation package

- **Request and owner:** Consent/permission configuration evidence where used, rights intake and identity-verification procedure, request/appeal log, response-quality checks, preference records, and training/remediation evidence from privacy and support owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a redacted sample from intake through assigned owner, verification step, source-system search, decision authority, response, and appeal handling. This can evidence an operated workflow; it cannot authenticate a requester, determine an exception, or prove every request was handled correctly.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may produce a de-identified sampling index and flag missing workflow fields. Humans authenticate, decide responses and appeals, and send communications. Recheck after a request trend, workflow defect, or channel change.

### 3. Assessment, processor, and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Human-approved assessment/risk inputs for relevant processing, processor due-diligence and contract records, security test references, exceptions, remediation/retest, management review, and source-change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processor or assessment input to authority, evidence, date, limitation, action owner, and retest. This supports accountable review; it cannot make an assessment conclusion, accept risk, or attest to compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble review packets and flag expired approvals. Legal, privacy, security, and leadership owners approve risk, exceptions, representations, and closure. Review quarterly and renew scope annually.
