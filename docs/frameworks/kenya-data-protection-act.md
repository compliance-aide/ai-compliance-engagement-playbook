# Kenya Data Protection Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Consult the [Kenya Law consolidated record](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng%402022-12-31) and current regulator guidance.

## Engagement focus

Refresh processing and accountable-owner maps, assess changed processing and vendors, test notice, rights, breach, registration, and filing-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own inventories and test artifacts; independent reviewers sample systems and vendors. AI may index authorized evidence and detect changes, but cannot determine legal status, approve data handling or transfers, decide notification obligations, access personal data without authorization, sign off, or take regulator actions. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [Kenya Law consolidated record](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng%402022-12-31) and regulator material, checked 2026-07-31. Verify currency, regulator guidance, transfer/registration conditions, and rights before use with qualified Kenyan counsel. This original plan is not legal text, an applicability decision, or a regulatory filing.

### 1. Processing, data-protection-impact, and owner package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, product, security, and business owners provide processing and data-flow inventories, stated purposes, data categories, systems, controller/processor assumptions, notices, retention inputs, accountable owners, and counsel-reviewed impact-assessment questions for changed processing.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one changed data flow to its source, purpose record, owner, system, notice version, and impact-review input. This supports a factual change record; it cannot determine role, lawful basis, assessment necessity, or processing permissibility.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved inventories with release metadata and flag unreviewed changes. Counsel and accountable humans decide classification and release. Refresh for new categories, sensitive processing, automation, or material changes.

### 2. Rights, access, and retention-operation package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy operations, support, and records owners provide approved request/identity-verification procedures, de-identified request metadata, response approvals, access-review evidence, retention/disposal execution records, and training or quality-review outputs.
- **Validate and limit:** Reperform a redacted request or disposal trace from intake to human escalation, source evidence, action, and closure. This supports selected-workflow traceability; it cannot authenticate a person, decide an exemption, or establish a response meets law.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may generate an approved evidence index and deadline prompts, but may not resolve requests or release data. Authorized humans decide outcomes. Recheck after complaints, missed service targets, or process changes.

### 3. Processor, transfer, breach, and regulator-decision package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, privacy, security, legal, and incident owners provide processor/subprocessor records, contract/instruction references, transfer-review inputs, security and incident timelines, communications approvals, remediation, and filing/notification decision records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected provider change, transfer question, or incident exercise to facts, accountable authority, action, and retest. This supports governance traceability; it cannot approve a transfer, determine breach reportability, or prove provider performance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may surface stale provider reviews and organize redacted workpapers. Humans decide contracts, reporting, external communications, and closure. Refresh after a provider or transfer change, incident, or regulatory update.
