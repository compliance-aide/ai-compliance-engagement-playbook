# Canada ITSP.10.171 specified-information protection engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Canadian security-requirement text, a contractual determination, or an assessment conclusion. Confirm current scope through [ITSP.10.171](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/protecting-specified-information-non-government-canada-systems-and-organizations-itsp10171).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a contract-aware record of specified information, in-scope non-government systems, security-domain decisions, accountable owners, supplier handling, operating evidence, assessment results, changes, and remediation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Government and supplier authorities determine contractual scope; system and security owners retain evidence; independent reviewers test traceability. AI prepares evidence maps and change questions, but cannot determine contract applicability, select security requirements, or declare conformance. Revalidate scope on contract, supplier, and architecture changes, and keep human approvals for external representations.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use [ITSP.10.171](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/protecting-specified-information-non-government-canada-systems-and-organizations-itsp10171), the governing contract or direction, and authorized source material; checked 2026-07-31. This is original evidence-planning guidance, not reproduced Canadian requirements, contract interpretation, a requirement selection, or a conformance conclusion. Contract, legal, and designated security authorities confirm applicability, source rights, and handling limits.

### 1. Contractual information and non-government system-scope package

- **Request and owner:** Contract, data, and system owners provide applicable clauses/directions, specified-information inventory and handling assumptions, non-government system boundary, data-flow and location records, external-party register, and unresolved-scope log.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one documented information flow from receipt through storage, processing, transmission, and return/disposal point to its system, location, external party, and accountable owner. This cannot decide whether information is specified, interpret a contract, or establish complete scope coverage.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved diagrams and inventories for missing handoffs or stale versions. Contract and designated security humans approve applicability, boundary, and handling decisions. Refresh before a new contract, data source, supplier, application, location, or material architecture change.

### 2. Protection-operation and supplier-handling package

- **Request and owner:** System/security operators and supplier managers provide original protection narratives, permitted configuration/process evidence references, account/asset populations, supplier handling commitments, operating-review records, exception register, and evidence-period details.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one in-scope system, account, or supplier handoff from population definition through evidence reference, owner explanation, exception/limitation, and corrective action. A sample does not prove complete implementation or determine that a security requirement is met.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized metadata and flag missing populations, dates, or owners; it cannot alter a system, expose protected information, select compensating measures, or decide sufficiency. Human system, security, and supplier owners direct remediation. Refresh on recurring cadence and after a material access, supplier, incident, or operating-model change.

### 3. Assessment, remediation, and representation-governance package

- **Request and owner:** The assessment owner provides authorized test methods/results, limitations, findings, remediation milestones, risk/exception decisions, retest evidence, source-change watch, and any contract-governed representation draft with named human signer.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one finding or proposed representation to source evidence, reviewer challenge, accountable decision, target date, retest/open status, and residual limitation. This cannot score an assessment, accept risk, approve a plan, or authorize an external representation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag inconsistent dates and prepare controlled evidence indexes. Authorized humans approve remediation priorities, risk decisions, and external representations. Refresh after failed testing, missed milestones, material incidents, source changes, and before a representation.
