# NIST SP 800-84 test, training, and exercise engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST guidance, an operational command, a certification decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-84](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/84/final) and the organization’s approved continuity, incident-response, recovery, and training decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a disciplined test, training, and exercise program for information-technology plans and capabilities that prepares people, validates procedures, reveals dependencies, and tracks corrective action to closure. Connect exercise objectives to material services, realistic scenarios, participants, observer independence, evidence collection, evaluation criteria, post-exercise findings, and accountable remediation. Keep an exercise result separate from a certification, a risk-acceptance decision, or an assertion that an organization will perform successfully in a real event.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, exercise-program, security, technology, business-continuity, incident-command, human-resources or training, legal, communications, supplier-management, and service-owner roles. Operators maintain plan inventories, annual exercise schedules, scope and scenario approvals, participant lists, facilitator and observer records, exercise artifacts, evaluation results, lessons learned, corrective-action records, and closure evidence. Review schedule, scenario coverage, dependencies, and remediation status quarterly; conduct and document representative tests, training, or exercises at least annually and after material plan, service, supplier, architecture, or threat changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples exercise evidence and corrective-action closure; auditors test the evidence trail without directing the exercise, accepting residual risk, approving remediation, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied exercise records, identify missing scenarios or closure evidence, summarize observed patterns, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot select final scenarios alone, direct an exercise, evaluate participant performance as a personnel decision, accept risk, approve remediation, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [NIST SP 800-84 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/84/final) at engagement start and record the version retrieved and applicable use terms. This guide is original engagement planning, not reproduced NIST content, an operational command, a certification decision, or a compliance claim.

### 1. Plan, scope, and exercise-design package

- **Request and owner:** Exercise-program, service, continuity, incident-command, security, training, legal, communications, and supplier owners provide current plans, service/dependency inventory, annual schedule, approved objective and scenario record, participant and observer roles, assumptions, and scope exclusions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one planned event from material service and scenario assumption through accountable sponsor, approved scope, required participants, observer independence, and documented success criteria. This can assess traceability of exercise design; it cannot establish that a scenario is sufficient or that a real event will be handled successfully.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare supplied plans, schedules, and dependencies and flag unsupported scope assertions. Humans approve scenarios, participation, operational safety, and exercise objectives. Refresh after plan, service, dependency, supplier, or threat change.

### 2. Execution, observation, and learning package

- **Request and owner:** Facilitators, technical operators, incident-command, training, and observer owners provide exercise injects or agendas, attendance, execution timeline, communications artifacts, system/test outputs, observer notes, and documented deviations.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an objective through execution evidence, contemporaneous observation, stated limitation, and result record, preserving whether the event was a discussion, training activity, test, or exercise. This cannot rate an individual for employment purposes or prove operational performance under real conditions.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized artifacts and identify missing time, owner, or evidence references; it cannot direct an event, score people, or revise the record. Human facilitators and observers determine conduct and factual observations. Recollect after an aborted exercise, material deviation, or substantive lessons learned.

### 3. Corrective-action, retest, and governance package

- **Request and owner:** Exercise-program, service, risk, and executive owners provide after-action reports, lessons learned, corrective-action register, accountable owners, due dates, exception/risk decisions, completion evidence, retest results, and management review records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected finding from observed condition through human assignment, remediation evidence, retest or documented rationale, remaining limitation, and closure decision. This can support follow-through evidence; it cannot accept residual risk, approve remediation, or certify preparedness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag overdue actions and draft a review packet. Humans approve priorities, risk decisions, closure, and external statements. Review quarterly and after material plan, event, recovery, service, or threat change.
