# OCC Heightened Standards — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or board action. Confirm live scope and rulemaking at the [OCC proposal notice](https://www.occ.treas.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2025/bulletin-2025-51.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain covered-status, board-facing risk governance, strategic, control, escalation, remediation, and rule-change evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Management owns risk information; independent reviewers challenge governance effectiveness. AI prepares evidence maps and alerts, but cannot decide scope, act as board, approve appetite, give legal opinions, or attest. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Plan status:** Draft; independent source and skeptical review required before reliance.

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use current [12 CFR Part 30, appendix D](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30/appendix-Appendix%20D%20to%20Part%2030) and the [OCC's heightened-standards materials](https://www.occ.treas.gov/topics/supervision-and-examination/safety-and-soundness/heightened-standards.html); checked 2026-07-31. Counsel and the bank's authorized governance owners determine applicability, binding status, and interpretation. This plan requests original evidence categories, not board action, regulatory advice, or an attestation.

### 1. Covered-bank, governance, and risk-appetite record

- **Request and owner:** Applicability analysis, board/management committee charters and agendas, risk-appetite and strategic-planning approval records, and accountable risk-management ownership from legal, corporate secretary, risk, and executive owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected material risk or strategic decision to the documented authority, information package, challenge/escalation, and resulting action. This supports that governance records exist; it cannot determine legal applicability or assess the quality of board oversight.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may map approved artifacts to owners and flag overdue reviews. Board members and authorized executives decide appetite and strategy. Refresh for leadership changes, significant strategy/risk shifts, acquisition, or annual review.

### 2. Risk-data, escalation, and control-assessment record

- **Request and owner:** Material risk inventory, risk-data lineage/quality records, control/self-assessment and independent-challenge outputs, escalation criteria, and issue records from risk, data, audit, and control owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected risk indicator or escalation to source data, ownership, challenge, decision, and remediation. This can show traceability; it cannot establish data completeness, risk sufficiency, or independent-audit assurance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved reporting metadata and identify unresolved or unowned items. Humans validate data, challenge management, and approve remediation. Refresh after a material data issue, risk event, or control failure.

### 3. Remediation, succession, and effectiveness-review record

- **Request and owner:** Issue-aging/remediation records, accountability and succession/planning evidence for risk leadership, and periodic effectiveness-review materials owned by management, HR, risk, and internal audit.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample an issue from identification through accountable closure and independently inspect whether stated evidence and approvals are retained. This supports the existence of a management process; it cannot attest that remediation is effective or that governance meets supervisory expectations.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a redacted dependency and aging view; humans accept residual risk, close findings, and communicate with supervisors. Refresh after a failed review, leadership transition, material issue, or annual governance assessment.
