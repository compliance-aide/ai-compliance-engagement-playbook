# Philippines Data Privacy Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a National Privacy Commission conclusion. Consult the [NPC Act and IRR hub](https://privacy.gov.ph/the-data-privacy-act-and-its-irr/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing system, controller/processor, notice, rights, security, breach, sharing, vendor, and privacy-program evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide compliance, notification, and data-subject outcomes; independent reviewers test records. AI identifies missing review dates, but cannot notify the NPC. Review quarterly and annually.
