# Philippines Data Privacy Act — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not a National Privacy Commission conclusion. Consult the [NPC Act and IRR hub](https://privacy.gov.ph/the-data-privacy-act-and-its-irr/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing system, controller/processor, notice, rights, security, breach, sharing, vendor, and privacy-program evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide compliance, notification, and data-subject outcomes; independent reviewers test records. AI identifies missing review dates, but cannot notify the NPC. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm current material through the [NPC Act and IRR hub](https://privacy.gov.ph/the-data-privacy-act-and-its-irr/) and qualified Philippine privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This original plan does not decide controller/processor status, lawful processing, a data-subject outcome, breach notification, or an NPC filing.

### 1. Processing, notice, and accountability record

- **Request and owner:** Privacy owner maintains a processing-system register with personal/sensitive-data categories, purpose, collection channel, data-life-cycle systems, controller/processor roles, notices, consents or other approved basis rationale, sharing, retention, and accountable owner.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace selected processing systems to approved inventory, notice/version, owner, source, recipient, and retention execution. This supports evidence traceability; it cannot determine legitimacy, scope, or a legal basis.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved inventory and flag missing fields. Privacy and legal humans approve role, notice, and processing decisions. Refresh before a material collection, purpose, sharing, system, or retention change.

### 2. Rights, security, and breach-readiness evidence

- **Request and owner:** Rights/complaint log, identity-verification procedure, access-control and security-operation records, privacy-impact/risk inputs where used, incident exercise/timeline, remediation, and training evidence from privacy, security, and service owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a rights case and an incident exercise from intake through authorized review, source system, action, escalation, and closure using redacted workpapers. This cannot decide identity, a request outcome, incident scope, notification, or security sufficiency.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare redacted traceability and stale-evidence prompts; it cannot contact individuals or regulators. Recollect after complaint, incident, failed exercise, control change, or material system release.

### 3. Sharing, processor, and renewal record

- **Request and owner:** Sharing/processor register, contract and instruction evidence, third-party review, transfer context, exception/risk decisions, corrective-action/retest, annual management review, and regulatory-change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected processor or sharing route to owner approval, contract/instruction, current operating evidence, expiry, and corrective action. This can support a human review; it cannot approve sharing, transfers, exceptions, or attest compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may assemble a review packet and flag overdue records. Privacy, legal, supplier, and accountable owners approve decisions and closure. Review annually and upon vendor, transfer, or regulatory change.
