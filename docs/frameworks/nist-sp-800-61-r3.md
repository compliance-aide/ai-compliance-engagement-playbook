# NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 — incident-response engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST content or a reportability decision. Confirm status at the [NIST publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain incident preparation, detection sources, triage records, authority matrix, containment and recovery evidence, communications, lessons, risk integration, and improvement ownership.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Incident leaders decide classification, notification, and containment; independent reviewers test cases and exercises. AI builds timelines and identifies missing records, but cannot make incident, legal, or notification decisions. Exercise quarterly and review annually.
