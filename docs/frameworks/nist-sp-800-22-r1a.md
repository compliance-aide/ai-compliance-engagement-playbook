# NIST SP 800-22 Revision 1a randomness-testing engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST test-suite content, a generator certification, a cryptographic approval, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-22 Revision 1a](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/22/r1/upd1/final) and the organization’s approved cryptography, security, architecture, legal, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Operate a controlled engagement for in-scope statistical testing of random or pseudorandom outputs used in cryptographic contexts. Connect the test purpose and scope to generator and application inventory, approved methodology decisions, input and sampling provenance, execution environment, result handling, interpretation and limitations, remediation tracking, supplier dependencies, changes, incidents, and retained evidence. Statistical results are evidence to assess; they do not by themselves establish unpredictability, cryptographic soundness, a suitable generator, secure operation, or compliance.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Assign accountable executive, security, cryptography or platform engineering, architecture, key-management operations, application and data owners, supplier-management, quality or validation, legal and privacy where relevant, change-management, incident-response, and records-management roles. Operators maintain scoped generator and application inventories, approved test plans and interpretation criteria, input-provenance and execution records, results and limitations, validation environments, supplier materials, exceptions, remediation and incident records, change decisions, and retirement evidence. Reconcile tested generators, owners, applications, dependencies, and suppliers quarterly; review significant results, methodology or implementation changes, exceptions, incidents, and unresolved risks at least quarterly; and complete an annual management review after material generator, cryptographic, product, provider, threat, or publication changes. Before annual renewal, an independent reviewer samples scope-to-test-to-interpretation-to-remediation traceability; auditors test supplied evidence without generating or accessing protected material, changing generator configuration, approving exceptions, accepting risk, or attesting for management.

AI may organize supplied test plans, provenance, results, limitations, remediation, and review evidence, identify missing owners or stale decisions, and draft workpapers for human review. AI cannot select or certify a generator, produce protected random material, change generator configuration, approve an exception, accept risk, make a compliance conclusion, attest for management, or replace independent review.
