# ISO/IEC 27005:2022 — information-security risk engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a risk-acceptance decision. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80585.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain decision authorities, tolerances, assets, services, change, supplier, incident, vulnerability, assumptions, treatment, escalation, and remediation evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans validate likelihood and impact, select treatment, and accept residual risk; independent reviewers test the evidence chain. AI normalizes signals and scenarios, but cannot accept risk. Review quarterly and annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm the applicable edition through the [ISO/IEC 27005:2022 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80585.html) and use an authorized copy only as permitted; checked 2026-07-31. ISO content is licensed. This original plan names evidence categories rather than reproducing requirements, and it makes no risk-acceptance, conformity, or certification claim.

### 1. Risk context and decision-boundary package

- **Request and owner:** Risk owners provide the service and asset context, material assumptions, risk criteria/tolerances, accountable decision roles, and approved assessment scope.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected risk entry to the stated context, owner, criteria, and decision boundary. This supports transparent governance; it cannot show that all material risks were identified or that a tolerance is appropriate.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile inventories and flag absent owners or assumptions. Humans set criteria and scope. Refresh after a material business, technology, supplier, legal, or threat change.

### 2. Assessment, treatment, and exception package

- **Request and owner:** Risk, security, and service owners provide assessment records, evidence references, treatment options, selected actions, milestones, dependencies, and time-bounded exceptions.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected risk from evidence through human assessment, chosen treatment, action owner, and status. This supports traceability of the selected decision; it cannot validate likelihood, impact, residual risk, or treatment effectiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index evidence and highlight overdue actions; it may not score risk, select treatment, or accept an exception. Recollect after a significant finding, missed milestone, or material control/service change.

### 3. Oversight and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Risk leadership provides aggregate themes, escalations, accepted-risk records, review minutes, remediation verification, and improvement decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected escalation or exception to its authority, expiry, mitigation, follow-up, and review record. This supports accountable oversight; it cannot replace management judgment or close a risk decision.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a source-linked review packet and flag expiry dates. Humans approve acceptance, funding, external statements, and closure. Review quarterly and close the annual cycle with documented follow-up.
