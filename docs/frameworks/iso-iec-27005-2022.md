# ISO/IEC 27005:2022 — information-security risk engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a risk-acceptance decision. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/80585.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain decision authorities, tolerances, assets, services, change, supplier, incident, vulnerability, assumptions, treatment, escalation, and remediation evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans validate likelihood and impact, select treatment, and accept residual risk; independent reviewers test the evidence chain. AI normalizes signals and scenarios, but cannot accept risk. Review quarterly and annually.
