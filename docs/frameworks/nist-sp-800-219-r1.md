# NIST SP 800-219 Revision 1 macOS-configuration engagement guide

> This is original operational guidance, not NIST or mSCP content, a configuration baseline, an authorization to change endpoints, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-219 Revision 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/219/r1/final), current platform information, and the organization's approved security, privacy, legal, operational, and change decisions.

## Engagement focus

Manage a year-round engagement for in-scope macOS endpoint configuration and assessment. Maintain the relationship among device populations, operating-system versions, owners, approved baselines, local tailoring decisions, configuration artifacts, deployment tools, assessment outputs, exceptions, changes, support status, and retirement. Treat automated configuration and assessment as a controlled operating capability, not as an automatic proof of secure or compliant endpoints.

Preserve the human rationale behind every baseline selection and local deviation. A generated profile, script output, assessment score, inventory export, management-console report, or remediation ticket is evidence to evaluate against actual scope, version, device state, user impact, and authorized decisions. Keep records of how findings are confirmed, prioritized, changed, deferred, or closed, including where an endpoint is unavailable or cannot safely receive a change.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Endpoint, security, IT operations, system, privacy, legal, accessibility, help-desk, change, asset, and records owners establish scope and make accountable decisions. Operators maintain inventory and ownership, baseline and tailoring records, version coverage, deployment and assessment evidence, exception approvals, remediation history, user-impact records, support status, and disposal evidence. Reconcile device inventory, platform versions, baseline applicability, tool coverage, and exceptions at least quarterly; review new operating-system releases, high-impact findings, and configuration changes as they occur; and perform management review annually.

Independent reviewers sample devices and records from approved baseline through deployment, assessment, exception, and corrective action. Auditors evaluate supplied evidence without changing endpoint settings, running unapproved tools, approving tailoring, accepting risk, authorizing production rollout, or issuing an attestation. AI may organize records, identify stale versions or incomplete evidence, compare supplied results with approved baselines, draft workpapers, and prepare review questions. AI cannot set or approve baselines, alter endpoints, execute remediation, approve exceptions, accept risk, make legal or privacy conclusions, or attest to conformance.
