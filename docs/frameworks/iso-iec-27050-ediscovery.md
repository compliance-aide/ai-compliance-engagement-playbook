# ISO/IEC 27050 electronic discovery — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content, legal advice, or a preservation decision. Check the [ISO/IEC 27050-1 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/78647.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain legal-hold authority, matter scope, data-source inventory, preservation and collection records, access restrictions, review decisions, production approvals, chain-of-custody, and closure evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal and authorized matter owners make preservation and production decisions; independent reviewers verify handling evidence. AI inventories approved records and drafts status questions, but cannot identify legal obligations, collect data, or produce records. Test readiness annually and review on each matter.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [ISO/IEC 27050-1 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/78647.html), applicable legal-hold and records directions, and authorized matter records; checked 2026-07-31. This is original e-discovery handling planning, not ISO text, legal advice, a preservation decision, or a production conclusion. Do not put ISO content, protected matter data, privileged communications, or personal data into AI prompts without approved authority.

### 1. Matter authority, source inventory, and preservation package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, records, privacy, information-governance, security, IT, and matter owners provide matter/hold authority reference, approved scope statement, custodial/data-source inventory, preservation instruction and acknowledgment metadata, source-owner map, retention/hold conflicts, and access-authorization record.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected approved source to matter authority, source/custodian owner, preservation status, authorized access boundary, and review date. This can support handling traceability; it cannot decide legal duty, identify all responsive sources, or authorize collection.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved metadata and flag missing acknowledgments, owners, or preservation dates. Authorized humans decide holds, scope, collection, and legal boundaries. Refresh after a new matter, source/custodian, legal direction, retention conflict, or material data change.

### 2. Collection, processing, and chain-of-custody package

- **Request and owner:** Authorized legal, e-discovery, IT, security, privacy, and vendor owners provide collection authorization, method/tool record, evidence identifiers, transfer/custody log, integrity/quality checks, processing-access restrictions, exception record, and vendor handling record.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample a closed or exercise collection from approval through source identification, documented collection method, transfer/custody, integrity check, restricted access, exception, and handoff record. This can assess record continuity; it cannot collect data, determine admissibility, or validate substantive responsiveness.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied identifiers, dates, and custody metadata and draft redacted workpapers. It cannot access unapproved data, collect records, alter evidence, or decide processing outcomes. Humans approve collection and exceptions. Recollect after a handling failure, vendor change, access exception, or material tool/process change.

### 3. Review, production approval, retention, and closure package

- **Request and owner:** Legal, review, privacy, records, and authorized production owners provide review-workflow authorization, access/restriction records, production-approval reference, privilege/privacy escalation record, retention/disposition decision, matter closure approval, and lessons/remediation evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected review or closure event to its responsible authority, approved process record, stated limitation, production/retention decision point, and follow-up. This can show controlled governance; it cannot identify legal obligations, make privilege determinations, produce records, or decide disposition.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag incomplete status metadata and prepare question lists for human review. Humans decide review, privilege, privacy, production, retention, and closure; independent reviewers test handling traceability. Review per matter, annually for readiness, and after legal, tool, data, vendor, or process changes.
