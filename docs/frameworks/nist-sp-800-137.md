# NIST SP 800-137 continuous-monitoring engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST text, a determination of control effectiveness, a risk decision, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 800-137](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/137/final).

## Engagement focus

Operate an evidence-driven monitoring cadence that connects assets, dependencies, exposure signals, configuration and change records, safeguard observations, source limitations, accountable owners, thresholds approved by accountable people, exceptions, response decisions, and review dates. Define what each signal can and cannot establish. Preserve source, collection time, transformation history, scope, and known gaps so that a recurring report remains testable instead of becoming a stream of unverified alerts.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Security, risk, system, operations, privacy, supply-chain, data, and executive owners retain accountable decisions. Monitoring owners maintain coverage and data-quality records; system owners investigate assigned observations; governance authorities decide risk treatment; and auditors evaluate evidence in their independent role. Independent reviewers trace selected observations from source collection through normalization, review, decision, response, closure, and retained support, challenging coverage claims, missing assets, stale data, weak thresholds, and unsupported effectiveness statements.

AI may organize authorized observation metadata, detect stale reviews or missing source fields, compare approved expectations with available evidence, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot access unapproved systems, alter monitoring telemetry, set a threshold, determine safeguard effectiveness, close an exception, authorize remediation, accept risk, or claim compliance. Reassess monitoring design after material mission, asset, architecture, data, supplier, threat, incident, or risk-tolerance changes; review evidence routinely and conduct an annual independent readiness review.
