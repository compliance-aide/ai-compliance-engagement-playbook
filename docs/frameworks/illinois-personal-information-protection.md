# Illinois Personal Information Protection Act engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Illinois statutory text, legal advice, a breach determination, notice advice, or a compliance claim. Confirm current law through the [Illinois Personal Information Protection Act](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=2702&ChapterID=67) and qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a privacy and incident-readiness record that connects personal-information assets and owners, data flows, providers, safeguards, event intake, investigation evidence, decision authority, notification and regulator-facing records, recovery, and review dates. Preserve a dated source trail for material facts and human decisions. Do not treat an evidence register, automated workflow, or generated notice as a legal conclusion about coverage, an incident, a required action, or a completed obligation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal, privacy, security, incident response, technology, procurement, records, communications, business, and executive owners retain accountable decisions. Information owners maintain lifecycle context; incident leaders coordinate fact collection; counsel directs legal interpretation and notification decisions; authorized officials make submissions; and auditors evaluate evidence in their independent role. Independent reviewers sample data paths and incident exercises through ownership, safeguards, providers, event triage, evidence preservation, decision records, communications, recovery, and lessons learned, challenging missing scope, untested contacts, unsupported timing, and closure without evidence.

AI may organize authorized metadata, flag missing ownership or review fields, draft factual questions, compare evidence with approved workflows, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot determine statutory coverage, decide whether an event is a breach, select notification recipients, calculate legal duties as conclusions, submit filings, give legal advice, transmit personal information, alter evidence, or claim compliance. Reassess before material data, system, provider, incident, contract, or legal changes; exercise incident readiness periodically and conduct an annual independent readiness review.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the live [Illinois Personal Information Protection Act](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=2702&ChapterID=67), checked 2026-07-31, with qualified Illinois counsel. This original plan links to the public statutory source and does not reproduce statutory text or determine coverage, breach status, notice content, timing, recipients, or regulator-facing obligations.

### 1. Personal-information boundary and safeguard package

- **Request and owner:** A human-approved data and system inventory, data-flow diagrams, information-owner assignments, access and encryption configuration references, vulnerability/remediation records, retention/deletion records, and relevant service-provider inventory from privacy, security, engineering, and procurement owners. State the in-scope business units, environments, data stores, and review period.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected data flow from collection or receipt through storage, access roles, transfer, service provider, and disposal evidence; inspect whether dated artifacts are attributable and access-controlled. This supports a factual safeguard and ownership record; it cannot classify information legally, prove every safeguard operated effectively, or establish statutory applicability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved evidence and flag missing owner, system, or review fields. Human privacy, security, and legal owners decide scope, classifications, safeguards, and exceptions. Refresh after a material data flow, system, provider, safeguard, or ownership change.

### 2. Security-event investigation and preservation package

- **Request and owner:** Event intake records, triage criteria, incident timeline, preserved log/export references, access to forensic chain-of-custody records, investigation assignments, scope hypotheses, containment/recovery work records, and counsel-directed decision records from incident-response, security, records, and legal owners. Use de-identified indexes in the public workpaper.
- **Validate and limit:** Reperform a tabletop or trace a closed, de-identified event from intake to evidence preservation, scope updates, human decisions, recovery, and lessons learned; verify that access to sensitive evidence was limited. This supports readiness and traceability, not a conclusion that an event is or is not a breach, that investigation scope is complete, or that a duty was triggered.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may sequence authorized metadata and identify timeline gaps; it cannot interpret facts as a breach, calculate deadlines, communicate externally, or alter evidence. Counsel and authorized incident leaders determine legal analysis, containment, and escalation. Trigger on an event, material logging/forensic change, or exercise finding.

### 3. Human notification-decision and recovery-assurance package

- **Request and owner:** Counsel-approved decision log, contact and communication-owner roster, notice-template governance references, regulator/consumer communication approval records where applicable, recovery validation, corrective-action register, and post-event management review from legal, communications, executive, and security owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a sampled exercise or incident file for source facts, authority, decision date, communication approval, recovery evidence, corrective action, and retest. This supports accountable decision documentation; it cannot approve a notice, prove timing or recipient sufficiency, substitute for counsel, or establish compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft factual checklists and flag missing approvals. Named legal and authorized business officials approve communications, submissions, risk decisions, and closure. Refresh after an incident, contact-path change, legal-source update, remediation completion, or annual readiness review.
