# Texas Cybersecurity Framework engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Texas framework content, an assessment, a maturity rating, or a compliance claim. Confirm current scope and program context through the [Texas Department of Information Resources framework page](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/security-policy-and-planning/texas-cybersecurity-framework).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a cybersecurity engagement record that connects organization scope, accountable leaders, asset and service inventories, risk decisions, operating evidence, incident and recovery evidence, training records, supplier dependencies, remediation, and maturity-review inputs. Preserve distinctions among voluntary framework use, agency-specific obligations, independent assessment, and an organization’s own risk decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive sponsors and information-security owners set the engagement scope; technology and operations teams preserve evidence; risk and legal owners determine obligations; independent reviewers challenge traceability and assessment readiness. AI may organize approved records, identify evidence gaps, and draft questions, but cannot conduct or score an assessment, determine maturity, approve risk, make a government submission, direct production changes, or claim compliance. Review at least annually and after material system, service, supplier, incident, or organizational changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [Texas Department of Information Resources framework page](https://dir.texas.gov/information-security/security-policy-and-planning/texas-cybersecurity-framework), checked 2026-07-31, with organization-approved scope, risk, and governance records. This is original evidence-planning guidance, not Texas framework content, an assessment result, or a maturity conclusion. Named organization and government authorities retain all applicability and assessment decisions.

### 1. Organizational boundary and risk-governance package

- **Request and owner:** Executive sponsor, CISO, enterprise risk, legal, privacy, and business-service owners provide approved scope statement, service and asset inventories, data/sensitivity references, governance and role records, risk-register extracts, accepted-risk decisions, external-obligation assumptions, and exclusions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one material service or risk item from approved boundary to accountable owner, asset/service record, risk decision, and review date. This supports evidence completeness; it cannot determine statutory duties, assess maturity, or validate risk adequacy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile supplied metadata and identify missing owners or review dates. Human leaders approve scope, obligations, risk acceptance, and exceptions. Refresh after reorganization, material service/data change, significant risk, or annual scope review.

### 2. Cybersecurity operation and resilience package

- **Request and owner:** Security, IT/OT operations, engineering, identity, incident-response, continuity, and supplier owners provide policy/process references, access/change/asset records, monitoring and vulnerability records, incident/exercise timelines, recovery evidence, supplier responsibilities, and corrective-action register.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one change, alert/incident, recovery exercise, or remediation item and trace it through source record, accountable owner, approved action, follow-up, and closure/review. This checks documentary traceability; it cannot prove protection effectiveness, resilience, or a maturity rating.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index authorized non-sensitive records and flag absent period coverage or unresolved actions. Humans choose samples, interpret results, approve operational actions, and accept residual risk. Refresh after an incident, recovery exercise, material platform change, or supplier failure.

### 3. Assessment-readiness and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Security governance, internal audit, risk, legal, records, and executive owners provide internal assessment/readiness plans, evidence register and provenance fields, findings and exception records, remediation milestones, leadership-review minutes, source-change log, and communication approval records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected observation or improvement action to its evidence basis, accountable owner, human decision, due date, retest/review record, and current status. This supports improvement management only; it cannot issue an assessment, score maturity, or claim compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a read-only action queue and flag stale evidence or source versions. Named humans approve assessment assertions, closures, external representations, and risk decisions. Refresh following a framework/source change, material finding, assessment cycle, or annual renewal.
