# ISO/IEC 42006:2025 AI management system certification-body engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, an accreditation decision, a certification audit result, or a certification claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 42006:2025 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/42006).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a certification-body engagement record that connects audit competence, impartiality and independence safeguards, client scope, authorized assessment planning, workpaper provenance, findings, review and decision separation, appeals or complaints handling, and independently reviewable quality evidence. Preserve the separation between AI-assisted administration and accountable certification, accreditation, audit, and client-management decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Certification-body leadership, accreditation, competence, impartiality, audit, quality, and decision authorities retain accountable decisions; auditors and technical experts preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and impartiality safeguards. AI may organize authorized work records, flag omissions, draft non-authoritative questions, and prepare quality-review packets, but cannot select or assign auditors, decide competence or impartiality, determine client scope, make findings, decide certification, alter evidence, access client data without authorization, or claim a certification result. Review annually and before material standard, accreditation, competence, client-scope, auditor, complaint, appeal, or program changes.
