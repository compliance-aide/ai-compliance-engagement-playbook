# ISO 27799:2025 health-information security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or clinical, privacy, or certification advice. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/84647.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain care-context scope, information flows, access decisions, workforce roles, supplier dependencies, security events, continuity records, and evidence linking safeguards to patient-information handling.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, security, clinical, and service owners validate operating reality; independent reviewers test evidence separately. AI indexes evidence and flags drift, but cannot make care, privacy, or incident decisions. Review monthly, test quarterly, and assess annually.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Confirm the current edition at the [ISO 27799:2025 record](https://www.iso.org/standard/84647.html); checked 2026-07-31. ISO material is licensed. This original plan identifies operational artifacts without reproducing requirements and makes no care, privacy, security, conformity, or certification determination.

### 1. Care-context scope and information-flow package

- **Request and owner:** Clinical, privacy, security, and service owners provide approved care/service boundaries, information-flow diagrams or inventories, data classifications, role assignments, patient-information access purposes, supplier dependencies, and material-change records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one patient-information flow to its stated service context, accountable owner, authorized purpose, and documented supplier or internal handoff. This supports evidence traceability; it cannot determine legal permission, clinical necessity, or completeness of an information inventory.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved metadata and identify missing owners or stale flow records within authorized access boundaries. Humans decide care purpose, privacy interpretation, access authorization, and vendor approval. Refresh after a new service, integration, supplier, or material workflow change.

### 2. Access, operation, and event-response package

- **Request and owner:** Security and service owners provide access-review evidence, privileged-access approvals, workforce onboarding/offboarding records, relevant operational monitoring references, incident records, investigation decisions, and exception approvals.
- **Validate and limit:** Select one access change, review, or security event and trace it through an authorized human decision, supporting records, escalation path, and closure evidence. This tests the evidence chain; it cannot validate clinical appropriateness, determine breach obligations, or independently conclude an incident is resolved.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify missing approvals, aging reviews, or record inconsistencies, but may not grant access, triage a clinical matter, notify individuals, or close an incident. Recollect after an incident, material access-role change, or unresolved exception.

### 3. Continuity, supplier oversight, and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Service, privacy, security, and leadership owners provide continuity exercise records, supplier assurance materials, issue registers, corrective actions, leadership review minutes, and follow-up evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one supplier, continuity issue, or recurring security theme to its owner, documented evidence, human decision, action, and follow-up. This supports accountable oversight; it cannot certify supplier performance or prove continuity capability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compile source-linked review material and flag stale evidence. Humans approve service continuity decisions, risk acceptance, supplier changes, external notifications, and closure. Review quarterly and close the annual cycle with independent sampling.
