# Israel Protection of Privacy Law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Israeli legal advice. Confirm controlling Hebrew sources with the [Privacy Protection Authority legal portal](https://www.gov.il/en/collectors/legalinfo?officeId=4aadba43-3d71-4e7c-a4fe-5bf47b723d4e).

## Engagement focus

Maintain database, controller/processor, security, vendor, overseas transfer, rights, complaint, incident, and executive-governance evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide applicability, security requirements, and rights; independent reviewers test reconciliations. AI summarizes variance, but cannot contact the PPA. Review quarterly and annually.

### 1. Database, purpose, and accountable-owner package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy, business, product, and data owners provide a database and processing register; purpose, collection, category, system, owner, recipient, retention, notice, and database-governance records; and material-change questions for counsel.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one processing route to the database record, accountable owner, system, documented purpose, recipient, and change review. This supports an operational inventory; it cannot determine database registration, role, lawful use, or adequacy of notice.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may reconcile approved inventory and release metadata and flag unowned databases. Counsel and named humans decide scope and filings. Refresh for a new database, purpose, data category, recipient, or release.

### 2. Individual, access, and direct-marketing control package

- **Request and owner:** Privacy operations, marketing, support, security, and records owners provide request and verification procedures, redacted case indexes, preference/direct-marketing suppression records, access-review references, retention/disposal proof, and training evidence.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a de-identified request, preference, or removal action from intake through human decision, relevant system action, communication approval, and closure. This supports selected workflow traceability; it cannot authenticate an individual, decide an exception, or determine marketing permissibility.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag incomplete queue metadata and stale suppression-review dates; it cannot contact people or change preferences. Authorized humans decide outcomes. Recheck after complaint patterns, campaign changes, or workflow defects.

### 3. Supplier, security, overseas-transfer, and incident package

- **Request and owner:** Procurement, security, privacy, legal, and incident owners provide supplier/processor records, instruction and assurance references, access/security evidence, overseas-transfer review inputs, incident/exercise records, remediation, and executive decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected supplier or transfer change, access exception, or incident exercise to its facts, human authority, corrective action, and retest. This supports governed oversight; it cannot approve a transfer, prove safeguards effective, determine reporting, or accept risk.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved evidence and identify overdue reviews. Humans decide contracts, notifications, risk, and closure. Refresh after supplier, transfer, or security changes; incidents; or annual review.
