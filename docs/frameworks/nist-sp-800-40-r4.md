# NIST SP 800-40 Revision 4 — patch-management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not NIST content or an approval to deploy. Confirm status at the [NIST publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/40/r4/final).

## Engagement focus

Maintain technology inventory, update sources, prioritization criteria, test evidence, deployment approvals, exceptions, rollout status, verification records, recovery plans, and risk decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

System owners approve change windows and exceptions; independent reviewers sample the evidence chain. AI correlates inventory, advisories, tickets, and results, but cannot deploy changes or waive risk. Track continuously, test quarterly, and review annually.
