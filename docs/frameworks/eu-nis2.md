# EU NIS2 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a regulatory representation. Confirm national implementation and authority guidance from the [NIS2 Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a country/entity applicability register, critical-service dependency map, incident decision owners, security and supplier evidence, recovery records, and remediation decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators maintain evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and reporting readiness. AI can organize records and draft packets, but cannot decide scope, materiality, notification, or make legal representations. Review quarterly and annually.
