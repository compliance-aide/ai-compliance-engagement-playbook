# NARA CUI Program engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not CUI policy text, a marking or category determination, a dissemination authorization, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [NARA CUI policy and guidance page](https://www.archives.gov/cui/registry/policy-guidance), the CUI Registry, and applicable agency or contract authorities.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a controlled-information lifecycle record that connects authorized category and handling determinations, accountable owners, sources of authority, systems and locations, approved recipients, access and dissemination boundaries, markings or notices as determined by authorized personnel, incident records, decontrol or disposition decisions, training evidence, exceptions, and review dates. Treat the Registry and governing authority as authoritative; do not let an AI model infer a category, marking, or dissemination permission from a document’s contents.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Agency or contract program officials, information owners, security, privacy, legal, records, systems, procurement, training, and incident-response owners retain accountable decisions. Authorized CUI program personnel determine category and handling matters; information owners maintain lifecycle context; system owners preserve operating evidence; and incident authorities decide escalation and reporting. Independent reviewers sample information paths from authority and determination through access, transfer, storage, training, incident handling, decontrol, and disposition, escalating uncertainty to the authorized program authority.

AI may organize authorized, sanitized metadata; identify missing ownership or review fields; draft questions; and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot access restricted information, determine CUI status, apply or remove markings, authorize dissemination, transmit CUI, modify systems, interpret agreements, report an incident, accept risk, or claim compliance. Use AI only with permitted, minimized metadata. Reassess before material authority, category, system, recipient, contract, incident, or lifecycle changes; conduct periodic self-inspection and an annual independent readiness review.
