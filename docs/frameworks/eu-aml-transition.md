# EU AML package transition — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not EU legal advice or a transition conclusion. Track [Regulation (EU) 2024/1624](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R1624) and national developments.

## Engagement focus

Maintain country/entity matrix, current local baseline, milestones, owners, customer lifecycle, ownership, monitoring, reporting, records, and legal-horizon evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Humans decide applicability and alert closure; independent reviewers test readiness. AI watches official changes and consolidates evidence, but cannot restrict accounts, report, or issue legal conclusions. Review quarterly and before effective dates.
