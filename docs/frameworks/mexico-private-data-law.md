# Mexico private-party personal-data law — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice. Check the current [Mexican federal law source](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/ref/lfpdppp.htm) at engagement kickoff.

## Engagement focus

Maintain processing, service, provider, notice, collection, rights, marketing, sharing, incident, and evidence registers. Test intake-to-resolution routes and revalidate lifecycle changes annually.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own inventory accuracy and execution; independent reviewers reconcile systems and contracts to evidence. AI maps evidence and gaps, but cannot determine applicability, approve notice or rights decisions, or communicate externally. Review quarterly and annually.
