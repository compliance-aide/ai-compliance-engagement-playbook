# ISO/IEC 27002:2022 — security-practice engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a certification conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain scope, asset/service landscape, local risk-practice register, operating evidence, suppliers, tests, exceptions, approvals, remediation, and improvement records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Accountable owners approve actions; independent reviewers test samples and conclude separately. AI organizes evidence and drafts questions, but cannot reproduce controls or certify. Review monthly, quarterly, and annually.
