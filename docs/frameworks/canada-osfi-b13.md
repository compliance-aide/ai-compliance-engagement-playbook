# Canada OSFI Guideline B-13 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not OSFI content or a prudential conclusion. Confirm current guidance at the [OSFI technology and cyber risk page](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/risks/technology-cyber-risk-management).

## Engagement focus

Maintain technology and cyber risk decisions, governance evidence, service inventory, resilience tests, security operations records, incident decisions, remediation, third-party dependencies, and management reporting.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Institution leaders and accountable risk owners make prudential and incident decisions; independent reviewers test the evidence trail. AI maps approved records and flags gaps, but cannot rate compliance, notify OSFI, or accept risk. Review quarterly and annually.
