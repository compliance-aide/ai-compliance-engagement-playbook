# ISO/IEC 27032:2023 Internet security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or an Internet-security assurance claim. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/76070.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain Internet-facing service inventory, ownership, third-party dependencies, exposure decisions, monitoring signals, incident records, vulnerability handling, customer-impact communication, and improvement actions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Service and security owners decide exposure and mitigation; independent reviewers assess evidence and outcomes. AI inventories signals and drafts questions, but cannot expose services, alter defenses, or make public claims. Monitor continuously, test quarterly, and review annually.

### 1. Internet-facing service and ownership package

- **Request and owner:** Service, architecture, and security owners provide the Internet-facing service inventory, domains/endpoints, business purpose, asset ownership, third-party dependencies, data/service criticality, and approved exposure decisions.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exposed service to its owner, stated purpose, dependency, and approval or exception record. This supports accountable visibility; it cannot prove discovery completeness or authorize an exposed service.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may correlate inventory signals and flag unowned assets. Humans approve exposure and risk decisions. Refresh after a new endpoint, domain, provider, application, or material architecture change.

### 2. Monitoring, vulnerability, and incident-response package

- **Request and owner:** Security and service owners provide monitoring coverage references, vulnerability findings and remediation records, incident tickets/timelines, communications handoffs, and corrective actions.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect a selected finding or incident from detection through human triage, evidence preservation, mitigation, and follow-up. This supports the recorded response under observed conditions; it cannot prove continuous detection, full remediation, or incident reportability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize signals and identify aging actions; it may not change defenses, classify an incident, or direct containment. Recollect after a material finding, incident, missed service objective, or monitoring change.

### 3. Resilience and leadership-improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Security leadership provides test/exercise results, open Internet-risk themes, exception decisions, resourcing choices, retest evidence, and annual review records.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected gap to accountable owner, decision authority, mitigation, retest, and management review. This supports improvement governance; it cannot accept risk, demonstrate resilience across untested conditions, or replace independent review.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may draft a source-linked review packet and flag expired exceptions. Humans approve risk acceptance, investment, closure, and external claims. Review quarterly and close annually with follow-up.
