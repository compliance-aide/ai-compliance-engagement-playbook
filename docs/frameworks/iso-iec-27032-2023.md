# ISO/IEC 27032:2023 Internet security — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or an Internet-security assurance claim. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/76070.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain Internet-facing service inventory, ownership, third-party dependencies, exposure decisions, monitoring signals, incident records, vulnerability handling, customer-impact communication, and improvement actions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Service and security owners decide exposure and mitigation; independent reviewers assess evidence and outcomes. AI inventories signals and drafts questions, but cannot expose services, alter defenses, or make public claims. Monitor continuously, test quarterly, and review annually.
