# ISO/IEC 27102:2019 cyber-insurance engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, insurance advice, underwriting information, a coverage conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27102:2019 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/72436.html); the ISO page identifies a revision in development.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a cyber-insurance readiness record that connects organizational scope, accountable business and risk owners, risk-treatment rationale, policy and renewal artifacts, authorized insurer information, underwriting and claims evidence, exceptions, incident learnings, and independent review. Use AI only to organize approved material and questions; do not let generated summaries become insurance, legal, contractual, or risk-acceptance decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Business leadership, risk, security, legal, finance, procurement, privacy, and accountable insurance authorities retain decisions; evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test disclosure traceability, scope, change control, and claim-readiness boundaries. AI may organize authorized records, identify stale evidence, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot advise on coverage, make representations to an insurer, decide disclosure sufficiency, bind or renew a policy, accept risk, submit a claim, alter production systems, or claim compliance. Review evidence before underwriting, renewal, material business or technology changes, and incidents; perform a formal annual readiness review.
