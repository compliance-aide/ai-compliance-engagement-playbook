# IEC TS 62443-1-5:2023 IACS security-profile engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an IEC profile, licensed specification or conformity decision.

## Source and applicability

The [IEC publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67461), checked 2026-09-04, describes a scheme for defining IEC 62443 security profiles intended for publication in its dedicated sub-series and supporting comparability of assessed requirements. Full licensed scheme rules, current profile publications and intended-use applicability remain unverified. Confirm exact source editions, rights and publication route before detailed work.

This guide organizes profile governance. It does not turn a local checklist into an official IEC profile or approve exclusions from binding requirements. Do not reproduce restricted source text or proprietary mappings here.

## Engagement focus

Keep intended context, selected source requirements, interpretation decisions and profile versions traceable so two assessments are compared on an explicit common basis. Profile approval and assessment success are separate outcomes.

## Roles

The profile sponsor owns intended use and audience. Qualified technical, safety, operations and risk owners decide applicability and selections. Records owners maintain version history and authorized evidence. An independent reviewer challenges omissions and comparability. AI may organize permitted metadata and draft questions; it cannot define, approve, publish or select a profile, accept risk, change OT or claim conformity.

## Before starting

Record sponsor, use case, sector/system/product boundary, intended users, publication route and contractual basis. Obtain authorized source references and existing approved profile versions. Define permissions for restricted requirements and mappings. Documentation review does not authorize publishing a profile, industrial testing or changing configurations.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Confirm the purpose.** Separate development of a profile, adoption of an existing profile and comparison of assessment results. Record the desired outcome and approving authority. Resolve a generic request for a “62443 profile” before substantive selection.
2. **Freeze the source baseline.** Inventory every relevant part, edition, correction and approved reference. Record unavailable sources explicitly. Do not substitute a later edition silently or mix versions without qualified review.
3. **Document context and selections.** Qualified owners record selected requirements, applicability conditions and rationale in the authorized environment. Preserve exclusions, additions and unresolved interpretations. A difficult requirement is not automatically irrelevant.
4. **Check consistency.** Trace every selection and contextual statement to its source and owner decision. Identify contradictory scope, dependencies on omitted requirements and ambiguous terms. Retain conflicts for review rather than inventing a favorable interpretation.
5. **Review assessment comparability.** Record the exact profile/version, assessed object, conditions, methods and evidence boundaries for each result. Similar names or total scores do not prove equivalent coverage. Preserve differences explicitly.
6. **Authorize changes and release.** Prepare the complete change record and impact on users, requirements and prior results. Named authorities approve revisions and publication through the applicable route. A draft upload is not an approved or officially published profile.
7. **Maintain traceable use.** Link each downstream engagement to the profile version actually used. Reopen affected records after source, context or profile changes. Keep prior results and limitations instead of relabeling them as current without reassessment.

## Evidence and test plan

Account for all scoped profile statements, references, selections, changes and downstream uses. Preserve all evidence supplied for assessment without silent trimming or sampling. Complete accounting does not prove technical sufficiency or scheme compliance.

### 1. Profile-governance scope package

- **Request and owner:** Human-approved intended use, system/product context, profile sponsor, audience, scope assumptions, safety/operational constraints, and authorized source register.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace every scoped assertion to a sponsor, context, version, and source boundary. This supports governance traceability; it cannot establish a profile's technical sufficiency or applicability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved metadata and flag absent ownership. Humans approve scope and intended use. Reopen before material product, system, supplier, or safety change.

### 2. Versioned evidence-reference package

- **Request and owner:** Version history, approved configuration/evidence references, dependencies, change proposals, known limitations, and supplier-provided material retained under its terms, from profile/evidence owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace every scoped reference to version, owner, source rights, and stated limitation without copying licensed material into the guide. This supports version control; it cannot validate configurations or approve a profile revision.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare metadata and flag unresolved version links; it cannot publish or modify a profile. Refresh at each proposed version change.

### 3. Change, exception, and independent-review package

- **Request and owner:** Human change/exception decisions, risk and safety review inputs, follow-up dates, communications record, and independent review workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace each change and exception to authority, source basis, limitation, expiry, and follow-up. This supports accountable governance; it cannot accept risk, issue conformity conclusions, or replace technical review.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a read-only change packet and flag expired items. Humans approve all changes and external representations. Review at approved intervals and after material incident or architecture change.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Source version missing: stop dependent equivalence or applicability conclusions and assign retrieval.
- Local checklist presented as official profile: correct the label and verify the actual publication status.
- Same profile name has different versions: compare requirement coverage and conditions before comparing outcomes.
- Exclusion conflicts with a binding obligation: escalate to the accountable authority; profile adoption cannot waive the obligation.
- Revision changes assessed scope: identify affected results and require a documented transition/reassessment decision.

Use [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) assertions: supported, not_supported, inconclusive, not_applicable and not_tested. These are evidence statuses, not profile approval or conformity results.

## Cadence and renewal

Review each material source or context change and at approved governance intervals. Track transition decisions and obsolete versions. Do not invent universal annual profile renewal or certification duties.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the purpose and source baseline, selection rationale, complete traceability, review findings, approved version history and open actions with owners and dates.
