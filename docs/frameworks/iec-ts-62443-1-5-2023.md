# IEC TS 62443-1-5:2023 IACS security-profile engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a security-profile specification, a conformity decision, a technical selection, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC TS 62443-1-5:2023 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67461).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a profile-governance record that connects the intended IACS context, accountable profile sponsors, scope assumptions, authorized source material, configuration and evidence references, exceptions, version history, change decisions, and independent review. Use it to make profile-oriented engagement work traceable without presenting a generated profile, assessment comparison, or evidence summary as authoritative.

## Roles and annual rhythm

OT engineering, operations, safety, security, product, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; profile and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, version traceability, and the separation of profile governance from assessment conclusions. AI may organize authorized artifacts, identify version or evidence gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot define, approve, publish, or select a security profile, decide technical applicability, accept risk, alter OT or production configuration, initiate scans or changes, or claim conformity or compliance. Review each profile at least annually and before material system, product, supplier, safety, architecture, incident, or intended-use changes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [IEC TS 62443-1-5:2023 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/67461) and an authorized copy; checked 2026-07-31. IEC text is licensed. This original plan does not reproduce a profile, define or select one, determine technical applicability, or make a conformity or compliance conclusion.

### 1. Profile-governance scope package

- **Request and owner:** Human-approved intended use, system/product context, profile sponsor, audience, scope assumptions, safety/operational constraints, and authorized source register.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected scope assertion to a sponsor, context, version, and source boundary. This supports governance traceability; it cannot establish a profile's technical sufficiency or applicability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved metadata and flag absent ownership. Humans approve scope and intended use. Reopen before material product, system, supplier, or safety change.

### 2. Versioned evidence-reference package

- **Request and owner:** Version history, approved configuration/evidence references, dependencies, change proposals, known limitations, and supplier-provided material retained under its terms, from profile/evidence owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected reference to version, owner, source rights, and stated limitation without copying licensed material into the guide. This supports version control; it cannot validate configurations or approve a profile revision.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare metadata and flag unresolved version links; it cannot publish or modify a profile. Refresh at each proposed version change.

### 3. Change, exception, and independent-review package

- **Request and owner:** Human change/exception decisions, risk and safety review inputs, follow-up dates, communications record, and independent review workpapers.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected change or exception to authority, source basis, limitation, expiry, and follow-up. This supports accountable governance; it cannot accept risk, issue conformity conclusions, or replace technical review.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a read-only change packet and flag expired items. Humans approve all changes and external representations. Review annually and after material incident or architecture change.
