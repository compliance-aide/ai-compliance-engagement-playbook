# ISO/IEC 27010:2015 information-sharing engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a permission to share information, an agreement interpretation, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27010:2015 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/68427.html).

## Engagement focus

Manage inter-organizational information sharing as a living, evidence-backed engagement. Maintain a community and exchange register that connects participants, purpose, governance forum, information categories, handling assumptions, permitted flows, interface and service dependencies, accountable owners, agreement and policy references, onboarding evidence, exceptions, incidents, offboarding, and review dates. Establish one traceable decision record per material exchange so participants and auditors can distinguish approved sharing from observed data movement.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Community sponsors, participating organizations, information owners, security, privacy, legal, procurement, technology, and operations leaders retain decisions within their authority. Information owners approve sharing under applicable agreements; technical owners attest to operating boundaries; legal and privacy owners determine handling constraints; and community governance resolves cross-organization issues. Independent reviewers sample exchanges from stated purpose through approval, technical evidence, participant obligations, exceptions, and offboarding readiness, then challenge unowned handoffs and ambiguous responsibility.

AI may organize authorized agreements and evidence metadata, identify mismatched participant records, draft review questions, prepare flow inventories, and assemble non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot authorize sharing, decide legal applicability, interpret contract terms, create participant access, transmit data, modify interfaces, accept risk, or claim compliance. Use AI only with the permissions appropriate to the data involved. Reassess before material participant, purpose, data-category, interface, incident, or jurisdictional changes; conduct quarterly governance reviews and an annual independent readiness review.
