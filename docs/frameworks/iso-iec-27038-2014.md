# ISO/IEC 27038:2014 digital-redaction engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a legal disclosure decision, a determination that material is safe to release, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27038:2014 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/44382.html).

## Engagement focus

Run redaction as a controlled disclosure process for digital documents. Keep a release record that connects the source document, authorized purpose, applicable handling decision, sensitive-content rationale, approved redaction method and tool version, operator, quality checks, verification evidence, version lineage, recipient, release authority, and retention. Separate document redaction from database transformation and do not assume that a visual cover-up removes underlying digital content.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Records, legal, privacy, security, technology, communications, and business owners retain accountable decisions. The content owner defines the release purpose and source; legal and privacy authorities decide handling constraints; trained operators perform authorized redaction; release authorities approve disclosure; and evidence stewards preserve the decision and verification record. Independent reviewers sample released documents to test source-to-output traceability, authorization, tool and method consistency, verification evidence, recipient restrictions, and exception handling.

AI may inventory authorized release records, identify missing approvals or verification fields, compare document versions, draft review checklists, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers. AI cannot decide what to redact, manipulate source documents, perform or certify redaction, approve release, infer legal obligations, disclose material, or claim compliance. Use least-privilege access and preserve originals separately from releasable copies. Reassess after material tool, workflow, legal, or content-type changes; review exceptions quarterly and perform an annual independent readiness review.
