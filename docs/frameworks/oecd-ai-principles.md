# OECD AI Principles — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for a voluntary reference, not a certification. Consult the [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/principles).

## Engagement focus

Translate principles into an organization-owned charter and link each use case to intended benefit, affected people, foreseeable harm, owner, evaluation, feedback, and remedy evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators document purpose and signals; independent reviewers test whether stated principles drive decisions and remediation. AI maps evidence to the charter, but cannot represent adherence or score trustworthiness as fact. Review quarterly and refresh annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
