# OECD AI Principles — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for a voluntary reference, not a certification. Consult the [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/principles).

## Engagement focus

Translate principles into an organization-owned charter and link each use case to intended benefit, affected people, foreseeable harm, owner, evaluation, feedback, and remedy evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators document purpose and signals; independent reviewers test whether stated principles drive decisions and remediation. AI maps evidence to the charter, but cannot represent adherence or score trustworthiness as fact. Review quarterly and refresh annually.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/principles); source status: current voluntary reference, retrieved 2026-07-31. This original plan links to, rather than reproduces, OECD material and does not determine adherence, trustworthiness, or a certification outcome.

### 1. Use-case purpose and affected-party boundary

- **Request and owner:** Request the approved AI-use-case register, purpose and expected-benefit statements, affected-party analysis, accountable product owner, deployment boundary, and charter linkage from product, risk, legal, and business owners for the review period.
- **Validate and limit:** Select a use case and trace its stated purpose to a named owner, approved scope, affected-party record, and periodic review. This supports provenance of a declared governance process; it cannot establish that the use is beneficial, fair, or trustworthy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize approved metadata and identify missing owner or review fields. Humans decide purpose, acceptable impact, deployment, and escalation. Refresh for a new or materially changed use case, affected population, purpose, or decision consequence.

### 2. Evaluation, transparency, and human-accountability evidence

- **Request and owner:** Request evaluation plans and results, data/model change records, human-oversight design, user-facing communication approvals, accessibility or feedback-channel evidence, and material limitations from engineering, product, compliance, and communications owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected release or model change to its evaluation record, accountable review, communication decision, and unresolved limitation. This evidences recorded review activity; it cannot validate model quality, prove a person understood a disclosure, or approve a consequential decision.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved test summaries and flag version or review gaps, but may not alter evaluations, decide a person’s outcome, or approve communication. Recollect after a model, data, interface, oversight, or disclosure change.

### 3. Feedback, incident, remedy, and annual governance record

- **Request and owner:** Request feedback and complaint metadata, incident or harm triage records, remediation decisions, escalation log, leadership-review minutes, exception register, and annual charter/source-change review from operations, legal, risk, and leadership owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected reported concern to triage facts, a human decision-maker, corrective action, and closure or open-risk record. This supports accountability for documented handling; it cannot determine harm, legal liability, or adequacy of remedy.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare a minimized evidence index and surface overdue actions. Humans decide remedy, risk acceptance, external communications, and closure. Revisit annually and after a material complaint, incident, regulatory change, or unresolved exception.
