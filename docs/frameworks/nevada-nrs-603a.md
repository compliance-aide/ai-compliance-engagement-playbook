# Nevada NRS Chapter 603A privacy and security engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Nevada statutory text, legal advice, a notice or request response, or a compliance claim. Confirm current requirements through the [Nevada Revised Statutes, Chapter 603A](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-603A.html) and qualified counsel.

## Engagement focus

Maintain a Nevada-specific record of in-scope consumer-information activities: entity and service scope, information categories and sources, web and online-service behavior, privacy notices, consumer-request handling, security measures, supplier relationships, retention and destruction, event evidence, and approved external communications. Keep statutory version changes and any health-data or sectoral applicability questions in the legal review record.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy and legal owners determine scope and response obligations; product and web-service owners maintain notice and workflow evidence; security teams retain incident evidence; supplier owners preserve third-party records; independent reviewers test traceability. AI may organize approved records, surface stale notices or incomplete request evidence, and draft questions, but cannot determine legal applicability, verify or respond to a consumer, send a notice, decide reportability, approve a sale or sharing decision, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material website, online-service, data-practice, supplier, notice, request, or incident changes.
