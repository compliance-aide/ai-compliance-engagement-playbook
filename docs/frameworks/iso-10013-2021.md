# ISO 10013:2021 documented information — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO text, a document-control approval or a conformity claim.

## Source and applicability

Confirm edition and scope using the [official ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/75736.html), checked through indexed publisher content on 2026-09-04. It describes guidance for developing and maintaining documented information supporting a quality management system, with application to other management systems. The record lists the 2021 publication; a review-stage entry does not itself establish a replacement edition.

The full licensed standard has not been reviewed. Confirm source-use rights before accessing protected content for AI work; do not place ISO text in prompts without permission. These are original operational instructions, not a prescribed ISO document hierarchy, clause checklist or retention schedule. Qualified reviewers must verify applicable requirements and rights before publication or assessment.

## Engagement focus

Ensure intended users can find and use the correct approved information, while historical records remain attributable and protected. Separate instructions that may be revised from records showing what actually happened. Updating today's procedure must not silently rewrite yesterday's evidence.

## Roles

Process owners approve content, intended use and effective dates. Document-control owners manage versions and distribution. Repository owners maintain access, availability and audit history; legal/records owners decide retention and holds. Users report confusing or inaccessible instructions. An independent reviewer tests traceability and practical retrieval. AI may reconcile approved metadata, detect stale links and draft changes, but cannot approve policy, publish an authoritative revision, revoke access, alter retained evidence or decide disposal.

## Before starting

Record processes, sites, systems, audiences, languages and review period. Identify the authoritative repository for each information class and obtain approved access. Request inventories, version history, approval records, distribution paths, retention decisions and user feedback. Keep sensitive content in approved private systems. A filename, modification date or search rank alone does not establish authority.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Reconcile the information population.** Account for all known scoped procedures, policies, templates, completed records, external references and copies. Give each a stable ID, owner, purpose, authoritative location and classification. Identify missing items and duplicate titles. Preserve complete evidence accounting without silent sampling, trimming or caps.
2. **Check usefulness and dependencies.** Map each instruction to its process, intended users, inputs, actions, decision authority, outputs and exceptions. Identify referenced forms, linked systems and prerequisites. Have a suitable user walk through an authorized synthetic case; record where wording or missing inputs prevents the next step. A document can be approved yet operationally unusable.
3. **Prepare a controlled change.** Record the problem, affected information, proposed version, rationale and dependencies. Preserve the previous approved version and a change comparison. Check that linked templates and instructions remain consistent. Escalate conflicting obligations or retention requirements to the appropriate owner rather than choosing whichever version is easiest to edit.
4. **Obtain exact-version approval.** Link the approving authority, date, approved artifact and intended effective date. Distinguish draft, approved-but-not-effective, effective and superseded states. Approval of a proposal is not approval of later modified content. Material edits after approval require the designated review path again; AI does not manufacture signatures or authorization records.
5. **Verify distribution and point-of-use retrieval.** After authorized release, inspect what intended users actually retrieve in each known distribution path: portal, shared folder, printed station, exported copy or embedded instruction. Confirm version, access and readability. Mark predecessors appropriately and retain required history. A repository upload is not proof that a cached copy or printed instruction has changed.
6. **Protect records and control disposition.** Associate completed records with their process and applicable instruction/template version. Correct errors through a traceable amendment, preserving the original event and explanation. Apply approved access, retention, legal holds and disposal rules. Verify retrieval and authorized recovery; do not assume an existing backup is readable or current. No destructive action occurs on an AI-inferred expiration date.
7. **Review failures and close the change.** Reconcile all affected documents, references, distribution paths and records with the approved change scope. Assign unresolved stale copies, broken links or access failures to owners with due dates. Verify corrective actions and user-facing behavior. Close the change only within supported scope; preserve any adoption or comprehension uncertainty separately.

## Evidence and test plan

The three packages below preserve the prior tailored plan, expanded beyond one selected document or change.

| Package | Minimum records | Reviewer challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authoritative information inventory | Complete known scoped IDs, classes, owners, users, locations, versions and dependencies | Can the right source be identified without relying on filenames or search order? |
| Change, access and supersession | Change rationale, exact approval, effective date, permissions, distribution readback and predecessor disposition | Is the approved version the one users actually receive? |
| Use, retention and improvement | Synthetic walkthroughs, user issues, record lineage, approved schedules/holds, recovery checks and corrective actions | Can users perform the process while prior evidence remains intact? |

Use the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md) statuses `supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` and `not_tested` for individual assertions. Split approval, availability, distribution, comprehension and operational use. A supported approval record does not support all five. Full inventory accounting does not imply every access role or retrieval path has been technically tested.

Fictional author desk case: procedure version 3 is approved and present in the central repository, but the shop-floor QR code opens version 2. Approval and central storage are supported; the current-version-at-point-of-use assertion fails. Assign the distribution owner to correct the link, verify the actual scan result and assess affected work. Do not rewrite older completed records to make them appear governed by version 3.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Two apparently authoritative versions conflict: preserve both, identify the process owner and block dependent conclusions until reconciled.
- User lacks access: use the approved access-resolution path; do not copy confidential material into an unrestricted folder.
- Urgent instruction change: follow the documented emergency authority and retrospective review requirements; urgency does not create AI publication authority.
- A retention hold conflicts with scheduled disposal: prevent the destructive action and obtain records/legal direction with a visible owner.
- External reference is revised or unavailable: identify affected instructions and obtain source review instead of silently substituting an unverified copy.

## Cadence and renewal

Set review intervals from process risk, obligations and owner decisions; this guide invents no annual ISO mandate. Reopen after process, source, audience, repository, access or retention changes and after failed retrieval or use. Track effective dates and disposal/hold obligations independently of review dates.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the scoped inventory, approved-version references, distribution results, record-protection evidence and owned open actions. State what was not verified about comprehension or adoption. Independent source, skeptical and rights review remain pending before guide publication. Author desk checks are not independent review or cross-model usability trials.
