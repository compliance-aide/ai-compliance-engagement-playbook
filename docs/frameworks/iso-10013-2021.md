# ISO 10013:2021 documented information — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a document-control approval. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/75736.html); do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain information inventory, ownership, approval and revision records, access boundaries, retention, system links, superseded-version handling, evidence of use, and improvement decisions.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Process owners approve authoritative information and retention decisions; independent reviewers test traceability. AI locates approved records and detects stale links, but cannot publish policy, approve content, or alter retention. Review on change and annually.

### 1. Authoritative-information inventory package

- **Request and owner:** The information owner supplies the approved inventory of policies, procedures, templates, records, repositories, accountable owners, intended users, and system-of-record locations.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected high-use document from inventory entry to accountable owner, authoritative location, and current approved version. This supports traceability for the sample; it does not prove completeness or suitability of every document.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare inventory metadata and flag missing ownership. A human authorizes content and classification decisions. Refresh after a process, system, ownership, or regulatory change.

### 2. Change, access, and supersession package

- **Request and owner:** Document-control and repository owners provide approval/revision history, access roles, change requests, withdrawal or supersession records, and user communications for a selected change set.
- **Validate and limit:** Inspect whether an approved change has a controlled version, stated effective date, access boundary, and disposition of its predecessor. This does not establish that every user received, understood, or followed it.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may identify conflicting version labels or stale links; it may not publish, revoke access, or approve a revision. Recollect after urgent updates, migrations, or access-model changes.

### 3. Use, retention, and improvement package

- **Request and owner:** Process owners provide use feedback, exceptions, retention/disposal decisions, records of retrieval or review, and improvement actions for a defined information set.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one issue through reported use, owner decision, retained record, and corrective action. This supports an accountable improvement trail; it cannot decide retention, accept legal risk, or prove operational adoption.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may summarize feedback and identify overdue review dates. Humans approve retention, disposal, remediation, and external statements. Review annually and after material use failures.
