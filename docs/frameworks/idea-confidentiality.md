# IDEA confidentiality — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a privacy certification. Consult [IDEA Part B confidentiality rules](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/f/300.610) and applicable overlays.

## Engagement focus

Maintain entity/record lifecycle, collection, access, consent, disclosure, logs, parental rights, retention, destruction, vendor, transfer, incident, and corrective-action evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own access and records; independent reviewers sample chains and local overlays. AI may access only authorized data and flag lifecycle inconsistencies; it cannot decide consent/disclosure, alter records, or communicate rights outcomes.
