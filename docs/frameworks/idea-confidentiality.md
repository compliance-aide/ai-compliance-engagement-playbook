# IDEA confidentiality — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or a privacy certification. Consult [IDEA Part B confidentiality rules](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/f/300.610) and applicable overlays.

## Engagement focus

Maintain entity/record lifecycle, collection, access, consent, disclosure, logs, parental rights, retention, destruction, vendor, transfer, incident, and corrective-action evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators own access and records; independent reviewers sample chains and local overlays. AI may access only authorized data and flag lifecycle inconsistencies; it cannot decide consent/disclosure, alter records, or communicate rights outcomes.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the official [IDEA Part B confidentiality rules](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/f/300.610) and have education/privacy counsel identify applicable state and local overlays. Record the source’s status and retrieval date in the engagement workpaper. This guide uses original language and does not restate the rule or decide a student-record disclosure.

### 1. Student-record inventory and accountable education boundary

- **Request and owner:** Request an approved record-system inventory, student/parent or guardian rights-contact process, data-flow and role map, authorized-school/contractor boundary inputs, and named records, special-education, privacy, security, and legal owners. Define the program, school year, and system population under review; exclude student identifiers from this public guide.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected record category from collection/use purpose to system owner, access role, retention cue, and approved boundary decision. Inspect metadata or sanitized samples in the authorized environment. This can support a documented records-lifecycle boundary; it cannot establish legal coverage, validate each record’s accuracy, or decide an individual’s rights outcome.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare approved inventories and ownership metadata for gaps in a segregated approved environment. Human education and privacy owners decide record classification, access, and local applicability. Trigger on a new system, program, vendor, school-year rollover, or material data-flow change.

### 2. Access, request, and disclosure-decision lineage

- **Request and owner:** Request access-role approvals, parent/eligible-party request intake and response references, disclosure request/authorization records, recipient/contractor accountability records, access logs, and exception/escalation decisions from records, school administration, privacy, legal, and vendor-management owners. Use an approved sample period and protected records environment.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected access or disclosure event from request/intake through authority verification, reviewer decision, released-record boundary, audit log/reference, and response/closure record. This can support process traceability; it cannot determine consent validity, legal exception applicability, actual recipient use, or compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may route redacted work items, detect missing approvals, and summarize approved metadata; it cannot verify identity, approve a disclosure, alter records, or respond to a family. Humans decide disclosure and rights outcomes. Trigger on a rights request, unusual access, proposed disclosure, contractor change, or annual sampling.

### 3. Retention, vendor, incident, and corrective-action record

- **Request and owner:** Request retention/disposition schedule references, deletion/destruction approvals, education-vendor data terms and access/offboarding evidence, incident intake/escalation records, corrective-action plans, and governance-review minutes from records, IT, security, procurement, and legal owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected disposition, vendor departure, or incident to applicable record boundary, authorization/decision record, execution evidence, follow-up action, and review. This can support accountable lifecycle handling; it cannot prove complete deletion, vendor compliance, incident absence, or notification duty.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale vendor attestations, missed review dates, or incomplete evidence links. Authorized humans decide retention, destruction, supplier action, incident communications, and remediation closure. Trigger on vendor onboarding/offboarding, retention cycle, incident, audit issue, or annual governance review.
