# ISO/IEC TS 27022:2021 ISMS process engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a management-system certification opinion, an effectiveness conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC TS 27022:2021 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/61004.html).

## Engagement focus

Operate the information-security management system as a traceable set of business processes rather than a static document collection. Maintain a process map that connects objectives, accountable owners, inputs, activities, outputs, dependencies, measures, evidence locations, exceptions, management decisions, change triggers, and improvement actions. Use the map to expose broken handoffs, unowned outputs, and measures that cannot be traced to an operational decision or underlying evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Executive sponsors, security leadership, risk, compliance, internal audit, technology, privacy, legal, people, procurement, and process owners retain their respective decisions. Process owners maintain current operating records and measures; management accepts priorities and resourcing decisions; and auditors evaluate evidence against the approved engagement scope. Independent reviewers sample cross-process paths from stated objective through operational output and measurement, test handoff completeness, identify stale ownership and exceptions, and report material evidence gaps without taking management decisions.

AI may map authorized records to process fields, identify incomplete handoffs, draft evidence requests, prepare measure summaries, and assemble non-authoritative audit workpapers. AI cannot assign process ownership, set risk appetite, decide process effectiveness, approve management actions, alter systems or records, select certification scope, or claim compliance. Reassess the map after material organizational, technology, supplier, or regulatory changes; perform quarterly process-owner reviews and an annual independent readiness review.
