# NIST SP 1800-23 energy-sector asset-management engagement guide

> This is original operational guidance, not NIST practice-guide content, an OT inventory assertion, a safety decision, a compliance claim, or professional advice. Confirm current material through [NIST SP 1800-23](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1800/23/final) and the organization’s approved engineering, safety, cybersecurity, regulatory, legal, and operational decisions.

## Engagement focus

Manage a year-round, safety-aware asset-management engagement for energy-sector industrial control and operational-technology environments. Connect each material asset, function, owner, location or logical zone, communications path, software or firmware state, supplier, monitoring source, criticality rationale, maintenance status, incident record, and lifecycle disposition. Treat discovery and monitoring results as evidence for review, not automatic proof of completeness or authorization to change operating systems.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Energy operations, OT engineering, safety, cybersecurity, network, asset-management, maintenance, supplier, regulatory, incident-response, and records owners establish scope and make accountable decisions. Operators maintain inventories, ownership, approved baselines, discovery and monitoring evidence, change records, exceptions, incidents, and review history. Reconcile material assets, ownership, connectivity, and monitoring coverage quarterly; review anomalies and changes promptly; and conduct an annual governance and safety review.

Independent reviewers sample an asset from inventory through monitoring, approved baseline, change history, and operational review. Auditors evaluate supplied evidence without operating OT, changing configuration, determining safety, approving suppliers, accepting risk, or issuing an attestation. AI may organize records, flag stale ownership or evidence, draft workpapers, and prepare review questions. AI cannot operate equipment, change settings, determine safety, approve action, accept risk, or attest to conformance.
