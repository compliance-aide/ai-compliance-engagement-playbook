# ISO/IEC 27004:2016 measurement — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or a performance conclusion. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/64120.html), including its revision status; do not place ISO text in AI prompts.

## Engagement focus

Maintain measurement objectives, data definitions, collection owners, quality checks, analysis decisions, exceptions, trend explanations, corrective actions, and management-review evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Control and data owners validate measures and their meaning; independent reviewers test traceability and integrity. AI detects missing or conflicting records, but cannot declare effectiveness. Monitor routinely, review trends quarterly, and refresh measures annually.

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
