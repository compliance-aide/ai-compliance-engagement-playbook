# Canada DND IT security-risk management engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not Department of National Defence direction or an authority-to-operate outcome. Confirm current applicability through [DAOD 6003-2](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/policies-standards/defence-administrative-orders-directives/6000-series/6003/6003-2-information-technology-security-risk-management.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a mission-aware record of system and asset context, security requirements, accountable authorities, assessments, implementation evidence, monitoring, risk decisions, and remediation through the system lifecycle.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operational, technical, and security authorities establish their delegated responsibilities; system teams retain evidence; independent assessors test the record and its currency. AI helps normalize evidence inventories, expose aging decisions, and prepare reviewer questions, but cannot make mission-risk decisions, assess classified material, or approve operational use. Update the engagement record at lifecycle gates and material changes, conduct recurring evidence reviews, and keep delegated human approvals auditable.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use [DAOD 6003-2](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/policies-standards/defence-administrative-orders-directives/6000-series/6003/6003-2-information-technology-security-risk-management.html), current departmental direction, and only authorized handling environments; checked 2026-07-31. This is original evidence-planning language, not DND direction, a reproduction of departmental requirements, a security assessment, or an authority-to-operate outcome. The engagement owner confirms source rights, scope, and handling restrictions before collection.

### 1. Mission context, asset, and delegated-authority package

- **Request and owner:** The operational sponsor, system owner, and designated security authority provide the approved mission/service description, system and asset inventory, boundary/dependency record, information-handling constraints, delegated-role record, and lifecycle or material-change log.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace one material asset or dependency to its mission use, accountable owner, current boundary entry, handling designation, and cited delegation. This tests the evidence trail only; it cannot determine classification, security requirements, or authority delegation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may index approved metadata and flag inconsistent owners, dates, or boundary versions. Authorized humans decide mission relevance, handling, delegation, and scope. Refresh at a lifecycle gate and after a material mission, asset, supplier, or architecture change.

### 2. Safeguard-operation and risk-treatment package

- **Request and owner:** System, security, and operational teams provide original implementation narratives, approved evidence pointers, assessment/test records, known-risk register, remediation actions, exceptions, and evidence-period details.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one documented risk through the implementation evidence, assessment observation, accountable action owner, target date, and current condition. A sample cannot establish effectiveness across the environment or decide whether risk treatment is adequate.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may prepare read-only traceability workpapers and identify missing dates or unlinked actions; it cannot inspect classified content without authorization, alter evidence, select safeguards, or close risk actions. Human security and operational authorities judge sufficiency and remediation. Refresh after a failed test, incident, material safeguard change, or new risk decision.

### 3. Assessment, decision, and ongoing-review package

- **Request and owner:** The assessment coordinator and designated authorities provide independent review records, decision/condition register, approvals, monitoring outputs, incident/change records, reassessment dates, and source-change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace one condition or change from detection to assessment, accountable human decision, required follow-up, and next review date. This demonstrates a decision record; it cannot issue an authorization, accept mission risk, or declare operational suitability.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag aging conditions and draft reviewer questions. Only appropriately delegated humans approve risk decisions, operational use, and external representations. Review on the approved cadence and before a material decision, renewal, or transition.
