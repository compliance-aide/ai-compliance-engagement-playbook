# ISO 37301:2021 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or legal advice. Recheck the [ISO version record](https://www.iso.org/standard/75080.html) and amendment status.

## Engagement focus

Maintain an obligations register, accountability map, change monitoring, evidence, exception, and program-effectiveness records.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Legal and compliance owners validate interpretation; independent reviewers test evidence. AI classifies gaps and drafts interview agendas, but cannot interpret obligations or certify. Monitor monthly, review quarterly, and renew annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
