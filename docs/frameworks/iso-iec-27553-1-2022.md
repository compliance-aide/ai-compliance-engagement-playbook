# ISO/IEC 27553-1:2022 local mobile-biometric engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a biometric-authentication decision, a security or privacy determination, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27553-1:2022 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71671.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a local mobile-biometric engagement record connecting mobile product scope, accountable owners, authorized security and privacy evidence, device lifecycle assumptions, supplier dependencies, exceptions, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Keep AI assistance confined to records and questions, with all biometric and access outcomes under accountable human control.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Product, security, privacy, identity, legal, operations, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test scope, local-processing assumptions, lifecycle traceability, privacy boundaries, and exceptions. AI may organize authorized evidence, identify missing records, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot capture or process biometric data, authenticate a person, grant access, determine security or privacy sufficiency, accept risk, alter production systems, initiate changes, or claim compliance. Review releases and material changes continuously, conduct quarterly evidence reviews, and reassess annually.
