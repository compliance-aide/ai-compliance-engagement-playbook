# Japan cybersecurity management guidelines — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance for voluntary management practice, not a certification. Consult [IPA cyber-management practice resources](https://www.ipa.go.jp/security/economics/csm-practice.html).

## Engagement focus

Set board-sponsored objectives, decision rights, risk, asset, supplier, incident, investment, recovery, and measurable outcome evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Operators run the program; independent reviewers challenge governance-to-practice evidence. AI synthesizes inventories and drafts decision materials, but cannot substitute for executive risk decisions or state conformance. Review quarterly and annually.
