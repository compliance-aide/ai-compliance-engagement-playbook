# IEC TS 63074:2023 security aspects of functional safety engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a safety case, certification, or permission to operate machinery.

## Source and applicability

The [official IEC publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/69228), checked 2026-09-04, describes a technical specification connecting IEC 62443 security considerations to machinery safety-related control systems. Its focus is malicious actions affecting safe operation. The publisher excludes non-safety security aspects, intentional physical hardware manipulation or physical bypass, and IT-product or SCS-device design security requirements. These boundaries do not make excluded hazards acceptable: route them to the accountable safety or security process.

This draft uses the public catalog description, not the full licensed specification. Before requirement-level work, a qualified reviewer must confirm the applicable edition, references, amendments, source permissions and machinery-specific obligations. Do not invent clauses, risk thresholds or validation procedures from this guide. Record unresolved applicability as a blocker to the affected conclusion, while continuing authorized inventory work.

## Engagement focus

Connect every scoped safety function to its operating context, security dependencies, credible threat paths, engineering decisions and validation evidence. A secure component, network assessment or completed checklist alone does not establish machine safety. Keep existing safety evidence distinct from the additional security-related argument.

## Roles

The machinery owner approves scope and assigns actions. The functional-safety authority owns safety functions, consequences, acceptance criteria and safe-operation decisions. Security engineers assess threat paths with control-system engineers; suppliers identify supported configurations and limitations. Operations and maintenance owners control access, changes and restoration. An independent reviewer challenges traceability and source interpretation.

AI may organize approved records and draft discrepancies. It cannot determine safety integrity, accept risk, select final safeguards, operate machinery, change configurations, initiate active tests, or approve a safety case. Route those decisions to the named qualified authority.

## Before starting

Obtain a scope record naming machines, sites, safety functions, operating modes, lifecycle stage, configurations, owners and intended decision. Obtain authorized diagrams, safety requirements, prior validation records, change history, supplier guidance and incident records. Record missing or inconsistent inputs with an owner and next action. Confirm evidence access and processing permissions before ingestion; preserve restricted records in their approved system.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Establish the baseline.** Reconcile the complete known scoped machine and configuration population against owner inventories. Give each machine, safety function and evidence item a stable identifier. Record unknown coverage explicitly; do not silently sample or discard evidence. Exit with an owner-confirmed boundary and a list of unresolved inventory gaps.
2. **Map dependencies and modes.** For each safety function, record inputs, logic, outputs and dependencies using approved engineering records. Include engineering stations, remote maintenance, credentials, communications and recovery arrangements where relevant. Separate normal operation, startup, maintenance and degraded modes. Mark undocumented connections as unresolved rather than assuming isolation.
3. **Develop the security-to-safety argument.** Have qualified engineers identify a threat entry point, affected dependency, possible change to the safety function and consequence for each scenario. Link supporting evidence and assumptions. Keep excluded physical-manipulation hazards in a separately owned record; do not delete them from the overall safety workstream. Exit with reviewed scenarios and explicit scope decisions.
4. **Define proposed treatment and verification.** Link each scenario to proposed safeguards, residual assumptions and engineer-approved acceptance criteria. Assess whether a security measure could impede a needed safety action, maintenance or recovery. Record evidence required to demonstrate both intended protection and preserved safety behavior. An AI proposal remains a proposal until the responsible authorities approve it.
5. **Review authorized validation evidence.** Before any new testing, require a separately approved plan specifying environment, personnel, safe state, stop conditions and restoration. This guide authorizes records review only. For each result, preserve exact hardware/software/configuration, mode, procedure, expected outcome, observed outcome and deviations. Explain applicability before reusing evidence from another machine or version.
6. **Resolve findings without erasing history.** Assign each failed or unverified assertion an owner, action and due date. Link corrective changes to affected functions and configurations, then obtain appropriate revalidation evidence. A configuration change, ticket closure or approved exception is not proof that the safety-related problem is resolved. Keep the original finding and any remaining limitations visible.
7. **Prepare the decision package.** Reconcile the entire scoped population with the scenario, treatment and evidence registers. List supported conclusions, failures, uncertainties and untested areas separately. The qualified safety authority makes the final disposition; record the exact scope, conditions and decision evidence. Without that decision, hand off a draft with named unresolved actions.

## Evidence and test plan

Use these original workpaper packages; they are not IEC forms or a substitute for the specification.

| Package | Minimum contents | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safety-function baseline | Machine/function IDs, version, mode, dependencies, source records, gaps and owners | Does the evidence describe the actual configuration and every scoped function? |
| Scenario and treatment register | Entry point, dependency, safety consequence, assumptions, proposed safeguard, residual issue, engineering approval | Is the security-to-safety link justified, and could the treatment introduce a new problem? |
| Validation and decision register | Acceptance criteria, exact subject, authorized procedure, observed results, deviations, retest links, final authority and conditions | Does each conclusion follow from evidence for this configuration and mode? |

Apply the [agent runbook](../agent-runbook.md): classify individual assertions as `supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` or `not_tested`. Split compound claims; a known failure must remain visible even when a different part is uncertain. A complete inventory does not mean every technical combination has been tested.

Fictional author desk case: a remote-access restriction is documented for normal operation, but maintenance mode permits a different connection. Evidence for normal mode cannot support a claim covering both modes. Record the maintenance-mode assertion as unverified, obtain its actual configuration evidence and send it for qualified assessment. Do not experiment on an operating machine to fill the gap.

## Failure branches and decisions

- Conflicting diagrams or versions: preserve both, identify the authoritative owner and block conclusions that depend on the disputed state.
- Missing licensed source or permissions: continue authorized record organization; defer clause interpretation and source ingestion requiring that permission.
- Evidence of an unsafe condition: promptly notify the designated safety/operations authority through the approved incident process. AI does not decide continued operation or independently command a shutdown.
- A safeguard passes its security test but affects a safety function: keep the overall treatment unresolved and require joint safety/security review.

## Cadence and renewal

The owner sets review dates from applicable obligations, risk and engineering plans; this guide invents no annual mandate. Reopen affected records after changes to functions, configurations, connectivity, suppliers, operating modes, threat knowledge, incidents or source requirements. Preserve which previous conclusions are invalidated and why.

## Completion and handoff

Deliver the baseline, scenario register, evidence index, unresolved findings, validation limitations and signed decision references to named recipients in the approved record system. Obtain independent source, skeptical and rights review before publishing this guide. Author desk checks are not independent review, machine validation or proof of AI-agent usability.
