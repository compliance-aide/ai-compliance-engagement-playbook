# IEC TS 63074:2023 security aspects of functional safety engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not IEC text, a functional-safety decision, a safety case, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [IEC TS 63074:2023 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/69228).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a safety-security engagement record that connects the safety-related control system, foreseeable security threats and vulnerabilities, accountable safety and engineering owners, intended operating context, authorized evidence, risk and design decisions, validation, incidents, remediation, and independent review. Preserve the distinction between AI-assisted evidence organization and accountable human judgment about safety integrity, threats, safeguards, system design, and safe operation.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Safety, engineering, operations, maintenance, security, risk, and supplier authorities retain accountable decisions; system and service owners preserve authorized evidence; independent reviewers test traceability and safety-aware readiness. AI may organize authorized records, identify evidence gaps, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot determine safety requirements, make a safety-case conclusion, select or approve safeguards, accept safety or operational risk, alter machine or OT configuration, initiate testing that affects operations, or claim compliance. Review annually and before material system, safety function, threat, supplier, incident, maintenance, or operating-context changes.
