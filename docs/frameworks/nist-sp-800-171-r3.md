# NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an official NIST publication or a claim of
> conformance. Review the current [NIST CUI publications](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/protecting-controlled-unclassified-information/publications)
> and contract terms before each engagement milestone.

## Engagement focus

Confirm contract-driven applicability and the flow of controlled unclassified
information. Establish a defensible boundary, document practical protection
decisions, preserve evidence from people, processes, and technology, validate
claimed implementation, and maintain a dated remediation plan.

## Roles

Operators own the data-flow and implementation record. Independent assessors test the
organization's claim against approved methods. AI may identify uncertain
boundaries, trace authorized evidence, and flag conflicts; it cannot infer compliance
from a document alone, approve a boundary or remediation decision, alter a system, or submit a representation.

## Annual rhythm

Review awards and data flows; update scope when a supplier, application, or
business process changes; perform evidence and test cycles; revisit remediation
dates; prepare for the assessment process named by the governing contract.
