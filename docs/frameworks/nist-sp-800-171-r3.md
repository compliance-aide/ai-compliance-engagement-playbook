# NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not an official NIST publication or a claim of
> conformance. Review the current [NIST CUI publications](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/protecting-controlled-unclassified-information/publications)
> and contract terms before each engagement milestone.

## Engagement focus

Confirm contract-driven applicability and the flow of controlled unclassified
information. Establish a defensible boundary, document practical protection
decisions, preserve evidence from people, processes, and technology, validate
claimed implementation, and maintain a dated remediation plan.

## Roles

Operators own the data-flow and implementation record. Independent assessors test the
organization's claim against approved methods. AI may identify uncertain
boundaries, trace authorized evidence, and flag conflicts; it cannot infer compliance
from a document alone, approve a boundary or remediation decision, alter a system, or submit a representation.

## Annual rhythm

Review awards and data flows; update scope when a supplier, application, or
business process changes; perform evidence and test cycles; revisit remediation
dates; prepare for the assessment process named by the governing contract.

## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Start with the official [NIST CUI publications index](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/protecting-controlled-unclassified-information/publications), the current approved 800-171 release, and controlling contract terms; checked 2026-07-31. This plan uses original language and does not reproduce NIST requirements, assessment content, or a contract-specific determination.

### 1. CUI applicability, boundary, and flow package

- **Request and owner:** Contract, data, and system owners provide applicable award clauses or directions, CUI categories and handling assumptions, approved system boundary, data-flow diagrams, external parties, locations, and unresolved scope questions.
- **Validate and limit:** Independently trace a selected CUI flow from documented intake through storage, processing, transmission, and disposal/return point, identifying the system and owner at each documented handoff. This cannot decide whether information is CUI, interpret a contract, or establish complete boundary coverage.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may compare supplied diagrams and inventories for missing handoffs or version conflicts. Contract and system authorities make applicability and boundary decisions. Refresh before onboarding a new data source, supplier, application, location, or award change.

### 2. Implemented-protection and operating-evidence package

- **Request and owner:** Security and system operators provide approved protection implementation records, relevant configuration/process evidence pointers, account and asset population definitions, training or procedural records where applicable, and evidence of recurring operation.
- **Validate and limit:** Sample one in-scope asset, account, or data-handling process from population definition to implementation evidence, collection time, exception record, and operator explanation. A sample is not a determination that all requirements are implemented or operating effectively.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize metadata, detect missing dates/populations, and draft questions; it cannot change a system, invent evidence, select a compensating measure, or determine conformance. Refresh on change, recurring evidence cadence, or material operational failure.

### 3. Assessment, remediation, and representation package

- **Request and owner:** The assessment owner provides authorized test methods/results, limitations, findings, remediation plan milestones, exception/risk decisions, retest evidence, and any contract-governed representation draft with the named human signer.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected finding or planned action through evidence, owner, target date, corrective work reference, retest result, and unresolved limitation. This does not score an assessment, accept risk, approve a plan, or authorize an external representation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag inconsistent dates and compile a controlled evidence index. Authorized humans approve remediation priorities, risk decisions, scores, and submissions. Refresh after failed testing, missed milestone, material incident, or before any representation.
