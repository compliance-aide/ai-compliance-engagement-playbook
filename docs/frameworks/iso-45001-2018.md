# ISO 45001:2018 — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO content or safety certification. Check the [ISO record](https://www.iso.org/standard/63787.html) and amendment/revision status.

## Engagement focus

Maintain worker/site scope, hazard, incident, contractor, competence, emergency, participation, control, and remediation evidence.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Supervisors and worker representatives validate reality; safety leaders accept decisions; independent reviewers test evidence. AI flags trends, but cannot make safety decisions, accept root cause, or certify. Review monthly, quarterly, and annually.


## Universal engagement contract

Apply the [universal engagement contract](../universal-engagement-contract.md) for the shared applicability, authority, evidence, technical-test, exception, source-change, and renewal requirements that govern this framework engagement.
