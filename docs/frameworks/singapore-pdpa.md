# Singapore PDPA — engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not legal advice or an attestation. Consult the [PDPC legislation page](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a DPO-owned inventory and test consent, purpose, notice, supplier-transfer, security, retention, request, and relevant DNC processes. Rehearse incident triage and independently trace collection through disposal.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Program owners operate the record; independent reviewers sample collection-to-disposal evidence. AI organizes artifacts and proposes test samples, but cannot determine exceptions or notifications, contact individuals, or attest. Review quarterly and renew scope annually.


## Tailored evidence plan

**Source and rights snapshot.** Use the [PDPC legislation page](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act) and qualified Singapore privacy review; checked 2026-07-31. This plan does not decide a PDPA exception, consent validity, notification, DNC result, or individual outcome.

### 1. DPO-owned collection, purpose, and transfer record

- **Request and owner:** DPO appointment/ownership record, personal-data inventory, collection channel, purpose/notice/consent materials, supplier and transfer register, and DNC-process inputs where relevant.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected collection-to-sharing path to notice/consent evidence, system, owner, supplier/transfer entry, and stated purpose. This can support a traceable fact base; it cannot determine legal consent, exception, or transfer compliance.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may organize authorized metadata and flag changed collection or vendor flows. DPO/legal humans approve classifications and decisions. Refresh for new collection, purpose, supplier, transfer, marketing/DNC channel, or material product change.

### 2. Retention, request, and security-operation evidence

- **Request and owner:** Retention/disposal rules and execution evidence, access/correction request log, security/access/change records, incident/exercise records, and remediation actions from privacy, records, and security owners.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace an approved selection to source, timing, owner, limitation, and follow-up while minimizing personal data. This can support observable workflow evidence; it cannot decide an individual's outcome, prove all data was disposed, or determine notification.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may create a redacted evidence index and internal deadline warnings; it may not contact individuals or decide outcomes. Recollect after an incident, request trend, retention/process change, or failed exercise.

### 3. Vendor, exception, and annual review record

- **Request and owner:** Vendor oversight/change evidence, privacy exceptions, management decisions, remediation/retest, DPO annual review, and source-change watch.
- **Validate and limit:** Trace a selected exception or vendor change to human authority, evidence, expiry, and retest. This can support accountable governance; it cannot accept risk or provide an attestation.
- **AI and trigger:** AI may flag stale approvals and prepare a review packet. Humans approve risk, notifications, public statements, and closure. Review quarterly and renew scope annually.
