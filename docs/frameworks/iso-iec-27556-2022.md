# ISO/IEC 27556:2022 privacy-preferences engagement guide

> Original operational guidance, not ISO or IEC text, a consent or preference determination, a data-use authorization, a legal conclusion, or a compliance claim. Confirm current material through the [ISO/IEC 27556:2022 publication record](https://www.iso.org/standard/71674.html).

## Engagement focus

Maintain a privacy-preferences engagement record connecting data uses, purposes, accountable privacy and product owners, authorized preference records, system and supplier dependencies, change history, exceptions, incident evidence, remediation, and independent review. AI can organize authorized material and expose conflicts but cannot choose, infer, or alter a person’s privacy preferences.

## Roles and annual rhythm

Privacy, legal, product, engineering, security, operations, risk, and service owners retain accountable decisions; preference and evidence owners preserve authorized records; independent reviewers test purpose traceability, preference handling, change evidence, exception treatment, and escalation paths. AI may organize authorized records, identify stale evidence, draft questions, and prepare non-authoritative workpapers, but cannot collect consent, infer preferences, change data-use settings, authorize processing, decide legal basis, accept risk, alter production systems, initiate changes, or claim compliance. Review material preference and data-use changes continuously, conduct quarterly evidence reviews, and complete an annual readiness review.
