# Tailored evidence-plan standard

This standard defines the public, original-language evidence plan each
framework guide must contain. It is an engagement-planning aid, not a
certification method, legal opinion, publisher substitute, or assessment
questionnaire.

## Source and rights snapshot

Record the official publisher URL, source status (current, historical,
proposed, or unknown), edition/effective date if available, retrieval date,
and a rights note. Link to protected or licensed material; do not reproduce
its requirements, mappings, questions, tables, or branded templates.

## Framework-specific evidence packages

Each guide contains at least three packages tailored to its own scope and
engagement focus. A package must state:

- an original evidence objective and applicability input for a named human;
- artifact classes to request, expected owner, population/boundary, and period
  or triggering event;
- a safe validation method such as trace, inspect, reperform, or interview,
  including integrity and access constraints;
- what the evidence can support and what it cannot prove;
- the AI action allowed, the human decision or escalation, and the relevant
  source, system, supplier, incident, or annual-renewal trigger.

Technical outputs and policy documents are evidence inputs only. They never by
themselves establish operating effectiveness, regulatory applicability,
conformance, certification, authorization, or a legal conclusion.

## Review and publication rule

Before a plan is marked reviewed, an independent source reviewer confirms the
official link, current-status statement, and rights boundary. A skeptical
reviewer checks that the packages are genuinely framework-specific, testable,
and explicit about non-proof and human authority. Record material
disagreement in the engagement workpaper; do not place customer evidence or
identifiers in this repository.
