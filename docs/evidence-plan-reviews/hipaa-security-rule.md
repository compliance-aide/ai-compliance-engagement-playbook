- **Source reviewer:** `hipaa_source_review`, independent of the drafting agent; source review completed 2026-07-31.
- **Skeptical reviewer:** `hipaa_skeptical_review`, independent of the drafting and source-review agents; skeptical review completed 2026-07-31.

## Review method and result

The source reviewer compared the evidence plan with current eCFR and HHS
materials, then required the plan to distinguish the playbook's annual cadence
from regulatory timing, use the broader business-associate-arrangement wording,
and link current HHS materials. The skeptical reviewer confirmed that the
corrected plan requests bounded evidence for ePHI scope, risk management,
physical/device-media operations, business-associate arrangements, and
operational resilience without collecting ePHI or implying a compliance result.
No remaining material correction was identified.

## Review boundary

This receipt approves engagement-planning language only. It does not determine
HIPAA applicability, covered-entity or business-associate status, a contract's
sufficiency, a safeguard's adequacy, breach/reporting duty, or compliance.
