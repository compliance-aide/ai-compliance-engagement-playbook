- **Source reviewer:** `owasp_top10_source_review`, independent of the drafting agent; source review completed 2026-07-31.
- **Skeptical reviewer:** `owasp_top10_skeptical_review`, independent of the drafting and source-review agents; skeptical review completed 2026-07-31.

## Review method and result

The source reviewer required edition-specific source/status language and a web-
application scope that includes relevant interfaces/APIs. The skeptical reviewer
then required the explicit rights result and evidence-integrity boundaries for
all validation packages: approved read-only/least-privilege access, preserved
provenance, redaction, and no credentials, secrets, or customer payloads. The
corrected guide was accepted as original, practical planning guidance that does
not authorize destructive testing, risk acceptance, or an attestation.

## Review boundary

This receipt approves engagement-planning language only. It does not authorize
testing, determine vulnerability presence, accept risk, establish security,
replace penetration testing, or declare conformance.
