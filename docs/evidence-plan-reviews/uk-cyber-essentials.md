- **Guide:** [UK Cyber Essentials](../frameworks/uk-cyber-essentials.md)
- **Official source:** [NCSC Cyber Essentials resources](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/cyberessentials/resources)
- **Source status and edition:** Current scheme resources; requirements version 3.3,
  effective 2026-04-27, checked 2026-07-31.
- **Rights result:** Link and original operational paraphrase only. No scheme
  questions, requirement text, mappings, or certificate claim were reproduced.
- **Source reviewer:** `evidence_sector` review agent, independent of the plan
  drafting agent; reviewed 2026-07-31.
- **Skeptical reviewer:** `evidence_sector` review agent, acting as a distinct
  critical-review role; reviewed 2026-07-31.

## Review method and result

The reviewer compared the draft against the current NCSC resource and
requirements metadata, then tested whether an agent could identify scope,
provider responsibility, evidence requests, validation limits, human authority,
and renewal triggers without reading a copied scheme checklist.

Three material corrections were made before this receipt was issued:

1. The plan explicitly identifies the five technical themes.
2. The scope package explicitly considers cloud services and end-user devices,
   with separate-scope agreement reserved to the certification body.
3. Provider-operated practices require contract or provider-security evidence
   and a responsibility record.

## Review boundary

This receipt approves the plan's source-linked engagement-planning language
only. It does not approve an applicant scope, evaluate customer evidence,
decide certification readiness, submit a response, or establish a certificate.
