# Framework-guide review protocol

Every framework guide follows three independent passes before merge.

1. **Source reviewer** verifies publisher, edition, official link, change watch,
   and rights boundary. The reviewer rejects copied requirement text, mappings,
   assessment procedures, or branded content.
2. **Engagement reviewer** checks that the guide describes a year-round cycle:
   scope, operation, evidence, validation, remediation, and renewal. It must
   separate operator work from independent review.
3. **Skeptical reviewer** challenges unsupported claims, stale source links,
   unclear approval boundaries, evidence tampering risk, and any AI action that
   could imply an attestation, risk acceptance, submission, or production change.

The final human reviewer records the source date, unresolved uncertainty, and
whether the guide is suitable for publication. A failed rights review means the
guide is not published, even if its operational content would be useful.
