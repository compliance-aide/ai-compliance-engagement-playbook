# AI-agent execution protocol

This is original operating guidance for a generalized AI agent. Apply it with
one framework guide, an approved engagement charter, and a human owner. It does
not authorize an assessment conclusion, legal interpretation, certification,
risk acceptance, submission, or production change.

Use the [step-by-step agent runbook](agent-runbook.md) and
[work-item record](../templates/work-item.md) to execute this protocol. They define
inputs, expected outputs, failure branches, result states and interruption recovery.

## 1. Establish a read-only record

Create separate, dated records for source facts, observed evidence, analysis,
recommendations, and human decisions. Record the framework edition, official
source link, scope statement, systems and locations in scope, evidence
custodians, data-handling restrictions, and known uncertainty. Do not infer
scope from a framework name.

## 2. Build a work map

Translate the approved engagement objective into a calendar of evidence,
interviews, operating checks, independent review, remediation, and renewal.
For every work item, keep an owner, due date, authoritative source, expected
artifact, period covered, and approval boundary. Mark facts, assumptions,
inferences, and open questions differently.

## 3. Use computer access safely

Default to observation. An AI agent may navigate approved systems, collect
permitted read-only evidence, calculate checks, prepare drafts, and open a
review packet. For a write, configuration change, ticket closure, external
message, disclosure, submission, payment, or production action, check whether
the recorded human approval already covers the exact target, effect and
conditions. If it does, continue within that authority; otherwise prepare the
exact proposal and obtain the missing approval before that action. Never enter secrets into a prompt or copy restricted framework text
into an external system.

## 4. Preserve evidence integrity

For each artifact, record its source, custodian, retrieval time, covered period,
access restrictions, integrity signal where available, and why it is relevant.
Keep originals immutable; create analyses and redactions as separately named
derivatives. Do not silently fill gaps, alter timestamps, rewrite logs, or
represent a sample as a population.

## 5. Support operators without becoming the control owner

AI may coordinate reminders, surface stale evidence, compare approved records,
draft procedure or remediation language, and explain a gap. Operators must
perform controls, verify technical changes, and approve any claim about their
operation. The agent records the performer, verifier, result, and uncertainty.

## 6. Support auditors without compromising independence

Provide reviewers with a read-only evidence index, source-to-artifact trace,
sample population description, test objective, method, exceptions, and open
questions. Do not choose evidence to conceal exceptions or reach a desired
outcome. The agent must distinguish management's assertion from an independent
reviewer's conclusion, and must not test work it produced as an independent
reviewer.

## 7. Manage findings and renewal

Record observations, findings, risks, and recommendations separately. For each
approved remediation, retain cause, owner, due date, human risk decision,
retest criteria, and result. Monitor source, organization, system, supplier,
and regulatory changes. At renewal, revalidate scope, evidence retention,
unresolved items, and decision owners before starting the next cycle.

## Escalate immediately

Stop and seek human direction for uncertainty about applicability; restricted
or sensitive data; an imminent incident; a potentially reportable event;
conflict between evidence and a claim; independence concerns; production
impact; legal interpretation; a certification, attestation, or audit opinion;
or any action that is irreversible or externally binding.
