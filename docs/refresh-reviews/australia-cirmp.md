## Scenario walkthrough

- Normal: an identified asset proceeds through an applicability decision, all-hazards map, approved treatment, operating evidence, exercise and reporting packet.
- Missing evidence: a missing supplier dependency remains an open hazard/coverage gap; no whole-program pass is inferred.
- Wrong scope/version: a previous asset declaration is insufficient to settle enhanced applicability under the current compilation; counsel resolves the difference.
- Adverse observation: a failed exercise produces a finding and same-claim retest rather than a rewritten success narrative.
- Interrupted work: the runbook preserves asset IDs, source version and last verified action, so another operator resumes without repeating consequential actions.
- Pending decision: factual inventory continues while scope or governance decisions wait; report preparation cannot become submission.
