## What changed and why

The short data-map narrative now distinguishes voluntary risk management from
legal decisions, traces processing across its lifecycle, separates current and
target state, gives concrete evidence checks and provides a renewal/handoff
route. PR #340's map, process and supplier/event evidence groups were inspected
and expanded. Its older review status does not apply to this rewrite.

## Author walkthroughs

- Normal path: approved processing scope produces a map, risk record, comparable
  profiles, workpapers, decisions, retests and a change queue.
- Missing evidence: an unknown recipient remains a scope gap; it cannot be
  inferred away because the security controls passed.
- Wrong edition: the agent pins the approved version and does not treat the 1.1
  draft as a final replacement for 1.0.
- Adverse result: actual processing inconsistent with a notice opens review;
  the agent preserves both records instead of rewriting the assertion.
- Interruption: the runbook requires original evidence references, saved state
  and authoritative readback before continuing.
- Pending decision: counsel resolves a rights or reportability question; the AI
  neither invents a legal deadline nor waits for annual review to escalate.
