## Scenario walkthrough

- Normal: processing facts lead to reviewed notices/decisions, fictional rights cases, observed operations and renewed records.
- Missing evidence: an unmapped overseas recipient remains a processing gap rather than an assumed approved transfer.
- Wrong scope: agency and organisation timing must be determined before assigning tasks; FOI is a separate route.
- Adverse observation: disposal inconsistent with a hold creates corrective work and retest; a policy document cannot prove deletion.
- Interrupted work: request IDs and approved clocks persist in the work item so the legal clock is not reset on resumption.
- Pending decision: counsel/privacy decides refusal, transfer and notifiability; the agent can prepare facts but cannot issue the response.
