## Scenario walkthrough

- Normal: system boundary and approved selection lead to observed implementation, assessment, conditions and monitoring.
- Missing evidence: absent provider assurance leaves an inherited claim inconclusive; the customer cannot inherit an unexplained pass.
- Wrong version: a new ISM edition triggers a delta review, not silent substitution in the approved plan.
- Adverse observation: failed safeguards enter corrections and milestone records, with a named officer deciding operating conditions.
- Interrupted work: saved system version, evidence references and last test state allow runbook resumption without a second deployment.
- Pending decision: an assessment report does not authorise operation; the officer decision is a separate required record.
