## What changed and why

The previous guide gave a short focus/roles/annual-rhythm narrative. The rewrite
adds an approved boundary, distinct Current and Target Profiles, evidence-linked
assertions, gap dependencies, test expectations, retesting and a concrete handoff.
PR #340's three CSF evidence categories were inspected and retained in expanded
form. Its old independent-review status was not inherited for rewritten content.

## Author walkthroughs

- Normal path: an approved service boundary produces a Current Profile from
  evidence, an approved Target Profile, gap tasks, reviewer workpapers and retest.
  Every transition names an output and accountable owner.
- Missing evidence: a current-state row without operating evidence stays
  inconclusive; it cannot become an exclusion or an achieved target.
- Wrong edition: a 1.1 identifier in a 2.0 record triggers reviewed transition,
  rather than a title-only conversion.
- Adverse result: an operating mismatch creates corrective work and retains the
  original adverse record until retest and authorized disposition.
- Interruption: the linked runbook resumes from the saved work item and target
  readback, preventing an unverified repeated action.
- Pending decision: target priorities remain draft while unaffected authorized
  evidence collection continues.
