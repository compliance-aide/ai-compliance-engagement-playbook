# Compliance work item

Store completed records in the authorized engagement system, not this public
repository. Use one record per check or action. All examples must be fictional.

## Identity and inputs

- Work-item ID and human-readable title:
- Engagement, selected guide and guide revision:
- Governing publisher source, section, edition/status and retrieval date:
- Parent request/coverage record and linked checks, if split:
- Objective/assertion to evaluate (one observable claim):
- Scope: entity, system/process, environment, population and period:
- Owner, independent reviewer and due date/timezone:
- Dependencies and required inputs:
- Existing authority: approver, permitted action/target, conditions and expiry:
- Evidence handling: allowed location/tool, sensitivity, retention and access:
- Current state (`queued`, `ready`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, `awaiting_review`, `complete`, `superseded`):

## Test or action plan

- Exact observation or action, tool/query/version and target:
- Expected result and decision rule, specified before execution (unit, required property, boundary and evidence method):
- Time-based criterion, if any: triggering event, timezone, clock source and approved calculation rule:
- Population, coverage method and any approved sampling rationale:
- Preconditions, operational risks and stop condition:
- Expected artifact and independent readback method:

## Execution receipt

- Start/end timestamp and performer:
- Actual target/account/environment observed (no secrets):
- Evidence IDs and immutable originals or authorized references:
- Expected/obtained counts, pages/batches, period and missing records:
- Actual output, errors and limitations:
- Result (`supported`, `not_supported`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable`, `not_tested`):
- Narrow conclusion supported and facts still unknown:
- Contrary observations and linked unresolved/untested units:
- Saved artifact and readback result:

## Review, follow-up and handoff

- Reviewer identity, independence, date and decision:
- Open disagreement or finding, owner and due date:
- Remediation approval and retest criteria, if needed:
- Authorized exclusion/closure decision, if applicable:
- Next review date and event/source-change triggers:
- Last verified step and exact next action:
- Superseded record or replacement link, if any:

Never use a blank field as an implied approval. Write `unknown` and its resolution
owner, or `not applicable` with the rationale/decision reference. A receipt records
what happened even when the result is adverse or execution failed.
