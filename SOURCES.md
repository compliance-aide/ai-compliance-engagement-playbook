# Framework source register

Each published guide identifies its own official source in the opening boundary
statement. The authoritative public catalog is the [framework index](docs/frameworks/INDEX.md);
the validator confirms that every published guide is linked from that index.

This repository intentionally publishes original operational guidance only. It
does not reproduce proprietary crosswalks, licensed framework text, publisher
assessment procedures, or customer evidence. Framework names are nominative
references, and each publisher retains its rights.

## Publication record required for every guide

- official publisher source or regulator record;
- edition, version, or a statement that applicability must be confirmed;
- rights boundary and excluded material;
- engagement scope and human decision boundary; and
- ongoing source-change monitoring by the engagement owner.

Follow the [source research policy](docs/source-research-policy.md) and
[review protocol](docs/review-protocol.md) before using a guide in a real
engagement. A public guide is a starting operating model, never an assessment,
certification, legal conclusion, or authorization decision.
